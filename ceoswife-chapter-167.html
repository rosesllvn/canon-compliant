A Visit 拜访

July 2026

~8 min read

Having slept late the night before, Jiang Qi was woken early by Mother Jiang, ate a bowl of longevity noodles, and crawled straight back into bed.

By custom, the morning of the first day of the New Year called for a bowl of longevity noodles with two eggs.

Ke Yan, on his side, rose very early, ate noodles, told his parents he was off, and drove to the Jiang house laden with gifts.

At the door, it was Mother Jiang, in an apron, who answered.

Seeing Ke Yan so early, she smiled. "Ke Yan, you're here early — come in."

He came in, smiling. "Mom, Happy New Year."

"Good, good, what a good boy — Happy New Year."

Ke Yan hadn't been called a good boy in an elder's doting tone for many years. He paused, then smiled.

Setting the gifts on the living-room table, Mother Jiang said, "This child — coming is enough, why bring gifts?"

"Mom, it's a small token of my heart, for you and Dad — don't stand on ceremony with me."

At that she smiled wider. "So thoughtful. Xiao Qi's still asleep — ate his noodles this morning and went back to bed."

"Did he?" Ke Yan smiled indulgently. "Jiang Qi works hard, let him sleep. As it happens, I've something to discuss with you and Dad."

"With me?" As he finished, Father Jiang's voice came.

He'd been reading in the study, and came out at the sound of Ke Yan, catching the mention of himself.

"Yes, Dad — Happy New Year. I've something to discuss with you and Mom."

"Come in, then." Father Jiang took him to the study.

Ke Yan carried a bag in with him and went into the study with the Jiang parents.

Meanwhile Jiang Qi still lay sleeping, unaware of what Ke Yan was discussing in secret with his parents.

Their talk done, Ke Yan came out first, leaving the Jiang parents to talk between themselves.

As he emerged, Jiang Lü was coming out for a drink of water; at the sight of Ke Yan her drowsiness fled — wide awake at once.

But Ke Yan didn't see her, going straight into Jiang Qi's room.

Jiang Lü hesitated, set down her cup, and crept to the door to eavesdrop.

Inside, Ke Yan found the expected lump beneath the covers. Jiang Qi had burrowed in so deeply that even his nose was hidden, and Ke Yan quickly tugged the quilt down.

Jiang Qi's face was flushed — whether from the stuffiness or the heating, who could say.

Ke Yan bent and kissed his lips, hard.

Jiang Qi woke to the kiss, saw it was Ke Yan, paused. "So early?" Then, thinking he'd slept too late, made to get up. "I'll get up now."

"It's fine, no rush." Ke Yan wouldn't let him; instead he lay down himself, beside Jiang Qi.

Jiang Qi lifted the quilt, motioning him under — but as he raised it Ke Yan stopped him, tucking it back over Jiang Qi. "It's fine, I'm not cold." And he lay beside him, gathering Jiang Qi, quilt and all, into his arms.

Jiang Qi nuzzled his face into Ke Yan's chest.

Outside the door, Jiang Lü left dejected — the soundproofing was too good; she couldn't hear a thing.

They lay a while, and when Jiang Qi had dressed and they came out, the whole Jiang family but Jiang Bu's son sat on the living-room sofa watching TV.

Not that the two had lain too long — but knowing Ke Yan had come, everyone had gotten up.

Ke Yan wished them all a Happy New Year, chatted a while, then prepared to set off with Jiang Qi for the Ke house.

At the entryway Jiang Qi put on his coat — not quite done when Mother Jiang brought out the gifts she'd readied for the Ke house. Jiang Qi took them in both hands, meaning to set them down to finish his coat, but Ke Yan, already dressed, naturally helped him into it, zipped it up, took the scarf from the entry hook, and wrapped Jiang Qi snug, sealing out every draft.

Then he took the gifts from Jiang Qi's hands and had him put on his shoes.

Jiang Qi bent and slipped them on carelessly, stood. "There, let's go." And made to leave.

"Wait." Ke Yan stopped him, set the gifts aside, crouched before Jiang Qi, and carefully retied a loosened shoelace.

Done, he stood, took the gifts in one hand and Jiang Qi's hand in the other. "We're off, then."

The Jiang family watched, a little dazed. Ke Yan's practiced movements and Jiang Qi's easy acceptance revealed their daily life at a glance: Ke Yan took care of him.

"Good — drive safely." Father Jiang recovered first and nodded.

Once they'd gone, Jiang Lü suddenly bit her pillow. "If I marry, it has to be to a man like that — so thoughtful, so caring, the very model of a good man, this good to my brother; I'm green with envy."

Mother Jiang heard her, and as if struck by a thought, smiled — marriage, yes; to a good man like that would be fine indeed.

Jiang Qi and Ke Yan went down, put Mother Jiang's gifts in the trunk, and drove to the Ke house.

They arrived not too late; stepping in, they saw Ke Xin and Lan Ruyin on the sofa, Ke Shao back too, and Ke Shao's husband playing with Xiao Ke.

Jiang Qi handed the gifts to Lan Ruyin and wished everyone a Happy New Year. "Dad, Mom, Happy New Year — Sis, Brother-in-law, Happy New Year."

He wore new clothes today, a deep-blue coat that set off his fair skin — good-looking and likeable.

"Good, sit." Lan Ruyin smiled; the more she looked at Jiang Qi now, the more pleased she was.

They sat, and Ke Xin said, "After the New Year, you two should think about holding the wedding — even if you can't register here, you can abroad." Same-sex marriage wasn't recognized at home, but many countries abroad recognized it.

"No need." Jiang Qi smiled. "Ke Yan and I think it's not worth the trouble — certificate or no, it's all the same."

Ke Yan nodded; certificate or not, his feelings for Jiang Qi were the same. He'd actually planned to take Jiang Qi abroad to register, but Jiang Qi, without a second thought, said no, finding it unnecessary trouble.

So now their views matched.

Ke Xin nodded, understanding.

Ke Shao glanced at Ke Yan, thinking of the secret he was plotting, and smiled. "Skip the certificate, then — but a wedding's a must. Jiang Qi, has Ke Yan said when the wedding is? Is he planning a surprise?"

Ke Yan had said he'd handle the wedding but not named a date, so Jiang Qi shook his head, not knowing.

Ke Shao smiled mysteriously and said no more — she'd caught her brother's warning look. Wanting to surprise Jiang Qi, was he? She'd keep the secret.

Ke Shao imagined Jiang Qi's face when he discovered the surprise tomorrow. Anyone would be moved by such a gesture.

Thinking it, she glanced at her own husband — his proposal hadn't been half so romantic — but luckily he was good and attentive to her, so she'd spare him kneeling on the washboard.

"It's fine — leave the matter to Ke Yan," Ke Xin said.

"Yes." Lan Ruyin nodded. "Oh — about time to have Auntie Li make lunch; after lunch, Ke Yan can drive you home."

At her words Jiang Qi looked at Ke Yan, puzzled — he'd thought they'd stay the night at the Ke house, and leaving after lunch seemed early.

Catching his puzzlement, Ke Yan only nodded.

Because tomorrow he had a surprise for Jiang Qi, he had much to do this afternoon.

Puzzled though he was, since Ke Yan nodded, Jiang Qi decided to ask him later, on the way out.

Auntie Li cooked fast, and soon lunch was ready.

After eating, and a bit of fruit on the sofa, Jiang Qi and Ke Yan left.

Outside, Jiang Qi asked, "I thought we'd stay tonight — why leave so early? Something urgent?"

"I've something to do this afternoon — I'll drop you home first." Ke Yan took his hand and nodded.

"What thing?"

"Can't tell you yet — you'll know tomorrow."

"Can't you tell me now?" Jiang Qi was curious.

"Not yet." Ke Yan kissed his face, refusing.

"All right then." Jiang Qi let it go, and they got in.

At the Jiang house, Ke Yan unbuckled, leaned over and kissed Jiang Qi's lips; Jiang Qi kissed back, and Ke Yan stroked his face. "I'm off, then — see you tomorrow."

"All right, drive safely." Jiang Qi got out, watching Ke Yan's car pull away before turning to go up.

Home, Mother Jiang was watching TV; seeing him back, she paused. "Why so early?"

"Ke Yan had something to do, so he dropped me off." Jiang Qi hung up the coat Ke Yan had put on him that morning.

"Right — Ke Yan must be busy today." She nodded.

"You know what Ke Yan's busy with?" Jiang Qi grew more curious.

She quickly shook her head. "I don't know."

Jiang Qi was only half-convinced — why this odd feeling?

What was Ke Yan keeping from him?
