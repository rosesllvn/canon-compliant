Chapter 133

Holding Hands at the Company 在公司里牵手

July 2026

~8 min read

After dinner, Mother Jiang had the two of them stay the night and go straight to work the next day.

They stayed. Back in the room, Ke Yan got out his phone and found it dead; he charged it on Jiang Qi's charger a while before it powered on.

The moment it did, a dozen-odd missed calls showed — Yang Shaoyu's, and Xiao Feng's.

He called Yang Shaoyu first.

Yang Shaoyu answered. "Ke Yan, did you forget there was a meeting this afternoon? Why couldn't I reach you? Xiao Feng called me too."

At that, Ke Yan finally remembered, and frowned. "I forgot. My phone died."

"Of course — only matters concerning Jiang Qi could make you forget work entirely."

"We'll hold the meeting tomorrow." Ke Yan said a couple more words and hung up.

"You shower first." Jiang Qi handed Ke Yan a set of pajamas.

"Yours?" Ke Yan took them.

"Mm — though mine are too small. Shall I get you a set of my brother's?" Jiang Qi was smaller than Ke Yan; the eldest brother was closer to his build.

"No need, yours are fine. Other people's things feel wrong." Ke Yan couldn't get used to anyone else's things — except Jiang Qi's.

Jiang Qi's room had no private bath, so it had to be the one outside.

Ke Yan showered and put on Jiang Qi's pajamas — too small indeed; loose as they were, they pulled tight on him.

Back in the room, Jiang Qi looked him over. "Loose on me, a size too small on you."

"You're too thin."

"I've put on quite a bit of weight lately."

"Have you?" Ke Yan sat Jiang Qi on his lap and felt his waist — soft and warm, very pleasant.

"That tickles." Jiang Qi squirmed in his arms.

Ke Yan pressed him down onto the bed and kissed his face and mouth. "Your mouth is sweet. What did you eat just now?"

Jiang Qi thought. "Mung bean cakes."

"How can you eat sweets right after dinner? What if you get indigestion?"

Jiang Qi tipped his head up and kissed him, stopping his nagging.

When the kiss ended, Ke Yan tried to continue his lecture. Jiang Qi kissed him again, tongue brushing and hooking his. Rarely met with such ardor, Ke Yan forgot indigestion and pulled him closer.

Before they knew it, Ke Yan's hand had slipped under Jiang Qi's clothes, pushing them up.

"Knock, knock—" The sudden rap at the door froze them both. Jiang Qi jumped and scrambled off Ke Yan, straightening his clothes. "Come — come in."

Ke Yan sat up too, and in came Mother Jiang with a hand-warmer.

She found them on the bed: Ke Yan composed and blank-faced, Jiang Qi flushed and visibly flustered. One look revealed what they had been doing.

Jiang Qi's mouth was red and swollen, his hands still tugging at his clothes.

She sighed. She'd had little hope of Jiang Qi being on top to begin with; now, by the look of it, the odds were precisely zero.

She set the hand-warmer on the bed. "It's warm — finish your shower and get to bed."

"I — I know." Jiang Qi tucked the warmer into the quilt.

"I'll go now. Sleep early." She kept stressing it on her way out, and the words left Jiang Qi's face burning; he grabbed his clothes and announced he was off to shower.

Ke Yan gave a soft laugh, then went out to fill the thermos cup with hot water.

By the time Jiang Qi came back from his shower, Ke Yan was lying in bed watching television. Jiang Qi burrowed quickly under the covers, and Ke Yan pulled him in. Jiang Qi started. "Why aren't you wearing anything?"

"Too tight. Took them off."

"Aren't you cold?" Jiang Qi rubbed Ke Yan's bare arm.

"Not cold. Good for warming you up."

Jiang Qi laughed. "No book tonight? Rare to see you just watching TV."

"It's not bad now and then. Warm yet?" Ke Yan was like an oversized hand-warmer — one that never went cold.

Better than a hand-warmer, and warmer too.

"Mm. So comfortable." Jiang Qi nestled against him.

"What are you doing at the door?" Father Jiang had come out for water and found Mother Jiang with her ear pressed to Jiang Qi's door, eavesdropping.

"Shh — just listening to what they're up to."

"Wife — it's not me, it's you doing this — what if the children catch you? And what are you trying to hear?" Father Jiang dropped his voice too, afraid Jiang Qi and Ke Yan would hear.

"Just a quick listen. Come on, back to the room." Hearing nothing, Mother Jiang gave it up and turned back.

Father Jiang watched her go, glanced at the door. Lucky they hadn't been caught — how mortifying that would have been.

The next day, after breakfast, Jiang Qi and Ke Yan headed to the company, with Jiang Lü along since it was on the way.

Jiang Lü sat in the back, Ke Yan driving, Jiang Qi in the passenger seat. Watching them, she thought: they really did look like a married couple. No — like two husbands.

She quietly pulled out her phone, muted it, and snapped a photo of the two of them.

The moment Ke Yan reached the company he had Yang Shaoyu announce a meeting shortly.

And the moment Jiang Qi reached his office, Xiao An fired off a string of questions: "Did Auntie really agree to you two being together?"

"When's the wedding?"

"Have you met the CEO's parents yet?"

Jiang Qi looked at her, helpless. "Didn't you used to come in late? Why so early lately?"

"I'm excited, so I came in early." Xiao An had been so thrilled she'd barely slept; the moment her alarm went off she was up.

"Well, since you're in early, get to work — I have to as well."

"Fine, just answer me one question."

"Ask, then." Jiang Qi figured if he didn't, Xiao An would camp here all day.

"Have you met the CEO's parents?"

"I have, and they've agreed too." Thinking it over, Jiang Qi felt he'd been lucky indeed — both sets of parents agreeing to him and Ke Yan.

"Wonderful. I'll get to work, then." With a satisfying answer, Xiao An felt charged with energy for the whole day.

She left, and Jiang Qi sat down with a smile. He was in a fine mood today.

Because yesterday's meeting hadn't finished, it had been pushed to today, and the senior staff arrived on time, hoping there'd be no hour-long wait this time.

There would be no wait; Ke Yan was already in the conference room. Beside Jiang Qi, Lin Zheng looked ill. Yesterday's hour-long wait for a meeting that never happened had knotted his stomach all day. Now the rescheduled meeting threatened to do it again.

Seeing Lin Zheng's pale face, Jiang Qi reassured him. "Don't worry, Ke Yan's in a good mood today."

"Really? That's a relief — at least I feel a bit better."

"Ke Yan's not so frightening, really, and he doesn't fly off the handle for no reason. Don't be so scared of him — he's very patient."

He's only patient with you.

Lin Zheng thought it silently.

Still — listening to Jiang Qi say it, Lin Zheng suddenly noticed Jiang Qi seemed different from before.

But different how? He couldn't put his finger on it.

After the meeting Ke Yan left first; only once he'd gone did the others rise and leave.

His stomach less wretched today thanks to Jiang Qi's reassurance, Lin Zheng meant to chat with him — but turned to find Jiang Qi long gone.

Where had Jiang Qi gone? The moment Ke Yan left the conference room, Jiang Qi had followed, and seeing him ahead, called out, "Ke Yan."

Ke Yan turned.

Xiao Feng and a secretary walking behind Ke Yan stopped too at the sound and stayed behind him.

Jiang Qi smiled. "It's nearly noon and I'm hungry. Come have lunch with me."

Ke Yan raised an eyebrow at him.

Jiang Qi's voice carried. The near-command made the staff stare and break into a cold sweat, though some gleefully awaited the spectacle of him receiving the cold shoulder.

After all — however good a friend you were with the CEO, don't forget that temperament of his; and here, in front of so many people, to order the CEO about like that, well, the man clearly had a death wish.

But — except for Xiao Feng — no one expected Ke Yan to actually smile at Jiang Qi, hand his documents to the secretary, walk over, and say, gently, "All right."

"Let's go, then." Jiang Qi took Ke Yan's hand.

Ke Yan's step hitched in surprise, but he recovered at once, gripped Jiang Qi's hand firmly, and walked on without pause.

Everyone stood rooted. Was this amiable man truly their CEO? And unless their eyes deceived them, he and Jiang Qi were walking away hand in hand. No matter how close two male friends were, they were not usually that intimate.

This was far too intimate — it made them look more like a couple. But what was going on between the CEO and Manager Jiang?

Watching it, Wang Siqi clenched her fists, nails biting deep into her palms, leaving curved marks.

Could it really be — they were lovers? Together?
