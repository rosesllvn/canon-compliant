The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 119

Isn't He a Man? 不是男的吗

July 2026

~5 min read

After hearing Sun Nianqing out, Wang Siqi spent the afternoon turning it over. She had doubts, but Sun Nianqing had not looked as though she were lying.

She'd better look into it properly.

Though she'd have to wait until tomorrow to have her secretary check; everyone had clocked off by now.

Wang Siqi gathered her things to leave too.

As she passed the secretary's desk by the door with her handbag, it struck her — wasn't her own secretary named Jiang Qian? Also JQ.

Could it be her? Wang Siqi stared at the secretary's desk, trying to think whether there'd ever been any sign — say, seeing her with Ke Yan.

But she'd never seen it once, and besides, Jiang Qian wasn't especially pretty.

Whereas she was forever seeing Jiang Qi at Ke Yan's side.

Wang Siqi shook her head, irritated. Why did she keep landing on Jiang Qi?

If Jiang Qi were a woman, he'd be worth suspecting — but he was a man, so it was impossible.

Unable to come up with anything either way, she took the elevator down.

And the Jiang Qi that Sun Nianqing and Wang Siqi were hunting for was, just then, buying cake across from the company.

Coming out, he glanced at the storefront being renovated next door, wondering what shop it would be.

Home again, the two of them ate first; after dinner Jiang Qi sent Ke Yan off to shower while he went to the study to check whether his hidden gift had been discovered.

Once satisfied, he reshelved the books to cover it again.

Back in the bedroom, Ke Yan had just come out of his shower.

"That was quick," Jiang Qi said, picking up his change of clothes from the bed.

"Mm. Go shower." Ke Yan toweled his hair.

"All right." Jiang Qi went into the bathroom.

Ke Yan left the room, and passing the study doorway, glanced in.

Then went on down for a glass of water.

He wondered what Jiang Qi had hidden in there.

The next day Wang Siqi came in earlier than usual. Her secretary Jiang Qian was already there — ordinarily Wang Siqi felt nothing about her, but today she found her especially hard to look at.

"You — come in here a moment."

Jiang Qian rose from her seat. Why did Manager Wang's look at her seem so strange today?

"Manager, is something the matter?" Jiang Qian stepped in, and at Wang Siqi's signal, shut the office door.

"Go look up everyone in the company whose name starts with JQ."

"JQ? All right, I understand — and, well, my own initials are JQ too."

"I know. You can go." Wang Siqi sent her out.

She planned to take Jiang Qian to the canteen at noon. Ke Yan ate there, and his eyes would reveal whether anything lay between them.

Sun Nianqing held the data she'd just pulled: five people in the company with the initials JQ.

Two were a security guard and a cleaning lady; besides them there were Jiang Qi, Jiang Qian, and Jing Qing.

Ruling out Jiang Qi left Jiang Qian and Jing Qing — Jiang Qian, Wang Siqi's secretary, and Jing Qing, who was already a mother of two.

So the likeliest now was Wang Siqi's secretary, Jiang Qian.

Sun Nianqing frowned, irritated. What was Wang Siqi playing at — hadn't she figured it out yet?

Irritated as the two of them were, their work didn't suffer. At last noon came, and Wang Siqi stepped out of her office. "Come on," she said to Jiang Qian, "let's get lunch."

"All right…" Toward the usually cold and today distinctly odd Wang Siqi, Jiang Qian didn't dare refuse.

They reached the canteen, but no Ke Yan in sight, and Wang Siqi's stomach tightened. Surely he wasn't skipping the canteen today?

Just as she thought it, the elevator opened and Ke Yan stepped out. Wang Siqi let out a breath.

But before she'd finished exhaling she saw Jiang Qi behind him, and her teeth ground together. Jiang Qi again.

No — right now the task was to determine whether Jiang Qian was the JQ on that ring.

Wang Siqi trailed after Ke Yan, thinking how to test it.

Then she saw Sun Nianqing, behind, give Jiang Qian a shove. Jiang Qian pitched forward — Ke Yan had been right in front of her, but Sun Nianqing's push had landed off, knocking Jiang Qian askew.

She lurched toward Jiang Qi instead. Believing she would fall against Ke Yan, Jiang Qian felt a flash of delight—but amid startled cries, Ke Yan turned, pulled Jiang Qi protectively into his arms, and let Jiang Qian sprawl across the floor.

Jiang Qi looked at Jiang Qian on the ground, then realized Ke Yan had him in his arms — but luckily everyone's attention was on Jiang Qian. Dazed, he asked Ke Yan, "What happened to her?"

"She fell." Ke Yan let go of Jiang Qi and watched the others help Jiang Qian up, not a flicker of tenderness for the fallen beauty.

"Maybe her heels were too high." Jiang Qi noted Jiang Qian's tall, thin heels.

"Right." Ke Yan picked up his tray and, taking Jiang Qi's hand, sat down.

Sun Nianqing fled the moment she shoved Jiang Qian, but Wang Siqi had seen everything. Ke Yan showed no concern for the fallen woman; the instant she threatened to topple onto Jiang Qi, he tensed and shielded him.

On top of the way Ke Yan's manner toward Jiang Qi was always so different.

The feeling grew and grew in Wang Siqi that Jiang Qi was the real "JQ" — but… wasn't Jiang Qi a man?
