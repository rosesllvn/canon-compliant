The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Monday meant no sleeping in, even in the bitter cold. At least Jiang Qi could drive instead of cramming onto the bus.

He got out as usual in the company lot, taking in the rows of filled spaces — only the few beside Ke Yan's stood empty, no one daring to park there but Yang Shaoyu.

Speaking of Yang Shaoyu, Jiang Qi thought of running into Lin Zirui yesterday, and wondered what he'd bought.

"Jiang Qi?" Ke Yan roused the woolgathering Jiang Qi, and the two rode up together.

Jiang Qi got off first; Ke Yan rode on to the 39th.

"Manager." Xiao An called him over.

Though Xiao An usually called Jiang Qi by name, in the office she sometimes called him "Manager."

"Have you bought the CEO a birthday gift?"

"What makes you so sure I'll get Ke Yan one?"

"Don't you live with the CEO?" Xiao An winked — meaning, don't bother hiding it, I already know.

"Jiang Lü told you?"

"Mm."

"Get back to work." Jiang Qi shooed her out of the office.

"Hmph, fine, keep your secrets." Xiao An went back to her desk — and suddenly noticed Sun Nianqing at the door.

She wondered what Sun Nianqing was doing there. Sun Nianqing, catching Xiao An looking, shot her a glare and left.

Sun Nianqing had come to find Jiang Qi, but that Xiao An kept hovering around him and gave her no chance to ask.

Back at her own desk, Sun Nianqing thought of the shop Ke Yan had gone into yesterday. Did he have a girlfriend, then? Why else buy a ring?

The day before, Sun Nianqing had spotted Ke Yan while shopping with a friend and followed him—never expecting him to enter a jewelry store.

At first she'd thought: a girlfriend? Buying her jewelry? But she hadn't expected Ke Yan to buy a ring.

And to have the clerk engrave it. Once Ke Yan had gone, she'd gone in to ask the clerk what he'd had engraved.

Recalling the clerk's words — it read "KY & JQ." KY was Ke Yan, but JQ? Who was JQ?

Sun Nianqing's face darkened. Did Ke Yan really have a girlfriend? And was he, perhaps, planning to marry?

No — she wasn't ready to give Ke Yan up that easily. But first she had to find out who this JQ was.

At that very moment, "JQ" was loafing in the break room with milk tea, gazing through the window. Across the street, workers renovated a former boutique. Was it being refitted or replaced?

Jiang Qi took another sip of milk tea, left the break room, and went back to work.

Sun Nianqing was set on finding this JQ, but had no idea where to start. Ke Yan rarely left the office early except to rest — so might JQ be someone in the company?

But of those close to Ke Yan, there was Yang Shaoyu, and Jiang Qi, and no one else.

Better just ask Jiang Qi, Sun Nianqing decided.

She hadn't found her chance with Jiang Qi, though, before she ran into Yang Shaoyu, who happened to be having lunch in the canteen. It struck her — why not just ask Yang Shaoyu?

"Assistant Yang, you eat lunch here too?" She came over to make conversation.

"Yes." Yang Shaoyu glanced at her.

"I'd like to ask you something." She got straight to it.

"What is it?"

"Does Ke Yan have a girlfriend?"

"A girlfriend? Why do you ask?"

"I saw him buy a ring."

"Did he? Didn't realize he was ready to propose." Yang Shaoyu muttered to himself.

Sun Nianqing clenched her fist. "So — he really does have a girlfriend?"

"Proposing, so naturally there's someone — though not necessarily a woman." That last bit Yang Shaoyu said under his breath, so Sun Nianqing didn't catch it.

She didn't press, only asked about the possibility she'd already guessed. "Is it someone in the company?"

"Mm, it is." Yang Shaoyu's phone rang. One glance transformed his perfunctory expression.

"Hello — Zirui, have you eaten? I'll come pick you up tonight."

Seeing Yang Shaoyu on the phone, Sun Nianqing tactfully withdrew.

But — who in the company?

Of the truly beautiful women in the company, there were only herself and Wang Siqi, and a couple of others.

If not her, then surely not Wang Siqi.

Sun Nianqing didn't notice the somewhat twisted look on her own face just then.

"Oho, you've been dumped? You look like you've seen a ghost."

"What did you say?" Sun Nianqing stood and glared at Wang Siqi.

"Gone deaf too, have we?"

"Hmph. You'll be looking like this soon enough." Sun Nianqing, far from angered, smiled.

"What's that supposed to mean?" Wang Siqi sat down across from her.

"Don't you know? Ke Yan's planning to propose."

"Is that true?"

"Would I make it up?"

"To whom?" Wang Siqi hadn't expected Ke Yan to have a girlfriend.

Sun Nianqing had no intention of kindly sharing her discovery — but then again, telling Wang Siqi might shake something loose.

So she told Wang Siqi what she'd found, and the moment Wang Siqi heard the letters JQ, they struck her as oddly familiar.

Suddenly a person came to mind — JQ. Jiang Qi?

"Could it be Jiang Qi?" Wang Siqi said it before she could stop herself.

"Are you sick? Jiang Qi's a man."

"True. And you're the sick one." Wang Siqi dismissed the thought. She noticed Jiang Qi only because Ke Yan treated him unlike anyone else, and because he was so often at Ke Yan's side.

But Jiang Qi was a man. It couldn't be him. So who?

"Well — got any leads?"

"All I know is it's someone in the company."

"Is that so?" Wang Siqi found herself thinking of Jiang Qi again, and smacked her own head. Was she losing it, fixating on Jiang Qi like this?

At the next table, Manager Lin Zheng overheard and laughed inwardly. Wasn't it obviously Manager Jiang Qi? How could they not know? Assistant Yang surely did. Lin Zheng had once dreaded knowing the secret; now it made him feel unlike the others, almost as though he belonged among the people the CEO trusted.

Though — all right, in truth, he'd only found out by accidentally seeing.
