The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 108

Glum as he felt, Yang Shaoyu still drove Lin Zirui to the hospital without a word of complaint.

He'd meant to wait in Lin Zirui's office as usual, but today Lin Zirui insisted he go home. "You head back — you've got work tomorrow. I'll call my second brother to come get me once I've finished up here."

"It's fine, I can rest in your office." Yang Shaoyu was thinking he'd take Lin Zirui for a late bite once he was done — couldn't have him going hungry.

"Go home, now." Lin Zirui wouldn't hear it, and simply shooed him off. "If you won't go home and rest, then clear off."

"All right, all right, I'm going home to rest." Seeing Lin Zirui annoyed, Yang Shaoyu said it quickly and went.

Before leaving, he bought some pastries and milk and set them on Lin Zirui's desk, and only then left.

In the car, he did as Lin Zirui said and went home to rest.

Meanwhile, Mo Lü and Ma Tong — now at Ke Yan's place — hadn't adjusted to the time difference and had no energy left for mischief, so they'd turned in early.

Before bed, Mo Lü made a point of handing Jiang Qi a disc, saying mysteriously, "This is a gift we put together specially for you and Ke Yan."

"What is it?" Jiang Qi turned the disc over.

"Can't tell you yet. You'll know once you watch it."

"Why so mysterious?"

"This is a classic we went to great trouble to track down — you absolutely must watch it." And with that she went off to bed.

Jiang Qi held the disc, looking at the pair's mysterious smiles — smiles with a distinctly lecherous edge to them…

Showered, Ke Yan and Jiang Qi lay in bed, Ke Yan reading, Jiang Qi watching television — the same melodramatic family soap he'd been following lately.

The heroine discovered her husband's affair and fled to a riverbank, where she met the true male lead. He too was married, had stormed out after fighting with his wife, and promptly lost his memory after a blow to the head. Amnesiac, he fell for the heroine—only to recover, remember his wife, and abandon her to accusations of home-wrecking. Meanwhile, her younger sister married his younger brother, who moved his pregnant girlfriend into their home.

Jiang Qi didn't even much like it; he was just idly curious how the story would unfold, watching the heroine's sister threaten divorce over and over without ever actually divorcing.

The shrill quarreling made reading impossible. Ke Yan glanced at the television and gathered Jiang Qi against him. "Is it any good?" He saw no appeal, least of all in an argument that had begun when he opened his book and somehow still continued.

"It's all right." Jiang Qi said it around a yawn.

"Sleepy?"

"Mm, but I don't want to sleep yet."

"Watch your show, then. Sleep when it's over." Ke Yan pulled the quilt up over him.

"What's this?" Ke Yan had just spotted the disc on Jiang Qi's nightstand.

"Oh — Mo Lü and Ma Tong gave it to me. Said it's a gift." Jiang Qi reached for it.

"Knowing them, it's probably nothing good." Ke Yan made to put it in the drawer.

"Let's see what it is." Remembering their mysterious looks, Jiang Qi was curious about the contents.

"All right, give me a moment." Ke Yan got up to load it. "Looks like an old one — a bit worn."

The disc began to play as they watched in the same position, Ke Yan's arms around Jiang Qi. Despite its worn appearance, it played perfectly. At first, Jiang Qi wondered whether it was a film he had not seen.

But by degrees something felt off. Why were two men getting all tangled up — and starting to undress?

Then it clicked. Wasn't this a GV?

"Stop watching." Jiang Qi reached for the remote to switch it off.

"Weren't you the one who wanted to watch?" Ke Yan stopped his hand.

Now Jiang Qi was mortified. Mo Lü and Ma Tong — those two!

"I'm not watching, I'm not, let's just sleep." Jiang Qi lay down, pulled up the quilt, and made to sleep.

"We won't watch it, then." Ke Yan had no interest in the thing and switched off the television — but began undoing the buttons of Jiang Qi's pajamas.

Jiang Qi shut his eyes and feigned sleep.

Ke Yan undid them one by one, kissing his way down, every place his mouth touched left with an ambiguous mark.

His hands, meanwhile, kept stroking at Jiang Qi's sensitive spots.

That was more than Jiang Qi could feign sleep through; he opened his eyes, Ke Yan gave a soft laugh, and Jiang Qi looped his arms around his neck. "Gently — I've got work tomorrow."

"Mm." Ke Yan agreed — but by the end of the night Jiang Qi was nearly falling apart.

Ke Yan woke first the next morning, Jiang Qi still asleep. He moved with deliberate care so as not to wake him, and after changing, before heading down, pressed a light kiss to Jiang Qi's forehead. Jiang Qi would rest at home today.

Mo Lü and Ma Tong were already up when Ke Yan came down, eating the breakfast Auntie Li had laid out.

Mo Lü took in Ke Yan's bright, well-rested look and smiled wickedly. "Well? Good night, was it?"

"Mm. Don't go bothering him today — let him rest." Ke Yan sat down to eat too.

"We know, we know, no need to nag."

Breakfast done, with Ke Yan about ready to leave for the office, he reminded Auntie Li not to forget the dishes Jiang Qi liked for lunch, and to have it ready early, then headed out.

He reached the company just as Yang Shaoyu arrived, and they rode up together.

Both men in the elevator were strikingly handsome and dignified, yet the contrast was stark.

One looked bright and thoroughly rested; the other ran on fumes after no sleep at all.

"What's with you? Weren't you with Doctor Lin last night?"

"How do you know I was with Zirui?" One look at Ke Yan and Yang Shaoyu could guess what he'd been up to last night — no wonder Jiang Qi hadn't ridden in with him today.

"Didn't you call Jiang Qi?"

"Hah. Something came up at the hospital last night."

Looking at the state of him, Ke Yan couldn't help a touch of schadenfreude.

Jiang Qi slept the whole morning before waking, patted his empty stomach — so hungry.

When he came down, Auntie Li had lunch ready, and Jiang Qi privately cheered the timing: starving as he was, and worried there'd be nothing to eat.

"Auntie Li, where are Mo Lü and Ma Tong?" he asked, once half-full.

"Oh, they couldn't sit still — went out to meet friends and go shopping."

"Oh." Jiang Qi had meant to settle scores with them over the strange thing they'd given him.
