Chapter 149

Trying On the Wedding Suits 试结婚要穿的西装

July 2026

~4 min read

Since they were wedding suits, Ke Yan even called Ke Shao to ask which shop was best.

"I thought the bridal place I went to was good — go take a look, you can get wedding photos done while you're there."

"All right, got it." Ke Yan hung up and asked Jiang Qi, "Shall we take wedding photos today too?"

"Wedding photos? But will they shoot them for us?" The fact that they were both men crossed Jiang Qi's mind, though that wasn't his only hesitation.

"Of course they will."

"But I'd rather not do wedding photos." Jiang Qi said why he hesitated.

"Why?" Ke Yan was surprised.

"I don't like that staged feeling. Better to take one at the wedding itself and hang it in the room." Jiang Qi preferred that — captured in a moment of real happiness, not posed.

"All right, that's a fine idea too." Ke Yan nodded; it really was.

He drove to the place Ke Shao had named.

True to Ke Shao's recommendation, the shop's décor was both lavish and tasteful.

The moment they entered, the staff chorused a welcome.

One came over. "Gentlemen — which of you is the groom?"

"Both of us," Ke Yan answered.

The clerk took in Ke Yan's bearing and knew at once he was no ordinary man. Even in plain clothes, he carried more presence than some men in suits. In plain terms: he was wealthy.

"For suits, this way, upstairs, please." The clerk led on.

Ke Yan followed, holding Jiang Qi's hand.

"Are you two friends, marrying on the same day?" the clerk asked warmly — then, turning to see their clasped hands, stammered, "Then — then where's the bride? It'd be best to bring the bride along."

"There's no bride. The two of us are marrying." Ke Yan glanced at her.

The clerk started, sensing that another clueless question would go badly for her. "This way, please." She looked calm, but inwardly reeled: in nearly ten years on the job, this was the first time two men had come in together to choose wedding suits.

She brought them to the men's suiting area and quickly pulled two suits, lifting one white one. "Both are our newest styles — and this white suit would suit this gentleman well." She pointed at Jiang Qi.

Ke Yan nodded; the clerk had an eye after all — he too thought Jiang Qi looked good in white.

"Go try it." He handed it to Jiang Qi.

"All right." Jiang Qi headed for the fitting room.

While he changed, the clerk pitched Ke Yan. "We can also take wedding photos here — many couples are very happy with theirs."

"Wedding photos? I see — no, thank you." Since Jiang Qi didn't want them, Ke Yan only nodded and declined.

But a sales chance wasn't easily given up. "A wedding only happens once in a lifetime, and wedding photos are the same…"

Before she finished, Jiang Qi came out changed, and Ke Yan stood at once and went to him.

The clerk swallowed the rest of her pitch.

"How is it?" Jiang Qi asked.

"Very handsome." Ke Yan's eyes lit; he smiled and adjusted Jiang Qi's collar.

"It really suits you," the clerk came over too. "Few people wear a white suit as well as you."

She truly meant it, not just for the sale.

"And you? Have you looked?"

"For this gentleman, a black suit might suit better." The clerk offered the suit she'd readied.

"Wear a white one." Jiang Qi hadn't seen Ke Yan in white.

"You pick for me?"

"All right."

Jiang Qi looked around the shop and quickly chose one he thought suited Ke Yan.

"How about this?"

"You picked it, so I'll try it." Ke Yan took it to the fitting room.

The clerk glanced at Jiang Qi. The other gentleman had declined wedding photos; should she try this one?

"Sir, we can take wedding photos here too."

"Wedding photos? No, thank you."

"But aren't wedding photos like the wedding itself — once in a lifetime?"

"I'd rather take ours at the wedding — it'd look better."

"True." The clerk thought it not bad either, and that today's customers were no simple pair; she'd hoped to make a little extra business.

Then Ke Yan came out changed, and at the sight of him Jiang Qi shot to his feet. "Is it my good eye, or do you just look good in everything?"

The clerk was surprised too. She hadn't expected white to suit Ke Yan, but with that face and figure he could wear anything—and Jiang Qi had chosen well.

"It clearly suits you. We'll take these two, then."

Once they'd changed back, the clerk wrapped the suits, ran the card, and saw them off to the car.

"We've been together seven years, and only now does it finally feel like getting married," Jiang Qi said, voicing his heart.

"And before?" Ke Yan had felt married long ago.

"Before it never felt quite real — that we'd marry, and with our parents' blessing besides."

"Then from now on, keep it firmly in mind: you're getting married." At a red light Ke Yan took Jiang Qi's hand and squeezed.

"I know." Jiang Qi smiled, the reality of it slowly sinking in.

Yes — he and Ke Yan were getting married, and would go on just as happy after.
