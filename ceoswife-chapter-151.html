Chapter 151

Our Family's Child 我们家的孩子

July 2026

~5 min read

Mother Yang's cooking wasn't ready yet, but someone arrived first.

They'd been chatting when the doorbell rang; a servant answered, and Lin Zirui saw a girl come in behind the servant, something in her hands.

"Xiao Lin, what brings you here?" Father Yang clearly knew the girl, calling her Xiao Lin at the sight of her.

"Oh, Uncle — when you were leaving just now, my dad forgot to give you something, so he sent me over with it." The girl called Xiao Lin came into the living room, and though she spoke to Father Yang, her eyes kept drifting to Yang Shaoyu.

"That father of yours really has no memory. Sit, sit." Father Yang took the thing and had her sit.

At that she went straight over and sat down beside Yang Shaoyu.

"Haven't seen Xiao Lin in ages — got a boyfriend yet?" the eldest brother asked.

"Not yet — I haven't met anyone I like." Xiao Lin was a little shy.

Lin Zirui glanced at her, guessing she was the daughter of one of Father Yang's friends, and close with the Yang family.

"Zirui, this is the daughter of a friend of mine — Xu Xiaolin." Father Yang made the introduction, knowing Lin Zirui wouldn't know her.

"Hello, I'm Lin Zirui." He greeted her.

"Hello, I'm Xu Xiaolin — Shaoyu and I are childhood friends." Xu Xiaolin looked at Lin Zirui curiously.

Shaoyu? At the familiar address, Lin Zirui couldn't help glancing at Yang Shaoyu. Seeing Xu Xiaolin seated so close to him, he frowned faintly, then smoothed it away. Getting worked up over a young woman would be petty—but childhood friends? He stole another look at Yang Shaoyu.

Yang Shaoyu caught every reaction with secret delight. Zirui was jealous. Still, there had never been anything between him and Xu Xiaolin, and he was about to explain when Mother Yang called everyone to wash up and eat. The explanation would have to wait.

Since the food was ready, Father Yang said, "Xiao Lin, stay for lunch today."

Xu Xiaolin had meant to go home for lunch, but seeing the rarely-home Yang Shaoyu, she nodded. "All right."

Everyone sat, Lin Zirui beside Yang Shaoyu of course, Xu Xiaolin on Yang Shaoyu's other side.

Perhaps because Father Yang had caught so many, fish appeared in nearly every form: soup, sweet-and-sour fish, and more.

"Here, have some fish soup first — not the least bit fishy, very fresh." The first thing Yang Shaoyu did at the table was ladle Lin Zirui a bowl.

Lin Zirui liked soup but had never cared for fish soup. Since Mother Yang had made it, however, he wanted to try. Yang Shaoyu knew his preference, but also knew his mother's version would change Zirui's mind.

Lin Zirui took a sip. It was unexpectedly delicious, and he immediately took another.

"Well? Good, isn't it." Seeing Lin Zirui enjoy it, Yang Shaoyu meant to have him drink another bowl shortly.

"Mm, it really is." Mother Yang heard him say it and smiled. "Eat plenty of the rest too — look how thin you are." And she put a piece of meat in his bowl herself.

Yang Shaoyu picked a shrimp for Lin Zirui too, peeled it, dipped it in sauce, and fed it to him.

Xu Xiaolin watched Yang Shaoyu's tender attentiveness and the whole family's warmth toward Lin Zirui. Could he really be only a friend? She held the question in until Shaoyu began removing the bones from Zirui's fish. "Shaoyu, are you two good friends?"

Yang Shaoyu was about to tell her outright — but Xiao De, seated beside Xu Xiaolin, answered first. "No, he's not Uncle's friend, he's Auntie."

Mid-bite, Lin Zirui froze, nearly dropping the fish back onto his plate; Yang Shaoyu recovered first and patted Xiao De's head.

"Auntie?" Xu Xiaolin didn't follow.

"Yes — he's Uncle's wife, so Xiao De calls him Auntie." Xiao De, thinking she hadn't understood, explained again carefully.

"A — Auntie? Wha — what's this now?" Xu Xiaolin was sure she wasn't seeing wrong — wasn't Lin Zirui a man?

"Zirui is the person I love." Yang Shaoyu told her plainly.

Xu Xiaolin felt she must be dreaming. In the time she hadn't seen him, how had Shaoyu gotten a girlfriend—no, a boyfriend?

"Shaoyu, he — isn't he a man?" Xu Xiaolin could barely keep her seat.

"What difference does it make that he's a man? I thought I'd never love anyone, but Zirui came into my life. Before I knew it, I'd fallen for him and couldn't let him go." Yang Shaoyu said it for his family to hear.

At his side, Lin Zirui quietly took his hand.

The Yang family was silent a moment, until Father Yang spoke first. "Zirui is our family's child too."

That one sentence declared Lin Zirui's place in the Yang family: he was one of their own. Though he'd long known they had accepted him, hearing it aloud made his nose sting and brought tears close.

"Yes — our child. Now eat up, the food's going cold." Mother Yang smiled gently at him.

The eldest brother and his wife smiled too — one more family member; how good that felt.

"Mm — eat, Dad, Mom." Lin Zirui picked out for Father and Mother Yang the things they each liked.

Xu Xiaolin had no trouble keeping her seat now — because she had no chance at all. She ate in silence, and the moment she finished said she'd be going.

Father and Mother Yang didn't keep her, thinking the girl must be quite low just now.
