The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 70

Jiang Qi's Blind Date 江奇相亲

July 2026

~7 min read

The encounter with Lan Ruyin left Jiang Qi happy for two days—so happy that he forgot Mom wanted to send him on a blind date.

So when Mom called today to tell Jiang Qi to go on a blind date, he was stunned for a full two or three seconds before reacting, then promptly refused: "Mom, I won't go; don't set it up."

Hearing this, Mom didn't get angry, only said, "Then I won't force you. Come have a meal with your mother this weekend; it's been a long time since we ate together."

"All right, Mom." Jiang Qi agreed.

Her easy surrender struck Jiang Qi as strange, but he was happy enough to dismiss the feeling.

The night before their meal, Mom called specifically to tell Jiang Qi to dress well and arrive early. He thought nothing of it and obediently chose a nice outfit the next morning.

Watching Jiang Qi leave, Ke Yan felt something was off, but didn't think much of it, only telling Jiang Qi to call him if anything came up.

Jiang Qi arrived at the place he'd arranged with Mom, a coffee shop; walking in, he saw Mom sitting alone at a table drinking juice. Seeing Jiang Qi come, she called the waiter to settle the bill.

"Mom, you're here so early."

"Mm. Jiang Qi, come shopping with your mother."

"Okay." Jiang Qi agreed.

At the mall, Jiang Qi offered to buy Mom some clothes. She refused and pulled him toward the jewelry floor instead. Assuming she wanted something for herself, he was delighted; she was so frugal that she usually scolded him for wasting money whenever he bought her a gift.

In the past, when Jiang Qi bought Mom jewelry or necklaces, she never wore them, storing them in a box, saying she'd give them to his wife when he married.

Today Mom wanted to buy of her own accord, which made Jiang Qi quite happy.

Jiang Qi looked at the dazzling gold and silver chains and asked, "Mom, which kind do you like?"

Mom was studying the bracelets intently, clearly not having heard Jiang Qi, and only asked, "Miss, could you take out the bracelet styles young girls like these days for me to see?"

"Mom, what are you buying a young person's bracelet for?" Jiang Qi was puzzled.

"You wouldn't understand." ...Jiang Qi said no more, leaving Mom to pick, and wandered over to the men's counter to look.

Thinking that apart from birthdays and holidays it had been a long time since he'd given Ke Yan a gift, he wondered what to buy. He looked and looked but saw nothing good.

"Xiao Qi, come look." Hearing Mom call him, Jiang Qi went over and saw she seemed to have picked something.

He walked over; Mom had chosen a very fine bracelet. Looking at the style, Jiang Qi said, "Mom, who's this for?"

One look at the style told Jiang Qi it wasn't to Mom's taste — probably for a young person. He thought about it, but really couldn't guess who Mom meant it for, and asked, "Mom, is this for sister-in-law or for Xiao Lü?"

"Neither." Mom said, all mysterious.

"For yourself?"

"What are you thinking? Could I wear this style? Stop asking; you'll find out."

Jiang Qi stopped asking and paid. He meant to buy Mom clothes and find something for Ke Yan as well, but she checked the time and hurried him away.

Mom pulled him into a Western restaurant across from the mall. The moment they entered, Jiang Qi understood. He gave her a helpless look. So much for escaping the blind date; no wonder she had behaved so strangely.

The two sat down, and Jiang Qi said helplessly, "Mom, didn't you say no blind date?"

"This isn't a blind date, just letting you meet someone," Mom retorted.

Isn't this exactly a blind date? Jiang Qi's face went black with lines.

"Son, look — if you take a liking to her, give her this bracelet; girls these days love a generous man."

So that was why she had bought the bracelet. Speechless, Jiang Qi excused himself to the restroom and immediately called Ke Yan. "My mom set me up on a blind date."

"Refuse it." Ke Yan said at once.

"But my mom's keeping watch outside; I'll think of a way, though."

"...Mm." Ke Yan answered. Jiang Qi could hear he was displeased; for the sake of his own backside, better settle this fast.

Hanging up, he went straight out, and at a corner nearly collided with a woman; Jiang Qi quickly turned aside to avoid her.

The woman, on the phone, paid no mind and walked past, saying as she went, "If the guy wants me to live with his parents, no way — I really can't stand living with parents. Always butting into everything."

Jiang Qi caught a couple of lines and couldn't help glancing at the woman — whatever poor man she meant, having a girlfriend like that; Jiang Qi really disliked people who looked down on parents.

He never imagined that he was the poor man in question. Across the table sat a mother and daughter, and his blind date was the very woman he had just overheard. Watching her put on a demure act made him queasy.

But — Jiang Qi suddenly thought of a plan, a way to get himself out of this blind date.

He took a sip of water and spoke up. "It's Miss Li, isn't it."

"Just call me Li Yan," Li Yan said, all bashful.

"Li Yan, very nice to meet you."

"Likewise."

"What do you like to eat? Let's order."

"Mm." Li Yan picked up the menu and looked.

After everyone had ordered, Jiang Qi went out of his way to chat with Li Yan for a good while; watching, Mom was thrilled.

Li Yan and her mother formed a decent impression of Jiang Qi too.

Then Jiang Qi suddenly switched topics. "I'd like to live with my parents after I marry; they're getting on in years, after all."

Li Yan's happy smile stiffened at the corners — she loathed living with in-laws most of all. So she didn't respond to Jiang Qi's words, only changed the subject.

Mom initially wondered why Jiang Qi had raised the subject, but Li Yan's swift deflection explained it. Her fondness cooled. She did not actually expect Jiang Qi and his future wife to live with his parents, but she had no desire for an unfilial daughter-in-law who despised the very idea.

So her smile cooled a few degrees too.

Li Yan's mother clearly noticed Mom's change as well, and quietly nudged Li Yan with a reminder.

"Li Yan, what do you think? I feel that once we're earning, the salary should go to the parents for safekeeping, and living with them lets us look after them."

"..." Li Yan didn't know what to say. If it were just living with parents she might have let it go, but handing the money over to parents — Li Yan really couldn't carry the conversation any further with Jiang Qi.

She forced a smile, said something had come up and she had to go, and ignored even her mother's reminders. What a waste of her time. Jiang Qi's conditions were decent in every respect, but there were plenty out there better than him.

Seeing Li Yan leave, her mother knew it was off; without even a courtesy to Mom, she left too.

Mom was furious with them, but before she could say anything Jiang Qi spoke. "Mom, about the girlfriend business — I think we'd best not force it; I don't want a girlfriend like that."

"I don't want a girl like that either." Mom said angrily — truly no upbringing.

"Mom, do you think I'm just not the type girls like? When it comes to finding a girlfriend down the line..."

Seeing Jiang Qi's "heartbroken" face, Mom got a fright and hurried to say, "Of course not."

"Then how about I find a girlfriend myself from now on?"

"All right, all right, as you like." Mom didn't dare say no, afraid Jiang Qi wouldn't dare look for a girlfriend in future.

Seeing Mom agree, Jiang Qi snickered inwardly.

Afterward Jiang Qi saw Mom home, then reported the matter to Ke Yan, and planned to go buy Ke Yan a gift.

As for that bracelet — it went to the covetous-eyed Jiang Lü.
