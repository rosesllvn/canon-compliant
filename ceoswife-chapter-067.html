Ke Yan Hit Someone 柯言打人了

July 2026

~6 min read

The next morning, Jiang Qi woke with an aching back. Before he could knead it twice, a pair of handsome, slender hands reached around from behind and took over.

With his back to Ke Yan, Jiang Qi couldn't suppress a few soft sounds. Ke Yan's hands paused, then resumed their work.

Lying in bed, Jiang Qi looked at the fine weather outside and said excitedly, "Ke Yan, we can go swimming today."

"Mm, careful in the water."

"Come swim with me — it's been ages since we swam together." Jiang Qi hadn't been able to swim before, but after Ke Yan's coaching he was decent at it now.

"Mm, all right."

They washed and went down for breakfast. When the elevator stopped at Jiang Lü's floor, the doors opened on a beaming Jiang Lü and a thunder-faced Xiao An. The contrast gave Jiang Qi a fright.

Once they were in the elevator, Jiang Qi asked, "What did you two get up to last night? Why such strange faces."

Jiang Lü gave him a sly grin that raised goosebumps—and made him think of the previous night. He awkwardly changed the subject. "Why does Xiao An look so awful?"

The words were barely out when he felt Ke Yan glance at him; while Jiang Lü and Xiao An weren't looking, Jiang Qi tugged Ke Yan's sleeve — just asking, no need to look at him with that cold a face.

"Xiao An's like this because she missed out on something good last night."

Jiang Qi was about to ask what good thing, but the elevator arrived, so he didn't.

Because they'd come down fairly early, the restaurant wasn't crowded; breakfast was buffet-style, and once they'd gotten their food they found a table. The tables seated four, so the four of them sat together.

Perhaps because they were on holiday—and because Ke Yan was thoroughly satisfied after the previous night—his usual cold expression had softened.

Jiang Qi ate from his own plate, then glanced at Ke Yan's and saw something he liked but hadn't seen on the spread; he decisively reached over with his chopsticks and took it, the move utterly natural, his eating utterly content.

Across from them, Jiang Lü and Xiao An watched this seamless maneuver and forgot the food halfway to their mouths. There was definitely something going on between those two. Definitely!

Ke Yan took Jiang Qi out of the hotel, while Jiang Lü and Xiao An gathered in the lobby downstairs to wait for the guide and go sightseeing.

They reached the beach, already crowded despite the early hour. The moment Jiang Qi saw the sea, he stripped off his shirt. Ke Yan had no chance to stop him.

At the thought of others seeing Jiang Qi's body, Ke Yan reflexively frowned — but then he saw the kiss-marks on Jiang Qi and his frown eased.

Jiang Qi, though, didn't see the kiss-marks on himself, and eagerly did his warm-up exercises.

Just then a little girl passing in front of Jiang Qi saw the speckled kiss-marks all over him and immediately turned to tell her father, "Daddy, look, this big brother's been bitten all over by mosquitoes."

The girl's words startled Jiang Qi; just as he was about to look down, the girl's father glanced at Jiang Qi and Ke Yan and said, "Shameless, disgusting." And he made a retching motion at Jiang Qi.

He turned to leave, only for Ke Yan to block his way with an icy stare. Ke Yan could tolerate insults aimed at himself, but not at Jiang Qi, whom he cherished above all else.

"Apologize." Ke Yan spat out the word coldly.

"Ke Yan, I'm fine." Realizing Ke Yan was angry, Jiang Qi tugged his hand.

The girl's father, seeing Ke Yan look ready to rush over and hit him at any second, lost his nerve, but not wanting to lose face either, said in a forced, perfunctory voice, "Ah, sorry."

He tried to leave. Suddenly, the arm holding his daughter's hand went numb; before he could react, a fist struck him hard in the stomach. His legs buckled, leaving him on his knees and unable to rise.

He looked up resentfully and saw the one who'd hit him was Ke Yan, while his daughter, off to the side, had her eyes covered by Jiang Qi.

Seeing Ke Yan was done, Jiang Qi let the little girl go; she looked at her father sprawled on the ground and asked Jiang Qi, "What's wrong with my daddy?"

"Your daddy's just tired; let him rest a while and he'll be fine."

"Oh." The girl nodded.

Leaving the girl and her father aside, Jiang Qi looked at Ke Yan; Ke Yan picked up Jiang Qi's shirt to help him put it on, but Jiang Qi stopped him. "Don't be angry; let's go swimming."

Ke Yan looked at Jiang Qi in surprise, looked a long while, then reached out to stroke his face. "I'm afraid you'll be unhappy."

Ke Yan feared the man's disgust had wounded Jiang Qi. From his expression, it surely had, but...

"Let them say their things; we'll live our lives. All right, hurry and take off your shirt; let's go swimming." So saying, Jiang Qi stripped off Ke Yan's T-shirt.

That done, Jiang Qi flung the shirt to the ground and ran for the sea.

It hurt, but it would leave no lasting wound. The person who loved him mattered far more than a stranger.

Jiang Qi swam happily that morning; it had been so long since he'd swum with Ke Yan that today he swam to his heart's content.

After swimming, they put on the clothes they'd tossed on the sand; now the two wanted only to get back to the hotel and shower, being all sticky and grimy.

Showered, Jiang Qi ate the takeout he'd ordered up and asked Ke Yan, "Want to see the sights this afternoon?"

"Mm."

"Then let's go with the guide."

"All right."...

Company trips meant following a guide through the obligatory sights, so that afternoon Jiang Qi and Ke Yan joined the group.

Ke Yan's presence initially put the relaxed staff on edge. But he and Jiang Qi stayed at the back, and without his usual cold expression, everyone gradually loosened up.

An afternoon out was rather nice.

Over the next three days they saw everything worth seeing, and Jiang Qi bought no small amount of local specialties.

The day to go back finally came. On the plane, Jiang Qi was sleeping soundly under a blanket, utterly unaware of how many blind dates his mom had lined up for him back home...

Asleep, Jiang Qi suddenly shivered and said, half-awake, "Mm, Ke Yan, I'm a bit cold."

Ke Yan tucked the blanket around Jiang Qi better and felt his forehead. "Want something hot to drink?"

"No." Jiang Qi shook his head. "Doesn't seem so cold now."

Sitting not far off, Wang Siqi watched them, gritting her teeth — you two are far too intimate! Just like a couple!!!
