The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 134

Merry Christmas Eve 平安夜快乐

July 2026

~5 min read

The two reached the company canteen. It wasn't lunch break yet — and even when it was nearly time, no staff came early to eat.

Jiang Qi and Ke Yan got their food and sat. Watching Jiang Qi eat with relish, Ke Yan said, "Why the sudden change of heart? You used to forbid me from making our relationship public — remember, when we'd just come to the company, you were afraid people would find out."

"You'd just taken over the company and were swamped. If we'd gone public, the senior staff would have hounded you to fire me. Our parents didn't know either, so I was afraid. Now they've all agreed, and there's nothing left to fear. I know you hate hiding, and I want us to be open too. Other people's stares shouldn't keep us secret."

Ke Yan took his hand. "I'm glad you feel that way."

"Eat up — no more hiding, but with you like this, so many people around, I still get shy." Jiang Qi flushed, glancing at the canteen ladies who kept watching him and Ke Yan.

Ke Yan pinched his cheek. "Eat up, then."

"Mm."

"It can't be—Ke Yan, gay." Wang Siqi had followed them to the canteen. Their intimacy drove her out again. So that was why every attempt by her and Sun Nianqing met ice: the person Ke Yan loved was Jiang Qi, a man.

Back at her desk, she sat down, feeling drained of all strength. At this rate, not only was the role of Ke Group's CEO's wife gone — Ke Yan was gone too.

No — she had to tell Sun Nianqing, get her to think of something fast.

Elsewhere, the staff who'd witnessed the morning's scene were busy telling colleagues about the two — they'd even held hands; was there something more between them?

"Surely they're not lovers?" Female colleague A said, chewing her pen.

"I thought that too — but aren't they both men?" Female colleague B sipped her milk tea.

"Isn't there a certain kind of relationship? Gay." The fujoshi among them smiled dreamily.

"No way — please, no. Men as handsome and rich as our CEO barely exist anymore. If the CEO's gay, I'll never believe in true love again."

"If the CEO and Jiang Qi aren't lovers, then I'll never believe in true love." The fujoshi colleague said it — and got a thorough beating from the others gossiping with her.

"What are you all squabbling about? Don't you have work to do?" Wang Siqi came to the break room for coffee and found several female employees chatting.

"Manager Wang." They fell silent at once.

"Hmph. Back to your stations." Wang Siqi poured a coffee, gave a cold snort, and clacked off on her heels.

"Why's Manager Wang so fierce?" Female colleague C ventured once she'd gone.

"Right? It's not even work hours yet — still lunch break."

"You don't know the half of it. Manager Wang likes the CEO — but however she tries, he pays her no mind at all."

"How do you know?"

"A while ago, I saw the CEO and Manager Wang at the coffee shop and thought they were dating. But Manager Wang did all the talking while he barely answered. Before leaving, he boxed up a slice of cake. The staff thought it was for his girlfriend. Now it seems it was for Manager Jiang."

"So that's how it is. Pity, though — both of them gay, especially the CEO; no chance he'd ever like me."

That earned a jab: "He'd never like you anyway."

And the two protagonists everyone was discussing were just then on the 39th floor, in Ke Yan's office. Jiang Qi checked the time — nearly time to head down to work. He told Ke Yan, "I'm going down. Make sure you keep tomorrow afternoon free."

"All right." Even unsaid, Ke Yan would have kept it free — he had something important to do tomorrow.

After Jiang Qi went down, Ke Yan took a call, and after hanging up went across the street — the shop was nearly finished to his specifications; this afternoon only the final touches remained, and Ke Yan had made a point of being told when completion neared.

His going over now was to see it finished.

At the hospital, Lin Zirui came back from his rounds to find a colleague he was close with waiting in his office.

"What is it?" Lin Zirui sat down to rest.

"Didn't you ask me to cover for you tomorrow afternoon, since you're off?"

"Yes. Why?" Lin Zirui dearly hoped he wasn't backing out.

"I did agree — but my girlfriend's giving me grief about it." The male colleague said it with a sheepish grin.

"No. I have something on tomorrow afternoon, I have to be off. And — how many times have I covered for you before? If you pull this, don't come asking me to cover your dates again." Lin Zirui was rarely so firm.

The colleague didn't dare press after that — it'd be a bad trade — and quickly said, "All right, all right, I'll stop — but you have to cover for me down the line, okay?"

"All right."

The colleague left, and Lin Zirui took a chocolate from his drawer and ate it.

He had something important tomorrow. Oh — he had to call Shaoyu and make sure he kept tomorrow afternoon free.

That evening, when Yang Shaoyu came to pick Lin Zirui up, the moment Lin Zirui got in, Yang Shaoyu handed him an apple. "I hear you're supposed to eat apples today. I washed it."

Lin Zirui took it and bit in — sweet. It was the first time anyone had given him an apple on this day. He held it up to Yang Shaoyu's mouth. "Let's share it."

"All right." Yang Shaoyu took a big bite.

And on the other side, Jiang Qi and Ke Yan, after dinner and showers, shared an apple too.

Tomorrow was Christmas. Jiang Qi thought of the hidden ring — tomorrow he was going to propose to Ke Yan.

Merry Christmas Eve.
