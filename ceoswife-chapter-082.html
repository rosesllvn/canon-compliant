The Business Trip 出差

The next day, when Jiang Qi got up, he couldn't help pulling down his trousers while washing to check on his backside; it felt a bit uncomfortable.

Trousers down, Jiang Qi twisted around, looked down — fine if he didn't look, but looking left him glum: it was actually a little bruised.

Thinking about it made him angry — Ke Yan had hit that hard!!!!

He pulled his trousers back up indignantly, washed, changed, and went downstairs to find Ke Yan sitting at the dining table reading the paper.

"Hmph!" Jiang Qi gave a deliberate cold snort.

Ke Yan set down the paper to look at Jiang Qi, only to find Jiang Qi ignoring him.

"What is it?" Ke Yan asked.

"Nothing." Jiang Qi answered crossly.

"Is your backside sore?" Ke Yan could guess.

"Hmph, you hit so hard last night and now you ask." Jiang Qi, for once, wouldn't let it drop.

"Let me see." Ke Yan rose to look at Jiang Qi's backside.

Jiang Qi dodged, embarrassed — eating breakfast first thing in the morning, what's there to look at.

"Hurry and eat; we've got work, and it'll be busy again today."

Seeing this, Ke Yan didn't insist on pulling down his trousers to look, and after breakfast they went to work together.

Jiang Qi's business trip began tomorrow afternoon, so he had to put all his work in order today. His competitor was formidable; without extra preparation, Jiang Qi would likely be crushed.

At noon, after lunch, Jiang Qi went back to his office to keep working.

Ke Yan knew Jiang Qi probably wouldn't come up to see him today — first, too much work; second, having had his backside spanked last night, he was probably still a bit sore and embarrassed.

After all, a grown man, suddenly spanked — there's bound to be some shame in it.

At this thought Ke Yan felt like laughing again.

He got up from his chair and decided to go see whether Jiang Qi was still cross.

"Knock knock—"

Jiang Qi said come in, then lowered his head and went back to work.

Ke Yan stood before the desk watching Jiang Qi; Jiang Qi looked up. "Why'd you come down?"

Ke Yan shut the door, locked it, then walked to Jiang Qi's side, bent down, and undid Jiang Qi's belt.

Jiang Qi jumped and stopped him. "What are you doing?"

"Seeing whether it's bruised."

Jiang Qi's face stiffened. "Hmph, it's all bruised."

"Then all the more reason to look."

"Don't look; it's fine, really." Jiang Qi didn't want Ke Yan pulling his trousers down in the office.

Ke Yan didn't listen, deftly pulled Jiang Qi's trousers down, and had him lie on the sofa.

Ke Yan looked, reached out and touched. "It really is a bit bruised; seems I was too rough last night."

"Since you feel guilty, then let me top once."

Ke Yan doubted he'd understood Jiang Qi right. "Top me?"

"Yeah, let me have you once." Jiang Qi said, nodding along as he spoke.

Now Ke Yan doubted he'd heard right; he gave Jiang Qi's backside a light pat. "All right, when you're back from your trip."

"Really?" Jiang Qi was delighted.

"Of course; when have I ever gone back on my word."

"Then good." Jiang Qi's eyes crinkled into a smile.

Ke Yan stayed at Jiang Qi's a while longer before going out; coming out, he happened to run into Li Jing returning to the office. Li Jing was startled, and called, "Hello, CEO."

"Mm." Ke Yan walked off.

Li Jing looked at Jiang Qi's office, then in the direction Ke Yan had gone.

Jiang Qi left on his trip the next day, taking only Li Jing and Sun Ye.

Sun Ye was Jiang Qi's subordinate.

This trip of Jiang Qi's could only be called less than smooth — first, the company they were to partner with leaned more toward partnering with Jiang Qi's competitor, and then there was another matter...

Jiang Qi came back to the hotel from a business dinner, and Yang Shaoyu's call came in. "Jiang Qi, Ke Yan came to see me today — you know who else he brought?"

"Who?" Jiang Qi asked, not much minding, still flipping through the revised partnership proposal in his hand.

"Sun Nianqing."

"Who?" Jiang Qi stopped flipping.

"Sun Nianqing, you know — the one the company just hired."

"I know; how'd she end up there with you?"

"She heard Ke Yan was coming to visit a patient and said she'd come see me too; I'd say her real interest wasn't in the visiting."

"Really?"

"Of course; could I be wrong about reading people?"

"I'll hang up first." Jiang Qi hung up and called Ke Yan. "You took Sun Nianqing to see Yang Shaoyu today?"

"Yes; she said she'd come with me to see Shaoyu."

"From now on don't get too close to her outside of work."

"Are you jealous?" Ke Yan found this answer put him in a very good mood.

"Yes, I don't want you getting too close to other women." Jiang Qi admitted it outright.

"All right, I don't like getting too close to others either." Ke Yan really didn't like being too close to anyone except Jiang Qi and his friends' and parents.

"Mm, I'll head back once I've handled things here. Only the other side doesn't seem too keen to partner with us."

"It doesn't have to be them." Ke Yan said just those few words, and Jiang Qi understood.

After a few more words with Ke Yan, Jiang Qi hung up, thinking he'd better settle things here fast and get back to top Ke Yan.

The thought that Ke Yan had agreed to let him top made him happy.
