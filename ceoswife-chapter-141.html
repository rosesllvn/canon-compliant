The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 141

Men 男人

July 2026

~6 min read

After Lin Zirui went home that day to come clean, the Lin family never called him back again.

As though they really did mean to sever ties. The eldest and second brothers didn't come find him either, but Lin Zirui lived as usual — work, rest, time with Yang Shaoyu.

If they called him, he'd go home; if not, he wasn't anxious about it.

Saturday — the day Jiang Qi's parents and Ke Yan's parents would meet.

Jiang Qi woke early and roused Ke Yan to get up.

They'd just finished washing when the doorbell rang. Ke Yan answered it — Father and Mother Jiang.

"Dad, Mom, you're here — come in." Ke Yan said it easily.

"We're not too early, are we? Did we wake you?" Mother Jiang, worried about the distance and a possible traffic jam making them late, had set out early.

"No — Jiang Qi and I are up, we woke early."

They came in, and Ke Yan, seeing the gift in Father Jiang's hand, hurried to take it for him.

"Dad, Mom, you're so early." Jiang Qi came down too.

"Have you eaten breakfast?" Mother Jiang saw him hurry down.

"Not yet."

"I'll make you something." She rose and headed for the kitchen.

"Mom, I'll help." Jiang Qi rolled up his sleeves.

"No, no, no — you only make things worse." She stopped him at once.

Without his help she soon had breakfast ready: toast, two fried eggs, and pancakes.

Ke Yan and Jiang Qi ate with relish.

Finishing the last bite, Jiang Qi gave a full burp. "Mom, that was delicious."

"Of course it was. If you had a tenth of my skill you could cook for yourself." She found Jiang Qi's cooking an embarrassment.

Jiang Qi fell silent. She was quite right.

Ke Yan looked at him, amused. It seemed many had suffered Jiang Qi's cooking.

Just then Ke Yan's parents arrived too, and Father and Mother Jiang went to the door to greet them.

"Hello, in-laws."

"Oh, in-laws — you're here so early, and sorry we're late."

"Not at all, we came early."

Greetings done, they went in and sat, exchanging the meeting gifts they'd each bought.

The air was, admittedly, a touch awkward.

Lan Ruyin spoke. "The truth is, at first I didn't agree either. But I came around in the end — I couldn't bear to stay angry at my child, or to see them grieve."

"Yes." Mother Jiang felt the same.

"You'll know the two have proposed. Next comes the wedding — do you have any thoughts on it?"

"I've no particular views — whatever you decide is fine," Mother Jiang said graciously.

"Better to let the children decide. We'll just help out." Lan Ruyin smiled.

The two women talked away merrily, while the two fathers could only listen, putting in a word here and there.

Jiang Qi and Ke Yan sat by, watching their parents talk, and Jiang Qi found it wonderful that they could all sit together like this.

At first, none had approved. Now they sat together discussing the couple's wedding.

Meanwhile, on Lin Zirui's side, the family that had gone silent finally called him home today, and the moment he hung up he told Yang Shaoyu.

Yang Shaoyu wanted to go with him, and Lin Zirui didn't refuse — with Shaoyu along, he wouldn't have to come home alone after being driven out by the Lins.

When the two arrived, the whole Lin family except Lin Zirui sat on the sofa, faces grave — only Lin Le watching him with malicious glee.

Seeing Lin Zirui bring Yang Shaoyu, Mother Lin's face turned spiteful. "What's the meaning of this — bringing this man here?"

"He's the person I want to be with." Lin Zirui was expressionless.

"I won't allow it. Must you be this disgusting — with a man?"

That stoked Yang Shaoyu's temper. "How can you speak like that?"

"I'll speak however I like. He's my son; I'll say what I please."

"Mom." The eldest brother said it, signaling her to stop.

"Zirui, hurry and break up with this man. We can still forgive you — youthful folly, a moment's confusion." Father Lin looked at the son he'd never much cherished, without a flicker of guilt.

"I don't need your forgiveness. I came only to inform you — not to ask your agreement."

"To say such a thing — I truly regret giving birth to you." Mother Lin was beside herself.

"You call yourself Zirui's mother? You never cherished him as a child, and now you say you regret giving birth to him? What mother says that? I'll take Zirui away. You don't deserve to be his parents." Yang Shaoyu was furious.

"Watch your mouth." Lin's second brother shot up; he'd disliked Yang Shaoyu from the start, and now Yang Shaoyu was speaking to his parents like this.

He strode up to Yang Shaoyu and threw a punch.

Yang Shaoyu caught the fist and struck back.

Lin Zirui jumped at the sudden fight; Lin's second brother took Yang Shaoyu's punch and went straight to the floor.

Yang Shaoyu had put his full strength into it.

Lin Zirui asked anxiously, "Are you all right?" — checking Yang Shaoyu carefully for injury.

Lin's second brother was helped up, and seeing Lin Zirui show him no concern at all, felt a complicated pang. His little brother was growing ever more distant…

He had repeatedly claimed to cherish Xiao Rui, yet in the end still favored Lin Le. How deeply must that have hurt his brother?

"Shaoyu, let's go. Even if my family won't agree, I have family who supports us. Didn't you say your parents are my parents? I have parents who love me."

"All right, let's go." Yang Shaoyu didn't want Lin Zirui to stay and hear more of his family's heartless words.

"Wait — stop right there, Lin Zirui!" Lin Le rose from the sofa, unwilling to let it go; a man this handsome falling for Lin Zirui, and Lin Zirui just walking off — what did he take their parents for?

"Who are you? So ill-bred."

"I'm Lin Le — and Lin Zirui's the ill-bred one."

"Oh, I remember now — you're Zirui's sister. But Zirui's so good-looking; how did you come out looking like a toad? Revolting. Ugly's one thing, but no manners on top of it."

"Wh — what?" Lin Le stood frozen.

"Get out." Mother Lin couldn't stand anyone insulting her daughter.

"So the toad takes after the mother." With that Yang Shaoyu took Lin Zirui and left.

And far from sad, Lin Zirui found himself nearly wanting to laugh.

He had a man to protect him now — one who'd take his part, who'd put people in their place for him.
