The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 126

Trust Me 相信我

July 2026

~5 min read

At the Jiang house, neither parent could sleep. Yun Hui had coaxed her son to bed, while excitement kept Jiang Lü awake. She had to share such thrilling news with Xiao An. When her call went unanswered, she resorted to texting:

—Didn't the CEO come to our house for dinner today? It wasn't just dinner, actually — he and my brother came out.

—Really. The two of them confessed their love for each other to my parents together.

—The CEO even got a hole knocked in his head by my mom, but he shielded my brother beautifully.

—My brother is 100% the uke, the CEO's the seme.

Jiang Lü fired off several texts to Xiao An, but none came back, and she grumbled, "Where has that dolt Xiao An gotten to?"

In the end she fell asleep waiting for a reply.

Jiang Qi woke once in the night and felt Ke Yan's forehead — the doctor had said he might run a fever, had given him a dose at the hospital, and if he spiked in the night he'd need a fever reducer.

Luckily Ke Yan's constitution was strong, and he had no fever.

"What is it?" Jiang Qi had woken him.

"Just checking if you have a fever."

"I'm fine, go back to sleep." Ke Yan drew him into his arms again.

"Mm." Jiang Qi sank back into deep sleep.

The next day Jiang Qi woke very early. He'd meant to sleep a while more but couldn't, and lay still so as not to wake Ke Yan.

He wondered what his mother was doing now. Was she up? Or — because he'd kept her up all night — did she not want to see him?

Facing away from Ke Yan, the more Jiang Qi thought the sadder he felt; but to leave Ke Yan was truly impossible.

He sighed — and cut it off halfway, lest Ke Yan hear; luckily Ke Yan was still asleep.

"Don't sigh." Ke Yan, awake at some point, kissed the back of Jiang Qi's neck.

"Mm — does your head still hurt?" Jiang Qi turned to face him.

"It doesn't, don't worry." Ke Yan took his hand and gave it a squeeze.

"What'll you do at the office tomorrow?" Jiang Qi touched the bandage wound round Ke Yan's head.

"I'll go just like this." Ke Yan plainly didn't mind.

"True — no one would dare ask you to your face."

"Shall we go home today?" Ke Yan asked suddenly.

"I don't know." Jiang Qi looked at him.

Ke Yan met his eyes. "Let's go. We have to get Mom to agree. I want you happy — I don't want you wretched over family."

"All right, let's get up, then go after breakfast." Jiang Qi smiled.

This was the second time coming home with Ke Yan, and his mood was nothing like yesterday's — but Jiang Qi pressed the doorbell all the same.

Why the doorbell? Because he'd left his key at home and hadn't brought it.

Jiang Lü opened the door, and smiled at the sight of them. "Brother, you're here — come in."

"Xiao Lü." Jiang Qi gave her a grateful smile.

"Thank you," Ke Yan said to her too.

Jiang Lü grinned foolishly.

"Who is it?" Mother Jiang's voice came from inside.

"Mom, it's me," Jiang Qi said.

No reply came for a while, then: "Go away."

Jiang Qi's heart ached. "Mom…"

"Let me think. Go for now — I don't want to see you right now."

The words instantly reddened Jiang Qi's eyes and stung his nose. He refused to cry and drew a deep breath.

Ke Yan took his hand. "Let's go for now. Let Mom think it over."

"Mm. Xiao Lü, we're leaving."

Jiang Qi said goodbye to her.

"All right — I'm on your side, definitely." Jiang Lü saw them off, reluctant.

Back in the car, Jiang Qi leaned wearily against the seat, and Ke Yan kissed the corner of his eye. "If you're sad, then cry. Lean on me and cry it out."

Jiang Qi hadn't meant to, but holding it in hurt, and at Ke Yan's words he buried his face in Ke Yan's chest and let out a sob. "Will Mom stop wanting me?"

"She won't. She's this angry precisely because she loves you — she could never not want you. Trust me. I promise, she'll forgive us before Christmas."

"Really?" Jiang Qi's trust in Ke Yan was total.

"Really. Trust me, Jiang Qi." Ke Yan held him tight.

"All right." Jiang Qi nodded and nodded, wiping his tears on Ke Yan's shirt.

Jiang Lü saw off her brother and "brother-husband"—the title she had privately chosen for Ke Yan.

Back in her room, she picked up her phone to check whether Xiao An had texted back, only to find the phone had switched off at some point.

She plugged it in to charge, and the moment it powered on, it rang. She unplugged it and answered. "Hello."

"Do you know how many times I called you? Is what you said true?" came Xiao An's voice.

"It was off — I only just charged it."

"Is it true, what you said?"

"It is. Brother and brother-husband came again just now, but Mom sent them away."

"What? That won't do — hurry and think of something to make them agree." Xiao An was anxious.

"I'll think of something. We'll just have to wait for my eldest brother to get back and help Dad work on Mom."

"All right — but tomorrow I'll get to see them at the office. I'm more excited than meeting my idol — I never dreamed they'd actually be together." Xiao An couldn't wait for tomorrow; she absolutely had to cheer Jiang Qi on.

Whatever happened, he had to keep his spirits up.
