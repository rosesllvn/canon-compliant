Chapter 156

The Meal 吃饭

July 2026

~5 min read

Afraid of being late—and eager to have dinner with Lin Zirui—the brothers had left the office early. Still, they couldn't admit how long they had been waiting. "We just got here too."

"Good — come in." Lin Zirui unlocked the door.

Inside, he switched on the lights, the brothers following.

The eldest looked over the décor, the sofa, and thought it nice — and somehow familiar.

After putting the groceries in the kitchen, Yang Shaoyu came out to greet them properly. He took Lin Zirui's hand and inclined his head. "Eldest Brother, Second Brother—I'm Yang Shaoyu. Our first meeting wasn't pleasant, but you're Zirui's brothers, so you're my brothers too. Second Brother, I'm sorry I hit you last time."

For all Yang Shaoyu's apologetic manner, the second brother wasn't pleased to hear it; his first impression of Yang Shaoyu had been poor, so his face stayed sour.

The eldest brother nodded. "Just be good to Zirui."

"Don't worry, Eldest Brother." Yang Shaoyu promised.

"Oh — Eldest Brother, Second Brother, what would you like to drink?" Lin Zirui asked.

"Anything."

Lin Zirui made to go to the kitchen; Yang Shaoyu stopped him. "I'll go — you chat with your brothers."

"All right." Lin Zirui sat, and the second brother asked at once, "Is this fellow really good to you?"

"Of course — Shaoyu's very good to me." Lin Zirui took off his scarf and coat; when the room wasn't cold he liked to wear just a sweater.

"Good, then." The eldest brother nodded, finding the room rather hot.

"Want to take your coats off?" Lin Zirui hung his up and asked.

The brothers shed their coats too. "The heating in here's too warm."

"I'll turn it down, then." Lin Zirui lowered it.

Yang Shaoyu brought out tea for the brothers, a cup each, and hot cocoa for Lin Zirui.

Lin Zirui set it on the table and undid the buttons of Yang Shaoyu's coat. "Take your coat off."

Yang Shaoyu shed it, and Lin Zirui hung it up. Then Yang Shaoyu fetched a soft house jacket from the bedroom, helped Lin Zirui into it, and went to cook.

The brothers didn't understand — so warm, yet putting a coat on Xiao Rui? The eldest asked, "Xiao Rui, it's hot — won't you take the coat off?"

"No — I get cold easily."

The eldest brother suddenly understood why the heating had been so high. Xiao Rui felt the cold, and Yang Shaoyu had still thoughtfully added another layer. No one could be this attentive without loving him dearly.

"Eldest Brother, Second Brother — sit, watch some TV; I'll go help Shaoyu." Lin Zirui couldn't bear to leave Yang Shaoyu cooking alone.

"All right, go on." The eldest nodded.

Once Lin Zirui was in the kitchen, the second brother said to the eldest, "Why let Xiao Rui go help? He never cooked at home — and here he's helping cook?"

"Don't you see? The man in this house dotes on Xiao Rui more than anyone. At home, Xiao Rui may not have cooked, but he wasn't happy. Here, someone loves him this much. We should be glad for him."

"Really? Of course I'm glad for him, but…"

"I know your impression of Yang Shaoyu is poor, but he's good to Xiao Rui. You can't argue with that."

"All right." The second brother thought it over and conceded.

Reassured by that answer, the eldest looked again at the décor — no wonder it felt familiar; the place suited Xiao Rui's taste exactly.

In the kitchen, Yang Shaoyu was washing vegetables; seeing Lin Zirui come in, he asked, "Why not chat with your brothers outside?"

"Couldn't bear to leave you cooking alone — came to help." Lin Zirui made to wash vegetables.

Yang Shaoyu wouldn't let him touch the water. "I'll wash — it's cold."

"It's fine, I'm not cold now."

"Go check if the water in the pot's boiling." Still he wouldn't let Lin Zirui touch the water.

Lin Zirui lifted the lid. "Not yet."

"All right. Drank your cocoa?"

"No — thirsty, but I don't want something sweet."

"Have some juice, then — though it's cold, only a little." Yang Shaoyu made to juice some.

"No need — I'll just have warm water; you're so busy, don't trouble yourself juicing for me." Lin Zirui said it, but couldn't keep the corners of his mouth from lifting.

"For you, nothing's a trouble. Now go out and chat with your brothers, watch some TV — I'll be done soon." Knowing Lin Zirui wanted to keep his brothers company yet couldn't bear to leave him busy alone, Yang Shaoyu steered him to the living-room sofa.

Pulled out by Yang Shaoyu, Lin Zirui was asked by the second brother, "What's wrong?"

"Shaoyu won't let me help." Lin Zirui watched, helpless, as Yang Shaoyu went back to it, and didn't insist — afraid he'd only get in the way.

The second brother nodded, satisfied, and with what the eldest had said, he understood too: as long as Yang Shaoyu stayed good to Xiao Rui, that was enough; he wouldn't begrudge him anymore.

Without Lin Zirui's help, Yang Shaoyu soon had dinner ready — quite a spread: green-pepper pork, steamed fish, sweet-and-sour pork, stir-fried potato, stir-fried greens, tomato-and-egg soup.

They sat; the eldest tried a bite first, found it good, surprisingly so.

He nodded. "Very tasty."

"Glad you like it, Eldest Brother — eat, Second Brother." Yang Shaoyu picked a large piece of fish, boned it, and gave it to Lin Zirui.

Lin Zirui took the bowl naturally and ate it.

The second brother ate too. "It really is good."

Yang Shaoyu glanced over, sensing the shift in the second brother's attitude, smiled, and went on boning fish for Lin Zirui.

Everyone ate to satisfaction. Yang Shaoyu cleared the dishes, then joined Lin Zirui and his brothers in front of the television. When the eldest brother finally said it was time to leave, Lin Zirui reluctantly told them, "Come for dinner whenever you're free."

The brothers both said yes.

Yang Shaoyu and Lin Zirui saw them down together, and only after the car drove off did the two go back up.
