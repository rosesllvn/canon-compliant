Chapter 88

A Talk 谈话

July 2026

~5 min read

Lin Zirui sat quietly in his chair, eyes blank, staring ahead, his heart aching. Why had it turned out this way? If he hadn't been so greedy, he could still be Yang Shaoyu's friend now, but now he was nothing.

Just then the door opened; Lin Zirui looked up, then lowered his head in disappointment — it wasn't Yang Shaoyu.

The waiter set the dishes on the table and was about to leave; Lin Zirui stood and handed her his wallet. "The bill."

"You're leaving after eating only..." The waiter looked at Lin Zirui strangely, then at the untouched dishes.

"The bill." Lin Zirui said again.

"Yes." The waiter took Lin Zirui's wallet.

Lin Zirui stood, back slightly bent, and walked slowly out of the private room; right now he only wanted to leave this place.

"Sir, you forgot your wallet and your things."

Lin Zirui walked out of the restaurant, only to hear the waiter call him; he took the wallet, but she handed him another bag too.

"This isn't..." Lin Zirui was about to say it wasn't his, but recognized it as Yang Shaoyu's luggage — he must have forgotten it earlier.

He looked at the bag of luggage a long while, then reached out a slightly trembling hand to take it.

Then he turned and walked slowly down the street.

Maybe he never should have let himself feel this way in the first place.

Yang Shaoyu got home, shut the door, and rubbed his temples with a headache.

He really hadn't known Zirui felt that way about him...

He took a bottle of red wine from the cabinet, poured a glass, and drained it.

Only now did his pulse, racing earlier, seem to settle.

If he'd known Lin Zirui was gay...

Yang Shaoyu considered: if he'd known from the start Lin Zirui was gay, would he still have become friends with him?

Lin Zirui's face came to mind; Yang Shaoyu frowned, walked to the balcony — maybe he still would have, because it seemed he couldn't quite bear to part with him.

A sleepless night; the next day Yang Shaoyu got up and went to the company before it was fully light.

The guards at the company gate had just changed shifts too, squinting, wanting only to stand there and catch a bit more sleep.

Suddenly someone came walking up; the guard squinted to look — oh, wasn't that Assistant Yang?

The guard quickly straightened up, but Assistant Yang walked in as if he hadn't seen him.

The guard was puzzled — something felt off.

Lin Zirui left the restaurant and walked home instead of taking a cab. He had no idea how long he wandered; by the time he arrived, cold had seeped through him. Why torment himself? Yang Shaoyu simply did not like him.

Lin Zirui went to his room, took a shower, then lay in bed, turned over, looked at the pitch-black window, sniffled — feeling a little wronged, but more an unspeakable sadness. He didn't know whether Yang Shaoyu would tonight... whether he'd go find a girlfriend.

Lin Zirui sat up in bed, wanting to find his phone; it wasn't on the desk. He searched the room — not there. He went straight to the bathroom to look — not there either; Lin Zirui got anxious — where was the phone?

He went downstairs to look, made a round without finding it, and sat down on the sofa.

Eldest brother came down for water and was startled to see Lin Zirui sitting motionless on the sofa.

"Xiao Rui, are you all right? Why are you sitting here?"

"Me? I'm fine." Lin Zirui shook his head.

"If you're fine why are you sitting here? Aren't you cold?"

"Brother, what do you do if the person you like finds you disgusting?" Lin Zirui leaned back on the sofa.

At this eldest brother was truly shocked — who was it that had made Xiao Rui fall for them? No — eldest brother shook his head, then grew angry — who dared find his Xiao Rui disgusting? Too detestable.

Looking at Xiao Rui's grief-stricken face, eldest brother's heart ached; was the other person so wonderful? Seeing Xiao Rui's dazed look, eldest brother felt like cursing aloud.

But thinking and doing are different; eldest brother decided to offer some guidance instead.

"Xiao Rui, do you really like her?"

Lin Zirui nodded; he didn't even know when he'd fallen — he'd clearly warned himself before not to fall for a straight man, but thinking of Yang Shaoyu, Lin Zirui felt both bitter and like laughing.

"When did you fall for her?" Eldest brother indicated he hadn't caught the slightest wind of it.

"I don't know." Lin Zirui glanced at eldest brother.

"Well, if you still can't let her go, then go after her; even if you fail once, don't give up — don't leave yourself with regret and remorse down the line."

"Is that so?" Lin Zirui murmured to himself.

"Mm, go on; I support you."

"Mm." Lin Zirui smiled, and his mood felt lighter than before.

Eldest brother still didn't know the person Lin Zirui liked was actually a man; no telling whether, once he found out later, he'd regret supporting Lin Zirui's pursuit today.

Hearing eldest brother's words, Lin Zirui stood, deciding to get some sleep first. As for Yang Shaoyu — he really couldn't let go, so why not give it another try? If Yang Shaoyu truly didn't like him this time, then he'd give up.

Yang Shaoyu, though glad to be back at work and out of the hospital, got little done all day; in the end he just went home to rest — only to run into Lin Zirui at his door.

Yang Shaoyu frowned and gave a sigh at Lin Zirui's sudden appearance.
