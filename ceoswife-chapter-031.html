The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 31

Angry?? 怒了？？

By the time Jiang Qi came ambling downstairs, Ke Yan had changed and was sitting on the living-room sofa reading the newspaper. Jiang Qi stood before him, wanting to say something, opened his mouth — and nothing came. He hesitated, then tried: "Um — could I borrow your bathroom for a shower?"

Ke Yan didn't look up, turning a page. "Go ahead. Though — after, are you going to put that same outfit back on?"

Jiang Qi plucked at the clothes on him, crushed and reeking of wine after a night's wear. "Then what should I wear?"

Ke Yan set down the paper, went into the bedroom, and came out with a set of clothes, which he handed over. It was the second time he'd lent Jiang Qi his clothes; the last set was still sitting unreturned on the top shelf of Jiang Qi's dorm wardrobe.

In the bathroom Jiang Qi held the clothes — the top a white shirt. He'd noticed Ke Yan most often wore a white shirt, dark jeans, canvas shoes: simple, and yet good-looking, the sort a lot of girls liked. Why do so many people like him? Showering and thinking his scattered thoughts, he didn't find it the least bit strange that he was praising a man.

After his shower he felt much refreshed. Coming out, he found Ke Yan in the doorway, and said reflexively, "I'm done."

Ke Yan nodded. "Let's go eat."

They walked slowly under the shade of the roadside trees, side by side. "Hey, Ke Yan."

"Mm?"

"Did I get drunk last night?"

Remembering it, Ke Yan pressed at his brow. "You did. Drunk."

"How did I end up at your place?"

"You shamelessly insisted on coming home with me." A wicked little smile tugged at his mouth.

"No way." Jiang Qi was certain.

"How do you know — weren't you drunk?"

"How could I be so shameless — I'd never do something like that," Jiang Qi defended himself staunchly.

"Don't you know? The things you wouldn't ordinarily do are exactly the things you do once you've had a drink."

"Surely not." A note of doubt now.

The place they ate was a small eatery, surprisingly clean and nicely done up, set off with green bamboo. The two sat and each ordered a bowl of congee and some side dishes.

After eating his fill Jiang Qi said goodbye and headed back to school alone. At the dorm, Liu Hao and Meng Qing were out; only Ye Xingwei was there, lying in bed, seemingly asleep. Jiang Qi softened his steps without meaning to — and before he'd gone two paces Ye Xingwei abruptly sat up. "Oh, you weren't asleep."

Ye Xingwei pointed at his outfit. "Ke Yan's?"

"Yeah." Jiang Qi rubbed the back of his head. "How'd you know?"

"Hmph." Ye Xingwei gave a cold snort, said nothing, and walked off to the bathroom.

What's wrong? Why so prickly and odd? If Ye Xingwei had known that was the thought, he might well have thrown Jiang Qi straight into the toilet and flushed.

It was evening before Liu Hao and Meng Qing came back. Jiang Qi asked where they'd been, but they only stammered and couldn't get a word out.

"What happened?"

The question only made Liu Hao's face worse — a little green, even. Jiang Qi looked at him with some concern, and after a long while Liu Hao squeezed out a single sentence. "I'm fine."

Not in the least like a man who's fine, Jiang Qi thought, though he didn't say it; Liu Hao's expression really was dreadful. He turned to Meng Qing, who only shrugged — a long story.

Jiang Qi looked between them, mystified. Why is everyone acting so strange today?

The next day he took down the clothes he'd washed and hung, felt them — dry — and put them in a clean paper bag. He checked the time and decided to return Ke Yan's clothes. No sooner thought than he grabbed the bag and rushed out.

Liu Hao had just been about to find him for a talk, and opened his mouth to call him back — only to see him bolt out the door, and the words went back down his throat.

Where's he off to in such a hurry? Liu Hao fumed; and Ye Xingwei, watching, felt only — irritated.

Ding-dong— Jiang Qi pressed the bell. After a while Ke Yan opened the door. "What is it?"

Jiang Qi held out the bag. "Your clothes."

Ke Yan took it and looked — he hadn't expected Jiang Qi to actually return them, though now that they were back he wouldn't wear them anyway; he had a mild germophobia, and naturally wouldn't put on clothes others had worn. "Anything else?"

Jiang Qi pursed his lips. So cold. We've known each other all this time — how is it like we've barely met? Displeased as he was, he still said, "Where are my clothes? The set from yesterday — I only just bought them, hadn't even worn them twice."

Clothes? Ke Yan remembered — he'd thrown them out the day before. He looked at the earnest face, and at the bag in his hand that seemed a touch heavier now, and said slowly, "I threw them away."

Threw them away? Jiang Qi was instantly furious. He snatched the bag back and left — what was the point of all that effort washing his clothes?

Watching him go in anger, Ke Yan didn't call him back — only felt, rarely for him, a twinge of guilt.
