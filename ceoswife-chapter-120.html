Let's Go Home Together 一起回家吧

July 2026

~5 min read

Flustered, Wang Siqi went back to her office without even eating lunch, and texted Sun Nianqing: Could it really be Jiang Qi?

—Impossible. Ke Yan likes women.

Sun Nianqing replied at once.

Reading it, Wang Siqi felt it made sense. Maybe she'd been overthinking.

Because Wang Siqi did not want Ke Yan to be gay, she found Sun Nianqing's words reasonable—even with the answer directly before her.

Lin Zirui hadn't taken his usual weekend off; he'd shifted his day off to today. Yang Shaoyu, planning to take Lin Zirui somewhere, had cleared the important work in the morning and clocked off an hour after lunch to go collect Lin Zirui, handing the rest to Xiao Feng.

The car reached the hospital just as Lin Zirui came out.

In the car, Lin Zirui asked, "Where are we going today?"

"To buy furniture."

"Furniture?"

"Mm. A friend of mine's getting married soon but has no time to shop for it, so he asked me to handle it."

"Oh." Lin Zirui nodded.

They began with sofas. "What kind do you like?" Yang Shaoyu asked.

"What kind does your friend like?"

"Whatever you like, he likes — his taste is very close to yours."

"Is it? In that case, I'd want a big soft one, with lots of cushions."

"Then we'll get that."

"Are you sure it's all right? What if he doesn't like it?"

"He won't dislike it." Yang Shaoyu was firm.

"This one, then. I really like it." Lin Zirui had found a sofa — big, and soft when he tried it.

"Done. This one."

They went on to look at all sorts of furniture — beds, cabinets, dining table, chairs…

By the time they'd more or less finished, it had gone dark. Lin Zirui marveled at this friend of Yang Shaoyu's whose taste ran so close to his, down to the very styles he favored.

Afterward they had dinner together, and then Yang Shaoyu drove Lin Zirui home to rest, since he had work the next day.

At home, the elder Lins, both older brothers, and Lin Le were watching television together.

At a funny part, they all laughed together.

Lin Zirui watched without pain; it had always been this way. Even when his brothers tried to be kind, he felt shut out, as though he were not truly one of the Lins.

He sighed, and rather than go disturb them, went straight upstairs.

Upstairs he meant to call Yang Shaoyu, but the moment it connected he remembered Yang Shaoyu was driving and hung up.

Yang Shaoyu called right back.

"Still driving?"

"I've pulled over." Yang Shaoyu had stopped at the roadside to take his call.

"Oh." Lin Zirui lay on his bed, eyes a little vacant on the ceiling.

"What's wrong?"

"Nothing. Just wanted to tell you to drive safe."

"I'll hang up, then — I'll call when I'm home."

"Mm, all right."

Lin Zirui hung up and lay there, not wanting to move, then rolled over and wrapped himself in the quilt.

He hadn't lain there long before the phone rang again.

He answered without checking who it was.

"Zirui, come down."

"Shaoyu — are you home?"

"I'm downstairs." Yang Shaoyu stood by his car, looking up toward Lin Zirui's second-floor room.

"Downstairs… you're downstairs at my place?" Lin Zirui guessed, a small hope stirring.

"Mm. I'm right downstairs. Come down."

"But — didn't you already go home?" Lin Zirui scrambled up off the bed and ran to the balcony.

And there was Yang Shaoyu, standing by his car, phone to his ear, looking up at him.

"I came to take you home with me." Yang Shaoyu's voice was low but warm, and the words made Lin Zirui's eyes prickle — he'd said, let's go home together.

"All right." Lin Zirui hung up, grabbed a few changes of clothes from the wardrobe without even packing them, and ran straight down.

Because the Lin stairwell wasn't connected to the living room, no one saw Lin Zirui go, and Lin Zirui didn't hear the cheerful talk in the living room either.

"Let's go home."

"All right." Yang Shaoyu smiled. Zirui was coming home with him now.

Yang Shaoyu had been driving home when Lin Zirui called. He pulled over to return the call, and one sentence revealed the sadness Lin Zirui tried to hide. Yang Shaoyu immediately turned the car around.

Watching Lin Zirui come out clutching an armful of clothes, Yang Shaoyu went to take them and set them on the back seat.

Then they got in, and once inside, Yang Shaoyu glanced at him, stroked his face, and said, "Silly. Let's go home."

Home, Lin Zirui felt hungry again and had a sudden craving for instant noodles.

Yang Shaoyu rolled up his sleeves and went to make them.

"Make it spicier," Lin Zirui put in.

"Not too spicy at night — just a little." Yang Shaoyu got the noodles out.

"All right."

Lin Zirui thought Yang Shaoyu looked rather dashing making noodles — clumsy at it, but managing to get them done for him all the same.

Lin Zirui slurped them right down, and gave a small burp.

Yang Shaoyu poured him a glass of juice, and Lin Zirui downed it in one.

Yang Shaoyu did not ask what had happened. He knew Lin Zirui's family and would not make him relive the hurt.

"Let's go put your clothes away." Yang Shaoyu picked up the clothes off the sofa.

"All right."

Though they hadn't been packed, they were soon put away — but looking at his clothes hanging beside Yang Shaoyu's, Lin Zirui couldn't help smiling.

This would be his home now: with someone who loved him, cared for him, kept him company.

It made Lin Zirui's heart feel happy. Content.
