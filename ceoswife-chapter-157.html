Asked to Mind a Child 拜托带孩子

July 2026

~6 min read

The shop had grown busier, but with only two employees besides Jiang Qi, they sometimes couldn't keep up.

Today the noon crowd kept Jiang Qi from meeting Ke Yan for lunch. Ke Yan wouldn't pressure him, but he had an errand afterward and worried Jiang Qi would skip his meal in the rush, so he called to insist that Jiang Qi eat.

Jiang Qi agreed readily, saying he'd go shortly.

But the moment he hung up he went back to shelving books, meaning to eat soon.

Ke Yan ate alone under the curious glances of Ke Group's staff. Had the CEO quarreled with Manager Jiang? Why else would he be alone?

At the shop, once noon passed and the crowd thinned, Jiang Qi sent his two staff to eat first; only when they'd returned did he go, and so hungry by then that he didn't fuss, grabbing something quick.

While he was out, Ke Yan came by the shop, and not finding Jiang Qi, asked, "Where's Jiang Qi?"

"The boss went out for lunch." Wang Xiaoyu, reshelving a book a customer had left, knew the moment Ke Yan came in that he must be here for the boss; she'd long suspected the two's relationship.

"He's only just gone to eat, this late?" Ke Yan frowned.

"Yes — it's been especially busy these two days."

Ke Yan sat on the sofa to wait. Coming downstairs, Zhu Zihao didn't recognize him, but the man's quiet authority was unmistakable. Ke Yan put on no airs, yet inspired an instinctive awe. Zhu Zihao glanced at Ke Group across the street. Did he work there?

Hungry, Jiang Qi hadn't fussed, eating a quick bowl of noodles before coming back.

He hadn't expected Ke Yan at the shop. "What brings you here?"

Ke Yan stood and came to him. "I went out on business and thought I'd come see you." Just that, nothing more, watching Jiang Qi.

Cold outside, and warm from the noodles, Jiang Qi felt his nose about to run; he sniffed, looked at Ke Yan, and said honestly, "Just went to eat — too busy at noon, only free now."

"If staff still aren't hired this while, I'll handle it. Oh — I went to the wedding-planning company just now; next is decorating the wedding."

"Really? I can't wait." Jiang Qi was thrilled.

"Look forward to it — I'll give you the wedding you'll love most." Ke Yan liked seeing Jiang Qi's delighted face, and dipped to kiss his forehead.

Jiang Qi startled, then, seeing no one downstairs, didn't push him off.

Ke Yan kissed down from the forehead — nose, face — slowly, lightly, like a tickle; Jiang Qi's face tickled, and he tilted his head up a little, but Ke Yan kept from his lips, kissing only his face, until Jiang Qi itched to kiss him back.

Just before Jiang Qi grew flustered with embarrassment, Ke Yan finally kissed his lips, licking lightly, parting them, hooking his tongue, the kiss deepening.

Jiang Qi closed his eyes, tilted his head up, gripping Ke Yan's suit; Ke Yan bent his head, one arm at Jiang Qi's waist, the other stroking his back…

Sunlight slanted in through the window, falling over them — and how lovely it was.

"Mama—!" A sudden, shrill cry broke them apart. Jiang Qi and Ke Yan looked toward it.

Wang Xiaoyu stood by a shelf, eyes wide, mouth open, staring at them in shock. For some reason Jiang Qi found her face funny.

Interrupted, Ke Yan frowned. "What is it?"

He was asking what? She had stepped out from behind the shelves and found them kissing. The shock had made her cry out, but now a flicker of excitement joined it.

But she didn't dare look at Ke Yan; though she'd never seen him angry, she'd interrupted them, and didn't know if he was. She shrank into her collar.

"All right, get back to work." Seeing the girl frightened by Ke Yan, and a little embarrassed himself at being caught, Jiang Qi told Ke Yan to head back.

Sorry as he was not to keep kissing, Ke Yan wasn't angry; he kissed Jiang Qi's cheek and went back to the office.

Seeing Ke Yan kiss him so heedlessly, Jiang Qi knew he had no reservations now — they'd been seen anyway.

He touched the kissed spot, smiling. The feeling hadn't changed — every time Ke Yan kissed him: excitement, nerves, racing heart, a happiness he couldn't suppress.

Wang Xiaoyu watched the boss smile, reminiscing, seeming not to blame her, with nothing to say about being caught — and shrank back behind the shelf to keep tidying.

Over the next few days the shop grew busier still. Jiang Qi missed meals and came home so exhausted that he fell asleep the moment he reached the bed, leaving Ke Yan frustrated. Worse, his long-quiet stomach began to ache again.

Ke Yan's heart ached — until, just as he was near losing his temper, Jiang Qi hired two more: a man and a woman, a couple, applying together.

Jiang Qi wasn't one to mind a couple working together, and seeing them both decent, told them to start the next day.

The couple settled in quickly. Within two days, Jiang Qi was back to handling payments while the four employees managed the rest. Ke Yan was visibly happier too: Jiang Qi no longer collapsed straight into sleep, leaving them energy for "other things" at night.

One day, in Ke Yan's office, Ke Shao came carrying Xiao Ke, and had Ke Yan call Jiang Qi over.

Ke Yan called Jiang Qi first, then asked Ke Shao, "What brings you here — and with such a big bag?"

"Can't I come without a reason?"

"Without a reason you'd never bother."

Ke Shao ignored him, playing with her son.

Soon Jiang Qi arrived; seeing him, Ke Shao handed Xiao Ke over.

Jiang Qi hadn't seen Xiao Ke in a while and took him at once.

Seeing how Jiang Qi loved Xiao Ke — and that Xiao Ke didn't cry — Ke Shao said, "Jiang Qi, since you love Xiao Ke so much, why not mind him? Watch him for a day, get familiar — so when you have a child of your own, you'll know how."

"Where are you going tomorrow?" Ke Yan cut in.

"Tomorrow Mom and I are going to the hot springs, and Dad's got golf with friends. Auntie Li's off, and Xiao Ke's father is away on business. I was going to have a servant mind him, but after his illness he only clings to me and Mom — anyone else picks him up and he cries. Today he didn't cry with Jiang Qi, so I'm asking you two for the day."

"You want us to mind a child?" Ke Yan plainly wouldn't agree.

"Sure, that's fine — though I'm afraid I'll do it badly." Jiang Qi, however, agreed without a second thought.

Ke Shao said quickly, "Don't worry, he's easy — I've written down everything you need to know, so no worries." She said it watching Ke Yan, afraid Jiang Qi's yes wouldn't matter if Ke Yan refused.

Jiang Qi looked at Ke Yan. "Ke Yan, what do you say?"

"If you want to, then do." Seeing Jiang Qi's eager face, Ke Yan couldn't refuse.

Ke Shao, seeing them agree, knew now that whatever Jiang Qi said went — no need to worry about her brother objecting.

"These are Xiao Ke's clothes, formula, and bottle. I wrote down how to prepare everything; the note's in the bag. Xiao Ke is yours for the day. Bye!" Ke Shao bolted, leaving one child and two grown men in the office.
