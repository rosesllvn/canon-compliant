Chapter 89

Coming Around 想通

July 2026

~8 min read

Yang Shaoyu stepped out of the elevator with his suit jacket in hand and saw Lin Zirui standing at his door, a bag in hand, head bowed toward the floor, his profile looking somewhat pale.

As if sensing someone watching him, Lin Zirui raised his head, and a smile came onto his face.

Yang Shaoyu was startled, then frowned, not knowing how Lin Zirui had turned up here — or rather, turned up before him this fast.

"You're back." Lin Zirui walked up to Yang Shaoyu, looking at him with a smile.

"How are you here." Yang Shaoyu didn't look at Lin Zirui, leaning against the wall behind him, suit jacket in one hand, the other in his trouser pocket, looking rather offhand.

Lin Zirui felt a flinch inside, but drew a deep breath and went on looking at Yang Shaoyu with a smile.

"You forgot to take your clothes yesterday." Lin Zirui handed him the bag.

Only then did Yang Shaoyu remember he'd left in such a rush yesterday he'd forgotten even his luggage; he reached out and took it.

"Shaoyu." Lin Zirui called softly. "My brother said if you like someone you shouldn't let go so easily; I — I like you. Can't we be together?" He finished and looked at Yang Shaoyu hopefully.

"I don't like you; why do this to yourself?" Yang Shaoyu turned his head away from Lin Zirui's eyes.

"Do you really not like me?" Lin Zirui lowered his head, but clenched his fists.

"Really; don't come again." With that Yang Shaoyu turned to go inside.

Lin Zirui stood frozen, his body trembling a little; hearing the sound of the door opening behind him, he suddenly jolted awake and stepped forward to catch Yang Shaoyu's hand, and while Yang Shaoyu wasn't paying attention, rose on his toes and kissed Yang Shaoyu's mouth.

Yang Shaoyu froze; Lin Zirui's lips were very soft, a little cold; Lin Zirui's lips moved, brushing carefully against Yang Shaoyu's.

Yang Shaoyu reacted at once and pushed Lin Zirui away.

But the push sent Lin Zirui slamming into the wall with a thud.

His head hit the wall especially hard.

A silence; Lin Zirui braced on the wall and straightened up, then gave a self-mocking laugh and covered his eyes with a hand — but it couldn't stop the tears seeping through his fingers.

Yang Shaoyu took a step forward, wanting to stop Lin Zirui from crying.

Lin Zirui took his hand from his eyes, his expression carrying the resolve of someone who'd seen through it all. "Just take these two days as me being shameless; I won't pester you anymore." With that he left.

Yang Shaoyu hesitated, and didn't go after him.

He turned back inside, sat on the sofa, his head full of Lin Zirui; thinking of that kiss just now, his heart warmed.

In the elevator Lin Zirui looked at his face in the mirror — a sorry sight; he laughed, and wiped away the tears.

From a young age Lin Zirui had understood one truth: what isn't yours isn't yours, and can't be forced.

Like his parents' care, his parents' love — Lin Zirui had once thought these were things he could never force into being his whole life.

Now Lin Zirui thought Yang Shaoyu's love was something he couldn't force either; perhaps this love would never be his...

This truth Lin Zirui had grasped as a child — grasped from the day eldest and second brother forgot him at the amusement park. Lin Zirui thought himself always a petty sort; for a while in childhood he'd much disliked his two brothers, until he grew up and disliked them less.

Ask why he'd disliked only his two brothers and not his parents or little sister? Because his two brothers had given him love, and then used their love for the little sister to wound him.

But Yang Shaoyu had never given him love; Lin Zirui didn't dislike him — he'd only given up on him, and wouldn't tell him he liked him again. Liking someone was so tiring.

This was Lin Zirui's conclusion.

The days went on at their unhurried pace; Yang Shaoyu went to work as before, busy with his job — only, while working, he couldn't help thinking of Lin Zirui, the way he'd covered his eyes and wept that day, the kiss that day.

And Lin Zirui, after that day, often took others' night shifts, trying to keep himself busier, because in the quiet of being alone he always thought of Yang Shaoyu, and his heart still ached.

Eldest brother paced his office with a frown; the secretary at the door, seeing his gloomy face, didn't dare go in.

"What is it?" Second brother came over and saw this scene.

"Get in here, quick." Eldest brother waved second brother in.

"What?" Second brother leaned back on the sofa and stretched.

"Xiao Rui's got someone he likes."

"What?" Second brother sat bolt upright.

"And that person doesn't like Xiao Rui; he's confessed twice and failed both times."

"So that's why Xiao Rui's been off lately?" Second brother realized he hadn't seen Lin Zirui in nearly three days; lately Lin Zirui was always on night shift, never home in the daytime, and not to be found even when he went to the hospital.

"Yes." Eldest brother's heart ached too; Xiao Rui had lost who-knows-how-much weight lately.

"How'd he confess twice — given Xiao Rui's nature, once would..." Second brother stopped mid-sentence at the odd look on eldest brother's face.

"Don't tell me you were the one who told Xiao Rui to go confess again?" Second brother narrowed his eyes.

"I thought the other person definitely wouldn't refuse Xiao Rui..." Eldest brother was rather mortified too.

"You idiot." Second brother cursed eldest brother in exasperation.

Eldest brother let him curse, and though his face was ugly he said nothing, only sighing, and decided to have a nourishing soup stewed at home for Lin Zirui.

"Shaoyu?" Jiang Qi looked at the Yang Shaoyu across the table, who was staring blankly at a bowl of soup.

"Hm?" Yang Shaoyu answered, still looking at the soup.

Today Yang Shaoyu was at Ke Yan's home for dinner, and Jiang Qi had specially asked Auntie Li to make a pot of chicken-and-mushroom soup, because Yang Shaoyu had particularly liked it during his hospital stay.

Yang Shaoyu looked at the chicken-and-mushroom soup and remembered Lin Zirui liked it too — though Lin Zirui insisted Jiang Qi's was the better-tasting.

He smiled, then suddenly realized he hadn't seen Lin Zirui in many days, and sighed again.

Jiang Qi watched Yang Shaoyu's face shift like a conjurer's, not knowing what was the matter.

After dinner, Jiang Qi quietly pulled Ke Yan into the kitchen. "Did you see the state of Shaoyu?"

"Want me to go ask?"

"Mm." Jiang Qi nodded. "Go on, ask; I'll wash some fruit for you two."

Ke Yan walked out of the kitchen, sat on the sofa, looked at the weary-faced Yang Shaoyu, and said, "What's with you lately? You don't go out for fun, you're distracted — what happened?"

Yang Shaoyu glanced at Ke Yan. "Tell me — don't two men strike you as strange?"

Ke Yan looked at him. "You sure you want to ask me?"

Yang Shaoyu knew it was a pointless question — weren't Ke Yan and Jiang Qi two men?

"What matters isn't that we're both men. What matters is that he's Jiang Qi. If it weren't him, what difference would the person's sex make? Jiang Qi happens to be a man, but I love the person he is," Ke Yan said slowly.

It was rare for Yang Shaoyu to hear Ke Yan say such a thing; he was a little stunned.

"As long as you like someone, why look at gender?" Ke Yan went on.

It's precisely because you love that you then go and fret over the other person being the same sex as you — but since you've already fallen for them, why fret?

"I can see Doctor Lin likes you, and I'd guess this is what you've been fretting over — but do you really not like him? My instinct says you do." Ke Yan read people fairly well, and after so many years of friendship with Yang Shaoyu, how could he not know what he was thinking?

"How do you know Zirui likes me?" Yang Shaoyu asked back.

"It's obvious."

Yang Shaoyu remembered again the way Lin Zirui had wept in grief before, and suddenly very much wanted to see him; he suddenly understood that this ache he felt at the sight of Lin Zirui wasn't regret over losing a good friend — it was heartache.

At this thought Yang Shaoyu got up to go find Lin Zirui, wanting to see him.

Ke Yan walked into the kitchen and saw Jiang Qi sneaking bites of fruit; he went over and patted Jiang Qi's backside. Jiang Qi turned and asked, "Finished talking?"

"Mm; Shaoyu and Doctor Lin really are—"

"Got something going on?" Jiang Qi cut in to finish.

"Yes." Ke Yan scooped Jiang Qi up. "They'll sort it out themselves; let's get some good rest."

Ke Yan carried Jiang Qi upstairs and tumbled him onto the bed.
