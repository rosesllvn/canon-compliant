Chapter 59

A Few Things About Recovering 康复三两事

July 2026

~6 min read

Back home from the doctor, Jiang Qi's stomach was already rumbling with hunger. Changed and downstairs, he looked at the tableful of dishes, mouth watering — when suddenly Auntie Li set a bowl of chicken-and-egg-drop congee in front of him.

Jiang Qi looked from the tasty chicken-and-egg-drop congee to the even tastier dishes and quietly pushed the bowl away. Auntie Li pushed it straight back, confiscated his chopsticks, and left him only a spoon.

Jiang Qi looked silently at the spoon in his hand. No help for it; he scooped up a mouthful of congee. Tasty it was, but the flavor was a bit bland, and it tasted blander still when he looked at the sweet-and-sour ribs and steamed fish on the table.

Because Ke Yan had taken a phone call just as he was about to come down, he wasn't down yet. Jiang Qi watched Auntie Li putting her shoes on at the door, thinking inwardly — once Auntie Li leaves, I can sneak some.

"I've got something to attend to, so I'm heading off early — and no sneaking food, mind."

"..." His plan seen through, not a trace of awkwardness showed on Jiang Qi's face.

Once Auntie Li had shut the door behind her, Jiang Qi picked up Ke Yan's chopsticks and took a piece of sweet-and-sour ribs. Mm, delicious.

Jiang Qi ate quickly, one eye on the staircase in case Ke Yan appeared.

By the time Ke Yan came down Jiang Qi had eaten a good many pieces, even spat the bones into the kitchen trash, and was feigning innocence as he ate his egg-drop chicken congee.

Ke Yan took a bite of rice and looked at Jiang Qi, who calmly avoided looking back.

"Jiang Qi."

"Mm?"

"You've got sauce on your mouth."

"Huh?" Jiang Qi wiped the corner of his mouth — sauce, sure enough.

"Ha, ha, haha." Jiang Qi gave a dry laugh under Ke Yan's gaze.

"Tasty?" Ke Yan looked at Jiang Qi expressionlessly.

"I was wrong." Jiang Qi pursed his lips.

"Eat, then." Ke Yan picked up a piece of rib and held it to Jiang Qi's mouth.

Jiang Qi ate it in one bite, then looked at Ke Yan, puzzled.

"I never said you couldn't eat ribs, just that you should eat less."

"Mm." Jiang Qi nodded with a smile.

In a fine mood, Jiang Qi finished his congee and ribs, then took the water Ke Yan poured. The prescription came to over a dozen pills. Sighing, he tossed the lot into his mouth and washed them down.

When two people have been together long enough, their habits and routines bend toward each other — less accommodation than simple doting. That is love; at least, it is Ke Yan and Jiang Qi's small, everyday love.

Jiang Qi's cold came on fast, but with Ke Yan and Auntie Li's "care" it went fast too; sure enough, in under two days he was well, and the medicine ran out right on cue.

Cold gone, body comfortable, even his mood lifted; he smiled at everyone he saw, until others got goosebumps.

Jiang Qi raced through his work, planning to go to the supermarket with Ke Yan.

Jiang Qi liked shopping with Ke Yan; choosing things together made them feel like an old married couple.

Reaching the supermarket and parking the car, Jiang Qi pushed the cart ahead of Ke Yan; the first place the two went was the snack section — well, actually Jiang Qi led the way.

Jiang Qi liked the occasional couple of chocolates, but didn't much care for other snacks, especially potato chips, because they gave him a sore throat. He put two or three boxes of chocolate in the basket; though the supermarket's chocolate wasn't as good as what Ke Yan brought back, the flavor was decent — and even a small box cost nearly a hundred yuan.

Chocolate got, Jiang Qi looked at nothing else, winding his way over to another aisle to grab two small jars of sweet-and-sour bayberry, then turned to ask Ke Yan, "Ke Yan, what do you want to eat?"

"Anything." Ke Yan stepped up and took the cart from Jiang Qi's hands. Every time they came to the supermarket Jiang Qi asked the same question and got the same answer, but he never tired of asking, and Ke Yan always answered.

Hearing "anything," Jiang Qi added a few favorites; before he noticed, his snacks filled half the cart.

He hurried out of the snack section and picked out shampoo and body wash with Ke Yan. Two grown men buying these things together inevitably drew a few extra glances, but Jiang Qi was used to it, and Ke Yan had never cared about such things in the first place.

The two went to the pajamas section, and Jiang Qi's eye landed at once on the two sets of couple's pajamas on the mannequins. Whether by mistake or for some other reason, both sets of couple's pajamas were men's styles. Jiang Qi fell for the pattern at first sight — a koala bear hugging a tree trunk. He snuck a glance at Ke Yan; Ke Yan noticed, and asked, "What is it?"

Jiang Qi pointed at the pajamas; Ke Yan glanced over and shook his head. "No."

Jiang Qi looked at the pajamas regretfully — he'd known Ke Yan wouldn't agree.

Jiang Qi looked back every few steps. Ke Yan pretended not to notice; he had no wish to wear such childish pajamas.

Back home, Jiang Qi's eyes still glittered, though he didn't let Ke Yan see, because if Ke Yan saw he'd surely know what Jiang Qi was scheming.

The next day, the moment Jiang Qi got off work he texted Ke Yan to head home first, then took a cab alone to the supermarket and bought the two sets of pajamas.

From the moment Jiang Qi came home he acted furtively — sneaking to the bedroom clutching his briefcase, then staring through dinner. Ke Yan couldn't make sense of it.

It wasn't until the next day that Ke Yan understood. Jiang Qi kept urging him off to shower the moment they'd eaten, and insisted on fetching Ke Yan's pajamas — well, ordinarily Jiang Qi fetched them anyway, so Ke Yan thought nothing of it.

Only after his shower did Ke Yan discover the reason: Jiang Qi had bought the pajamas. Half exasperated, half amused, he reluctantly put them on.

Coming out, he found Jiang Qi had already showered and was sitting on the bed waiting for him in pajamas exactly the same as his.

Jiang Qi, in his blue cartoon pajamas, sat quietly on the bed, and Ke Yan couldn't help a smile; his up-and-down mood all day quieted along with it.
