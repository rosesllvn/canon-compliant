Chapter 162

Yang's Little Brother 杨家小弟

July 2026

~5 min read

They were dining with the family that night, so Yang Shaoyu finished early and went with Lin Zirui to meet his younger brother at the airport.

The eldest brother was supposed to fetch him, but couldn't get away from the office, and Father and Mother Yang found the airport too far, so they had Yang Shaowen, the little brother, take a taxi back himself.

With the rest of the family occupied, Yang Shaoyu and Lin Zirui were the only ones free to meet him.

On the way, Lin Zirui realized he knew only that Yang Shaoyu had a younger brother who traveled abroad. "Shaoyu, what's your little brother like?"

"Him — a flighty little thing, always dressed up flashy, but with a very literary name, Yang Shaowen. His temperament's good, though, easygoing and cheerful — a bit like Dad."

"Is your little brother often abroad?"

"No — he travels abroad for a stretch each year. When he's in the country he helps out at the family company. In a moment, if you see him at the airport, you definitely won't pick him out as my brother."

"Really?" Lin Zirui was all the more curious what the little brother was like.

The drive ran into some traffic, so by the time they reached the airport Yang Shaowen's flight had already landed — they'd arrived just before, with no delay. They went to check whether the flight was delayed; it wasn't, so Yang Shaoyu took out his phone to call Yang Shaowen, meaning to ask where he was now.

"The number you have dialed is powered off…" Off. Yang Shaoyu hung up. "Shaowen's phone is off; let's look for him — if we can't find him we'll have him make his own way back."

"All right, let's split up."

"Wait." Yang Shaoyu reminded Lin Zirui once more, "If you see someone at the airport dressed very flashy, that'll be Shaowen."

Lin Zirui and Yang Shaoyu split up — but in an airport this size with this many people, how to find him? And, he'd forgotten, he had no idea what Yang Shaowen looked like. Lin Zirui recalled Yang Shaoyu saying Yang Shaowen dressed flashy, drawing eyes.

Then his gaze caught on a spectacularly dressed man: a vivid green coat, deep-red trousers, black boots, sunglasses, and a long, wavy wig. He pulled a suitcase behind him, and somehow the red-and-green ensemble looked more striking than ridiculous—though Lin Zirui still found it rather gaudy.

Could this be the little brother?

Lin Zirui stared at the man, not even noticing Yang Shaoyu come up beside him.

"I couldn't find Shaowen. You?" Yang Shaoyu asked.

Lin Zirui hesitated. "Shaoyu, look — is that Shaowen?"

Yang Shaoyu followed Lin Zirui's pointing finger, and at a glance saw the flashily-dressed Shaowen, and said, "Yes — that's Shaowen." And he pulled Lin Zirui toward him.

"Shaowen." Yang Shaoyu called out.

Yang Shaowen turned, saw Yang Shaoyu coming, glad. "I thought I'd have to taxi back myself."

"Your phone — how is it off?"

"Out of battery." Yang Shaowen tapped the phone in his hand.

"Right — this is Zirui, my partner." Yang Shaoyu introduced him.

"I know, Mom told me. Hello, Second Sister-in-law." Yang Shaowen shook Lin Zirui's hand. "Second sister-in-law — er—"

Second sister-in-law? Lin Zirui glanced helplessly at Yang Shaoyu and ducked his chin.

"You little brat — don't call him second sister-in-law from now on, call him second brother; second sister-in-law makes Zirui uncomfortable," Yang Shaoyu said.

"Got it, Second Brother." Yang Shaowen changed it naturally, calling Lin Zirui Second Brother.

"Little brother — half a day on a plane, your stomach must be empty too; let's get in the car and chat." Lin Zirui's first impression of Yang Shaowen hadn't been great, but he warmed to him at once: this little brother really was like Father Yang; though his looks weren't much like, his way of talking and his laugh were just like Father Yang's.

The three got in, Yang Shaowen in the back, chatting away happily with Lin Zirui. "Second Brother, you don't know how shocked I was when I heard my brother had taken in a partner — I thought I'd misheard."

"Why so shocked?" Lin Zirui didn't follow.

"Because my brother used to be a total heartbreaker, drifting from one woman to the next and never settling down."

Lin Zirui glanced at Yang Shaoyu, and Yang Shaoyu, getting that look, went a little cold behind the eyes.

"Your brother really was such a heartbreaker before?"

"Yes — you don't know, my mom used to worry he'd go on like this and never marry, just float around among the flowers." Yang Shaowen, of course, had no idea his second brother's heart was set against this; far from holding his tongue, he chattered on cheerfully at Lin Zirui.

"Was he, now." Lin Zirui's eyes turned up at the corners.

"Yeah — you don't know, before he met you he really was a flighty one." Yang Shaowen, blithe, didn't catch the unspoken between his brother and Lin Zirui.

"Is that so?" Lin Zirui raised a brow.

Lin Zirui really did want to have Yang Shaoyu pull over and explain himself properly, but they were on the highway — no stopping.

So he could only say to Yang Shaowen, "Hush for me, go to sleep, talk when we're home."

"But I'm not sleepy." Yang Shaowen was genuinely wide awake; how could he sleep?

"Sleep." Yang Shaoyu had no wish for Yang Shaowen to keep talking nonsense.

"All right, all right." Yang Shaowen, his second brother stiff-faced, lay obediently down to sleep — but really, a little bit, he couldn't.

"Zirui — I admit I really was a heartbreaker before I met you, but after I met you I was never one again." That said out loud, he didn't fear Yang Shaowen hearing.

And Yang Shaowen, only now realizing he'd let something slip, promptly shut his eyes — he seemed to be in a spot.

"All right, drive on — we'll talk at home." Lin Zirui wasn't too bothered either; he knew Shaowen's nature, knew how he doted on him, and Yang Shaoyu had behaved himself well enough.

Yang Shaoyu let out a breath, relieved Lin Zirui trusted him — though privately he regretted it: how could he have been such a flighty one before?

At some point the jet-lagged Yang Shaowen had fallen asleep, curled up peacefully in the back seat.
