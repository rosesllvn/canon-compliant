The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 33

That Makes Sense 有道理

July 2026

~4 min read

Jiang Qi held a love letter in his hand. Where had it come from?

He'd been on his way to class when he ran into the beauty from next door, who thrust a love letter at him the moment she saw him. He froze three seconds, then took it; he was about to ask, for me? when the beauty spoke first. "Could you give this to Ye Xingwei for me?"

Seeing it was a delivery and not a confession, Jiang Qi wasn't annoyed. "Wouldn't it be better to give it to him yourself?"

The beauty was not pleased. "What if I give it to him and he won't read it?"

"He might not read it from me either."

"You're in the same dorm — better than me, anyway. The point is, you absolutely have to give it to him." And she walked off, leaving Jiang Qi rooted there. How had something this troublesome landed on him?

With class about to start, he had no choice but to make the trip. But holding the letter made him uneasy — losing it would be no good — so he hurried off to find Ye Xingwei, not knowing whether he'd still be in the classroom.

Walking fast, at a corner he collided with a boy; the letter dropped, and as Jiang Qi dodged toward the wall, the shoulder about to hit it was caught by someone's arm.

He looked up, delighted. "Ke Yan."

Ke Yan picked the pale-pink envelope off the floor and handed it back. "Watch where you're going."

"Okay."

Ke Yan glanced at it. "What's this?"

"Hm? A love letter."

His brow furrowed. "For whom?"

"For Ye Xingwei."

For Ye Xingwei? It hadn't occurred to Ke Yan that Jiang Qi might like him; he was startled — and, more than that, displeased. He looked at Jiang Qi, noticed the thin sheen of sweat on his forehead, and instinctively reached out and wiped it away.

That single touch threw Jiang Qi into disarray — heart racing, face hot, a strange feeling he had no idea how to handle. He stood frozen five or six seconds, then turned and left without so much as a goodbye — no, not left: fled.

Ke Yan didn't stop him. He looked at his own hand, and clenched it.

Heart pounding, Jiang Qi walked back to the classroom, listened to the lecture with his mind elsewhere, and absently set the letter on his desk — forgetting, this time, that he mustn't lose it. He wasn't stupid; he knew the way he felt around Ke Yan was different from how it was with anyone else. He simply didn't want to think too deeply about it. He wanted to read with Ke Yan, walk the roads with him, exchange a couple of words, eat with him. Round and round his thoughts went, and by the time he sighed and decided to stop, class was over.

Tidying his desk, he smacked his forehead — he'd nearly forgotten the letter. He picked it up and ambled off to find Ye Xingwei.

"Here." He found him in the canteen and set the letter before him.

Ye Xingwei tore it open. "A love letter?"

"Mm."

"From you?"

"The beauty next door asked me to give it to you." Jiang Qi rolled his eyes. "As if I'd write you a love letter."

Ye Xingwei glanced at it twice, then stopped. "Boring."

"Then what's not boring?"

He pondered. "I don't know."

"You don't know? Well — is there anyone you like?"

"Anyone I like?" He shook his head. "No."

"Maybe if you found someone, you'd find things interesting," Jiang Qi suggested.

Ye Xingwei shrugged. "Someone to like is too hard to find."

Jiang Qi wasn't one to be late, so he always arrived before Ke Yan — but today Ke Yan was there even earlier, and the moment Jiang Qi sat down he found him staring. "What is it?"

"You gave the love letter to Ye Xingwei?"

"Yeah — nearly forgot to, actually."

"Someone asked you to pass it on?" On reflection, Ke Yan had calmly concluded this was the likelier explanation.

"Mm, the beauty asked me to."

"Oh." The tightness that had been in his chest all this while eased. "Don't go playing courier for people in future."

"Why?"

"Once there's a first, there'll be a second. Do you want to be everyone's postman?"

Jiang Qi thought about it, and nodded. "That makes sense."
