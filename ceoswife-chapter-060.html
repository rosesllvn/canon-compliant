The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 60

Xue Cheng 薛成

July 2026

~5 min read

"Jiang Qi?"

About to leave work that day, Jiang Qi heard someone call his name and turned to look — a man in a suit carrying a briefcase, with two people behind him. Jiang Qi felt the man looked a little familiar, but couldn't recall where he'd seen him.

"You're Jiang Qi, aren't you?" the man asked again.

Jiang Qi nodded. "Yes, I'm Jiang Qi; and you are..."

"You don't recognize me? It's me, Xue Cheng — I was in your class before."

"Sorry, I've a poor memory." Jiang Qi smiled politely. The name brought a little back: they'd been high-school and university classmates, in the same class once. Xue Cheng had played basketball well, was handsome, and kept the girls swooning.

"It's fine — but you ought to remember now, eh."

"Mm, definitely, definitely."

Jiang Qi answered with a smile.

Xue Cheng glanced at the briefcase in Jiang Qi's hand — not high-end, an ordinary, very ordinary bag.

Not Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Dior, or Prada; Jiang Qi's suit was no famous brand either. Beside Xue Cheng's new tailored suit, he might have been a subordinate.

Xue Cheng was disappointed. He'd been negotiating a contract with Ke Group — without so much as seeing Ke Yan — and had hoped Jiang Qi might help. But dressed this "shabbily," his old classmate could hardly hold a senior post.

"How are your talks going?" Jiang Qi glanced at Xue Cheng and the two behind him. Xue Cheng masked his expression well, and Jiang Qi read nothing from it, but the two behind Xue Cheng had probably gathered that Jiang Qi would be no help to their contract, and so looked a bit impatient.

"Oh, well enough." Xue Cheng answered vaguely.

"I'll be off then; we'll chat another time."

Jiang Qi tactfully said goodbye to Xue Cheng and the two behind him, and left. Jiang Qi wasn't put out — since they weren't really acquainted, there was no need to be.

Coming out of the company, Jiang Qi found Ke Yan's car outside too; he walked over, and the window came down.

"What are you doing here?" Jiang Qi said in a low voice.

"Come to pick you up, of course."

"Didn't I say I had driving practice today?" Jiang Qi had been working on his license lately and often had to practice.

"I'll take you over."

At that Jiang Qi got in without another word, glad enough to go with Ke Yan.

After practice Ke Yan drove them to a seafood restaurant Jiang Qi had long wanted to try; Auntie Li was busy, and neither felt like cooking.

Garlic abalone, steamed grouper, lobster, crab, blanched squid — they filled the table, and Jiang Qi ate without pause. Ke Yan shelled morsels, dipped them in sauce, and fed him until both their bellies were round.

By the time the two came out of the restaurant Jiang Qi had already taken off his suit jacket and tie, his stomach a bit full — no, very full.

"Burp—" Jiang Qi gave a satisfied belch and was about to say something to Ke Yan when he suddenly spotted someone, pulled Ke Yan off to the side, and turned his face away to keep Xue Cheng, approaching from the opposite direction, from seeing him.

"What is it?" Ke Yan asked, puzzled.

"I just saw an old classmate — saw him at the company today too; seems he's trying to talk business with you."

"Is that so?"

Ke Yan understood Jiang Qi's move just now — he didn't want the other to see him with Ke Yan, in case the man, failing to land the deal, came to Jiang Qi instead.

He took Jiang Qi's hand and squeezed it lightly, a faint feeling stirring in him that even he hadn't quite noticed...

Suddenly the hand Ke Yan held turned and gripped Ke Yan's hand back, pulling at him; Ke Yan looked at Jiang Qi, not knowing what he was up to.

And before Ke Yan could react, Jiang Qi rose slightly on his toes and kissed the corner of Ke Yan's mouth.

Ke Yan was half a head taller; unless he bent, Jiang Qi couldn't reach however high he stretched.

Kiss done, standing straight again, Jiang Qi said softly, "It's not that I don't want him to know about us, nor that I'm afraid of him knowing — only that I don't want people to learn of my relationship with you and then use that relationship..."

Ke Yan gave a soft laugh and took Jiang Qi's hand again, this time not a light squeeze but a firm, heavy clasp. "I understand. Don't worry."

"Mm." Jiang Qi nodded with a smile.

Two men walking hand in hand through the crowd — perhaps strange, but... let it be strange, then.

Meanwhile Xue Cheng was taking his girlfriend to the same restaurant when he realized he'd left his phone. "Yinyin, wait inside; I'll get it from the car."

Lin Yinyin shot Xue Cheng a glare, then said coquettishly, "Be quick, then; how are you so scatterbrained."

"Isn't it because I only had eyes for you? Who told you to dress so beautifully today."

"Hmph, smooth talker. All right, hurry up; I'll wait inside." For all her words, Lin Yinyin's face had bloomed into a smile, her mood improved.

"Mm, I'll go get it." With that Xue Cheng walked back, and the moment he turned away the impatience surfaced on his face; he muttered inwardly, if you weren't the boss's daughter I wouldn't look twice at you.

Phone retrieved, Xue Cheng saw two men beside another car — one tall, the other half a head shorter. The taller had his back turned, but the shorter looked remarkably like Jiang Qi. Before Xue Cheng could get closer, they drove away.

Xue Cheng stared at the departing car — a car he couldn't have bought even if he'd wanted to. If he hadn't misseen and that really was Jiang Qi, then Jiang Qi was no simple matter: either he was very rich, or he'd made a rich friend.

Xue Cheng actually believed the second more. He tugged the corner of his mouth — maybe there was something to be made use of here.
