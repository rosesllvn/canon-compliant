Chapter 17

What Are You Doing Here?? 你在这做什么？？

July 2026

~4 min read

A night breeze stirred, threading a little cool into the sweltering summer dark. The trees rustled, and the small lamps along the path always had any number of insects whirling around them, which made it all seem less lonely. Before half past nine the school gate stood quiet. Two hundred meters out, turn left, and in the shrubbery along a broad road three figures shifted about furtively — one of them sitting on the grass fiddling with a camera, his back to the other two.

"Are you sure we're doing this?" he asked.

"Of course. We've no way out now."

"That's right — the two of us already took the money."

"And why do I have to come along?"

One of the figures clapped him on the shoulder. "As your friend, you ought to share in the good fortune. A money-making chance comes up, and naturally I wouldn't forget you — be grateful."

"I'd much rather go back to sleep."

"Not allowed."

"You're here now anyway — wait a bit and we'll go back together."

Jiang Qi was thoroughly fed up. He held the camera, yawned, and looked up at the sky; they'd said it might rain. For the past two days Liu Hao and Meng Qing had been up to who-knew-what, sent by those girls to tail Ke Yan, and the moment dinner ended they'd dragged Jiang Qi out to feed the mosquitoes. Ke Yan, again. He felt as though he heard that name everywhere now.

"Are you two even sure he'll come this way?" Jiang Qi really did think the pair unreliable.

"Of c—" Before Meng Qing could finish, Liu Hao's excited voice cut in. "Hey, look — here he comes."

The three looked toward the road together. "Quick, Jiang Qi — shoot," Meng Qing urged.

Jiang Qi raised the camera and took two shots, thinking Ke Yan looked familiar.

"That's enough, isn't it? Let's go back." He turned to leave — and Meng Qing caught him by the collar. "Our mission's not done," Liu Hao said. "We have to find where he lives."

"Are you serious?" Jiang Qi said, loud. "What if we get caught? And the dorm's about to lock up — shouldn't we head back?"

"Shh, keep it down. If we're careful, nothing'll go wrong. Hurry and keep up, or we'll lose him."

So the three crept along behind, Liu Hao in the lead, Meng Qing hauling a Jiang Qi who kept trying to slip away. Jiang Qi felt very much a stalker just then.

With their clumsy technique they followed Ke Yan some ten minutes, watching him walk up to a house, take out a key, and go in. Jiang Qi lifted the camera, snapped two more, then asked, "Now can we go back?" No one answered. He looked around — empty — then ahead, at the two crouched low against Ke Yan's window, peeping in. All at once the camera felt heavy in his hands; he badly wanted to fling it away.

Helpless, he stooped and shuffled over to them. "You two really aren't afraid of being caught?"

They pretended not to hear. "Jiang Qi, go wait for us up front," Liu Hao said. "We'll check the back window." ... "Fine."

Jiang Qi went and sat on the steps before the door, looking through the photos, and thought they'd come out rather well.

"What are you doing here?" A cold voice came suddenly from behind him. He jumped — clearly not expecting anyone at his back. Standing and turning, he saw Ke Yan, and instinctively hid the camera behind him.

"Who are you?"

"J-Jiang Qi," he stammered.

"What are you doing here?"

The question sent a guilty jolt through him. "I'm just — j-just passing by."

Ke Yan frowned. Did Jiang Qi really imagine he hadn't noticed the lot of them tailing him?

Rumble— a crack of thunder rolled across the sky, and pat, pat, the rain began to fall.

Ke Yan watched it gradually soak through Jiang Qi's clothes, and stepped aside. "Come in."

Jiang Qi blinked, gripping the camera tighter. An inexplicable thread of guilt ran through him. This person really is kind.

Ke Yan found the dazed look funny. "Hm? Not coming in?"

"Ah — yes, I'll come in." At the words, Jiang Qi's heart sped a few beats, which felt very strange. He was about to go in when he remembered the camera. He glanced left and right, swiftly tossed it into the bushes, and walked into the house.

Ke Yan looked toward the bushes, then followed him in.
