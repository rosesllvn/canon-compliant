Chapter 34

Don't Say I'm Good-Looking 不准说我好看

July 2026

~4 min read

"There seem to be a lot more people in the library lately." Jiang Qi chatted idly with Ke Yan as he read — a habit of his now. Ke Yan didn't like talk while he read, but lately he'd grown used to it.

Ke Yan turned a page. "They're loud."

"They're not, though — nobody's talking, everyone's reading quietly." Jiang Qi wondered whether Ke Yan's idea of "loud" matched his own.

"I mean they keep looking over," Ke Yan explained.

Jiang Qi lifted his head and observed a while. "You're right — they do seem to be looking at you." Lately the library had been strange: apart from him and Ke Yan the numbers had swelled, and every last one of the new arrivals was a girl — girls who plainly hadn't come to read. He looked at Ke Yan across from him and couldn't help blurting, "Ke Yan, you're really good-looking."

The words were no sooner out than Ke Yan's eyes narrowed and he pinched Jiang Qi's face. "Don't say I'm good-looking."

"Whuh fur?" Jiang Qi mumbled, face squished in his grip.

"Just remember it. Don't ask so many questions."

Jiang Qi wanted to ask more — but Ke Yan seemed to read his mind and pinched harder, drawing a yelp, at which he eased off.

While the two were busy with their pinching, the girls watched until their eyes went green, thinking how nice it would be to have their own faces pinched by Ke Yan — though they breathed a sigh of relief too: thank goodness Jiang Qi's a man; if he were a woman, we'd skin her alive. But — how could that ever be.

When Ke Yan let go, one side of Jiang Qi's face had gone red. As he withdrew his hand he quite naturally gave the cheek a rub — at which both of Jiang Qi's cheeks went red, and he quickly ducked his head so Ke Yan wouldn't see.

The two had plainly forgotten there were others around, and had already lowered their heads to read in earnest, leaving a whole crowd of girls grinding their teeth.

Over here, the previously irritable Ke Yan was calm again after pinching Jiang Qi's face — but Jiang Qi had turned irritable as anything.

They didn't read long today before giving up; with a crowd staring, who could keep reading?

They went to the canteen, and halfway through his meal an arm draped around Jiang Qi's neck. He looked up — Ye Xingwei. "What is it? Something the matter?"

Ye Xingwei blithely ignored Ke Yan's dangerous look from across the table, pulled Jiang Qi up out of his chair, and started walking him out.

Chopsticks still in hand, Jiang Qi was towed along. "What is it — I'm still eating."

"I'll take you to see and you'll know."

"Hey..."

Ke Yan didn't come after them, only went on eating. After a while he set down his chopsticks and sat there, half in thought, half in a daze.

Ye Xingwei hauled Jiang Qi back to the dorm, where Liu Hao and Meng Qing sat at the computer. He pulled Jiang Qi to the screen and gestured for him to look. It took a good while to understand: they were selling the clothes Jiang Qi had given Ye Xingwei. Embarrassed, Jiang Qi said, "Er — well, you weren't going to use them anyway, were you? You said you wouldn't wear them."

Ye Xingwei braced a hand on the desk. "Even so, you didn't have to go and sell them. Imagine someone else walking around in your clothes — it's a strange feeling."

Jiang Qi spread his hands. "Those clothes belong to Liu Hao and Meng Qing now — if you want them back, take it up with them."

Ye Xingwei sighed, walked over, and flicked Jiang Qi's forehead. "You're the one who gave them away in the first place."

"So you figured that out. How about a ten-percent cut if it sells well?" Jiang Qi offered.

"No." Ye Xingwei refused, then thought about it. "I'll name my condition later."

When did I ever agree to let him name a condition? But on reflection — never mind; he had been somewhat in the wrong himself. Ai-ya-ya, Jiang Qi — best look at the wicked little grin on Ye Xingwei's face.
