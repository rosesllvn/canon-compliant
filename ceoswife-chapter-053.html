The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 53

Ordinary 普通

July 2026

~5 min read

Lan Ruyin walked into Ke Yan's office; Ke Yan glanced up when the door opened, then lowered his head and went back to work.

Lan Ruyin walked up to the desk. "Son, your mother's here — the least you could do is lift your head."

"What is it?"

"Why so cold — exactly like your father used to be."

"You say the same thing every time you see me."

"Fine, I won't say it. Right — I heard something."

"Hm?"

"Someone's living at your place?"

"Mm." Ke Yan wasn't the least surprised his mother knew, since there was still Auntie Li at the house.

Lan Ruyin despaired of her son's coldness. How had she borne a child with his father's exact temperament — uninterested in everything and frosty besides?

"Man or woman?"

"A man."

"What's his name?"

"You wouldn't know?"

This was definitely a dig at her.

"That boy is called Jiang Qi?"

"Mm." Ke Yan paused. "Not a boy anymore."

Lan Ruyin's expression soured. As with girl and woman, there was a difference between boy and man — and she understood his emphasis perfectly.

"Heh, heh — I suppose he has a girlfriend." Lan Ruyin tried to pry Jiang Qi loose from Ke Yan.

Ke Yan gave Lan Ruyin a meaningful look. "He has no girlfriend, nor will he ever have one."

"Then he's married."

"If he were married, how would he be living with me?" Ke Yan's half-revealing answers left her no recourse. He knew she knew; the whole performance was a warning not to trouble Jiang Qi.

Lan Ruyin was gratified and angry at once: gratified that her son had learned to cherish someone, angry that the someone was another man.

"Mom just finished reading a manga, a pair of gay men, in love with each other, who later broke up because of the obstacles. The road of homosexuality is hard; those who don't understand will always outnumber those who do; people who see them as vermin are everywhere — how can the same sex be together."

"How can different sexes fall in love."

Lan Ruyin was stumped by Ke Yan's reply — she hadn't expected her son to grasp such an incisive point. "Hmph." Lan Ruyin gave a snort, not having expected her usually taciturn son to be so talkative today. "Mom, don't think I don't know there's manga of this sort on your bookshelf."

Lan Ruyin was furious, and ignored Ke Yan, walking straight out.

Lan Ruyin came out of Ke Yan's office, looked at the working Yang Shaoyu, and hauled him up by the arm.

The two walked to the tea room, and Lan Ruyin asked Yang Shaoyu in a low voice, "Do you have a photo of Jiang Qi?"

Yang Shaoyu looked at Lan Ruyin, puzzled. "Who's Jiang Qi? Do I know him?"

"Hmph hmph — stop pretending, I already know."

"Know what?"

"Ke Yan and that Jiang Qi are together now, aren't they?"

"Eh? Isn't Ke Yan working in there?" Yang Shaoyu kept playing dumb.

Lan Ruyin gave Yang Shaoyu's ear a twist. "Still playing dumb — I already know they're even living together."

"Aiya, Auntie Lan, let go — even if I knew I couldn't tell you; Ke Yan would kill me."

"Fine, then just tell me which floor Jiang Qi's on."

"Can't you just look it up?" Yang Shaoyu had no wish to say more — say more, err more; say less, err less.

"Not telling, eh? Then I'll go look it up, and once I find it I'll say you told me; see how you explain that."

"Okay okay okay, I'll tell, I'll tell — he's on the 17th floor."

"Hmph, wouldn't that have been easier said sooner?"

Watching Lan Ruyin's retreating back, Yang Shaoyu rubbed his pinched, aching ear — Ke Yan's mom was as fierce as ever.

On the seventeenth floor, not wanting to expose herself, Lan Ruyin crept along the wall and pretended to check her phone whenever anyone passed — furtiveness utterly at odds with her elegant clothes.

"Manager Jiang."

A voice suddenly came from the corner ahead. Surnamed Jiang? Lan Ruyin was now extremely sensitive to the two characters "Jiang" and "Qi."

Hearing "Manager Jiang," she took out her phone and pretended to make a call, head dipped low so her face couldn't be seen.

Two people came toward her, a man and a woman — the man on the phone, the woman beside him holding documents, seemingly to show him.

Lan Ruyin stole a look, then lowered her head. Not bad-looking, quite clean-cut — but only that. Ke Yan had been impossibly picky from childhood. If Jiang Qi were a great beauty, she might understand, but he wasn't nearly as handsome as her son. What did Ke Yan see in him?

Lan Ruyin went home disappointed. Yes, she read BL manga, and yes, she could accept homosexuality — but accepting it in general wasn't the same as accepting her own son. And the partner wasn't even remarkably good-looking. Not that she judged by appearances; she simply couldn't see what was so special.

When Ke Shao (Ke Yan's older sister) came home she found her mother lying on the sofa lost in thought.

Ke Shao went over and patted her. "Mom, what are you thinking about?"

"Thinking about your brother."

"Then go to the company and find him."

"Already did."

"Then what are you still thinking about?"

"Thinking about your brother and his boyfriend."

"Oh — ... boyfriend?"

"Mm."

"Mom, are you sure you didn't get it wrong? Shouldn't it be a girl, not a boy?"

"No — I saw him today; a hundred percent a man."

"Wow, who'd have thought Ke Yan never took a girlfriend, and the moment he did, it was a boyfriend."

Lan Ruyin was fretting, and at Ke Shao's words said, "Hurry and think of something."

"Think of what? Make Ke Yan break up with that man?"

"Best if so; if not, then go sound out this Jiang Qi — what's so good about him, why does your brother like him so much." "All right, leave it to me." "Good." Lan Ruyin nodded; she had great confidence in her daughter. "Right — why didn't you bring the child over today?"

Youthful as Ke Shao looked, she was already a mother in her thirties.

How would Sister Ke sound him out? She smiled over the investigation file in her hands. With Jiang Qi's work schedule and rest-day outings, it would be simple.

Jiang Qi — just you wait.

Jiang Qi sneezed.
