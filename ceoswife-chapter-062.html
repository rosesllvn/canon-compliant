Chapter 62

The Wedding Banquet (Part Two) 喜宴下

July 2026

~6 min read

"Liu Hao, over here." Oblivious to the standoff beside him, Jiang Qi waved to Liu Hao and Meng Qing.

Hearing Jiang Qi calling from a distance, Liu Hao tugged Meng Qing over — then stopped halfway, because the two of them saw Ke Yan and Ye Xingwei standing behind Jiang Qi glaring at each other, as if they might come to blows any second. Liu Hao, who'd hardly ever seen Ke Yan like this, took a step back and was caught by Meng Qing.

Liu Hao and Meng Qing steeled themselves and walked over; Jiang Qi smiled and exchanged a couple of words with them.

"Ke Yan, look." Just then Jiang Qi seemed to spot something and turned to call Ke Yan to look. The Ke Yan who a second before had been all daggers with Ye Xingwei, hearing Jiang Qi call him, instantly softened despite himself and looked where Jiang Qi pointed.

It was the groom and bride, greeting them from the second floor.

Ye Xingwei glanced at the excited Jiang Qi and frowned — he hadn't expected the two were still together.

From the look of Jiang Qi, he seemed quite in love with Ke Yan...

When Ye Xingwei learned they were together, he'd felt irritable and a little bereft — nothing more, he'd believed. Yet for two or three years, a certain reluctance had lingered.

Now, seeing Jiang Qi, Ye Xingwei tugged the corner of his mouth into a smile. Apart from looking less green than back then, Jiang Qi's character and all the rest really hadn't changed a bit — just as... likeable.

The crowd filed into the hall, where the groom and his parents stood at the door, beaming, clearly delighted; the bride wasn't in sight, probably off touching up her makeup, waiting for the ceremony to begin.

They took their seats among tables of old classmates. Friends were already talking; those seated with strangers had little to say.

Jiang Qi chatted merrily; besides Liu Hao, Meng Qing, Ye Xingwei, and Ke Yan, everyone at the table had once been close to him.

Chatting in a group like this, things might be a little stiff at first, but a couple of sentences in, that small stiffness was long gone.

Talking made Jiang Qi thirsty; suddenly Ke Yan handed him a glass of water, which Jiang Qi took and drank as a matter of course.

Ke Yan sat on Jiang Qi's right, Ye Xingwei on his left; seeing Ke Yan hand Jiang Qi a glass of water, Ye Xingwei stiffened.

A glass of water down, Jiang Qi let out a comfortable little breath.

Liu Hao, meanwhile, winked at Meng Qing — look, look, what's the deal with these three. Meng Qing wasn't the gossip Liu Hao was; seeing Liu Hao wink at him, he pinched the hand Liu Hao had under the table.

Before long the ceremony began; the bride walked step by step from the entrance toward the groom on her father's arm, the groom standing tall and straight before the officiant, waiting for her to come.

Step by step by step, until the bride reached the groom, and her father placed her hand in his.

The two said their vows and exchanged rings.

Jiang Qi clapped hard, and then the food began to come out; Jiang Qi ate while listening to the bride's and groom's families and old friends give their blessings on stage.

Jiang Qi ate with real relish, the dishes and the meat in his bowl — as for the seafood, Ke Yan shelled it for him, of course.

Suddenly another shrimp appeared in his bowl; Jiang Qi ate it reflexively, assuming Ke Yan had shelled it.

Only after two bites did Jiang Qi notice the shrimp had come from the wrong direction — Ke Yan sat on his right, but the shrimp had been placed into his bowl from the left.

Jiang Qi looked at Ye Xingwei on his left and smiled a thank-you.

"Jiang Qi, when did you turn into a young miss?" a man at the same table suddenly called out.

Jiang Qi looked at him, puzzled; the bespectacled man went on, "A grown man like you, and you need someone to wait on you to eat seafood."

"You're just jealous no one's waiting on you, aren't you." Jiang Qi was stunned at first by the bespectacled man's words, but recovered quickly and shot back with a mocking smile.

Whether Jiang Qi had hit the mark or not, the man muttered a couple of things and fell silent.

Jiang Qi let out a breath inwardly. Used to doing these intimate things with Ke Yan, he'd forgotten — not everyone knew about his relationship with Ke Yan, and not everyone could accept such a relationship.

For the rest of the time Jiang Qi went on eating and chatting, while Ke Yan picked food into his bowl, so that the things Jiang Qi liked wouldn't all get eaten by others while he was busy talking.

Ye Xingwei ate his own food, occasionally putting a couple of bites into Jiang Qi's bowl.

The mood at the table was good, though the chill radiating off Ke Yan made the tablemates think the air-conditioning had been turned up too high.

When the newlyweds came round to toast, the merry table drank too much. Jiang Qi melted drunkenly into Ke Yan's arms and was eventually carried out.

Ye Xingwei watched the two depart, then turned and walked off in another direction.

Back when he'd learned Jiang Qi and Ke Yan were together he'd felt nothing much, just a little lost; but coming to this wedding, hearing Jiang Qi had been invited too, hearing that name, the Ye Xingwei who hadn't wanted to come decided he might as well come and see.

Ye Xingwei had never clung to anything. Seeing the couple so close brought inevitable loss, but he could take it up and set it down.

Ye Xingwei was musing thus when someone beside him suddenly bumped into him, sending him staggering, nearly falling. He steadied himself and looked at the one who'd bumped him — a fair, soft-looking young man. Why "fair and soft"? Because the young man's skin looked fair and tender, his height a touch on the short side, his face wearing a pitiable look as he stared nervously at Ye Xingwei, flustered. "Sorry, sorry."

"It's fine." Seeing it wasn't on purpose, Ye Xingwei didn't make things hard for him, just said "it's fine" and walked off.

The young man watched Ye Xingwei depart, a blush rising on his face despite himself. So, so handsome a man.
