The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 112

Always Together 一直在一起

July 2026

~5 min read

Yang Shaoyu had heard about Jiang Qi coming out to Ke Xin. When he had liked women, parental approval never concerned him. He had no plans to marry—or even think about marriage—and his parents wanted him settled so badly that any woman who could bear children would have satisfied them.

Now it was different. He loved Lin Zirui and wanted to be with him openly, which meant facing his parents. But Lin Zirui was a man, and Yang Shaoyu feared they would object.

To be together in secret — setting aside that he himself wouldn't stand for it, even less could he bear to have Lin Zirui live a wronged, hidden life with him.

So he had no choice now but to face the matter of his parents.

Fortunately, Yang Shaoyu's married older brother already had a three-year-old child. With no fear of the Yang line dying out, the situation was less fraught.

Thinking of Lin Zirui, Yang Shaoyu's mood always lifted inexplicably; fretful with worry a moment ago, he felt a little better now, and thought he'd go find Lin Zirui for dinner that evening.

Lin Zirui wasn't on the late shift today and could leave as soon as it got dark. Stepping out of the hospital, he found, sure enough, Yang Shaoyu's car just pulling up, Yang Shaoyu coming toward him with a hand-warmer.

"Where shall we eat tonight?" He pressed the warmer into Lin Zirui's hands.

Lin Zirui's cold hands warmed a little from it, and his heart warmed more. This feeling was wonderful.

"Let's buy groceries and cook at home tonight." Lin Zirui suddenly wanted exactly that — like lovers, going to the supermarket, going home to cook together, eating happily, then fruit and television after, then sleep.

And the next morning, breakfast together, then off to work together.

It was, perhaps, more like newlyweds.

Lin Zirui liked the feeling of it — so warm, so happy.

"All right, let's stop at the supermarket near home first." Yang Shaoyu was more than willing, of course.

At the supermarket, Lin Zirui set down the warmer to get out of the car, and the moment he did, cold wind slipped down his collar and chilled him through. Yang Shaoyu hurried to wrap the scarf around him.

They walked from the lot toward the store, taking a cart at the entrance.

Yang Shaoyu pushed; Lin Zirui walked beside him. The supermarket was heated and crowded, and before long Lin Zirui felt warm and took off the scarf.

"By the way — can you cook?" Lin Zirui asked suddenly.

"A little." Yang Shaoyu cooked for himself now and then, though more often his housekeeper did it.

"I can a little too, but I haven't in so long I don't know if I still can."

"It's fine, we'll keep it simple. What do you feel like?"

"Stir-fried pork with green peppers, and sweet-and-sour spare ribs."

"All right, let's get the meat first."

"Mm." Lin Zirui glanced at him. This feeling — it really was wonderful.

"Have some soup." Over here, Jiang Qi and Ke Yan had gone for conveyor-belt sushi after work, and Ke Yan fetched a bowl of soup for Jiang Qi.

"Mm-hm." Mouth crammed with sushi, Jiang Qi could only manage a muffled reply.

"Eat a bit less, or you'll give yourself indigestion."

"Mm-hm." He nodded.

Ke Yan laughed helplessly. Was this a healthy appetite, or merely proof that the fear had passed? Yesterday Jiang Qi could barely eat; today his appetite had come roaring back.

But Ke Yan didn't plan to point it out, since pointing it out might rob Jiang Qi of his appetite.

"Wife, eat up — you took some grief today, didn't you."

A couple had sat down beside Ke Yan, the man fawningly setting a plate of sushi before the woman.

"Eat, eat — don't fill up on temper instead. Why didn't you say your parents don't approve of me? I brought a whole pile of gifts for them today and still got the cold shoulder."

Ke Yan caught their conversation and looked at Jiang Qi, who'd heard it too — but showed no reaction.

Ke Yan picked a grain of rice off the corner of Jiang Qi's mouth. "Not nervous anymore?"

Jiang Qi shook his head, then said suddenly, "As long as you love me, that's enough. Getting your parents' agreement matters — but what matters most is that you love me."

The words lifted Ke Yan's mood higher, and he dipped his head and kissed Jiang Qi. Jiang Qi started and glanced around — thank goodness no one was looking.

He didn't scold Ke Yan for it, just picked up his drink and took a sip.

Yang Shaoyu and Lin Zirui finished the groceries and added some fruit. Passing the underwear section, Yang Shaoyu picked up a pair in Lin Zirui's size.

Lin Zirui glanced at him, and Yang Shaoyu smiled. "Stay over at my place tonight."

"…All right." Lin Zirui dipped his head and murmured it.

"My underwear was too big for you last time, wasn't it?" Yang Shaoyu said, wickedly.

"…Mm." Lin Zirui's voice was smaller than a mosquito's.

Home again, Lin Zirui shed his heavy coat first, then took the bags from Yang Shaoyu and carried them to the kitchen.

He set out the groceries, and they divided the work: Lin Zirui washed the vegetables while Yang Shaoyu handled the meat. Finished, Lin Zirui reached for the cutting board and knife.

"Wait." Yang Shaoyu stopped him and took the knife. "You're a surgeon — those hands are for scalpels. Don't go using a kitchen knife and cut yourself."

"Ah? I won't." No one had ever said such a thing to Lin Zirui. Laughing, he fell into Yang Shaoyu's arms. "Shaoyu, I feel so silly. A little care makes me happy; one look makes me nervous. Everything you say and do tugs at my heart. My pulse races. I'm so happy."

Startled, Yang Shaoyu hastily set down the food and held him tight. "Me too. When you gave me one chance, I was happier than I'd ever been. I want to cherish you. I hope we'll always be together."

"Mm. Always together." Lin Zirui laughed until his eyes crinkled into crescents.
