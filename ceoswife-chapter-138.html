Chapter 138

The Yang Parents 杨家爸妈

July 2026

~5 min read

Shaoyu's mother—and she seemed to know him? At a loss, Lin Zirui looked at Yang Shaoyu. Shaoyu had promised somewhere that would make him happy; right now, Lin Zirui only felt bewildered.

"Come in." Seeing Lin Zirui frozen there, Mother Yang glanced at Yang Shaoyu, who nodded — your turn.

"Oh — oh, hello, I'm Lin Zirui, I — Shaoyu and I…"

"I know all about it, dear. Don't be nervous, come in, it's cold out." Mother Yang drew him inside.

"This…" Lin Zirui looked to Yang Shaoyu. What was going on?

"Sit, sit." Mother Yang had him sit and asked, "What would you like to drink?"

"I — anything's fine." Lin Zirui's hands were clasped tight on his knees.

"He likes hot cocoa." Yang Shaoyu sat beside him and took his cold hands.

"All right." Seeing Lin Zirui's nerves, Mother Yang went to the kitchen herself to have it made.

Yang Shaoyu soothed him. "Don't be nervous. My family's all agreed to us being together, and they know about your family situation. They'll be very good to you from now on."

"But how do they know?"

"I told them. I know your family's treatment has always hurt you. I can give you a lover's devotion and a warm home, but not a parent's love. My parents are yours now. They'll be good to you and give you that too."

"Mm." Lin Zirui nodded hard. "But — they don't object to us?"

"Don't worry. They couldn't quite accept it at first, but now they've fully agreed." Yang Shaoyu reassured him.

"When did you tell your parents?" Lin Zirui had had no idea.

"A while ago. They know my temperament, and there's an elder brother and a younger brother at home, so they don't worry about heirs — naturally there was nothing to object to. Don't be nervous, don't be afraid." Yang Shaoyu thought of how shocked his family had been when he'd first come home and said he'd fallen for a man.

"All right. From now on they're my parents too." Lin Zirui broke into his first genuinely happy smile since coming back from the Lin house today.

"Here, the cocoa's ready." In the kitchen Mother Yang had figured the two had finished talking. Really, that son of hers — not even explaining things clearly, leaving the poor boy so frightened.

"Thank you… Mom?" Lin Zirui worked up the courage to call her Mom.

Mother Yang blinked, then smiled and answered quickly, "Yes, good boy — I'm your mom now."

The unfamiliar title startled Mother Yang, but it also delighted her. Remembering how his own parents treated him made her heart ache for the boy.

"Mama." Lin Zirui said it again. It had been so long since he'd had this feeling — the feeling of calling someone Mom.

Mother Yang came over, edged Yang Shaoyu aside, and patted Lin Zirui's shoulder. "From now on you're my son. You and Shaoyu take good care of each other. This is the first time Shaoyu's ever brought someone home and said he loved them. You're both men, but his father and I aren't stuffy old types. We truly hope you two will be happy together."

"We will."

Though pushed aside, Yang Shaoyu was glad to see them getting along. He would slowly heal the wounds in Lin Zirui's heart and make him happy every day. Zirui's family still needed confronting; would they blame him for loving a man?

Lin Zirui's feelings just then were strange and wonderful. Looking at Mother Yang, he suddenly thought of his own parents — if they knew he was with a man they'd be furious, force him to break up with Shaoyu, maybe even cut off the parent-child bond.

"Your father's gone out — he should be back soon. Your eldest brother has moved out; he comes home on weekends. Your father and I liked the setting here, so we moved to the villa, but it's too far from work, so your eldest brother usually stays in the city center. His child fell ill a few days ago, though, so they haven't come this week. And Shaoyu has a younger brother too, but he's gone abroad lately — he'll be back for the New Year." Mother Yang carefully filled Lin Zirui in on the family.

"Eldest brother's kid is a bit mischievous, but he's adorable." Yang Shaoyu handed Lin Zirui the hot cocoa.

"Have we got a guest?" A man's voice came from the doorway.

Lin Zirui glanced at Mother Yang, who smiled. "That's your father, home."

She rose and went to the entryway, where Father Yang was indeed changing his shoes, fishing rod set beside him.

Father Yang loved to fish, and went often — though the times he actually caught anything were few.

"Who's home?" He'd seen the shoes at the door.

"Shaoyu's brought Zirui home. He's a good boy — be nice to him." Mother Yang didn't trust Father Yang's blunt manner.

"Don't worry." Father Yang walked into the living room. A young man stood. Father Yang first shot a glare at Yang Shaoyu on the sofa, then smiled at Lin Zirui. "You're Zirui? Sit, sit, no need to stand on ceremony."

"All right."

"Call him Dad," Yang Shaoyu prompted.

"Dad." With Mother Yang's support behind him, Lin Zirui worked up the nerve.

"Hahaha—it feels like I've gained another son." Father Yang was hearty and easygoing. He had beaten Shaoyu soundly when first told about the relationship, but raised no objection now that Lin Zirui stood before him.

The words eased Lin Zirui's heart further, and he felt how genuinely good and warm Father and Mother Yang were — it moved him.

"I hear you're a doctor — work must wear you out. Eat plenty, build yourself up."

"Yes, Dad — Zirui's got low blood sugar but never eats on time, sometimes forgets meals altogether," Yang Shaoyu put in.

"That won't do. You have to eat — how else will you have the strength to treat people?"

"I understand. I'll remember to eat from now on." Lin Zirui agreed obediently.

"Good." Father Yang nodded.

The four of them — Father Yang, Mother Yang, Lin Zirui, Yang Shaoyu — sat talking a long while.

Lin Zirui spent the whole day at the Yang house genuinely happy. A lover's love, a family's love — he was truly content.
