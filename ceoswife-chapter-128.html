Ke Yan returned to the company, and Jiang Qi stayed at work rather than coming upstairs. Seeing Ke Yan's bedraggled state, Yang Shaoyu teased, "I didn't expect Jiang Qi's mom to tell you off too. Seems you're not in your mother-in-law's good graces."

Ke Yan shot him a look and said coldly, "Wait until you go meet Lin Zirui's parents, then come laugh at me."

Yang Shaoyu's smile stiffened. True — he'd have to face Lin Zirui's parents one day too. Best not gloat now.

That evening, home for dinner, Jiang Lü heard her mother bring up Ke Yan's visit that day, and how she'd given him a sound beating.

"Mom, how could you hit him?" Jiang Lü couldn't help it.

"I hit Ke Yan on purpose — he's the one who turned your brother like this."

"How can that all be the CEO's fault? If Brother didn't like him, he'd never have brought him home. And love is a thing between two people."

"Jiang Lü, are your hides itching for a smack?" Mother Jiang, mortified, rounded on her.

"All right, all right, I'll stop." Jiang Lü shut her mouth fast — but slipped back to her room and secretly texted her brother: —Brother-husband came over today, but Mom gave him a beating.

Jiang Qi got the text while Ke Yan was in the shower. Remembering Ke Yan's talk of a lunch meeting today, he realized he must have gone to the Jiang house then — and gotten beaten. He wondered if it hurt, whether his mother had struck hard.

Worried, Jiang Qi went to the bathroom door and opened it.

Ke Yan was showering; feeling the door open, he turned to look, and saw Jiang Qi standing there.

"Want to shower together?" Ke Yan thought he'd come to join him.

"All right." Jiang Qi glanced first at Ke Yan's body but saw no bruise — Ke Yan had his back turned, so Jiang Qi couldn't see it. At Ke Yan's suggestion, this time he didn't refuse, and undid his shirt buttons.

Just watching Jiang Qi do that sent heat through Ke Yan; it had been many days since they'd done anything.

Jiang Qi undid the buttons one by one, shed the shirt, stepped into the shower stall, hooked his arms around Ke Yan's neck, and offered up a kiss of his own accord.

Kissing him, Ke Yan undid Jiang Qi's belt, button, zip; one tug and the trousers loosened and dropped to the floor.

Twining his tongue with Jiang Qi's, he stroked at Jiang Qi's sensitive places.

Jiang Qi's body warmed too — it really had been a while, and he was so sensitive now; he slid a leg between Ke Yan's and found him already hard.

He gave a soft laugh and sucked at Ke Yan's Adam's apple.

Ke Yan shivered, then promptly pinched the two points on Jiang Qi's chest.

A tingling went through Jiang Qi — yes, too long without, every part of him sensitive.

"So sensitive." Ke Yan, who knew Jiang Qi's body inside out, felt it too.

"Because it's been days — mm — since we did it." With Ke Yan's hand working him below, Jiang Qi gripped Ke Yan's arm tight, his legs going weak from the pleasure.

"Then from now on, every day." Ke Yan smiled wickedly.

"That won't do — mm — there's still work."

"Then for now, just enjoy it."

Jiang Qi had come only to check Ke Yan for bruises, yet somehow ended up naked in his hands and dizzy with pleasure.

Ke Yan lifted him; Jiang Qi hooked his legs around Ke Yan's waist, and Ke Yan pressed him to the wall — his back touched the cold tile and he shuddered.

"I forgot, it's cold." Ke Yan pulled him off the wall at once.

"It's fine." Jiang Qi came to his senses a little.

Arms around Ke Yan's neck, he glanced at Ke Yan's back — and saw a bruise there, and his heart ached at once; he could see how hard his mother had struck.

"Shall we go back to the bed?" Ke Yan, afraid of the cold — the bathroom was warm, but Jiang Qi felt the cold, and a cold Jiang Qi wouldn't do.

"All right."

Ke Yan grabbed a robe to wrap Jiang Qi in, then carried him to the bed.

He pulled the towel away, kissing Jiang Qi's neck; Jiang Qi tipped his head back, legs hooked around Ke Yan's waist, and panted, "Come in."

Ke Yan nearly couldn't hold back from going straight in, but still gently prepared Jiang Qi. Jiang Qi knew Ke Yan's nature and didn't rush him — though he noticed Ke Yan prepared him far faster than usual…

Afterward, Jiang Qi lay panting in Ke Yan's arms. Ke Yan stroked his back, then went to run the bath, brought Jiang Qi to the bathroom and cleaned him up, set him to soak, and went to change the sheets.

Once everything was tidy, Jiang Qi touched the bruise on Ke Yan's back. He did not ask whether Ke Yan had visited his mother; he knew him too well. They had agreed to face this together, but Ke Yan could not bear to involve him. Jiang Qi made his own decision: tomorrow he would go home. Likewise, seeing Ke Yan hurt broke his heart.
