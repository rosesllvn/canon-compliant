The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 165

Home for the New Year 回家过年

July 2026

~5 min read

With the New Year nearly here, the company was always busy before the holiday; even Ke Yan had to work late.

Today Ke Yan was working late, so Jiang Qi closed the shop early as usual — bitter cold, let the staff get home early.

Locked up, he went to the company to find Ke Yan.

Usually the office clamored at quitting time, but tonight everyone remained quietly at their desks.

Jiang Qi took the elevator to the 39th. The secretaries were all at their screens; no sign of Yang Shaoyu. He didn't disturb them, going straight into Ke Yan's office.

Inside, Ke Yan was working with a knit brow.

He didn't even notice Jiang Qi come in.

Jiang Qi walked to the desk, leaned over, and smoothed the furrow between Ke Yan's brows.

Ke Yan startled, looked up to see Jiang Qi, and his brow eased. "When did you get here?"

"Just now. Why the frown?"

"Nothing — frowned without meaning to…"

"Oh — where's Shaoyu? I didn't see him."

"His little brother's back; his family's having dinner together today."

"Oh — work, then; I'll sit here and wait."

Ke Yan returned to work. Jiang Qi read on the sofa, gradually stretched out, and eventually let the book slip to the carpet as he fell asleep.

Time slipped by; when Ke Yan finally looked up it was eight.

Seeing Jiang Qi asleep on the sofa, he crouched before him, touched his face, then quickly tidied his papers, locking the important ones away.

He laid his suit jacket over Jiang Qi and gently lifted him.

Jiang Qi slept so soundly he didn't even wake.

Outside, the secretaries checked the time — past nine; why hadn't the CEO left? With him still in, they couldn't leave either.

Just as they thought it, the office door opened, and they saw the CEO come out carrying someone.

They froze as one. Was that Manager Jiang? They hadn't seen him come in.

The unmarried secretaries felt their hearts ache. They were dreading their parents' questions about marriage, while Manager Jiang had somehow won a peerless man—cold to everyone else, but tender and doting with his lover.

Though Jiang Qi was no longer a manager, everyone at the company still called him Manager Jiang.

"Go home, you've worked hard," Ke Yan said.

"Yes." The secretaries nodded.

Carrying Jiang Qi to the lot, the guard, seeing the CEO carry someone, quickly ran to open the car door once Ke Yan unlocked it.

Ke Yan settled Jiang Qi gently into the seat; Jiang Qi woke with a start, opened his eyes.

"Sleep a bit more — I'll wake you at home."

He closed his eyes again.

Home, Ke Yan woke him, and they ate the dinner that had been prepared.

The next days Ke Yan was very busy, but soon got through this stretch, handing out thick bonuses to the staff.

Jiang Qi let his shop staff finish their trial period early, all made permanent, gave them red packets, and started the holiday.

Then came the New Year break.

Jiang Qi and Ke Yan parted to go to their own families on the 29th.

Maybe it was his imagination, but Jiang Qi felt Mother Jiang seemed especially glad to see him home this New Year.

The moment he arrived she set down her work and fried him a plate of nian gao.

Jiang Qi loved Mother Jiang's fried nian gao.

She beat eggs, sliced the cooled nian gao into even strips, dipped each one in egg, and laid them in the hot oil.

When they came out, Jiang Qi, heedless of the heat, popped a piece in his mouth — that crisp, eggy fragrance, the soft, glutinous, faintly sticky nian gao — utterly delicious.

He polished off the whole plate before remembering to text Ke Yan that he was home.

Ke Yan got the text just as he pulled up at his own home.

Stepping in, he saw his parents chatting on the sofa.

Lan Ruyin saw him too, and looked past him.

"Mom, what are you looking at?" Ke Yan asked knowingly, seeing her peer behind him.

"Nothing. But where's Jiang Qi? He didn't come back with you?" She tried to sound indifferent, though she had expected Jiang Qi to accompany him.

"Jiang Qi went home for the New Year, of course. Didn't you used to not want to see Jiang Qi, Mom?" Ke Yan sat down and said it deliberately.

"That was before — now I don't mind seeing Jiang Qi." She didn't catch the tease, even growing a little anxious — surely Jiang Qi wasn't afraid of her?

Ke Xin couldn't watch it. He kindly tipped off his wife, "Fooled by your son and you still haven't caught on?"

At that, Lan Ruyin finally got it.

"Jiang Qi went home for the New Year; he'll come pay you a visit on the first day." Ke Yan came clean, seeing his mother about to get cross.

"Hmph, fine." She looked utterly unbothered.

At her contradictory front, Ke Xin couldn't help a laugh.

Ke Yan thought of something and couldn't help smiling too. "Dad, Mom — keep the second day of the New Year free."

"Why — something on?"

"That day, come to mine and Jiang Qi's wedding."

"What?" Ke Xin and Lan Ruyin said it together, equally astonished — Ke Yan hadn't breathed a word; they'd thought the wedding would wait until spring.

"Isn't that too rushed? You haven't even sent invitations." Lan Ruyin feared Ke Yan, inexperienced, didn't grasp the details.

"No invitations needed. Jiang Qi and I discussed it — that day we'll just have some of our friends and family."

"That's fine — anyway, if you need help, come to me and your mother." Ke Xin trusted Ke Yan's competence.

"All right." Ke Yan nodded. Everything was ready now but for telling Jiang Qi's parents; he'd even called Mo Lü and Ma Tong to help that day. Jiang Qi knew none of it — but he wanted to surprise him, loved seeing him happy. Thinking of Jiang Qi, Ke Yan's whole face softened. A few more days, and he and Jiang Qi would hold their wedding.
