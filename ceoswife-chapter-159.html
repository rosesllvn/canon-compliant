Chapter 159

Did He Make a Poo? 难道拉臭臭了？

July 2026

~5 min read

Secretary Wu followed Jiang Qi to the break room. As a company employee, she of course knew Jiang Qi — and knew, too, of his relationship with Ke Yan, though she'd never thought much about whether to accept it or not; as long as Jiang Qi wasn't difficult to work with, that was enough for her.

In the break room, Secretary Wu found that the kettle they usually used to boil water for noodles would do to boil water for the formula.

Jiang Qi held Xiao Ke, watching her work; minding the baby now, he figured he could manage mixing formula himself later when Xiao Ke wanted his milk.

Ke Yan started the kettle again.

Then, while it heated, Jiang Qi felt the office had gone a little quiet, and chatted with Secretary Wu. "Secretary Wu, how old is your child?"

"One's already in school, the other still in preschool — both boys." Secretary Wu smiled.

"Then you must have wanted a girl. When my mother had me, after my older brother, she wanted a girl too."

"Yes, I really did want a girl."

"By the way, this child is so young. What can he eat besides formula?" Jiang Qi asked.

"Soft noodles or a little congee. You can cook it in chicken broth, but don't make it oily or salty, and don't add MSG or seafood." A mother of two, Secretary Wu soon warmed to the subject. Seeing Jiang Qi listen so earnestly improved her opinion of him as well.

Just then the formula was ready; Secretary Wu, practiced, scooped it out, poured off the water she'd just boiled, and explained, "Don't use boiling water for the formula — let it cool to around 40 to 60 degrees."

So saying, she poured the cooled water into the bottle, added two scoops of formula, capped it, and shook.

At last she tested a drop on her wrist, then handed the bottle to Jiang Qi for him to feed Xiao Ke.

Jiang Qi held the bottle to Xiao Ke's lips; Xiao Ke opened his mouth at once and latched on, gulping several mouthfuls in a row, then gripping the bottle with his own little hands as he sucked.

"Thank you." Jiang Qi smiled his thanks to Secretary Wu, then went back with Xiao Ke.

Back outside the office, Jiang Qi took the formula tin and sat beside Yang Shaoyu's desk rather than interrupt the meeting.

Yang Shaoyu glanced at Xiao Ke and gently squeezed the baby's hand. "Children look exhausting."

"Not really. It's only hard when he cries and you don't know why."

"You and Ke Yan are planning to adopt?"

"Mm."

"Zirui and I will wait a few more years. I still want more time with just the two of us."

"That makes sense. Zirui's work keeps him busy too."

Yang Shaoyu handed the tin back. "You'd better go in. If you wait much longer, Ke Yan will come looking for you."

Jiang Qi entered with Xiao Ke in one arm and the formula in the other. The meeting was still underway, so he greeted the managers apologetically.

Ke Yan rose at once, closed the door behind him, and took the tin. "I'll rest in the lounge for a while," Jiang Qi said.

"All right." Ke Yan accompanied him inside.

Left alone, the managers exchanged looks. One finally asked whose baby Jiang Qi was holding, but no one dared speculate too loudly with the CEO in the next room.

In the lounge, Jiang Qi urged Ke Yan to return to his meeting. "I know. We'll leave early when it's over."

"All right." Once Ke Yan left, Jiang Qi looked down. Xiao Ke's small hands clutched the bottle as he drank, his cheeks puffing with every swallow. Jiang Qi's heart nearly melted. He kissed the baby's forehead, then nuzzled the milk-scented warmth of his neck.

Ke Yan resumed the meeting, but everyone understood his unspoken desire to finish quickly. Even so, it lasted another half hour.

The instant it ended, Ke Yan returned to the lounge. Xiao Ke was crawling over the bed while Jiang Qi crouched at the edge, tempting him closer with the remote control. The baby crawled halfway, abruptly flopped onto his back, and stared peacefully at the ceiling.

Jiang Qi sighed in disappointment and switched on a cartoon. Behind him, Ke Yan suppressed a laugh, crept closer, and suddenly lifted him onto the bed.

"Meeting over?" Jiang Qi asked after the surprise passed.

"Mm. Playing with Xiao Ke?" Ke Yan kissed Jiang Qi's forehead, then his lips and throat.

"Stop. Xiao Ke's right beside us."

"I know. Otherwise I'd already have your clothes off."

"Don't say that—it's embarrassing. Get up."

"One more minute." Ke Yan tugged down the collar of Jiang Qi's sweater and kissed the pale skin over his collarbone, one hand slipping beneath the hem.

Jiang Qi turned his head and found Xiao Ke staring at him, unblinking. He went rigid under that solemn little gaze.

Ke Yan remembered their audience and, after two final kisses, released him and straightened his clothes.

Jiang Qi noticed an odd smell and sniffed. "Ke Yan, do you smell something?"

He sniffed again, leaning toward Xiao Ke — and the smell was stronger.

Ke Yan caught it too, and his face slowly darkened, looking at the cheerfully bottom-shuffling Xiao Ke beside them.

Jiang Qi blinked, then looked at Xiao Ke as well. Did he — did the baby make a poo?
