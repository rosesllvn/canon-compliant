The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 139

A Conversation 谈话

July 2026

~5 min read

Monday, a workday again. As on any Monday, Ke Group's staff arrived by car, bus, or taxi, the entrance crowded with employees.

Some bought sandwiches at the coffee shop across the street for breakfast — but today a few noticed that the shop being renovated next to the café was finished, and very beautifully done.

The signboard startled Ke Group's staff into an uproar. This was Manager Jiang's shop? At local property prices, whether rented or bought, it could not have been cheap.

What kind of shop was it, anyway? The name was so unusual.

Several employees clustered outside, talking.

"Doesn't look open yet."

"No — but the fit-out's exquisite."

"Did the CEO give it to Manager Jiang?"

"What kind of shop, though? Looks like a bookshop."…

Riding in with Ke Yan, Jiang Qi also saw the crowd outside his shop, and a thought struck him. "Ke Yan, if I open the bookshop, who takes my job at the company?"

"Look at your people — see who's up to it."

"I find myself reluctant, suddenly — reluctant to leave the company." He'd worked there several years; he complained of being tired, but now felt loath to go.

"The bookshop's right across from the company. When you miss it, just come over. And even with the shop, you'll likely be at the company often."

"You're so sure?"

"Of course. I'll be here; when I'm not busy, come see me. And come for lunch at noon."

"True enough."

The car pulled into the lot. Jiang Qi and Ke Yan got out, and the people in the garage stole glances at the two of them — while a few who didn't know felt a sudden odd air about it.

Wang Siqi hadn't looked across the street on her way in, so she didn't know — but heard others discussing it, and ran to a window to look. Too high to read the sign, but she could see the shop was finished. So it really was a gift from Ke Yan to Jiang Qi?

Much as she'd wanted to win Ke Yan, he truly seemed to love Jiang Qi so. How could she compete?

But to give up now galled her.

Sun Nianqing knew too, of course — but unlike Wang Siqi, her thinking was: whatever she wanted, she'd have.

All day, Sun Nianqing watched for a chance to catch Ke Yan alone. Work hours were impossible, and he spent lunch with Jiang Qi. Frustration nearly made her spit blood.

But if she couldn't reach Ke Yan, she could go to Jiang Qi.

She knew Jiang Qi went to the break room most afternoons for a snack, so she went there to find him.

The company laid out bread and pastries, and Jiang Qi always ate earlier than others, getting hungry early.

When Sun Nianqing reached the break room on Jiang Qi's floor, he was indeed there.

"Manager Jiang." She called to him.

"Miss Sun? Is something the matter?"

"There is something I want with you." She sat across the little round table from him.

"What is it?" Jiang Qi paused his eating.

"Please leave Ke Yan." Sun Nianqing said it outright.

"What?" Jiang Qi thought he'd misheard.

"You're not suited to be at Ke Yan's side, and I like Ke Yan."

"Miss Sun, I understand why you'd say this to me — but whether I'm suited isn't for you to decide. Only Ke Yan has that right." Jiang Qi frowned.

"Don't you see? You're a man. At Ke Yan's side, you harm rather than help him. What will the world, his family, and the business press think? Under that pressure, he'll only leave you. Better to end it now and spare the trouble." Sun Nianqing grew heated as she spoke.

"If Ke Yan were that kind of man, I wouldn't love him so much." Once she'd cooled a little, Jiang Qi spoke. "That you'd say this shows you don't understand Ke Yan. He isn't that kind of person. Even if everyone else were, Ke Yan wouldn't be."

"You're that confident?" Sun Nianqing sneered.

"It's not confidence. It's that I trust Ke Yan."

"I'm confident that with my looks and talent, Ke Yan will fall for me and leave you." She said it with great assurance.

Jiang Qi was quiet a moment. "My man isn't that shallow." He hadn't wanted to put it that way, but Sun Nianqing left him no choice.

"What?" The "my man" had so thrown Sun Nianqing that she forgot what she'd meant to say next.

"Look." Jiang Qi showed her the hand with the ring.

"A — a ring?" Sun Nianqing saw it. This was — Ke Yan had bought it? She'd seen him buy a ring before. JQ — wasn't that Jiang Qi?

Now Sun Nianqing wasn't merely surprised — she was stunned.

"There are plenty of good men out there." Jiang Qi couldn't bear to see her like that, and said it.

"It's none of your business." She glared. "Ke Yan's parents will never agree to you two."

"Actually, they've agreed. Ke Yan has already taken me to meet them. But there are plenty of good men out there." Somehow Jiang Qi had ended up consoling her. He was one step from offering to arrange a date.

"Could any good man be as handsome as Ke Yan? Give me the position of CEO's wife? Get out of my way, it's none of your business." She stood and left.

Jiang Qi quietly finished the rest of his bread, then went back to his office to work.

A man being too good-looking really wasn't a good thing.
