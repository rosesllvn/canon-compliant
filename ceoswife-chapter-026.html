The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 26

Blushing 脸红

July 2026

~4 min read

Ke Yan came back to school, and Jiang Qi's ordinary life — and his energy — returned. He wasn't the only one revived: the girls were all bursting with vigor, as though they'd swallowed elixirs.

The days passed uneventfully. Jiang Qi went on reading with Ke Yan, eating with him, catching sight of him now and then on campus, listening to the girls debate who was the more handsome. But one thing was a little different now: lately a certain person kept appearing at Ke Yan's side — Yang Shaoyu.

That fellow who threw flirtatious glances at every girl he saw, swapping girlfriends one after another, the one Liu Hao envied and resented and seethed over — a thorough philanderer. Handsome men really do have capital to spend, even Jiang Qi couldn't help sighing.

Toward evening, Jiang Qi was packing up to leave the classroom when Liu Hao blocked his way. "Jiang Qi, don't even think about leaving today."

"Why?"

Liu Hao wore the same expression Meng Qing got when he was after something — sleazy, with a touch of the rogue. One look and Jiang Qi knew it boded ill. Sure enough, Liu Hao grinned a while and said, "Let's go to a group dinner today."

"A group dinner? I'm not going." Jiang Qi knew "group dinner" meant a mixer. He turned for the other exit — and Liu Hao let him, because Meng Qing was waiting there for him to walk straight into the net.

Both exits blocked, Jiang Qi was working out what to do when his eyes lit up and he called toward the space behind Meng Qing, "Meng Qing's cousin — what are you doing here?"

Meng Qing whipped around — no cousin anywhere; Jiang Qi was bluffing. By the time he realized he'd been had, Jiang Qi had shoved him aside and bolted in a streak.

Watching him race off, Meng Qing and Liu Hao ground their teeth; they hadn't expected the kid to be such a fast runner.

Jiang Qi pelted, panting, to the library, and wiped the sweat from his brow with the back of his hand. "Phew, so hot~~"

"Phew, so hot." Just then a boy came running out of the library, hands braced on his knees, somewhat bedraggled, and wiped his brow with a gesture that looked careless but was in fact dashing, elegant, and a touch theatrical. Jiang Qi took in the gesture, the handsome face, and placed him — Yang Shaoyu. The one who'd been turning up at Ke Yan's side so often lately.

The two came face to face. Yang Shaoyu looked at Jiang Qi two seconds, then shifted his gaze, glancing around as though for someone. Before long he waved toward the distance. "Ke Yan, over here."

The shout stopped Jiang Qi mid-stride, and he looked over.

Ke Yan came ambling up, and passed in front of Yang Shaoyu as though he weren't there.

Yang Shaoyu didn't take offense; he fell in beside him with easy familiarity, grinning. "How can you ignore me like that?"

"Don't smile so hideously." Ke Yan shot him a sidelong glance.

Hideous? Yang Shaoyu touched the corner of his mouth and decided to let it go.

Jiang Qi stood where he was, watching Ke Yan draw nearer and nearer, until he stood before him, and dazedly raised a hand in greeting.

"Come on," Ke Yan said.

Yang Shaoyu narrowed his phoenix eyes. Since when did Ke Yan get so courteous?

"Oh." Jiang Qi fell in behind him.

Yang Shaoyu didn't follow. He took out his phone and dialed. It rang a while before a woman's voice came through: "Xiao Yangzi, what is it?"

"Don't call me Xiao Yangzi." He thought the name made him sound like a eunuch — dreadful. "Anyway — remember that guy you saw the last time you came to the school?"

The voice was intrigued. "Of course. Who is he?"

"As if I'd be fool enough to tell you."

"Then why call?"

"Give me what I want, and I'll tell you."

"Heh heh~~" An eerie laugh came down the line, and Yang Shaoyu's skin prickled into gooseflesh on the spot. "Xiao Yangzi, you're still a bit green. I'll find out on my own." And the line went dead.

Yang Shaoyu glared at the dead phone, then dialed another number.

In the library, Jiang Qi wasn't reading; he sat chin in hand, looking at Ke Yan. "Something on your mind?" Ke Yan asked. He'd learned that when Jiang Qi looked like this, he had a question, and until he got his answer he wouldn't be able to read.

"Is Yang Shaoyu your friend?"

"Mm."

"Doesn't seem like it — and yet it seems perfectly natural too." Jiang Qi said the baffling little thing, and Ke Yan, hearing it, tugged the corner of his mouth into a smile. Jiang Qi's eyes went wide. He's so good-looking when he smiles — so good-looking that his heart pounded fiercely, his face going a little hot.

He quickly raised his book to hide the flush. "What's wrong?" Ke Yan asked.

"N-nothing." Even stammering.

Watching him, Ke Yan seemed to understand something; his eyes flickered, and he lowered his head to read, betraying nothing.
