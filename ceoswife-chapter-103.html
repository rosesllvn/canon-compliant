The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 103

Out of Sorts 郁闷

July 2026

~4 min read

Lin's second brother brought Lin Zirui home. The eldest brother wasn't back yet, which left Lin's second brother fretting over Lin Zirui with no one to talk it over with.

Lin's second brother could be a hothead, but he was no fool. Lin Zirui's expression suggested something he had missed at first—something absurd. Surely Xiao Rui liked girls.

He looked at Lin Zirui on the sofa and hesitated. "Xiao Rui…"

"What is it?"

"Oh — have some fruit, it's good for you." In the end Lin's second brother lost his nerve and didn't dare ask, afraid of embarrassing Xiao Rui.

"Oh — you have some too, Er-ge." Lin Zirui speared a piece of the fruit his brother liked and offered it over.

"Right, right." Lin's second brother took it with a smile.

"I'm home." They were still talking when Lin Le came in laden with shopping bags. She shot Lin Zirui a glare, then said, "Er-ge."

"Mm, sit down and rest. Been shopping again?" Lin Zirui was his younger brother, but Lin Le was also his little sister. He would not ignore her for Lin Zirui's sake, nor rebuke her unless her treatment of him went too far. He still hoped they might learn to get along.

"Mm, went with friends." Lin Le took a sip of juice.

"If you're short on money just tell Er-ge — but don't be out shopping all the time, either."

"I know. San-ge, what are you thinking about?" Lin Le had noticed Lin Zirui staring off into space, taking in nothing of her conversation with their brother.

"Oh — nothing. You two talk, I'll go up." Lin Zirui rose and headed upstairs, his mind entirely on whether Yang Shaoyu had been upset just now.

Lin Le watched him leave with open dislike. Because of this third brother, her eldest and second brothers could not lavish all their care on her or make her the center of everything. She hated him for claiming any share of their attention. Her one consolation was that their parents cared nothing for Lin Zirui.

Oblivious to his little sister's mind, Lin Zirui got back to his room and rang Yang Shaoyu. The phone hadn't rung twice before it was snatched up.

"Home yet?" Yang Shaoyu asked before Lin Zirui could even speak.

"Mm, I'm home. Still driving?"

"Mm, but nearly there."

"I'll call back in a bit, then. Drive safely." And he hung up.

Yang Shaoyu smiled and accelerated, eager to get home and call back. Then he remembered Lin Zirui's warning to drive carefully and eased off again.

The moment he got home, before he'd even sat down, he called Lin Zirui. "Hey — I'm home."

"Oh — what about dinner? You haven't eaten yet, have you?"

"No. Tomorrow we're definitely eating together." The thought of the date that hadn't happened left him out of sorts.

"All right." Lin Zirui laughed softly.

They talked a few minutes more, and only then, reluctant on both sides, hung up.

Ke Yan's first day back was swallowed by meetings, along with the day of every other senior manager shut in the conference room.

After a whole morning of it, Ke Yan finally got a moment's rest at midday and lay back, worn out, on the office sofa. Jiang Qi sat resting on the other sofa — he'd had a far easier time of it than Ke Yan.

"A bit tired. But it's about all sorted now."

"Mm. Let's go eat together in a minute." Ke Yan sat up and gestured Jiang Qi over; Jiang Qi came obediently and settled beside him, and Ke Yan slung an arm around his shoulders, the two of them leaning back against the cushions together.

"Tired?" Ke Yan took Jiang Qi's hand in his other one.

"Not as tired as you." Jiang Qi gave his hand a squeeze — and his stomach chose that moment to growl.

Ke Yan laughed at the sound, and looked over to find Jiang Qi's face gone a little pink. "Come on, let's get lunch, before my wife starves."

"Don't say that." Jiang Qi had no defenses whatsoever against the word wife; one mention and he colored.

"All right, all right. Let's go."

They went down to the company canteen. Thanks to Ke Yan's earlier instructions, there wasn't a single dish with cilantro in it anymore.

Jiang Qi insisted that Ke Yan sit while he fetched the food. Finding nothing particularly nourishing, he settled for the dishes Ke Yan liked.

Watching Ke Yan eat, Jiang Qi felt a small pang of dejection. Why couldn't he get any better at cooking? If only he could, he could make Ke Yan a good soup.

Ke Yan was a picky eater and flatly refused anything not to his taste. Yet no matter how badly Jiang Qi cooked, he ate every bite. Moved, Jiang Qi resolved to learn something Ke Yan liked that would restore his strength. He had been working far too hard.

Jiang Qi mulled it over in silence, but his face ran through a whole range of expressions as he did — and one look at that face and Ke Yan knew his wife was off scheming something again.
