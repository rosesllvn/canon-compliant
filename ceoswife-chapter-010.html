The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 10

Injured 受伤

July 2026

~2 min read

Jiang Qi carried the food to the table and looked at Ke Yan, his face full of expectation. "Ke Yan, come and eat."

Ke Yan looked at the plates of what resembled poison laid out before him, swallowed, sat down, and picked up his chopsticks. He hesitated — then, seeing that expectant face, took a piece of the red-braised ribs. One bite, and he spat it straight back out and downed most of the glass of water beside him.

"Is it that bad?" Jiang Qi asked, puzzled. His cooking was always terrible, true — but still. He tried a piece himself, and spat it out too. Sweet. And of all things Ke Yan loathed sweet food most. But how had it come out sweet?

He went to the kitchen and picked up the salt jar — sugar. And the sugar jar held salt.

He thought back: just after getting home, wrapped up in working through the steps, he'd noticed the seasonings running low and topped them up from the cupboard — and must have swapped them then.

Though even without the mix-up, the cooking still wouldn't have been any good. The hard-won lesson of his mother's teaching came down to this: Jiang Qi was a complete kitchen idiot — whatever you do, don't eat anything he makes.

Ke Yan came in and saw the crestfallen set of him. He crossed over, took the jar from his hand and set it down, then wrapped his arms gently around him from behind, chin on his shoulder. "It's all right. Let's go out to eat."

"But..."

Ke Yan rubbed his stomach. "Aren't you hungry? Your belly's gone flat."

Jiang Qi realized he was hungry — and Ke Yan must be too. "All right, then."

He turned on the tap to wash his hands; turning it off, he carelessly brushed the fruit knife beside him and it cut his hand. The pain drew a hiss from him. "Sss~~"

Ke Yan seized the hand at once, pulled two tissues from the box nearby, stanched the blood — and then swept Jiang Qi up sideways into his arms.

Jiang Qi gave a startled cry and was carried to the sofa and set down. Ke Yan fetched the first-aid kit from a side cabinet, disinfected the cut, and put a bandage over it.

"Can't you be a little more careful?" he sighed. He turned to put the kit away — and Jiang Qi caught him, leaned in, kissed the corner of his mouth, and murmured, "I know. I'll be careful from now on." He meant to sit back up — but Ke Yan caught the back of his head, pressed into his mouth, and bore him down onto the sofa, one hand slipping in under his clothes—

"Mm..." A helpless sound escaped Jiang Qi, and earned a fiercer assault.

Then his stomach growled. Reluctantly Ke Yan drew back, looked at the burning red of his face, and laughed.

Jiang Qi's face went redder still.
