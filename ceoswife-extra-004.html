Though the shop was crowded, their order came fast.

Ye Xingwei sipped his unsweetened soy milk and watched Jiang Bai add spoonful after spoonful of sugar to both his soy milk and tofu pudding.

But Jiang Bai liked sweet, and seeing him add none, asked, "Don't you find soy milk no good without sugar?"

"No."

Jiang Bai sipped his — sweet enough — and put the spoon back in the sugar jar.

He poured vinegar into a dish, dipped a steamed dumpling, and bit into shrimp, mushroom, finely minced pork, and cabbage brightened by the vinegar's tang.

Jiang Bai narrowed his eyes in pleasure, swallowed, and reached for another.

Watching that "so good" look on Jiang Bai's face, Ye Xingwei found he wanted a taste too, and reached over to take one from Jiang Bai's plate, dipped it in vinegar, ate it.

Jiang Bai smiled, eyes crinkling. "Good, isn't it?"

"Mm." Though Ye Xingwei didn't find it as good as it had looked, eaten by Jiang Bai.

Anyone who ate with Jiang Bai discovered the same thing: however little appetite you began with, watching his enjoyment made you join in and eat more than usual.

Before they knew it the breakfast was gone, Ye Xingwei having eaten a third, Jiang Bai the rest.

Ye Xingwei was genuinely amazed at Jiang Bai's appetite.

After, they went to the company; Ye Xingwei got out at the entrance, Jiang Bai took the car to the lot.

Ye Xingwei had just heard Secretary Jin's report on the day's work when Jiang Bai knocked.

He looked up; Jiang Bai came in with a damp towel. "Didn't you dirty your trousers? Wipe them with a wet towel first."

But for Jiang Bai, Ye Xingwei had nearly forgotten; he stood, took the towel, and wiped the cuff.

Done, he handed it back with a thanks.

Jiang Bai took it, smiled, and went out.

Ye Xingwei worked on, finding his mood unexpectedly good today — perhaps because of that decent breakfast.

The workday seemed to fly; at lunch, his assistant had ordered a refined bento from a hotel.

Ye Xingwei took a few bites of the elegant dishes, then remembered breakfast with Jiang Bai and abruptly lost his appetite for eating alone. He set down his chopsticks.

Should he ask Jiang Bai to eat together next time?

As he thought it his phone rang; he answered, and his mother's voice came.

"Xingwei, Mom's at the hot-spring villa — come over tonight."

Hearing she was at the villa, Ye Xingwei knew at once she'd quarreled with his father again.

His father, like his grandfather, was rigid and hot-tempered. His parents often fought, and his mother would retreat to the villa until his father came to fetch her.

Ye Xingwei thought it over and decided to go tonight; he'd been swamped lately and hadn't seen his mother in a while.

At quitting time he took his keys, meaning to drive himself.

In the lot he found Jiang Bai by the car, seeming to wait for him.

He walked up. "No need to drive me tonight — I'll drive myself."

Jiang Bai felt a pang of regret but could only nod. "All right, then."

Ye Xingwei drove off; Jiang Bai sighed, pocketed his keys, and prepared to go home.

Halfway there, it began to rain on Ye Xingwei, thunder rumbling now and then.

The rain came harder; Ye Xingwei frowned and slowed.

The car ahead suddenly braked hard. Even at his reduced speed, Ye Xingwei couldn't stop in time and struck its rear. A moment later, the car behind rammed into him.

By the time Ye Xingwei's secretary got the call, he'd already been put in an ambulance.

He'd been conscious when they loaded him, and had the paramedics notify Secretary Jin.

Secretary Jin, getting the nurse's call, was alarmed, asked the hospital address, and rushed over.

Jiang Bai only learned of the crash the next day at the company, from Secretary Jin.

Jin had spent the whole night at the hospital and come straight to the office at dawn, looking exhausted.

Seeing his fatigue, Jiang Bai asked with concern, "Secretary Jin, what's wrong?"

"Jiang Bai, you've come at the right time — I need to ask a favor. The CEO had a car accident, but thank goodness his injuries aren't serious; the hospital can't get a caregiver right now — could you go today and look after the CEO?" Jin, fretting over the unfound caregiver, with himself needing to be at the office, saw Jiang Bai like a savior.

At "car accident," Jiang Bai's heart clenched, his hands fisted, and cold sweat broke over him. Only the words "not serious" let him breathe again.

He couldn't get to Ye Xingwei fast enough; he agreed at once, asked the address, and drove straight to the hospital.

Both Ye Xingwei's arms were hurt, wrapped in bandages and casts, and one leg too, casted and resting outside the quilt.

Ye Xingwei was asleep, lying still on the bed, his once-fair face pale now.

Jiang Bai went to the bedside and lightly touched Ye Xingwei's face, afraid to wake him.

Feeling Ye Xingwei's breath on his hand, the heart he'd held tight finally eased.

He'd been terrified. But Ye Xingwei was alive and would recover; that was enough.

Unable to help it, he stroked Ye Xingwei's face twice — and suddenly Ye Xingwei opened his eyes.

Jiang Bai startled so badly he nearly jumped, his hand tensing — and with a smack, landed a slap on Ye Xingwei's face.

Ye Xingwei froze, plainly not expecting to wake to a slap; light as it was, it stung.

He fixed a cold eye on the slap's owner, waiting to hear him explain.

Jiang Bai looked at him, apologetic. "Sor — sorry, I didn't mean it, are you all right?"

Seeing his fluster, Ye Xingwei teased, "What were you doing just now?"

"I, I…" Jiang Bai faltered, unable to say he'd been stroking his face.

Seeing him flush with anxiety, Ye Xingwei let it go. "Pour me some water."

"All right." Jiang Bai breathed out, turned to pour it.

Since Ye Xingwei couldn't easily drink lying down, Jiang Bai found a straw and put it in the cup for him.

Ye Xingwei drained it in a few sips.

"More?" Jiang Bai saw how thirsty he was.

"No."

Jiang Bai set the cup down, sat in the bedside chair, and after a silence, "Does — does the wound still hurt?"

"Not anymore…" The anesthetic had worn off, and of course it hurt. Yet the worry on Jiang Bai's face compelled him to lie.

Ye Xingwei didn't like worrying those close to him, which was why he hadn't even told his family — telling his mother only that something had come up and he couldn't make it.

But now, seeing Jiang Bai worry, he didn't want him to worry too much.

"Good — can you eat fruit now? Want an apple?"

"Peel one." It had been long enough since the procedure to eat.

Jiang Bai found a small knife, sat, and slowly peeled an apple, the sunlight through the window falling just on his peeling hands, making them look paler, more slender.

The apple peeled fast; with both of Ye Xingwei's hands hurt, Jiang Bai cut it into small pieces on a plate for ease.

No toothpicks, so Jiang Bai washed his hands clean and fed Ye Xingwei a piece by hand.

Maybe he'd cut the pieces too small; each time he fed one, Ye Xingwei's lips brushed Jiang Bai's fingers.

Each brush of Ye Xingwei's warm lips and breath against Jiang Bai's fingers deepened his blush.

Ye Xingwei finished the last piece, looked up at Jiang Bai, and was duly startled.

"Are you all right? Why's your face so red?" As red as the apple he'd just eaten.

"Is, is it? Haha, it's nothing, maybe it's too warm, haha." Jiang Bai covered his face with a dry laugh, unable to say it was all because of you.

Ye Xingwei didn't find it that warm, but didn't press; he just had Jiang Bai turn on the TV.

Jiang Bai found the remote. "What do you want to watch?"

Ye Xingwei hadn't watched TV in ages; apart from at the office, at home he sometimes read or looked at his phone.

"Anything."

Jiang Bai flipped channels, landing on a variety show where the guests were playing a water-bucket relay. Ye Xingwei watched the water sloshing out and the splashing sounds…

And, having just drunk water, Ye Xingwei suddenly needed the toilet…

"Jiang Bai."

"Mm?" Jiang Bai looked at him.

"I need the toilet." Ye Xingwei wasn't embarrassed — both men, after all.

"Toilet? But you can't easily get out of bed — what do we do?" Jiang Bai glanced at his injured leg.

"…There's a urinal." Ye Xingwei pointed it out.

"A—a urinal?" Jiang Bai looked at Ye Xingwei's lap. With both arms injured, he would need help. Jiang Bai wasn't ready for this. Wasn't it far too soon?

"What's wrong? Hurry up." Ye Xingwei saw him not reacting.

"Isn't — isn't this too fast?" Jiang Bai blushed, suddenly bashful.

Ye Xingwei stared at him. What did Jiang Bai mean by calling an urgent bodily need "too fast"?

Ye Xingwei looked at Jiang Bai, deadpan.

The still-blushing Jiang Bai saw Ye Xingwei's expression and knew he was near losing patience.

He drew a deep breath, took the urinal from under the bed, lifted Ye Xingwei's quilt, fixed his gaze on Ye Xingwei's lap, drew another deep breath, looking nervous beyond belief.

Watching Jiang Bai's nerves, as if facing something unspeakable, Ye Xingwei felt a throb at his temple.

Why is he this nervous? We have the same anatomy. Must he look so grimly resolved? Is he disgusted?

At the thought, Ye Xingwei suddenly didn't want Jiang Bai's help with his urgent need.

But Jiang Bai wasn't disgusted — he was genuinely nervous, hand trembling as he reached for Ye Xingwei's waistband.

The hospital trousers had a very loose waist; a light tug and it loosened.

Jiang Bai grew more nervous with every second. His hand shook so badly that he had to draw a third deep breath.

Ye Xingwei forced himself to ignore Jiang Bai's movements, because he truly needed to go, and Secretary Jin wouldn't come until afternoon, and no caregiver yet.

Jiang Bai gently drew down Ye Xingwei's trousers and saw it at once. Since the surgery had required it, Ye Xingwei's underwear had long been off, only the trousers on.

Jiang Bai felt his brain about to crash, his face impossibly hot.

With a trembling hand he took hold, and from nerves gripped a little too hard.

Ye Xingwei felt the pain at once. "Gentler."

"Yes, yes." Jiang Bai eased his grip and aimed at the urinal.

Thirty seconds passed…

A minute passed…

Still nothing came.

Having only just had the catheter out, the first time was hard.

Jiang Bai stared fixedly at what he held. It was much larger than his own.

Another dozen seconds, and Ye Xingwei, still unable to go, seeing Jiang Bai's gaze locked on his lower body, couldn't help saying, "What are you looking at?" — the blushing, bashful Jiang Bai looking, just now, like some lecher.

"No — nothing." Jiang Bai quickly shifted his eyes.

Seeing Ye Xingwei truly couldn't go, Jiang Bai softly went "shhh—."

Ye Xingwei looked at him.

Jiang Bai stammered, "This — this might make it come easier; I've seen parents do this when little kids need to go." He said it without daring to meet Ye Xingwei's eyes.

Suddenly treated like a small child, Ye Xingwei gave up on speaking and let Jiang Bai continue his soft "shhh—shhh—" sounds.

And whether or not it was psychological, after a moment it really did come.

Blushing, Jiang Bai gave it a shake for Ye Xingwei, then, still red, pulled a tissue to wipe, and tucked it back into the trousers.

For his part, Ye Xingwei, seeing Jiang Bai so bashful, felt a flicker of awkwardness himself.

Done, Jiang Bai grabbed the urinal and dashed for the toilet.

Watching his retreating back, Ye Xingwei's awkwardness eased a little.

In the toilet, Jiang Bai looked at his own hands a while before washing them, his face still red.

He could hardly believe he had touched Ye Xingwei there. It was mortifying.

Hands washed, he found himself suddenly too shy to go out; he touched his burning face, drew a breath, and slowly opened the door a crack to peek.

Ye Xingwei saw the little motion and found it funny. "Not coming over?"

Jiang Bai came slowly.

Seeing him approach, the awkwardness that had just receded returned; Ye Xingwei cleared his throat. "We're both men… thanks for just now; if it's uncomfortable for you, I won't trouble you next time."

"Ah? No, no, not uncomfortable — I'm glad to help you."

The instant he said it, an indescribable tension settled between them. Their eyes met and darted apart; the awkwardness refused to lift.

Yet beneath it all, Jiang Bai was secretly delighted.
