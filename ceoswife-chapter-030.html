The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 30

Drunken Jiang Qi 喝醉酒的江奇

July 2026

~5 min read

Down in the lobby, Ke Yan towed the unsteadily weaving Jiang Qi toward the exit by the hand. Jiang Qi hiccupped on the wine and shook him off. "I'm not leaving, I want to sleep." And he made for the big long sofa in the center of the lobby, three steps sideways for every five forward.

Ke Yan moved to block his way. Squinting at the white shirt planted in front of him, Jiang Qi backed up two steps — then charged forward, every trace of drunken unsteadiness gone, ran to the sofa, kicked off his shoes, and flopped facedown onto it.

Ke Yan looked at him, set on a nap, and gritted his teeth. Quick about it, isn't he.

Finding facedown uncomfortable, Jiang Qi rolled onto his back.

Ke Yan sighed, stepped over, bent down, and pinched his cheek — hard.

The pain made Jiang Qi open his eyes, their rims gone red, and try resentfully to pull free. But the more he struggled, the harder Ke Yan pinched, until his cheek flushed scarlet — and only then let go.

Jiang Qi rubbed the sore cheek. Awake now. Thoroughly awake.

Ke Yan stood and looked down at him sitting up on the sofa. "Come on, let's go back."

Jiang Qi rubbed his face and said muzzily, "Don't want to go back."

"Goodbye, then." Ke Yan turned to leave — took two steps and was caught fast.

Jiang Qi gripped his hem hard, crushing the clean white shirt into wrinkles in an instant. "Don't go, I want water, I'm thirsty." He softened his voice into a plea.

What a nuisance. So Ke Yan thought — yet he turned all the same and asked a waitress, who'd been hovering nearby a while, for a glass of water. Blushing, she handed it over.

He set it in front of Jiang Qi, who pushed it away with both hands, shaking his head. "I — I want..." His head wobbled dizzier before he could finish; he rubbed it. "I want juice."

The hand holding the glass tightened. This Jiang Qi. Then Ke Yan paused: like this, Jiang Qi really was rather like a grade-schooler.

So he ordered a juice too, and after a while the waitress brought a glass of fresh-pressed watermelon juice. Jiang Qi took it and glug, glug, drained it in two or three swallows, then gave a satisfied little burp.

The two came out of the restaurant one ahead of the other — Ke Yan strolling at ease in front, Jiang Qi staggering after, weaving the whole way, unable to hold a line.

Ke Yan checked the time — nearly twelve; he hadn't realized it had gotten so late. The dorms had long since locked. He looked back at the still-weaving Jiang Qi and sighed again. Looks like I've brought trouble on myself.

As they walked, he noticed Jiang Qi had stopped following — rooted in place, eyes wide, staring at him. He walked back. "What is it?"

Jiang Qi pursed his lips, and the moment Ke Yan wasn't looking, pinched both his cheeks — gently, this time.

Ke Yan was briefly startled, then took both his hands and pried them away. Jiang Qi didn't resist, only looked at him without a word.

Ke Yan took his left hand and led him forward.

Looking at their clasped hands, feeling the warmth of Ke Yan's palm, Jiang Qi spoke. "Hey, Ke Yan."

"Mm?"

"You're really good-looking."

"...Is that so." Ke Yan, who disliked — even hated — being told he was good-looking, found the corner of his mouth curving up despite himself. It quickly drew back in.

Home again, Ke Yan breathed out. "If you want to sleep, the guest room's upstairs." And he left Jiang Qi there and went into his own room to shower and sleep.

Coming out of the bathroom, he found a lump risen under the covers of his bed, and had a bad feeling. He pulled the blanket back — Jiang Qi, fast asleep on it, reeking of wine. Just then Ke Yan wanted nothing so much as to smother him with the blanket.

He laid the blanket back, drawing it up to Jiang Qi's shoulder, careful not to bury his head. There was nothing for it; he walked out, shut the door, and resolved to take the guest room himself.

The next day, sunlight slanting in, Jiang Qi found the glare harsh and slowly opened his eyes. A black-and-white nightstand, a desk lamp. Hm? Where is this? He rolled over and sat up — the world went briefly dark, his head throbbing in waves. He rubbed his temples, and remembered he seemed to have drunk quite a lot last night.

He got up and went to the window, drew aside the gauzy white curtains, and found the view outside somehow familiar, though he couldn't place it.

He opened the door, and the house felt more familiar still. That's right — isn't this Ke Yan's home? Then where's Ke Yan?

He made a circuit downstairs and found no sign of him. Looking at the white staircase, he wondered whether it would be rude to go straight up. After a moment he went. Jiang Qi liked Ke Yan's staircase — white steps, black banister, the white steps bumpy and uneven block by block like chocolate, so that climbing them was like treading on chocolate.

Three rooms upstairs. One was a bathroom; instinctively avoiding the two eerie doors, he opened the guest room — and there Ke Yan was, asleep on the bed. Jiang Qi softened his steps, crept over, crouched at the bedside, and studied him. Asleep, Ke Yan didn't give off that unapproachable cold, so very— hm?

"Whoa—!" He hadn't expected Ke Yan's eyes to open all at once, and reared back in fright, landing on his backside on the floor. Before he could rise, Ke Yan sat up and fixed him with a look Jiang Qi couldn't read. He managed only an awkward "M-morning."

Ke Yan threw back the covers, got up, and at the doorway turned back to the Jiang Qi still on the floor. "Idiot."

Idiot? How rude — in what way am I an idiot? Jiang Qi thought, unconvinced.
