Chapter 116

Christmas Is Almost Here 快到了，圣诞节

July 2026

~4 min read

When Jiang Qi came down, Lan Ruyin was back, several bags of clothes in hand.

"You're up?" she asked.

"Mm. Uncle's awake — and hungry. Oh, and his fever's broken."

"Is that so?" Her face brightened for a moment, then she smiled, slow and meaningful, and beckoned him over.

"Auntie?" Jiang Qi went to her, puzzled.

"Go make him something to eat, and take it up to him when it's done."

"Ah? But the things I make are awful." He hadn't expected her to put him to it.

"It's fine." She waved him off—it wasn't as if she had to eat it. Let fussy Ke Xin taste Jiang Qi's cooking and see whether he remained so particular.

"But isn't Uncle ill? What if eating it makes him worse?"

"It won't. Go on, don't worry. I'm going up to rest." And with that she left, leaving Jiang Qi alone in the living room. After a moment's thought, he went to the kitchen to find Ke Yan.

"It's fine, go make it," Ke Yan said, same as his mother.

Jiang Qi thought it over. What should he make?

When Lan Ruyin got back to the room, Ke Xin was reading. She saw the book and said, "Weren't you against the two of them being together? What's changed your mind?"

"You know your son's temperament well enough. Once he's set his heart on someone, is there any changing it?"

"Just like you, that."

"Yes. Just like me." Ke Xin went on reading.

She smiled, picked up a manga, and leaned against the headboard to read alongside him.

Finding pre-cut chicken in the refrigerator, Jiang Qi settled on chicken congee.

Ke Yan helped, chopping spring onion at his side.

The congee was soon done, and Jiang Qi felt it had gone well today; it should taste good.

He carried it up to Ke Xin, who looked at the not-bad-looking congee, picked up the spoon, and tried a mouthful.

"Well?" Lan Ruyin asked.

"Why is it sweet?" This was the strangest congee Ke Xin had ever eaten — sweet with an undertone of salt.

"Er — I may have put in too much sugar."

"Wife, just get me something else to eat, anything." He'd sooner eat anyone's cooking than Jiang Qi's.

"All right." Lan Ruyin bit back a laugh. That'll teach you to be so fussy.

Jiang Qi sighed and decided to go find Ke Yan downstairs — only Ke Yan never minded his cooking.

Ke Yan was lying on the sofa watching television. Jiang Qi went over, draped himself across him, and buried his face in his chest.

"What's wrong?" Ke Yan stroked his head.

"You're still the best."

Ke Yan gave a soft laugh, taking his meaning.

"Are we going home tonight?" Ke Yan asked.

"Let's not. We'll stay the night." Jiang Qi thought the Ke house was rather nice, actually.

"All right."…

And so Jiang Qi and Ke Yan stayed the night and went home the next day. As they left, Ke Xin and Lan Ruyin told Jiang Qi to come again.

At that, Jiang Qi broke into a grin, his whole face lit with it. "I'll definitely come again. We'll be off now."

"Dad, Mom, we're leaving."

"Drive safely."

Jiang Qi remained happy on the drive home—until a thought sank his mood again.

Ke Yan felt the shift. "What is it? Weren't you cheerful just now?"

"It's my turn — my parents."

"Aren't your parents my parents too? Don't worry. I'm sure they'll agree."

"How can you be so sure?" Jiang Qi looked at him.

"I'll work to make them accept me."

"Mm. But — how do we even tell them?"

"By my paying them a visit in person, of course."

"And when?"

At a red light, Ke Yan unbuckled, leaned over, and kissed Jiang Qi's lips. "Don't worry, stop fretting about it. Think about Christmas instead — it's nearly here. Think about what you'll get me."

"Oh, right — Christmas is almost here. Christmas is when we got together." Sure enough, Jiang Qi stopped fretting about parents and turned to wondering what to get Ke Yan this year.

Although he had soothed Jiang Qi, Ke Yan knew he must meet Jiang Qi's parents. And if they stubbornly refused to let them stay together…

He glanced at Jiang Qi. He knew this fool would be heartbroken. But he would do everything he could to make Jiang Qi's parents agree.
