The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 142

They'd Known All Along 原来早就知道了

July 2026

~5 min read

On Jiang Qi's side, once both families had eaten and gone home, the two saw them off and sat on the sofa, Jiang Qi's head against Ke Yan's chest. "They got on well, but it was exhausting."

"Too nervous, were you."

"By the way — I've picked the person to take my position. Working with him this while, I think Chang Qing can handle the manager's post." Jiang Qi raised it because lately his attention had been mostly on the shop, and Chang Qing worked well and hard.

"Ready to leave the company?" Ke Yan toyed with Jiang Qi's fingers.

"Not exactly — I just want the shop to open soon." Jiang Qi clasped Ke Yan's hand and gave it a coaxing shake.

"Is this you being coquettish?" Ke Yan raised a brow. Rare indeed.

"So — do you agree? Oh, and the company says you have to give a month's notice to resign. With our relationship, I don't have to, right?"

"Jiang Qi." Ke Yan bit his neck. "Now you know to use our relationship — when I told you to before, you wouldn't."

"Right — you told me to, so I'll use it without scruple now." Jiang Qi's neck tickled, with a faint sting; he shrank from it, uncomfortable.

"Go in next week to fully hand over, then you can resign." Ke Yan licked the spot he'd bitten.

"All right." Jiang Qi grew excited inside — bookshop owner, his wish.

"By the way — do you want a boy or a girl?" Ke Yan brought up the child.

"Hm? Why ask that?"

"Weren't we going to adopt?" Ke Yan meant to start looking for a suitable child now.

"I'd want a boy. I'm afraid two grown men couldn't manage with a girl." To Jiang Qi, girls seemed delicate. He had never cared for a child and could be careless; as she grew, he would not know how to guide her through certain troubles.

"A boy, then." Ke Yan had had no preference; if Jiang Qi wanted a boy, a boy it was.

"Do you think there are men who can bear children themselves?" Jiang Qi suddenly wondered.

"Why — do you want to bear one?" Ke Yan looked at him, eyes hot.

"No." Jiang Qi shook his head. "Just idly asking — how would a man do it, and I'd be scared." Imagining himself big-bellied and giving birth, he shuddered.

"I'd be scared too. Even if a man could, it'd be dangerous; I don't want you to suffer, still less to be in any danger."

"Adopting is better." Jiang Qi agreed.

"And the wedding — do you want it by the sea, in a church, on a lawn?" Ke Yan thought it time to plan.

"I want it in a forest."

"All right." Ke Yan nodded. "Leave the rest to me — I want to give you a surprise of a wedding."

"I can't wait." Jiang Qi trusted Ke Yan would give him something he'd never expect — like the shop, whose design he loved, perfectly to his heart.

"Since that's settled, let's go back to the bedroom now — wait on me." Ke Yan scooped Jiang Qi up and carried him upstairs.

Monday. The moment Jiang Qi reached his office he called Chang Qing in.

"Manager, you wanted me?" Chang Qing came in, shutting the door.

"This week I'll hand over the rest of my work to you. Starting next week I'm resigning." Jiang Qi gathered up work documents. "And these — look them over."

"Manager, you're really resigning?" Chang Qing felt a pang of reluctance; Jiang Qi truly looked after his staff, good to everyone.

"Mm. After so many years it's hard to leave, but I want my own wish more — to open a bookshop."

"All right then." Chang Qing knew the shop was right across from the company.

"Work hard from now on." Jiang Qi gave him a pep.

"Yes." Chang Qing nodded.

At noon, Ke Yan had a lunch meeting, so Jiang Qi ate alone. Lin Zheng saw him by himself and sat across from him with his tray.

"Manager Lin." Jiang Qi greeted him.

"Manager Jiang, the CEO's not lunching with you today?"

"He has a meeting."

"Ah — I'd thought you two would never let anyone know about your relationship. Had I known earlier, I wouldn't have been so frightened I ended up in hospital when I found out."

"What?" From Lin Zheng's words it seemed he'd known about him and Ke Yan a long time.

"Back then, when I happened to discover you and CEO Ke were together, I was scared stiff."

"When did you find out?" Jiang Qi had had no idea.

"That time the managers went out for dinner, we ran into CEO Ke and CEO Wei at the hotel. CEO Ke was dining with CEO Wei's daughter, and you insisted on finding him." Lin Zheng had assumed Jiang Qi was drunk and acting up. Now he understood the jealousy behind it.

"I dragged you away and told you to wait in the lounge. When I returned from the restroom, you were gone. I found you back in the lounge with the CEO, in the middle of…" Lin Zheng's expression turned indecent.

Jiang Qi flushed crimson, remembering what he and Ke Yan had been doing there — and that they'd gone to a room afterward. He was suddenly grateful they hadn't done it right there.

Lin Zheng laughed at his red face. "I thought I'd hidden well, but the CEO spotted me afterward."

"Ke Yan never told me." Jiang Qi figured Ke Yan hadn't wanted him to feel awkward seeing Lin Zheng at the company again.

It was rather awkward, though — being seen with Ke Yan like that.

And he'd thought their secrecy so airtight, never imagining it had been known.

After lunch, Jiang Qi went back to his office — and found Wang Siqi waiting inside.
