The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 117

Buying Gifts 买礼物

July 2026

~4 min read

Sunday. Jiang Qi and Ke Yan slept until they woke naturally, and Jiang Qi thought he'd go to the mall that afternoon to scout out a Christmas gift for Ke Yan.

There was still a while until Christmas, but he wanted to pick something out early.

"Shall I come too?" Ke Yan watched Jiang Qi pull on his coat.

"No — I want it to be a surprise." Jiang Qi wrapped his scarf on.

"All right then. Drive carefully." Ke Yan kissed him.

"Mm, I will. I'm off." Jiang Qi headed out and took the elevator down.

Once he'd gone, Ke Yan decided to go out himself — the gift he meant to give Jiang Qi wasn't in hand yet.

Jiang Qi drove to the mall, parked, and racked his brain over what on earth to buy.

Wandering with no goal in mind, he suddenly spotted someone up ahead. Hm? Wasn't that Lin Zirui?

Lin Zirui saw him at the same moment, and Jiang Qi went over. "Zirui — what a coincidence."

"It is. No work today, so I came to browse." Lin Zirui smiled.

"Same here. Let's wander together, then."

"Sure." Lin Zirui nodded.

So the two little ukes browsed together. Meanwhile, Ke Yan drove to a shop across from the company, where a suited young man hurried forward and bowed. "CEO Ke."

"Mm."

Ke Yan went in and looked over the shop's fittings.

"CEO Ke, I've settled it with the owner — as long as you're satisfied, this place can be—"

The young man hadn't finished before Ke Yan cut him off. "Too small. Take over the shop next door as well, and break through to the floor above. Both units."

"But this…" The man hesitated — never mind whether the owner would agree, just the location, and two storefronts, this…

"Price is no object. Whatever it costs, get it bought for me."

"Right, right, no problem." What had he been thinking? The CEO of Ke Group would hardly lack money. He would get it done and leave a good impression in the bargain.

"Once it's settled, gut all the fittings here. Tell me when it's stripped. Two days — have it done in two days."

"Two days? But…"

"No buts. If there's a problem, I'll find someone else."

"No, no, no — I'll get it done."

"Mm."

Ke Yan checked the time. Still early; Jiang Qi wouldn't be back so soon. With the matter handled here, he went on to another place.

Meanwhile Jiang Qi and Lin Zirui, after browsing a while, found somewhere to sit with drinks.

"I really don't know what to get." Jiang Qi sipped his drink.

"I've pretty much decided what to buy." Lin Zirui had made up his mind after one circuit.

"What are you getting?" Jiang Qi asked.

"A few things." Lin Zirui kept it secret.

"All right then, I'll keep looking. Let's meet up again sometime." Jiang Qi said his goodbyes.

"Sure, next time." Lin Zirui went off to buy what he'd planned.

Jiang Qi, still wandering aimlessly, had already given everything obvious before. What on earth to give this Christmas?

Then he spotted a particular shop and smiled, pleased. Yes — he'd get this.

He wanted to give Ke Yan this.

He went in, had a look, and finally settled on something, telling the clerk to have it engraved.

When Jiang Qi finally got home, Ke Yan was already back. Jiang Qi tucked the thing he'd bought into his coat pocket; seeing Ke Yan, he instinctively pressed a hand over the pocket, then let go.

"I — I'll go up and change first." Jiang Qi got the words in before Ke Yan could speak, and ran upstairs before he'd even finished, hunting for somewhere to hide it.

One look at Jiang Qi's reaction told Ke Yan he had bought the gift and was hunting for somewhere to hide it.

When Jiang Qi had hidden it and come down, he found Ke Yan watching him, and froze. Had he been found out? Then again, impossible. "What are you looking at?"

"You. Come here."

Jiang Qi obediently went to him.

Ke Yan reached out and pulled Jiang Qi into his lap.

"Would you like to open a bookshop?"

"Mm." Jiang Qi nodded. "When I'm old and retired, I'll open one — read books, grow old with you."

"You don't have to wait until you retire."

"What do you mean?" Jiang Qi didn't follow.

"Nothing." Ke Yan pressed him down onto the sofa and kissed him.

The kiss left Jiang Qi breathless and hazy. As Ke Yan deepened it, he forgot what he had been wondering.

Lin Zirui, meanwhile, came home with a dozen-odd big bags, thinking he'd make another mall trip when he had time to pick up what he hadn't finished buying.
