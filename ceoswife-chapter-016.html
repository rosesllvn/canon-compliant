The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 16

Man in Black?? 黑衣人？？

July 2026

~5 min read

Jiang Qi ran up to Ke Yan and reached for the necklace. Ke Yan held the cord end; Jiang Qi pinched the pendant; Ke Yan let go, Jiang Qi tugged, and it was back with its owner.

"Than—" Meaning to thank him, Jiang Qi caught sight of Ke Yan's face and froze. This person is really good-looking. It was the first time he'd ever thought a man, too, could be this beautiful.

Ke Yan looked at the dazed Jiang Qi, frowned, and turned back into his dorm.

Bang— Watching the door close, Jiang Qi felt ashamed of himself — fancy going slack-jawed at the sight of a man.

He set off toward the fifth floor, and was halfway up when he remembered he hadn't actually thanked the fellow. He rubbed his nose and went back down.

Knock knock— After a while Ke Yan opened the door, and Jiang Qi hurried to thank him. "Thank you for just now."

"No need." And with those two words Ke Yan shut the door again.

Such a good-looking person — how can he be this cold? Even his voice is icy, Jiang Qi thought, rubbing his nose.

Back in his own dorm, he put the necklace away in a drawer, afraid that if it fell again some day he'd never get it back, then picked up his wallet to go eat — having clean forgotten his promise to lunch with Liu Hao.

Never mind the Jiang Qi off eating. Just then Liu Hao, about to go find him for lunch, came strolling brazenly into the dorm building with his girlfriend on his arm. Neither noticed the figure trailing behind: tall and strapping, dressed head to toe in black — black tee, black jeans, black canvas shoes — dragging a great black suitcase, a large pair of sunglasses on his face, his manner furtive. Passing students couldn't help thinking the man had killed someone and was off to hide the body; one look at that suitcase — was there a corpse inside? — and they quickened their steps to get well clear of this "man in black."

The "man in black" watched Liu Hao go into Room 505, raised an eyebrow, and followed him in.

Jiang Qi shoveled down his food, nagged by the sense he'd forgotten something, though he couldn't think what — so he gave up trying. Just then a few girls sat down behind him and got to gossiping, and the substance of it drifted into his ears.

"Let me tell you, I saw the most gorgeous guy today."

"I saw him too — at the school gate. Never in my life have I seen anyone so handsome."

A handsome guy? Are they talking about the one who caught my necklace?

"You all don't even know — his name's Ke Yan."

"How do you know?" the girls chorused.

"Oh, that — well..." The girl drew out the suspense.

"Aiya, don't keep us hanging, just tell us!"

"All right, all right — my cousin's in the student council, so she knows all the new students' files."

"Ah, I'd so love to see."

Jiang Qi set down his chopsticks. Ke Yan? Between him and that other man, which is better-looking? He gave his head a shake. How have I gone and turned into a gossip myself?

Back at the dorm he found several people in front of Room 505, discussing something. He squeezed forward and saw Liu Hao quarreling with a man he didn't recognize, dressed all in black, a girl about his own age standing beside them looking thoroughly awkward. A girl? In the men's dorm? Did the gate guard allow this? His face couldn't help going grim. He stepped inside and shut the door, sealing out the curious eyes. "What's all this you're doing?"

"Doing? I was about to introduce my girlfriend to you when this guy comes up and throws a punch at me — if I weren't quick he'd have decked me." Liu Hao was worked up, his fair baby face flushed red with anger.

The "man in black" offered no defense, only looked at the girl. Under his gaze her head sank lower and lower, until at last she called out helplessly, "C-cousin."

Liu Hao was astonished. "You're her cousin? Even so, you can't go about it like this — I didn't do anything improper."

"Hmph." The "man in black" gave a cold snort. "Then do you happen to know she's underage?"

"What?" Jiang Qi and Liu Hao went wide-eyed. "How was I supposed to know?" Liu Hao protested.

"It's not against the law for a minor to date. And anyway — what business is it of yours?" The girl wasn't having it.

"Then go tell your own mom and dad — they're the ones who asked me to help." The "man in black" said it with a thoroughly couldn't-care-less air, and Jiang Qi found that careless face especially infuriating.

At that the girl stamped her foot and flounced off, lips pushed out in a pout.

With her gone, the "man in black" turned a grinning, cheeky face on Liu Hao. "Sorry about that just now, man, don't be sore — I had to come on strong, or I'd never get the better of that cousin of mine."

"Hmph, as if I'd be so petty." Only a little put out, was all.

"Good to hear. I'm Meng Qing — same dorm as you two. Oh, right — let's go eat, I'm starving."

"I've eaten." Jiang Qi felt his stomach was quite full enough.

Meng Qing slung an arm over Jiang Qi's and Liu Hao's shoulders. "No matter — just eat a second time."

"Right, a second round. We clearly agreed to eat together — how'd you go off on your own?" Liu Hao added.

Now Jiang Qi remembered what he'd forgotten. Only — a second meal? You've got to be kidding...
