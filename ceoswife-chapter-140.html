Chapter 140

The Love He Gives Him 给他的爱

July 2026

~7 min read

Off work, Jiang Qi told Ke Yan about Sun Nianqing's talk with him, and hearing it, Ke Yan made up his mind at once.

Jiang Qi saw the shift in his expression and knew what he was thinking. "I'm not really that angry. Whatever you decide, I won't stop you — but work's hard to find these days, and Miss Sun, a girl on her own, is rather pitiable." Jiang Qi didn't not dislike Sun Nianqing; he simply also pitied her a little.

Ke Yan stroked his hair and only said, "I understand."

Jiang Qi said no more — he'd said his piece, and Sun Nianqing's attitude truly had repelled him.

The next day Ke Yan had Yang Shaoyu inform Sun Nianqing to come resign of her own accord.

Ke Yan had wanted to fire her outright, but dismissal would make future employment difficult. Since Jiang Qi had asked for restraint, he gave her the chance to resign.

Ke Yan frowned. He really was repulsed by Sun Nianqing.

Yang Shaoyu passed the word along, and on hearing it Sun Nianqing went to find Ke Yan.

The secretary at the door blocked her, but in the end Yang Shaoyu told Ke Yan, "Sun Nianqing's at the door wanting to see you. Will you?"

"Let her in." Ke Yan was impatient.

Yang Shaoyu let her through. Heart leaping with hope, she opened the door and walked into Ke Yan's office.

"What do you want?" Ke Yan's voice was cold.

"Ke Yan — the truth is, I've liked you a long time. What's so good about Jiang Qi? He's a man. I'm beautiful, and I'm a woman." And with that she actually began to undo her buttons.

Ke Yan's eyes went icy; he frowned and called security up.

"Why? Why would you like Jiang Qi? Look at me — I'm so much better than him. Jiang Qi isn't worthy of you."

"You aren't worthy to be compared to Jiang Qi. Get out, now." Hearing Sun Nianqing badmouth Jiang Qi, on top of her behavior, Ke Yan was truly enraged.

It was the first time Sun Nianqing had seen Ke Yan so angry — cold all over — and the look in his eyes frightened her.

"Since you won't take the courtesy I've shown you, I'll oblige you." Ke Yan walked up to her and reached out…

Far from glad, Sun Nianqing was afraid now, because the air around Ke Yan in that moment was terrifying, enough to make her tremble.

Ke Yan gripped her chin and squeezed, hard. "The thing I hate most is anyone speaking ill of Jiang Qi." With that he flung his hand, and Sun Nianqing was thrown to the floor.

Sprawled there, sweat dripping from her forehead, she felt her jaw had come half out of joint.

"I'm a petty man — and you still chose to test my limits. Hiring you into Ke Group was a mistake to begin with." Ke Yan pulled two tissues and wiped his hands.

Just then security arrived, the guard captain in front. "CEO, your orders?"

"Throw her out of Ke Group." Ke Yan didn't spare her a glance, his voice cold.

"Yes." Two guards hauled Sun Nianqing up off the floor.

And so the people in the secretary's room outside saw Sun Nianqing dragged out, disheveled and a sorry sight.

They exchanged knowing looks. So — Sun Nianqing had failed to seduce the CEO and was being thrown out.

Jiang Qi soon heard too. This time he didn't foolishly go plead with Ke Yan on her behalf, because she simply wasn't worth it.

Word of Sun Nianqing spread quickly through the company, and she was dismissed. Hearing it, Wang Siqi felt no gloating glee — only private relief that she hadn't tried to seduce the CEO the way Sun Nianqing had.

Others, learning of it, dared discuss it only in secret, and some said, "The CEO really is a good man — and truly loves Manager Jiang. Faced with a beauty throwing herself at him, he beat her and fired her on the spot."

"What — he hit her?" Someone was startled. "I thought she was just fired?"

"He beat her first, then fired her." This from someone who'd seen Sun Nianqing's jaw.

"I never thought the CEO would hit a woman."

"He'd only ever hit a woman like Sun Nianqing — she was asking for it."

The gossip followed many threads, but no one dared neglect their work. Being fired was bad enough; being dragged out like Sun Nianqing was worse.

The days passed one by one. Jiang Qi spent them going with Ke Yan to buy furniture for the attic and seeing to the bookshop; it should open soon.

Besides the shop, Jiang Qi kept working, though lately he'd handed most of it to a subordinate, Chang Qing — who'd be taking over the manager's post.

And there was the occasional meal out with Mo Lü and Ma Tong — though those two were truly swamped lately, not only taking over the branch but fending off the matchmaking dates their father kept arranging.

Their father knew they didn't want to run the company, so he hoped to marry them off soon and let a son-in-law take over.

But Mo Lü and Ma Tong liked matchmaking and marriage even less.

As for Lin Zirui, his family had returned from their trip, but he was in no hurry to see them; with a free day today, and a visit owed anyway, he decided to go home.

Without telling Yang Shaoyu, he went.

He arrived just as the Lin family had finished a meal, so everyone was there.

Seeing him, Lin's eldest and second brothers looked guilty, thinking of how the family had planned the trip together and had meant to call Lin Zirui along.

But Lin Le objected. She hated Lin Zirui, perhaps because their older brothers cared for him, and wanted him to see that they cherished her more. She refused to let them call, and their parents, unwilling to upset her, said, "Zirui's busy anyway—listen to your sister."

Lin Le wheedled the two brothers too, and they couldn't bring themselves to refuse her; the second brother thought: Zirui hasn't been home lately, he's probably busy.

Maybe by the time they got back Zirui wouldn't even know they'd gone.

They'd agreed readily then, but seeing Lin Zirui today they felt guilty, as though they'd wronged him.

Lin Zirui looked at the two brothers avoiding his eyes, said nothing, didn't even sit, and glanced over his family. "You're back from your trip?"

"Xiao Rui…" The eldest brother seemed about to speak and stopped; the second couldn't meet his eyes.

"I came home today to tell you something. I have someone I love. He's a man."

"What?" Mother Lin reacted first, her voice shrill.

"I love a man, and I'm with him. Whether you agree or not, I'll stay with Shaoyu. I came only to inform you; your permission doesn't matter." Lin Zirui remained expressionless.

That killed any appetite the Lins had left, and Lin Zirui turned to go the moment he'd finished, not wanting to see their faces — shock, disgust, revulsion, contempt. This home had hurt him deeply once; never again.

He walked out of the Lin house, thinking he'd cab home; he'd come in the Lin family car but didn't want to drive it back.

But at the door, unexpectedly, he saw Yang Shaoyu's car.

The door opened — it really was Yang Shaoyu, getting out.

"How did you know I was here?" Lin Zirui hadn't told him where he'd gone.

"I always know what's on your mind, and where you'd go I can guess. But — why didn't you tell me you'd come home?" Yang Shaoyu was puzzled.

"I didn't want you to see the family that grieves me, to feel I'd wronged you." Lin Zirui's hands were behind his back.

"Why say that?"

"Your family can give me warmth, love. Mine give you only insults and looks of disgust." His eyes dimmed.

"Silly." Yang Shaoyu cupped his head and kissed his lips. "I don't care about any of that. As long as I care about you, that's enough."

"Mm." Lin Zirui nodded, smiling. Yes — with Shaoyu, he was never sad; Shaoyu brought him only smiles.

This was the love Shaoyu gave him.
