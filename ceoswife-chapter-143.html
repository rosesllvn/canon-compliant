The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 143

All Seen 都被看到了

"Manager Wang, is something the matter?" Jiang Qi poured two cups of water.

"Jiang Qi, I'd meant to compete with you for Ke Yan — but after seeing how Sun Nianqing ended up, I didn't dare." Wang Siqi knew well enough which way the wind blew; she had no wish to share Sun Nianqing's fate, however unwilling she felt inside.

"So that's all you came for?" Jiang Qi sensed there was more.

"No — I want to ask you something."

"Ask, then."

"You and Ke Yan met in university — does that mean you've been together since then?"

"Yes." Jiang Qi nodded.

"…" She'd known the answer, but needed to hear it confirmed. A couple who had stayed together this long—and still loved each other this deeply—could hardly be pulled apart. How could she ever win against Jiang Qi?

"Sorry to bother you." Wang Siqi left his office, glad she hadn't gone as far as Sun Nianqing and torn off all pretense. She'd wanted to be the CEO's wife, but didn't want to lose both that and her job.

To think the coveted role of CEO's wife was Jiang Qi's — Jiang Qi was the CEO's wife, and the wife was a man.

Wang Siqi gone, Jiang Qi sat idly in his office. Most of his work was Chang Qing's now, and Ke Yan was out — so he thought he'd go look at the shop.

He unlocked the door. It was all set up now, even stocked with books. Breathing in the scent of them filling the room, he went to the children's section. It was beautifully done — cushions in every color, pale-green little tables and chairs, shelves full of children's stories, fairy tales, picture books, and some educational ones.

Most special of all was the fujoshi corner Jiang Qi had set aside, with BL novels and manga. He'd made it because of Xiao An's and Mo Lü's recommendations, and because he found fujoshi rather marvelous — willing to support two men being together, unconditionally.

There were magazines and stationery as well, while the second floor was devoted to adult readers. Everything seemed to be in place, yet Jiang Qi still felt something was missing.

Just then Ke Yan called. "At the shop?"

"Mm. You didn't drink, did you?"

"No — and I brought you back a pudding."

"Really? Where are you now?"

"At the shop door." Ke Yan hung up and came in.

"How are you back so soon?"

"It's almost work time." Ke Yan checked the time; he'd been out nearly two hours.

"I lost track of time." He hadn't realized he'd been in the shop so long.

"This hotel's pudding is excellent — try it." Ke Yan unwrapped it.

Jiang Qi took a spoonful. "Mm, good. Oh — Ke Yan, I keep feeling the shop's missing something I haven't prepared."

"You haven't hired staff." Ke Yan named at once the thing Jiang Qi couldn't pin down.

"Right — no wonder it felt like something was missing." It dawned on him, and he went straight for paper and pen.

"How many to hire?"

"Four."

"Won't that be too few?" Jiang Qi had been thinking two.

"No — it's a big shop, two floors; four is on the low side. Make it five."

"Five's too many — with me that's six. Four's enough."

He thought a moment and started writing, asking about pay. "How much a month?"

"A one-month trial first — 1,300 a month during the trial, 3,000 for permanent staff."

"All right." Jiang Qi went with it.

The notice written, he and Ke Yan posted it on the decorative board outside.

They locked up and went back to the company together. The driver had taken the car back to the lot, so for once they went in through the main entrance.

The front-desk staff shot to their feet. "Hello, CEO. Hello, Manager Jiang."

Only once the two had boarded the elevator did the front desk sit and start talking.

In the elevator, Jiang Qi mentioned, "I had lunch with Manager Lin today. He said he'd known about us a while ago — and even saw us…"

"What?" Ke Yan played dumb.

"That." Jiang Qi gave him a look.

"What's 'that'?"

"You know — kiss, kissing." He said it low.

"Like this?" Ke Yan dipped his head and kissed him.

"Mm—" His mouth was sealed.

"Ding—" The elevator opened just then.

Jiang Qi remembered where they were and shoved Ke Yan off — but the people waiting outside had all seen it. "…"

The people outside stared. Had they really just caught the CEO and Manager Jiang kissing in the elevator?

Jiang Qi flushed scarlet and glared at the culprit, praying no one would get in. But no one seemed afraid of the CEO anymore; they crowded aboard, while those left behind regretfully waited for the next lift.

Jiang Qi wished the floor would swallow him. He could feel everyone's eyes.

He sneaked a pinch at Ke Yan, who stood beside him as though nothing had happened.

Pinched, Ke Yan smiled — and in the silent elevator all eyes turned to him.

Ke Yan calmly slid an arm around Jiang Qi's waist and murmured in his ear, "Xiao Feng saw us kiss too."

Jiang Qi blinked. "What?" — a little loud, from surprise.

Then clapped a hand over his mouth.

The elevator arrived; Jiang Qi jabbed Ke Yan in the stomach with his elbow, and without even asking when Xiao Feng had seen, hurried off.

The office staff saw Jiang Qi come in red-faced, head down.

He covered his face. All seen.
