Chapter 24

A Ghost?? 有鬼？？

July 2026

~4 min read

In the library, Ke Yan and Jiang Qi sat facing each other, each reading. After a long while Jiang Qi set his book down and looked at the Ke Yan absorbed across from him. He watched a moment, then asked abruptly, "Ke Yan, why do you like to read?"

Ke Yan lifted his gaze. "And you?"

Caught off guard by the question turned back on him, Jiang Qi blinked and thought a while, noting that Ke Yan had nearly finished his book. "I don't know — I think I've liked it since I was little, it just became a habit. You?"

Ke Yan didn't answer; he only closed the book gently and stood. "Come on, let's head back."

"Oh, all right."

A summer night with no wind is a stifling thing. The two walked the little path back, Jiang Qi trailing behind, looking at Ke Yan's back and wondering: if he'd answered just now, what would he have said?

"Help — ahh." A cry came from somewhere in the distance. Both stopped. Jiang Qi pricked up his ears to place it — but as though they'd misheard, no further cry came. On the path empty but for the two of them, the streetlamps gave a feeble glow, insects whirling beneath and casting their shadows on the ground. A prickle crept over Jiang Qi, and he instinctively grabbed Ke Yan's hand.

Ke Yan tried to pull free; Jiang Qi held tight, looked around, and lowered his voice. "Ke Yan, should we go take a look nearby?"

Ke Yan's tone went cold. "Are you sure?"

"Mm. Let go of my hand first, then."

"No."

"Let go of me." Colder still.

"But I'm a little scared."

"Then hold your own hand."

"But that doesn't make me feel safe..."

In the end, Jiang Qi still holding his hand, the two went to find who was calling.

"Ke Yan, the voice just now sounded like a woman's."

"Mm."

"Could it be a female ghost?"

Ke Yan glanced at him, not having expected he'd be so afraid of ghosts. "How could it be — stop that."

They walked all about the area for ages and never saw a soul. Jiang Qi had gripped Ke Yan's hand sore. He looked around. "Doesn't seem to be anyone. Let's go back."

"We can't — what if someone really needs our help?"

"Then look for them yourself."

"We can't... wait — I think I heard it again, just up ahead."

Helpless, Ke Yan could only follow the earnest-faced Jiang Qi forward. After a little way they found the source. Jiang Qi hid behind Ke Yan, clutching his clothes, edging forward a small step at a time.

At a corner Ke Yan stopped, and Jiang Qi with him. The cry was very close now; from the other side came a rustling, scuffling sound. Jiang Qi wanted to lean out and look, but Ke Yan blocked him, signaling him to hold still. He obeyed.

Ke Yan stepped around the corner — and at the sight, could have thrashed the lot of them. Three people: two women and a man, dressed all in black, white masks over their faces, one in a skull mask. They seemed to be in some dispute, quarreling in low voices and waving a flashlight about. Caught, all three went guiltily silent, standing meekly where they were, not daring to move.

Behind him, Jiang Qi crept up and called softly, "Ke Yan?"

Ke Yan turned, reached out, and covered Jiang Qi's eyes. "Come on, let's go — it's a bunch of cats."

Eyes covered, Jiang Qi could see nothing and had to let himself be led. "How would cats cry for help?"

"You misheard."

"Did I?"

"Mm."

Behind them, the three who'd meant to frighten Ke Yan poked their heads out one by one to watch the two go. One said, "Who's that guy — how come he's with Xiao Ke?"

The second, biting a handkerchief, said, "The nerve of it — and here none of us have ever so much as held Xiao Yan's hand."

The first turned. "You — Yang — do you know?"

"I don't know either."

"Useless. At the same school as Xiao Ke and you don't even know this."

"Hey, I haven't been at school this whole stretch — how would I know?"
