Jiang Qi's Hospital Stay (Part Two) 江奇住院记下

July 2026

~8 min read

Jiang Qi woke needing the toilet. Outside the window, night had fallen.

He looked at Ke Yan asleep on the small attendant's cot beside the bed, slowly sat up, lifted the quilt aside, and got up quietly; because his right hand had the needle in it, he used his left to lift the bottle down, meaning to go to the toilet.

"Ding—" In lowering the bottle he accidentally knocked the stand it hung from, making a small ding.

Jiang Qi took it down ever so carefully — mustn't wake Ke Yan.

"Where are you going?"

Ke Yan's voice came from behind, woken by Jiang Qi, presumably. Jiang Qi held the bottle in one hand and said, "To the toilet."

Ke Yan came over and took the bottle from Jiang Qi. "Why didn't you call me."

"I didn't call you precisely because I was afraid I'd wake you; go back to sleep."

"I wasn't deeply asleep, and someone has to hold the bottle. Call me when you need anything."

"Mm." Jiang Qi knew Ke Yan meant to watch whether the bottle had finished dripping; thinking about it, he felt guilty — Ke Yan had class in the daytime tomorrow, and keeping him company like this must be exhausting.

Suddenly there was a weight on his head; Jiang Qi tried to lift it, but found it pressed down by Ke Yan, unable to move.

"Silly. Hurry up and finish in the toilet and get to sleep."

"Mm..."

Jiang Qi couldn't help the corners of his mouth tugging up. Why? Just an inexplicable, irrepressible smile.

The light on, the door shut — the hospital toilet was very small, and with two grown men squeezed in it was smaller still, hard to move about.

Ke Yan held the bottle for Jiang Qi with one hand and helped undo his waistband with the other.

Jiang Qi shrank back, embarrassed, but let Ke Yan pull down first his trousers, then the next layer.

With no one stopping him, Ke Yan naturally pulled Jiang Qi's underwear down easily, and just as he was about to take hold, Jiang Qi turned away, saying, "I can manage this part myself."

Ke Yan raised an eyebrow, ignored Jiang Qi, took hold, and urged, "Hurry up."

Poor Jiang Qi couldn't pee with Ke Yan holding him, his body trembling in an involuntary shiver.

Seeing Jiang Qi like this, Ke Yan gave him a light pinch; Jiang Qi jumped, his heart twisting once. Anyway, everything there was to see had been seen — what was there to be shy about.

Back in bed, Jiang Qi's face was still hot. Seeing the thin blanket on Ke Yan's cot, his heart ached; he shifted aside. "Ke Yan, let's sleep together."

"All right." Ke Yan lay down too and gathered Jiang Qi into his arms. Two grown men on one small hospital bed really was a tight squeeze, but Jiang Qi found being squeezed together very warm.

Lying in Ke Yan's arms, Jiang Qi kept his eyes open with no wish to sleep at all, having slept too long earlier.

Ke Yan stroked Jiang Qi's head, kissed his forehead, then made to kiss his lips; Jiang Qi blocked him. "I've got a cold."

"It's fine." Ke Yan drew aside the hand Jiang Qi was blocking with, and kissed him.

The kiss left Jiang Qi tingling, but he remembered his cold and quickly pushed Ke Yan away, afraid of infecting him.

Pushed off, Ke Yan helplessly gathered him in and they went to sleep.

"I don't want to eat it."

"Be good, just a little."

"No, it's awful."

Jiang Qi was woken bright and early by the sound of this exchange. He looked at the next bed and saw the woman throwing a tantrum at her boyfriend.

The man seemed at his wits' end, and had gone out to buy her something tasty.

The woman noticed Jiang Qi was awake and greeted him. "Hi, you're up — I haven't even asked your name yet."

"I'm Jiang Qi."

"Oh, I'm Bao Ruoruo."

"Oh, right — have you seen the man who's with me?"

"That handsome guy? He seems to have gone to buy you breakfast."

"Mm."

Jiang Qi looked toward the doorway; Ke Yan wasn't back yet.

Ke Yan returned before long, holding not congee but corn. Jiang Qi's eyes lit up — he liked corn; having had no appetite before, the sight of corn suddenly made him want to eat, his stomach a bit hungry too.

Ke Yan wrapped a cob in a clean plastic bag and handed it to Jiang Qi, who took it, smelled the corn's fragrance, then opened his mouth wide for a big bite.

Sweet and fragrant, the corn was delicious. Jiang Qi happily ate a cob and a half, then passed the remainder to Ke Yan.

"You two really are close."

Jiang Qi turned to look at the speaker, Bao Ruoruo, who went on, "Aren't you? You even ate your leftovers — someone who didn't know would think you were boyfriend and girlfriend, but you're both men."

Jiang Qi smiled at this; he and Ke Yan were plainly a man-and-man pairing.

"Xiao Ruo, I'm back." Bao Ruoruo's boyfriend came in with a big bag of treats; Bao Ruoruo glanced in the bag, satisfied now, and had her boyfriend set everything on the table.

Jiang Qi sighed inwardly. Girls these days really were little princesses; good thing he didn't like women. He couldn't wait on one — he still needed Ke Yan to wait on him.

"Ke Yan, I want the toilet." Jiang Qi tugged at Ke Yan beside him.

"Mm." Ke Yan picked up Jiang Qi's drip bottle.

As Jiang Qi walked toward the toilet he felt a gaze fixed on him from behind; he knew it was Bao Ruoruo. Jiang Qi didn't like Bao Ruoruo, but didn't dislike her either — simply put, they weren't acquainted enough for either.

Jiang Qi came out of the toilet and watched some TV with Ke Yan, then Ke Yan made to leave. Jiang Qi asked, "Where are you going?"

"Something to take care of."

"Mm, be careful on the way."

"Mm." And Ke Yan left.

Jiang Qi felt a little down to see Ke Yan go, but Bao Ruoruo beside him called him into a chat; Jiang Qi propped a pillow behind his back to lean more comfortably against the bed.

"My cousin's coming by in a bit — will Ke Yan be back soon?"

"Probably; what do you want him for?"

"Nothing, just to introduce my cousin to him."

Jiang Qi's alarm bells rang loud; he looked at Bao Ruoruo. "He has a girlfriend."

"I didn't say I wanted to set my cousin up as his girlfriend."

Chided, Jiang Qi wondered if he'd been small-minded and suspicious.

"Though — maybe once Ke Yan sees my cousin he'll find his girlfriend doesn't measure up? So since he's not married, anyone's got a shot."

So he hadn't been small-minded after all. She'd meant this from the start, and he had to stamp the notion out.

"You don't know? Oh, right, I haven't told you yet..."

"What?" Bao Ruoruo was intrigued by Jiang Qi's deliberately mysterious tone.

"What? Why, that Ke Yan actually doesn't like women."

"Huh?" Bao Ruoruo clearly hadn't expected Jiang Qi to say Ke Yan didn't like women. "But didn't you say before he has a girlfriend?"

"Right." Jiang Qi nodded. "But weren't you and I not acquainted yet back then?"

"Is that so?" Bao Ruoruo looked at Jiang Qi, half believing.

Jiang Qi nodded earnestly and solemnly.

At that, the previously unconvinced Bao Ruoruo now believed.

For a moment the ward fell silent; Bao Ruoruo's boyfriend had long since been catching up on sleep to one side, and hadn't heard a word of Jiang Qi and Bao Ruoruo's exchange.

"Then — his girl, er, boyfriend, you know him too?"

"Of course I know him; I'm with him every single day."

"Every day?" Since yesterday she'd seen Jiang Qi with no one but Ke Yan. A thought flashed too quickly to catch, but Jiang Qi resolved it for her.

"You should know — I'm actually his boyfriend."

Bao Ruoruo looked at Jiang Qi in disbelief; Jiang Qi looked at her stunned face and couldn't tell what she was thinking.

The atmosphere grew more awkward, but Jiang Qi didn't mind, only glancing at the door now and then to see whether Ke Yan was back, then going back to watching TV.

When Ke Yan returned he carried a lot of food, all things Jiang Qi could eat now and liked to eat; Jiang Qi was clearly delighted at the sight, and started eating at once.

Watching Jiang Qi eat, Ke Yan felt someone looking his way; he raised his head and saw Bao Ruoruo hurriedly turn over and lie down in her bed.

Ke Yan gave her a strange glance, but soon paid it no mind.

Lying in bed, Bao Ruoruo thought Jiang Qi shameless — they were plainly two men...

Two men what? She didn't know. Only that he shouldn't have admitted it so frankly — they were plainly two men...

And so, for the rest of his stay in the hospital, Jiang Qi never exchanged another word with Bao Ruoruo.

After three days Jiang Qi was finally discharged and happily pulled Ke Yan toward the hospital entrance.

"I haven't been on a drip in years." At the hospital entrance Jiang Qi held Ke Yan's hand, the medicine just prescribed in his other.

"You've still got the nerve to bring it up."

"Ke Yan, I want seafood."

"No."

"Why?"

"You're sick."

"..."

Perhaps this was exactly why Jiang Qi didn't want to tell Ke Yan he was sick — because when Jiang Qi got sick, unlike others who don't want to eat anything, he turned ravenous.
