Chapter 7

The Love Between Two People 两个人的爱

July 2026

~2 min read

After a while they let go of each other. Ke Yan looked at him a moment, then held out a hand. "Shall we take a walk?"

"Mm." Jiang Qi took it.

They walked on along the road, and after a time Jiang Qi spoke. "My mom wanted me to go on a matchmaking date today." He couldn't finish — Ke Yan's hand had tightened around his, faintly painful.

"Not allowed," Ke Yan said at last, in his domineering way. "If you dare go, I'll shut you away where you can't go anywhere at all. You'll stay right by my side."

Jiang Qi snorted a laugh. "Don't worry, I won't. Only — what do I do when my mom keeps herding me into it like a duck onto a perch?"

"Heh — herding you, my little yellow duckling?" Ke Yan couldn't help a small laugh. "Then we'll just tell her the truth. Don't worry — she'll agree to us." He said it with total certainty.

"Why are you so sure?"

"Because I, your man, will handle it." He paused. "And because your mother loves you the same as I do. She'd never let you be unhappy."

It was the first I love you Ke Yan had ever spoken to Jiang Qi — and the first of his whole life.

Jiang Qi's eyes curved into crescents, the old fear and unease gone; he knew Ke Yan had it in him to do exactly as he said. His heart brimmed. He stopped, turned, stood face to face, looked up. "Yan — I love you." It was the first time he'd ever said it too, and the shyness of it sent the color rising in his face; he ducked his head.

Ke Yan was struck dumb. Looking at that reddened, lowered face — Jiang Qi, his Jiang Qi — he couldn't hold back the surge in his chest. A wild joy swept through him; he tipped Jiang Qi's chin up and kissed him.

This is the roadside — what if someone — but his lips were already covered. Ke Yan bit at him gently, displeased that his attention had wandered.

Along that busy road, the true and earnest love between two men quietly unfurled.

By the time they got home it was late. They showered together, and back in the bedroom Ke Yan dried Jiang Qi's hair for him — and then pressed him down.

A night of tangled closeness. So the next morning Jiang Qi took one look at the plague of bodies crowding the elevator and struck out, without a second thought, for the stairs. Panting his way up, he cursed inwardly: Ke Yan, you enormous bastard!!
