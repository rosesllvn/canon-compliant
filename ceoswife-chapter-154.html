Closeness 亲昵

Over the next few days the shop grew busier — buyers and browsers both.

Jiang Qi grew busy too, rushing back after lunch with Ke Yan, and Ke Yan came to the shop to help out as well.

At evening's close, Ke Yan pulled the car up to the shop door, opened it, and saw two or three people inside.

Jiang Qi was ringing up a customer, slipping the books into a paper bag.

Seeing Ke Yan arrive, Jiang Qi checked whether anyone was left — two or three children with parents downstairs, and someone upstairs.

With customers still there he could hardly turn them out, so he told Ke Yan, "Wait for me a bit."

"It's fine, I'll wait, no rush." Ke Yan stood before him, the counter between them.

Coming down from the second floor with an armful of books, Zhu Zihao saw a suited man at the counter, looking down at the boss as Jiang Qi smiled up at him.

Zhu Zihao noticed the boss was a different person entirely whenever this man was around — laughing often, too.

He didn't know who the man was, only that he was very handsome.

After setting down the books, he couldn't resist another look—and saw the man bend to touch the boss's face. That was…intimate.

Ke Yan had touched Jiang Qi's face because it was flushed; he couldn't resist. Jiang Qi startled. "What are you doing?"

Ke Yan raised a brow. "Touching you."

Jiang Qi touched his own face. "What's wrong with my face?"

"Why's it so red?"

"Probably from being in the heating too long." He stretched.

Ke Yan flicked his forehead.

A while more and the customers had mostly gone; Jiang Qi put on his coat to leave.

They walked out together; Jiang Qi locked up and said to Zhu Zihao and Wang Xiaoyu, "See you tomorrow."

"Bye, Boss."

That said, Jiang Qi quickly got into Ke Yan's car — too cold out.

Wang Xiaoyu left first with her bag, while Zhu Zihao glanced at Jiang Qi and Ke Yan getting into the car. Going home together?

Because Lin Zirui had worked through the weekend, his day off had moved to tomorrow. Fresh from his shower and watching television in bed that evening, he said, "Shaoyu, I'd like to have Eldest and Second Brother over for dinner tomorrow. I'm off, so I'll buy groceries during the day, and you can cook when you get back. How's that?" His cooking was nowhere near Yang Shaoyu's.

"All right — wait for me after work tomorrow and we'll buy groceries together. I'll finish early." Yang Shaoyu wanted to go to the supermarket with him.

"Good — I'll call Eldest and Second Brother tomorrow to tell them." Lin Zirui nestled in Yang Shaoyu's arms and pulled the quilt up, watching contentedly.

The next morning, after breakfast, Lin Zirui saw Yang Shaoyu off at the door, and at the elevator urged, "Drive carefully. I'll wait for you to go to the supermarket — I want to sleep at home today." He'd been a little short on sleep these days.

"All right — go back in and sleep. I'll call you at noon to wake you for lunch." Yang Shaoyu reminded him.

"Ding—" The elevator came; Lin Zirui handed Yang Shaoyu his briefcase. "I know — off to work now."

Yang Shaoyu said no more, but suddenly dipped to kiss him. Lin Zirui startled and patted his arm. "We're outside."

"I wanted to kiss you." Yang Shaoyu kissed him again, then stepped into the elevator just before it closed, blowing Lin Zirui a kiss.

Lin Zirui laughed and watched the doors shut. When he turned, however, the neighbor's door stood open and a woman with a handbag was behind him. Remembering Yang Shaoyu's kisses, he flushed. She must have seen everything.

He hurried inside, shut the door, let out a breath. So embarrassing. Better just go sleep.

Lin Zirui slept the whole morning, woken at noon by Yang Shaoyu's call.

"Shaoyu…" he answered groggily.

"Lazy little pig, up you get — I've ordered the dishes you like, they should arrive soon; eat, then sleep more."

"I know — you go eat too."

"All right. Oh — did you make the call?" Afraid he'd slept through it and forgotten.

"What call?" Lin Zirui yawned.

"To have Eldest and Second Brother over tonight." Forgotten, as expected.

"Ah — I forgot." It came back now; he'd meant to that morning but forgotten after Yang Shaoyu kissed him.

"I'll hang up — call after you eat." Just then the doorbell rang; the delivery, probably.

Lin Zirui hung up. Yang Shaoyu was helplessly amused; Zirui had never been this scatterbrained before they got together.

Lin Zirui brought the food in, found his phone, and called the eldest brother.

"Eldest Brother, eaten yet?"

The eldest brother was glad at the call — surely about the meal.

"Your second brother and I are eating now."

Xiao Rui called? The second brother looked at him.

"Oh — let's have dinner together tonight, the one we keep saying we'll have."

"Sure — tonight? Your second brother and I will be there on time."

"Good — I'll text you the address; come after work."

"All right." The eldest brother knew the address, of course — he'd even been there in secret — but didn't dare say so.

Hanging up, Lin Zirui texted the address, then ate.

After, not wanting to sleep again, and remembering Shaoyu saying Jiang Qi had opened a bookshop across from the company, he decided to wait for Shaoyu there at the shop, then go straight to the supermarket — that way there'd be time to buy groceries and get home before the brothers arrived.
