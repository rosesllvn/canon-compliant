Ke Yan's Birthday Gift 柯言的生日礼物

~7 min read

Ke Yan hadn't yet received Jiang Qi's gift, but he did receive someone else's.

Ke Yan was reading documents when Wang Siqi arrived with the gift she had bought a few days earlier and a document for cover. Sensing a show worth watching, Yang Shaoyu stopped Xiao Feng from announcing her and picked up the phone himself. "CEO Ke, Manager Wang has an important document for you."

"Have her come in."

Wang Siqi walked into the office, set the gift on the desk, then handed Ke Yan the document; he took it and looked — nothing important. He glanced at Wang Siqi, and seeing she hadn't left, asked, "Anything else?"

Wang Siqi smiled and gave Ke Yan the gift. "It's your birthday today; this is for you."

"No need." Ke Yan didn't take it.

"Don't stand on ceremony with me; I give gifts to all the colleagues I'm close with on their birthdays, and you're my boss besides." Wang Siqi said it naturally and easily.

Ke Yan glanced at Wang Siqi, then at the gift in her hand...

"Shaoyu, what are you doing?"

Jiang Qi stepped out of the elevator and headed for Ke Yan's office, only to find Yang Shaoyu frozen at his desk, staring through the half-open door like a shameless eavesdropper.

"Hm? I'm — watching..." Yang Shaoyu turned, and his voice cut off short. "...Cough cough, watching nothing."

Yang Shaoyu looked excited, yet visibly struggled to contain himself, as though bursting with something he dared not say.

What on earth was going on? Jiang Qi figured Yang Shaoyu was about to develop a split personality.

Ignoring him, Jiang Qi opened the door—and saw Ke Yan reaching for the bag in Wang Siqi's hand. He froze. What was this?

Puzzled, but composed, Jiang Qi handed Ke Yan the document in his hand; Wang Siqi smiled at Ke Yan and said softly, "I'll get back to work then; I'll treat you to a meal another time."

Wang Siqi walked out, and Jiang Qi narrowed his eyes at Ke Yan. "What's this about?"

Ke Yan picked up the bag set to one side and gave it to Jiang Qi. "She said it's a birthday gift for me."

Jiang Qi opened the bag and looked — it was the very tie he'd had his eye on before, the one Wang Siqi had bought first.

He set the bag on the desk. "Then why's she treating you to a meal too?"

Ke Yan watched the jealous Jiang Qi, finding him terribly cute. "She wants to; I didn't agree."

"Hmph, if you dare agree you're done for." Jiang Qi finished, paused, then added, "And you're not allowed to wear that tie."

"I only wear the ties you give me." Ke Yan stood, pulled Jiang Qi over, and sat him on his lap.

"Hmph, it had better be that way. I'm not talking to you anymore; I'm going down."

"Wait." Ke Yan held Jiang Qi, not letting him go. "Where's my gift, then?"

"You're not getting one. That's what you get for attracting bees and butterflies." Jiang Qi broke free and walked out.

Ke Yan smiled and went back to his documents.

Yang Shaoyu came in from outside and looked at Ke Yan's expressionless face, unable to tell whether he was pleased or angry.

"Ooh, what a lady-killer." Yang Shaoyu picked up the bag on the desk and looked, crowing.

"Like it? Then it's yours. I'm leaving early tonight; you stay and work overtime."

"Huh? I don't want to."

"Don't want to? Then I'll dock this month's pay."

"What? You're going too far." Yang Shaoyu was on the verge of tears.

"Get to work." Ke Yan chased Yang Shaoyu straight out of the office.

Jiang Qi left work early, deciding the unfinished tasks could wait until tomorrow. Today he had something more important to prepare.

Riding home in a cab, Jiang Qi paid the fare; seemed having a license wasn't enough — he'd need to buy a car too.

At home, Jiang Qi showered, wrapped himself in a bathrobe, and went downstairs to check on Auntie Li's progress with dinner.

He had asked Auntie Li to make steak. Now he arranged the room—and made certain the lights would be off. Wearing that outfit was embarrassing enough in the dark.

When it was nearly time, Jiang Qi went upstairs and retrieved the boxed outfit he had hidden in a corner of the wardrobe. He slipped off his bathrobe and changed, then faced the mirror with burning cheeks. These clothes were utterly mortifying!

Downstairs, he took out the candles and lit them, then turned off the lights and waited for Ke Yan.

Ke Yan knew Jiang Qi had come home early and looked forward to his surprise. When he opened the door on a darkened room lit by only a few candles, he raised an eyebrow. A surprise indeed.

"Jiang Qi?" Ke Yan called as he walked in.

In the kitchen, Jiang Qi was pouring red wine. The candlelight revealed little at first. Then Ke Yan stepped closer, realized Jiang Qi wore no trousers, and nearly suffered a nosebleed.

Jiang Qi wore something like a white shirt—long enough to cover his backside, but nearly transparent. Ke Yan's gaze traveled down the sheer fabric, his eyes heating at the pert curves visible beneath it.

Jiang Qi, his back to Ke Yan pouring wine, felt Ke Yan's scorching gaze, his hand trembling a little — not from excitement, but from shyness.

Wine poured, he turned around, and seeing Ke Yan's eyes fixed unblinkingly on him, Jiang Qi nearly couldn't hold the glass steady.

Ke Yan stared at the faintly red points on Jiang Qi's chest, then lower, where the shifting hem half revealed and half concealed him. As Jiang Qi set down the glass and approached, Ke Yan wanted nothing more than to strip off the shirt and press him down.

Thinking this, Ke Yan pulled Jiang Qi into his arms, his hand sliding down to knead Jiang Qi's backside a few times, a bit hard, so that Jiang Qi nearly let out an "mm."

When Ke Yan's hand slipped beneath the shirt, Jiang Qi pushed at him. Ke Yan silenced him with one kiss, then another, drawing Jiang Qi against his arousal. Jiang Qi immediately broke free. "Eat first."

Red-faced, Jiang Qi sat down; Ke Yan looked at him and sat down too.

"Can you stop staring at me." Jiang Qi kept feeling Ke Yan's eyes on him.

"Dressed like that, how could I not look." Ke Yan gave a rakish smile.

Jiang Qi took a sip of wine to cover his embarrassment.

Ke Yan held back his desire and finished eating, then drained the wine in his glass in one go.

He scooped up Jiang Qi and carried him upstairs. In the bedroom, Ke Yan switched on the light, exposing every detail to his gaze.

Ke Yan's breathing grew heavier; he pressed Jiang Qi onto the bed and slowly took in Jiang Qi's body, all but unclothed.

Jiang Qi's hands trembled a little as he slowly undid the buttons of his shirt... but his hands shook too much to manage them. Seeing this, Ke Yan slowly undid the buttons one by one.

He drew the shirt off Jiang Qi's body...

A long while later, the Jiang Qi who'd been taken who-knows-how-many times by Ke Yan was washed clean and lay on the bed in Ke Yan's arms; Jiang Qi said softly, "Happy birthday."

Ke Yan stroked Jiang Qi's face. "I love this birthday gift."

Jiang Qi rolled his eyes. "You got your satisfaction; my back's sore."

Ke Yan smiled and kneaded Jiang Qi's back for him. "Wear this for me again next time."

"No, you beast."

"Calling me a beast? I'm only a beast for you, and you can only wear it for me to see."

Jiang Qi's face went a little red; he said quietly, "All right, I'll wear it again next time."

Ke Yan heard, lowered his head, and kissed Jiang Qi.
