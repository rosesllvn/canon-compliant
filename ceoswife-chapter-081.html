Chapter 81

Spanking Jiang Qi 打江奇

July 2026

~8 min read

Ke Yan woke to find Jiang Qi slumped at the foot of the bed; startled, he went over and helped him up. "Jiang Qi, what's wrong?"

"Nothing, nothing — just the toilet."

"The toilet, ending up like this?" Ke Yan saw Jiang Qi's color was a bit off.

"Just — just a touch of diarrhea." Jiang Qi didn't dare meet Ke Yan's eyes.

"How much barbecue did you eat tonight, and how much cold beer did you drink?" Ke Yan asked, watching Jiang Qi expressionlessly.

"Only a dozen skewers, and I barely drank any beer. It had probably just been too long, so I got diarrhea." Jiang Qi answered guiltily. In fact, he had eaten more than twenty hot, spicy skewers and washed them down with cold beer. It would have been surprising if he hadn't gotten sick—but he dared not admit that. Ke Yan would spank him.

"Didn't I tell you to eat less?" Ke Yan said, going to fetch Jiang Qi medicine.

Jiang Qi lay on his stomach waiting for Ke Yan to bring it.

Ke Yan tipped out two small black pills for Jiang Qi; Jiang Qi sniffed them — the black pills gave off a foul smell of who-knows-what — took the warm water Ke Yan had poured, and washed the medicine down.

Medicine taken, Jiang Qi lay on the bed, thinking he wouldn't have diarrhea anymore.

Ke Yan lay down too, looked at the nearly-asleep Jiang Qi beside him, reached out, and gave him a hard pinch.

Jiang Qi, just about to drop off, was promptly woken by the pain. But though it hurt, he didn't dare make a sound; he hugged Ke Yan, who reached out and kneaded the spot again.

Jiang Qi burrowed his head into Ke Yan's chest.

The next day, after breakfast, Jiang Qi went to work; in the car he rubbed his stomach — it felt a bit off.

At work he felt worse, a bit of pain — tsk, if he'd known he wouldn't have had the barbecue and beer; his stomach hadn't hurt in so long, and now it was acting up again.

Jiang Qi opened a drawer to look for stomach medicine but found none, because his stomach hadn't hurt in ages; after finishing the last of it last time he hadn't bought more.

Enduring the stomachache, he got through the morning's work, thinking he'd go buy stomach medicine after lunch.

Jiang Qi bought stomach medicine from a pharmacy outside, and coming back into the company lobby he saw Ke Yan; he was about to go over and honestly confess his stomachache — but before he reached him, a woman suddenly came over and took Ke Yan's arm.

Jiang Qi frowned, though he didn't think much of it; but after a moment, when Ke Yan still hadn't shaken the woman's hand off and let her keep hold of his arm, Jiang Qi grew puzzled — why hadn't he shaken her off?

Seeing them about to step into the elevator, Jiang Qi quickly walked over; getting in, he saw the woman was no longer holding Ke Yan's arm. Jiang Qi looked at Ke Yan, but Ke Yan didn't look at him, still talking with the woman.

From their conversation, the woman seemed to be the Sun Nianqing Ke Yan had told him about yesterday.

Looked like Ke Yan really did want to recruit her this time, or he wouldn't be standing here making small talk with Sun Nianqing.

The elevator arrived; Jiang Qi stepped out, while Ke Yan and Sun Nianqing were probably headed to Ke Yan's office.

Jiang Qi went back to his office with the stomach medicine, only to find his stomach seemed to have stopped hurting; since it didn't hurt, he couldn't be bothered to take the medicine. He'd thought of going to find Ke Yan, but figured Ke Yan was busy now, so he started work early instead.

Because Jiang Qi had a business trip in a couple of days, there was a fair amount of work these next two days preparing documents and such; once busy he worked till dark, and only noticed his shoulders aching when he checked the time — past nine. Jiang Qi decided to do the rest tomorrow.

Because he'd told Ke Yan he'd be working late tonight, only Jiang Qi went home tonight; a little hungry, but with no appetite, he skipped dinner, and once home just showered and got ready for bed.

Lying in bed, Jiang Qi felt he'd forgotten something — right, since coming home he hadn't said a word to Ke Yan; why hadn't Ke Yan asked whether he'd eaten today? Thinking this, Jiang Qi then felt he wasn't a child anymore, and Ke Yan not asking was nothing — only... his heart couldn't help a touch of letdown.

Jiang Qi felt a bit irritable, tossing and turning, unable to sleep.

Jiang Qi hadn't fallen asleep, but his eyes were indeed shut; when Ke Yan set down his documents and looked at Jiang Qi, he thought he was asleep, and kissed Jiang Qi's mouth — he hadn't said much to Jiang Qi today; how had he gone to sleep so early tonight.

Jiang Qi, just working up some drowsiness, was suddenly kissed by Ke Yan; his heart sped up a couple of beats, and his irritable mood calmed, drowsiness returning.

Unfortunately, Jiang Qi had barely fallen asleep when he woke again — stomachache. He rather regretted skipping dinner; now look, it hurt like mad. And the stomach medicine was at the office, not brought home, and he didn't know whether there was any at home.

Moving sluggishly, he sat up without turning on the light, lay across the edge of the bed, and used his phone for light, opening the drawer to look for stomach medicine.

Ke Yan was woken by Jiang Qi; he turned on the light and saw Jiang Qi, face pale, searching for something.

"What is it?" Ke Yan quickly steadied Jiang Qi.

"Stomachache."

Hearing it was a stomachache, Ke Yan frowned. "How'd it suddenly start hurting?" Jiang Qi's stomach hadn't hurt in a long time, and Ke Yan had thought it was much better.

"Probably from eating too much spicy food." Jiang Qi answered reflexively, then realized he'd let it slip.

Hearing this, Ke Yan got out of bed with a darkened face to fetch stomach medicine; luckily, afraid Jiang Qi's stomach might hurt again, he'd kept some on hand. Though it seemed it had been a while since he'd "educated" Jiang Qi — actually keeping it from him.

He saw Jiang Qi take the medicine, had him drink another glass of warm water, then had Jiang Qi lean against him and reached out to knead his stomach.

Ke Yan's hands were very warm; kneading like this always worked — in a while it stopped hurting, and Jiang Qi made comfortable little noises.

After kneading a while longer, Ke Yan asked, "How is it now?"

Jiang Qi nodded. "Much better, doesn't hurt."

"Is that so?" Ke Yan suddenly said in a soft voice.

Jiang Qi's neck shrank in; Ke Yan was very angry now, and Jiang Qi hadn't yet worked out how to handle him.

Suddenly his whole body was flipped over by Ke Yan, so that Jiang Qi lay across Ke Yan's lap.

Ke Yan, quick of eye and hand, stopped Jiang Qi from getting up, yanked his trousers down in one motion, and gave him a hard slap.

Jiang Qi was stunned, and tried to break free; seeing this, Ke Yan delivered two more slaps, very hard, so that his backside went a little red at once; Jiang Qi was furious, and a bit wronged too.

"Scoun— scoundrel Ke Yan, let go of me."

At that Ke Yan slapped several more times, hard. "Tell me — exactly how much barbecue did you eat, and spicy too? And spicy food with liquor?"

Jiang Qi wouldn't answer; unable to break free, he lay across Ke Yan's lap and simply refused to answer — what right did he have to hit him.

"What? Still won't answer?" His stomach was clearly bad, and he still wouldn't take care of himself; Ke Yan hadn't been this angry with Jiang Qi in a long time. Then something occurred to him, and he asked, "Did it start hurting during the day today? And you skipped dinner too?"

A surge of grievance rose in Jiang Qi; he cursed, "Scoundrel, I wanted to tell you today, but all you cared about was that pretty woman; you didn't even glance at me."

Ke Yan was startled — what pretty woman — then remembered.

He found it a little funny, and helped Jiang Qi up; seeing Jiang Qi's eyes had actually gone a bit red, and remembering he'd just been too heavy-handed, his heart ached at once, not a trace left of the fierceness with which he'd spanked Jiang Qi.

He kissed Jiang Qi. "Wasn't I afraid you'd wreck your health and suffer for it? All right, I was too rough just now — hit me back."

Jiang Qi shifted his backside awkwardly. "You scoundrel, hitting that hard."

Ke Yan looked at Jiang Qi's backside — red, all red. He couldn't help laughing. "It really is red."

Jiang Qi was provoked by Ke Yan's laughter; he pinned Ke Yan to the bed and pinched his hand hard.

Pinched, Ke Yan only laughed more merrily. "All right, will you do it again?"

"Hmph." Jiang Qi gave a snort, then said quietly, "I won't."

Hearing the answer, Ke Yan satisfiedly helped Jiang Qi pull his trousers back up.

In Ke Yan's arms, Jiang Qi screwed up his face — his behind hurt.
