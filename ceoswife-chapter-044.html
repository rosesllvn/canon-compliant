The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 44

Was It a Misunderstanding? 误会了没？

July 2026

~5 min read

At the Jiang family table, Jiang Qi's mom heaped food furiously into Jiang Qi's and Jiang Lü's bowls. Jiang Bu watched and shook his head — why isn't she giving me any? — when his wife Yun Hui picked up a piece for him; he ate it quickly. Nothing beats my own wife.

Jiang Lü stuffed her face as fast as she could — it was all so good; Jiang Qi ate just as eagerly, since his mom had made several spicy dishes for him today, and Jiang Lü got to ride his coattails.

"Second brother, how come I haven't seen the CEO at the company these past two days?" she said, swallowing her last bite.

"He's away on a trip. You didn't know?"

"I only just started."

"How are you getting on with your colleagues?"

"All right — I get on well with the few who started the same time as me, less so with the rest; some can't be bothered with you."

"Get along with them," Jiang Qi said.

"Mm. Second brother, is the CEO really that handsome? I keep hearing about it — I'd love to see him."

"Aren't there financial magazines?" Jiang Bu put in.

"He doesn't do magazines." Jiang Qi shook his head.

"Second brother, just answer me."

"Him? Truly very handsome." Jiang Qi nodded and nodded.

Jiang Bu frowned. "Xiao Qi, how can you go around calling another man handsome — what man these days admits another's better-looking than himself?"

"How is there no such man — you're behind the times."

"Wife, is this how you talk about your husband?"

Their mom laughed. "Serves you right." Their dad nodded too.

The family was warm and merry, and Jiang Qi missed Ke Yan a little — how nice it would be if he were here too.

After dinner the talk turned to a check-up his parents had the next day; Jiang Bu and Yun Hui both had things on, so Jiang Qi offered to take leave and bring them. "How could we let you go on your own? People who didn't know would think we two weren't filial to you," Jiang Bu's line successfully shut their mom up. And so the next day Jiang Qi took leave and brought his parents to the hospital, getting through every test and finally finishing a little past two — luckily not too crowded today.

As he was dropping his parents home, Jiang Lü called. "Second brother — treat me to dinner tonight, you promised, you agreed."

"Mm," Jiang Qi said, all right.

Evening came quickly. At the company, a front-desk girl happened to look up and saw Ke Yan getting out of his car at the entrance, and couldn't help raising her voice: "The CEO's back." At which the somewhat slouching front-desk girls hurriedly smoothed their clothes, stood, and chorused, "Good day, CEO." Ke Yan gave an "mm," and they all but frothed over — pink froth, at that.

In the office, Yang Shaoyu looked at him grievously. "I'm telling you, great CEO Ke, why is it that every time I walk beside you the beauties automatically overlook me?"

"Quit griping, go work."

"Aren't you going to see Jiang Qi? Isn't there a saying — a day apart feels like three autumns? How many days has it been?"

Ke Yan opened a file. "You're right — I'm heading back early tonight. You stay and work overtime; have tomorrow's meeting materials ready."

"No way — don't, please. Tomorrow's just as good for seeing him."

"Out." Ke Yan ignored him and threw himself into his work.

Yang Shaoyu sighed. Why's my mouth so big — overtime again, by the look of it.

Working through the backlog, Ke Yan glanced at his phone — he really did want to see Jiang Qi.

Click— the door opened again. Yang Shaoyu had come in without knocking and barreled up to the desk. "Last night Jiang Qi hooked up with some beauty who just joined the company."

Ke Yan narrowed his eyes. Seeing it, Yang Shaoyu hastily went "pah, pah, pah, pah." "Listen to me talk nonsense — last night Jiang Qi went off in a taxi with some beauty; word is they probably went home together."

"Who told you that?"

Yang Shaoyu noticed the voice had gone cold, and prayed inwardly that Jiang Qi had absolutely better not have done such a thing. "I heard it from people in the company."

"Tell Jiang Qi to come up."

"Jiang Qi took leave today." At this not only Ke Yan's voice but his face went cold, cold air radiating off his whole person. Yang Shaoyu swallowed, and was about to speak when Ke Yan stood and picked up his coat. "I'm leaving. You stay and work overtime."

Ke Yan drove out of the garage and called Jiang Qi; it rang barely twice. "Ke Yan? What is it?"

"I'm back."

"Really? Where are you now?" Jiang Qi said, delighted.

"At the company." Hearing his voice, Ke Yan's own went soft. "Where are you?"

"I'm out — I'll head back once I've had dinner."

"Eating alone?"

"No, with Xiao Lü — my treat."

"...Xiao Lü?" Just then what Ke Yan most wanted to do was give Jiang Qi a good spanking.

"Mm, yeah — Jiang Lü." Realizing Ke Yan might not know "Xiao Lü," he said the full name.

Jiang Lü? What a familiar name. After hanging up Ke Yan thought about it, couldn't place it, and drove slowly home.

By the time Jiang Qi had finished, seen Jiang Lü home, and gotten back, it was late. The moment he stepped in Ke Yan caught him in his arms, pressed him onto the sofa, and asked, "Who's Jiang Lü?"

Jiang Qi blinked, baffled. "My sister. Why?"

This time it was Ke Yan's turn to blink — that's why the name felt so familiar.

"What is it?" Jiang Qi pressed. "Why won't you say?"

"It's nothing." Ke Yan undid his clothes.

"Is that so?"

"Mm."
