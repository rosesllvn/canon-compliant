Chapter 45

Falling Asleep in the Meeting 开会睡着了

July 2026

~5 min read

The next day at the company Jiang Qi was uncomfortable all over — voice hoarse, back aching, even his behind sore — and worst of all, his spirits were sapped. Shouldn't have indulged like that, he thought, slumped over his desk.

A knock; Li Jing came in, set a file down, and reminded him, "The meeting's about to start."

"I know." He took out the file he'd prepared and headed for the door — he'd nearly forgotten the time. Li Jing looked at him strangely — why's Manager Jiang walking so oddly today? — then shook her head. Outside, Xiao An, seeing the odd look on Li Jing's face, asked what it was. "It's nothing — just, Manager Jiang's walking a bit strangely." A perfectly ordinary remark — but it made Xiao An's eyes flash green.

Jiang Qi rode up, and the elevator stopped at the twentieth floor; the doors opened on Manager Lin's sallow, waxen face, which gave him a fright. "Manager Lin, are you all right?"

Lin Zhengrui stepped in and answered weakly, "I'm fine — just diarrhea. Bad diarrhea."

"Have you taken anything?"

"I have. Didn't help."

Looking at that waxen face, Jiang Qi couldn't help a pang of sympathy. Manager Lin, you've had it hard. Take care of yourself. As the elevator arrived he yawned. So sleepy — hope I don't doze off in there.

Everyone filed in; coffee was already set out, and Jiang Qi took a sip and rubbed between his brows, hoping to clear his head. After a while Ke Yan arrived and the meeting began. At first Jiang Qi listened attentively, but before long his eyelids drooped against his will, his head dipping lower and lower, until at last his eyes shut and he was asleep.

The first to notice was Ke Yan. Presentations generally went in order; as it neared Jiang Qi's turn, Ke Yan spoke: "Manager Lin." Those three words turned Lin's already-waxen face still worse. Trembling, he reported his proposal — why so badly today? Because he knew there were holes in it. Finishing, he looked up, only to find Ke Yan seemingly not listening but looking off to the side. He was about to follow the gaze when Ke Yan spoke: "Mm. Next."

That's it? Lin sat back, delighted, his color improving dramatically — though he couldn't stop wondering where Ke Yan had been looking.

As the presentations wound down and it seemed the meeting was about to end, Wang Siqi suddenly spoke up: "Manager Jiang hasn't presented yet. Aiya, how has Manager Jiang fallen asleep — we're in a meeting."

Ke Yan couldn't help shooting her a glance, a flash of distaste. Had she known the thought behind it, she'd surely have regretted speaking.

Everyone turned in unison to look at Jiang Qi, still sleeping sweetly, head propped on one hand. The person beside him shook him awake; he came to, looked blankly around, then remembered where he was, stood, and said, "My apologies."

Ke Yan picked up his file and rose. "Manager Jiang, come to my office."

"All right."

Everyone watched him walk out, some with pity, some with the glee of watching another's misfortune, some with genuine sympathy — Manager Lin for one, muttering, "Brother Jiang, do take care of yourself," with a look as if Jiang Qi would be swallowed alive the moment he reached the office.

Inside, Jiang Qi dropped onto the sofa and rapped his own head — how did I fall asleep in a meeting? — but this time missed, his hand caught by Ke Yan, who handed him a cup of honey water. "Drink it, then go sleep a while."

He took a sip. "You're not going to scold me?"

Ke Yan raised an eyebrow. "Why would I? So you fell asleep. What of it."

Jiang Qi looked at him. So — actually, having a boss for a boyfriend is a wonderful thing. Draining the cup, he asked, "Where'd you get honey water?"

"Some honey I had Xiao Feng buy a while back." Xiao Feng was another of Ke Yan's secretaries, her family in the countryside, where they produced honey.

"I don't remember you liking honey water."

"Mm."

"I like it?"

"Mm."

"Ke Yan, you..."

"All right, off to sleep with you." Ke Yan, are you embarrassed? Jiang Qi wanted to say.

Ke Yan led him into the rest room, took off his jacket and shirt, and changed him into the pajamas kept there — his own, though he hardly ever wore them, since he seldom slept at the office. After all these years Jiang Qi's frame had filled out a little, but Ke Yan's clothes were still always a touch too big on him — sleeves long, trousers long — though at least the shoulders no longer slipped off the way they once had. Tucked under the quilt, he had a lovely sleep.

When he woke it was near evening. How did I sleep so long — though it was a good sleep. Out of the room, he found Ke Yan still at his desk reading files, came up behind him and leaned against his back. "Ke Yan, good thing you don't need glasses even working this hard."

Ke Yan set down his pen and pulled him around onto his lap. "Hungry?" Jiang Qi nodded; Ke Yan phoned Yang Shaoyu to bring lunch in. When Yang Shaoyu came he was grumbling — it's nearly dinnertime and you're still having me bring lunch.

After eating, Jiang Qi rode down alone; partway it stopped, and he ran into Manager Lin again, who patted his shoulder. "Brother Jiang, are you all right? You definitely got chewed out by CEO Ke today — don't take it to heart; falling asleep in a meeting was your fault too."

"It's fine. I'm fine..." I didn't get chewed out — and if I had, your consoling me like this would only make me want to cry.

"Look at me — chewed out nearly every day and I'm fine. By tomorrow no one will even remember." Hearing those last words, Jiang Qi was genuinely touched. Manager Lin really is a decent sort.

Off the elevator he ran into Jiang Lü. "Second brother, I heard you got called to the CEO's office after the meeting? And that you fell asleep in there?"

Does the whole company know now? Jiang Qi thought, mortified. Having reassured her, he walked back to his office and hurried to finish the day's work.
