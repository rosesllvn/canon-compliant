Chapter 144

Dependence 依赖

July 2026

~6 min read

Too embarrassed, Jiang Qi didn't call Ke Yan all afternoon.

At quitting time he was too sheepish to take the regular elevator, so he took Ke Yan's private one to the lot.

Ke Yan was already waiting in the car. The moment Jiang Qi got in he asked, "How did Xiao Feng find out?"

"You forgot already? When he came to deliver documents."

Jiang Qi remembered Xiao Feng bringing papers to the flat and discovering them. He smacked his forehead. "How did I forget that completely? My memory's been awful lately. Maybe I need something for my brain."

Ke Yan flicked him on the head.

Then started the car.

"What's good for the brain? Walnuts?" Jiang Qi was still on it.

"No need — a bit dim is better." Ke Yan couldn't resist teasing.

"I'm like this because with you I don't have to worry about anything, don't have to fret, so the unimportant things just slip my mind." Jiang Qi fretted at forgetting something so important — but then, "Ke Yan, did you forget too?"

Ke Yan glanced at him, not a trace of awkwardness. "I did, actually."

"You forgot too — so I'm not dim after all. Though you remembered before I did; at bottom, I'm too dependent." Jiang Qi reflected, then suddenly wanted to try not depending on Ke Yan, to see how it went. "Tonight I'm sleeping alone."

"Why?"

"Because I depend on you too much. And tomorrow I'm going to work on my own too."

"No."

"Why?" Jiang Qi protested.

"I can't sleep without you, and I don't sleep well. And don't you get cold at night?" Ke Yan had no wish to sleep apart.

"It's decided. The weather isn't that cold lately anyway — heating on, hand-warmer, I won't be cold. And I don't want to depend on you so much."

"What's wrong with depending on me?" Ke Yan didn't understand.

"It's fine, but depending on you all the time doesn't sit right — and I want to be someone you can depend on sometimes too." Jiang Qi didn't want it to run one way; he wanted, now and then, to be the one Ke Yan could lean on when tired.

"…" Ke Yan was silent. He understood Jiang Qi's heart — but he'd far rather Jiang Qi simply depend on him.

Neither said more, and they went home like that.

It had been a while since the two had felt this close to a cold war. Even so, Ke Yan wrapped Jiang Qi's forgotten scarf around him when they left the car, and Jiang Qi still laid out Ke Yan's clothes at shower time.

Still, at bedtime Jiang Qi took his pillow, set on the guest room.

Ke Yan stopped him. "I'll sleep in the guest room."

"No, I will." Jiang Qi insisted.

Ke Yan let go and watched him leave, then lay on the bed. What were they even fighting about? Not a fight, really — just different ideas.

He sighed. Dependence — he'd never given the word thought, but Jiang Qi seemed to mind it.

Jiang Qi minded, so Ke Yan settled in to think it over properly.

In the guest room, Jiang Qi set his pillow on the bed, turned on the heating, charged the hand-warmer.

Sitting there alone, he felt lonely. He turned on the television, suddenly unsure what he and Ke Yan were fighting about—not a fight so much as Jiang Qi sulking.

Once the hand-warmer had charged, he lay down. Even wrapped tightly in the quilt, he felt cold. The space beside him was painfully empty; he missed Ke Yan's warmth, his scent, his arms, his tenderness.

He really didn't know what had gotten into his head, sulking at Ke Yan like this.

Thinking it, Jiang Qi drifted into a hazy sleep — but never quite comfortable.

Ke Yan couldn't sleep at all, turning the word "dependence" over and over. He rolled over and, by habit, reached to pull Jiang Qi in — and found only empty space.

Jiang Qi was in the guest room. Without him beside him it wasn't comfortable at all.

In the dead of night, the restlessly sleeping Jiang Qi suddenly felt warmth, an arm tightening at his waist, his whole self going warm and comfortable — and he seemed to catch Ke Yan's scent.

He slept well the rest of the night.

But waking in the morning, the first thing he saw was Ke Yan's pajamas before his eyes; looking up, Ke Yan's handsome face.

No wonder he'd slept so well — Ke Yan had come in the night to hold him.

He touched Ke Yan's face, regretting the inexplicable sulk. Maybe he did depend on Ke Yan too much — but as Ke Yan had said, what was wrong with that? Jiang Qi liked depending on Ke Yan after all.

He kissed Ke Yan's chin, and managed to wake him.

"Awake?"

Hearing Ke Yan's habitual morning rasp, Jiang Qi owned his fault. "I was being unreasonable yesterday. I'm sorry."

"It's not your fault. I thought about it a long while too. The truth is, I've always depended on you — depended on you to knot my tie, lay out my clothes, on holding you to sleep at night and wanting to hold you when I wake. With you beside me when I'm tired, I'm filled with energy again."

"Mm, I understand now — and I think there's nothing wrong with depending on you; it feels like happiness, having someone to lean on." Jiang Qi nuzzled into his chest.

Ke Yan kissed his forehead.

They lay holding each other — then Jiang Qi suddenly pinched the flesh at Ke Yan's waist. "Why did you kiss me in the elevator yesterday, and get us seen, too?" The thought still mortified him.

"Couldn't help myself." Ke Yan sucked in a breath; Jiang Qi pinched hard.

"Hmph. Today at the company, keep your distance from me."

"No need for that."

Jiang Qi pinched harder. "Until everyone who saw forgets about it — until then, don't come near me at the company." Then let go.

"In that case, a little compensation at home isn't too much to ask." Ke Yan made to peel off Jiang Qi's pajamas.

"No — it's morning, we'll be late for work." Jiang Qi clutched at them.

"It's fine. The whole company knows you're the CEO's wife now; who'd dare say anything if you're late? You're about to resign anyway — enjoy a little CEO's-wife privilege."

"Even if I wanted to enjoy it, I don't want to do it in the morning." Jiang Qi held out — but was soon stripped bare by Ke Yan, kissed everywhere.

And so Jiang Qi got some "vigorous exercise" first thing in the morning. By the time they left for work, they were already late; Jiang Qi was limp and aching all over and, having changed his mind, rode in Ke Yan's car.
