Yang's Little Brother Is Coming Home 杨家小弟要回来了

July 2026

~8 min read

The next day, the moment Jiang Qi woke he saw Xiao Ke beside him, kissed the baby's cheek, and got up.

He made a simple breakfast. By the time Ke Yan rose, Xiao Ke was awake as well, so Jiang Qi changed his diaper and dressed him.

After mixing Xiao Ke another bottle, he and Ke Yan had breakfast together.

Today Xiao Ke would spend the whole day with Jiang Qi at the bookshop; Ke Shao would come collect him in the afternoon.

So before going out Jiang Qi packed all of Xiao Ke's things, including the plush dog they'd bought yesterday.

Xiao Ke was a good baby, playing quietly by himself at the shop. Whenever Jiang Qi wasn't busy, he spent every spare moment doting on him.

Then Jiang Qi's phone rang — Mother Jiang calling.

"Xiao Qi — are you coming home for the New Year this year?"

"Of course I'm coming home for the New Year — don't I every year?"

"No—I thought you might go to Ke Yan's for the New Year this year." In Mother Jiang's mind, her son had become rather like a daughter-in-law. Wouldn't he be expected to spend the holiday with Ke Yan's family?

"Oh — Ke Yan and I have it settled: New Year at our own families, then on the first day we go to Ke Yan's family to pay our respects."

"That's good, then — I'll let you go, I'm making New Year cakes today."

"All right, you go on." Jiang Qi hung up.

Before he and Ke Yan had told their families of their relationship, at New Years past they couldn't spend it together, and even a phone call had to be made on the sly; this year, at last, he could go pay a proper New Year's visit.

At noon Jiang Qi ate with Ke Yan, while the banquet-hall kitchen prepared a suitable lunch for Xiao Ke.

That afternoon Jiang Qi napped with Xiao Ke on the third floor of the bookshop; by the time they woke together it was past three.

Carrying Xiao Ke down, he saw Ke Shao seated downstairs flipping through a magazine.

"Sis — when did you get here?" Jiang Qi went over and handed Xiao Ke to her.

"Just now, not long ago." The moment Ke Shao took Xiao Ke she kissed him. "You two have taken wonderful care of him."

"Are you leaving already?" Jiang Qi felt a little reluctant to part with Xiao Ke.

"Mm — once the New Year's over and I come back to visit, you can see Xiao Ke again. And isn't my sister-in-law about to have one too? Soon you'll have your own to dote on." Ke Shao caught Jiang Qi's reluctance, but today she'd just gotten back from the hot springs and wanted to see Xiao Ke; now it was time to go.

"Then hurry home, careful on the road." Jiang Qi helped Ke Shao carry Xiao Ke's bag and the newly-bought plush dog out to the car outside the shop.

"You even bought him a toy? I'll be off, then — home for the New Year." Ke Shao, delighted Jiang Qi had bought Xiao Ke something.

"All right — visit again. Bye, Xiao Ke." Jiang Qi said goodbye to Ke Shao and the baby.

Once the car had driven off, Jiang Qi called Ke Yan. "Sis took Xiao Ke home — you said Xiao Ke would call me shushu soon, but he still hasn't."

"Ah — don't worry, I'll call over tonight and he'll be calling you shushu." Ke Yan gave a low laugh, reassuring him.

"Really?"

"Really." Ke Yan wasn't sure either, but said it anyway.

"Then you remember to." Jiang Qi made him promise before hanging up.

Ke Yan hung up and called Ke Shao. "Sis, a favor."

"What is it? You're awfully sweet-spoken today, calling me 'Sis' — what favor?" Her brother being nice to her, Ke Shao sensed something off.

"You teach Xiao Ke to say 'shushu' — tonight he has to manage it."

"Shushu? For Jiang Qi's sake? It took me days to teach him before. I can't promise he'll learn it in one evening."

"You don't have to pull it off — Xiao Ke managing is enough. Jiang Qi wants to hear Xiao Ke call him shushu; I'm counting on you. I'll hang up now."

Whether or not his sister could get Xiao Ke to say shushu, Ke Yan finished work and went to the bookshop to fetch Jiang Qi.

Jiang Qi was just locking up, and got in the car.

"Ke Yan, I've been thinking — that short a time, getting Xiao Ke to call me shushu seems impossible; let's wait until next time he visits and I'll teach him myself."

"Is it that you couldn't manage with Xiao Ke today?" Ke Yan, not entirely sure whether Xiao Ke would say shushu, held off committing until it was certain.

"Mm — but next time he visits I'll get to see him."

"All right — and once we marry, the little one can come to us too." Ke Yan meant the matter of raising a child.

"Really? Then when do we marry?" Jiang Qi was a touch impatient.

"In a hurry? After the New Year." The rest Ke Yan didn't say much of; Jiang Qi knew he wanted to surprise him, and didn't press.

After dinner, while Jiang Qi washed up, Ke Yan called Ke Shao. "Sis, how did it go?"

"Xiao Ke's clever this time — he'll say shushu now, though I don't know if he'll say it on cue; sometimes if he's in a mood he won't."

"Can you get him to say one for me to hear?"

"I'll try." The line carried the sound of Ke Shao coaxing Xiao Ke. "Xiao Ke, come on, say shushu, shushu."

But for half the day Xiao Ke wouldn't say a single shushu.

"Who are you on the phone with?" Jiang Qi came out from washing. "Is it Sis?" he asked eagerly.

"Mm, hang on." Ke Yan put the phone on speaker; Jiang Qi came to his side and called into it, "Xiao Ke?"

"Ahh. Ahh." On the other end Xiao Ke cooed twice, excited.

Ke Shao said softly at Xiao Ke's ear, "Say shushu."

"Shoo-shoo?" Xiao Ke said it, not quite right, twice.

"Xiao Ke said shushu? Xiao Ke's so clever." Jiang Qi laughed, delighted, laughing high and happy.

After a few more words with Xiao Ke, they hung up; Jiang Qi hugged Ke Yan. "That really did feel good."

Meanwhile, on Yang Shaoyu's side, he'd gotten a call from Mother Yang. "Shaoyu, your little brother's flying back tomorrow afternoon. We'll have dinner at a restaurant tomorrow night — are you free? Family meal."

Yang Shaoyu was free, of course.

The moment he hung up he told Lin Zirui.

Lin Zirui blinked. "Of course you're free — then I'll swap shifts with someone for the afternoon and finish early tomorrow."

"Good — I'll come get you tomorrow."

The two settled it, and just then the second Lin brother called to ask whether Xiao Rui was free tomorrow for a meal.

The company was at its busiest before the holiday, but tomorrow evening happened to be relatively free, so the Lin brothers had hoped to have dinner with him.

But tomorrow evening Lin Zirui wasn't free. "Tomorrow night I'm having dinner with Shaoyu's family; the night after, I'd like to have Eldest and Second Brother to dinner."

"That works — but where are you eating?"

"The hotel the Yang family usually goes to." Yang Shaoyu had told him the name earlier.

"All right, I see." The second Lin brother hung up, just as the eldest Lin brother, driving, happened to be near home; since the call had been on speaker, the eldest had heard it all.

Stepping in the door, they found their parents on the sofa watching TV — lately the Lin parents often stayed up late watching TV in the living room. Lin Le no longer pestered them; once dinner was done she'd go to her room.

The second brother didn't understand why they wouldn't go rest in their room, staying up so late in the living room instead.

The eldest brother had guessed why their parents lingered in the living room, though he wasn't certain. Remembering the call, he pretended to speak casually to his brother. "Xiao Rui said he's going to the Yang family's usual hotel tomorrow, didn't he? We should eat there sometime too."

"Oh—sure." The second brother caught on and played along.

The Lin parents' ears were sharp; the eldest brother wasn't sure whether they were as he'd guessed.

Father Lin went into the room, saw Mother Lin sitting there lost in thought, and asked, "Are you going tomorrow?"

"I don't know."

Father Lin fell silent; like Mother Lin, on the one hand he felt awkward about Lin Zirui loving a man and didn't want to see him, on the other he'd lately been unable to stop thinking of Lin Zirui, wondering how he was now.

"Let's go see, then," Father Lin decided. "Just a look — we'll eat while we're there."

"All right." Mother Lin agreed, opening the wardrobe to pick out clothes for tomorrow.
