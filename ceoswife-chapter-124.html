The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 124

Telling Them 说了

July 2026

~5 min read

While they played, Jiang Qi went to the kitchen and "brewed" two cups of tea—meaning he added leaves, rinsed them once with boiling water, and steeped them again.

The Jiang house had a tea set, though only Father Jiang used it now and then.

He set the tea beside them and watched the game, but soon drifted off to the television, bored.

Before long they finished a round — Father Jiang the winner, naturally.

He sipped his tea. "You haven't been playing long, have you."

"No — I only started a couple of days ago." Ke Yan was surprised he'd seen it at a glance, since he hadn't lost too badly.

"What — really? I'd assumed a month or two. I truly didn't expect such quick instincts in someone so young."

"You flatter me. I'd welcome a few pointers, Uncle."

"All right, let's go again."

They played two more rounds. By then, Mother Jiang and her eldest daughter-in-law had covered the table with dishes whose aroma awakened Jiang Qi's appetite.

Jiang Lü came out of her room to eat, and at the sight of the spread declared herself in luck today.

Everyone sat. Jiang Qi's eldest brother and his son weren't home — the brother away on business, the child off at his maternal grandmother's for the weekend.

"How does it taste?" Mother Jiang asked Ke Yan.

"Wonderful — really delicious." Ke Yan ate with relish, unable to help wondering how Jiang Qi's cooking could be what it was.

"Then eat plenty." She put a piece of meat in his bowl.

"Yes — don't stand on ceremony," said Yun Hui.

"I won't, I promise."

Jiang Qi ate fast; it had been so long since he'd had his mother's cooking, and it really was good.

Once full, they left the bowls in the kitchen, and Mother Jiang cut fruit and poured tea, and they sat on the sofa to talk.

Warming her hands on the teacup, she smiled. "Ke Yan, have you ever had Jiang Qi's cooking?"

"I have."

"Terrible, isn't it."

"It's not good — but whatever he makes, I always finish it."

"You poor thing. Jiang Qi's cooking really is dreadful."

"Mom, what kind of thing is that to say." Jiang Qi protested to his roasting mother.

"I'm telling the truth."

"By the way, your mom mentioned you two have known each other a long while?" the eldest sister-in-law, Yun Hui, asked.

Ke Yan glanced at Jiang Qi, who nodded nervously. "Yes," Ke Yan said, "we've known each other a long time. We used to be good friends."

"Used to?" No one understood.

Ke Yan reached over and took Jiang Qi's hand, and the two stood, walked to the front of the coffee table, and bowed to Father and Mother Jiang. Baffled by the sudden gesture, the parents exchanged a glance.

Jiang Lü's eyes were fixed on their clasped hands.

"Uncle, Auntie — I hope you'll allow Jiang Qi and me to be together," Ke Yan said first.

"Allow you to be together? Aren't you already living together?" Mother Jiang didn't grasp his meaning; it would never have crossed her mind that the two were lovers.

"That's not what I mean." Jiang Qi didn't dare look at her.

"What I mean is, I love Jiang Qi, and I hope you'll allow Jiang Qi and me to be together as a couple." Ke Yan said it looking at Father and Mother Jiang, and bowed again.

"…"

No one spoke. In the heavy silence, sweat broke across Jiang Qi's palms.

"—Crash!" The cup in Mother Jiang's hand fell to the floor.

The sharp sound jolted everyone awake, including Mother Jiang, who'd let the cup slip and shatter without meaning to.

"You—you—what did you say?" Her hands trembled, and her voice refused to steady.

Yun Hui found it hard to take in too — how could two men…

"Auntie, I hope you'll let us be together." Ke Yan bent low, pleading.

"Mom, I really do love Ke Yan." Jiang Qi finally spoke, working up the courage to look at his mother — and found her eyes red, red with agitation.

"Do you two even know what you're saying?" Father Jiang stood.

"We do — but please believe I'll be good to Jiang Qi, I'll never let him suffer." Ke Yan gripped Jiang Qi's hand tighter, a signal not to be afraid.

"Believe? How do you expect me to believe it?" Mother Jiang's voice climbed. "You're a man, Jiang Qi's a man — can two men last? No — can two men even be together at all? Is that normal?"

"Why isn't it normal? Whatever happens, I'm still your son!" Looking at his frantic mother, Jiang Qi's heart ached — but much as he'd wronged her, he truly couldn't leave Ke Yan, and didn't want to.

"You shut your mouth — doing a thing like this and you still have the face to say such things… don't you know it isn't normal!" It was the first time Mother Jiang had ever spoken to Jiang Qi like this.

"Wife." Father Jiang spoke up. Though shocked that his son loved a man, he did not blame him. The idea felt strange, but it did not make his child abnormal.

"Mom, calm down." Yun Hui couldn't accept it either, but her heart ached for Jiang Qi; several years his senior, she'd more or less watched him grow up.

"Mom, I'm sorry, but—"

"If you're sorry, then break up with him." Mother Jiang turned her face away from Jiang Qi.

"I'm sorry, Mom, but I don't want to break up with Ke Yan. I love him. I can't leave him — I can't."

"Brother…" Jiang Lü finally grasped it: her brother loved men, and the man he loved was the CEO. Her long-held fantasy had come true. She was overjoyed.

She couldn't help a laugh escaping — luckily their mother didn't hear, or she'd surely have been scolded.

Mother Jiang's attention was all on Jiang Qi just then. Hearing him say he couldn't leave Ke Yan, she glared at him, saw that he and Ke Yan still held hands fast, and her head throbbed with fury. She snatched up the unfinished tea Father Jiang had left on the table and hurled it at Ke Yan.
