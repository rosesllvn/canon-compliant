The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 147

A Meal Together 一起吃顿饭

July 2026

~5 min read

Friday — Jiang Qi's last day at the company; the shop would open next week.

That morning, the first into his office weren't his secretary Li Jing but Xiao An and Chang Qing.

"What is it?"

"Manager, today's your last day. We all want to have a meal with you—to give you a proper send-off."

"A send-off? I'll still be around the company all the time."

"It's not the same — and we just want to have a meal with you."

"All right then — tonight, since tomorrow's off." Jiang Qi agreed.

"Wonderful — I'll go tell everyone." Xiao An dashed off.

"What would you like to eat, Manager? Do you like barbecue?" Chang Qing asked.

Barbecue? That brought back the time he'd eaten barbecue, drunk too much, and gotten his backside smacked by Ke Yan.

"Something else — how about a seafood buffet?"

"Sure, I'll let everyone know and book a place."

Chang Qing went out too, and Jiang Qi thought: leaving like this suddenly, he was a little reluctant after all.

He called Ke Yan right away. "Ke Yan, I'm going out to eat with colleagues tonight — they want a meal together, so I'll be home late. Go on home first."

"What are you eating?"

"Seafood buffet."

"Call me when you're done — I'll come get you."

"No need, I'll cab home."

"I'll come get you. And if you want to drink, drink — I'll let you, just this once." Ke Yan thought it was Jiang Qi's last day, out with colleagues; let him drink freely for once.

"Really? Wonderful — I'll hang up, then; I'll call after dinner." Jiang Qi was relieved; now he wouldn't have to worry about someone pressing him to drink while fearing Ke Yan would find out.

Once Jiang Qi hung up, Ke Yan returned to work. Near quitting time, Jiang Qi gathered the things he wanted to take from his office.

Xiao An burst in. "Jiang Qi, let's go."

"All right." He hefted the storage box of his things.

Stepping out, everyone had already packed up and was waiting outside.

They took the elevators down in groups.

Downstairs, a few went to hail cabs, and Jiang Qi told them, "Let me drop my things at the shop first — wait for me a moment."

"Sure, Manager, no rush."

Xiao An went along to the shop.

Jiang Qi unlocked it, Xiao An behind him, to see how it had turned out; she'd given input but never seen it.

At a glance she was drawn to the wall paintings and made to study them — but Jiang Qi had set his things down and was ready to go.

"I haven't finished looking." She didn't want to leave.

"Look next week — everyone's waiting." He saw her studying the chibi mural.

"All right then." She took a last reluctant look.

When they rejoined the others the cabs were ready, three to a car, off to the seafood buffet.

Jiang Qi rode with Xiao An and Chang Qing — Xiao An up front, the two of them in back.

Xiao An had thought Ke Yan might come, but he hadn't; unable to see the two of them being affectionate, and feeling cheated of it, she asked Jiang Qi, "Jiang Qi, the CEO's all right with you coming alone? Why didn't he come with you?"

Jiang Qi glanced awkwardly at Chang Qing; the whole company might know about him and Ke Yan, but said outright to his face it still felt strange, probably from hiding it so long. Then again, there was nothing to be awkward about. "What's there to mind?"

"Really? The CEO's not worried about you drinking?"

"He said to go easy today." Ke Yan had said drink freely, but Jiang Qi wouldn't say that — to guard against being plied drunk.

"All right." Hearing that, Xiao An didn't dare press him to drink much.

The cabs pulled up one after another. It was lucky they had booked; the restaurant joined several tables into one long row to seat them all.

First everyone fell to eating and drinking heartily, and once full, Chang Qing suddenly raised his glass to toast Jiang Qi.

"Manager, thank you for thinking so well of me, for handing me the position. I'll work hard from now on."

And he downed it in one.

Jiang Qi drained his too. "Good — do even better than I did."

"Yes." Chang Qing nodded.

Jiang Qi poured another and toasted everyone. "Thank you all. I've been glad to be your manager. After today I'm leaving, and Chang Qing will be your new manager. I wish you all greater success and smooth sailing." He drained the glass again.

Then ate a piece of fruit; after so long without drink, he was a little dizzy.

"Manager, wishing you and the CEO happiness," someone said, and Jiang Qi only smiled and said thank you.

Seeing how easygoing Jiang Qi was, those who wanted to drink with him drank, those who wanted to talk talked — quite the lively scene.

Eating and drinking on, it was ten o'clock before they knew it. At home, with no call from Jiang Qi, Ke Yan simply went to the restaurant — Jiang Qi had given him the address.

At the door, through the glass, he could spot Jiang Qi at a glance — and saw he seemed a little drunk, sitting there rather dazed.

He got out, walked in, came up behind Jiang Qi, and laid a hand on his shoulder. "Jiang Qi. Jiang Qi."

Seeing the CEO come for Manager Jiang, the noisy table fell silent.

"Ke Yan?" Jiang Qi turned.

"It's me. Shall we go home? You're a little drunk."

"Mm." Jiang Qi nodded, stood, swayed on his feet, and toppled into Ke Yan's arms; Ke Yan caught him.

The table watched in fascination. Tonight they were finally seeing all the tenderness they usually missed.

"We'll be off." Ke Yan said his goodbyes for Jiang Qi.

"Bye." Jiang Qi waved too.

"Bye, Manager. Bye, CEO."

Watching Manager Jiang half-carried to the car by the CEO, the girls still without boyfriends slumped over the table in tears. "When will we ever find a boyfriend as gentle as the CEO…"

All the good men taken by other men.
