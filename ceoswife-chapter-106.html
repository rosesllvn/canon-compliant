Chapter 106

Sleeping at Your Place 去你家睡

July 2026

~4 min read

"Excuse me — is there a doctor here named Lin Zirui?"

Mercifully, the two weren't entirely hopeless and knew to ask at the nurses' station.

"Oh, Doctor Lin Zirui is in orthopedics, on the third floor," the nurse said with a smile.

"Thank you." With their thanks, the two set off for the third-floor orthopedics department.

"What do you suppose Lin Zirui looks like?" Ma Tong said suddenly, in the elevator.

"No idea — though he's probably very pretty."

Behind them, Lin Zirui: "…"

He'd come down to fetch something and ended up in the elevator listening to two women he didn't know discuss him.

"Er — excuse me. Do you two know me?" Lin Zirui spoke up.

Mo Lü and Ma Tong stopped mid-sentence and turned together. "You're Lin Zirui?"

Lin Zirui nodded good-naturedly.

"Oh." A sound of indeterminate meaning.

"…" Lin Zirui felt a sudden chill.

"We're Yang Shaoyu's sisters — not by blood. We came to see what you look like."

Not blood sisters — friends, then? And they wanted to see what he looked like?

While he was thinking, the elevator arrived. As they stepped out, Lin Zirui registered the name Yang Shaoyu and went still — then, without quite meaning to, felt himself grow a little nervous. "Hello, I'm Lin Zirui. What may I call you?"

"Oh, I'm Mo Lü."

"Ma Tong."

"I have to get back to work, but if you don't mind, come sit in my office a while."

"Oh, sure."

Lin Zirui took the two to his office, poured them each a cup of tea, and went off to his rounds.

Those rounds, as it happened, ran rather long, and by the time he hurried back, Mo Lü and Ma Tong were gone — only Yang Shaoyu sat inside.

"Hm? Where are your friends?"

"Oh, something came up; they had to go."

A vague unease had crept over Yang Shaoyu at work. Realizing the two women might seek out Lin Zirui, he dumped his work on Ke Yan and left early—only to find them in Lin Zirui's office, just as feared.

He shooed them away and settled in to wait. After Lin Zirui's shift, they could have dinner, browse the shops, perhaps catch a movie—a real date.

"Oh — I'd actually meant to go to dinner with them." Lin Zirui took Yang Shaoyu's words at face value.

"What rotten timing. When do you get off?"

"I can leave now."

"Perfect. Let's go grab a meal."

"Mm." Lin Zirui thought of how the dinner they'd planned before had fallen through.

Out of the hospital, Yang Shaoyu took Lin Zirui's hand at once — inside, he didn't, for fear Lin Zirui's colleagues might see, which would be bad for him.

"Where shall we eat?" Lin Zirui asked, not pulling away.

"What do you feel like?"

"Hotpot. It's cold out — hotpot to warm up."

"Done. I know a great hotpot place." Whatever Lin Zirui said now, Yang Shaoyu agreed to.

He took Lin Zirui to his usual hotpot restaurant. Lin Zirui liked spice but could not tolerate much, so he ordered mild. Yang Shaoyu could eat anything and happily followed his choice.

Lin Zirui didn't have a big appetite and was soon full, sipping broth and watching Yang Shaoyu eat.

Once he'd had his fill, Yang Shaoyu looked over and noticed the dark circles under Lin Zirui's eyes. "Shall I take you home?"

"Hm?" Lin Zirui had assumed Yang Shaoyu wouldn't just send him home like that.

"You're worn out." Yang Shaoyu brushed a thumb beneath the shadows under his eyes.

"I'm all right." Warmth filled Lin Zirui's chest. Yang Shaoyu's tender concern differed from his brothers' care. It made him feel as though Yang Shaoyu's heart held only him. It felt like love.

He dipped his head and smiled. "All right then. Take me home."

"I'll take you home." Yang Shaoyu missed the meaning under the words.

Not until they were in the car, and Yang Shaoyu had pointed it toward Lin Zirui's home, did—

"Let's go back together tonight?"

"Ah?" Yang Shaoyu blinked.

"Tonight I want to sleep at your place."

"Ah?" Now Yang Shaoyu was thoroughly dumbstruck. What did Zirui mean by that? Surely not — not the thing he was thinking?
