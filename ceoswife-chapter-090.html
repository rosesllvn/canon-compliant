Chapter 90

Want to Chase Me? Dream On 想追我？做梦去吧

July 2026

~5 min read

Heedless of whether Lin Zirui was even at the hospital just then, Yang Shaoyu hurried there.

And he really did find Lin Zirui there; his heart eased, then turned displeased at once — how was it Lin Zirui was always on night shift.

Could his body take it?

Lin Zirui sat in a chair rubbing his brow, clearly tired; after rubbing a while he stood, only to feel a wave of dizziness, and braced a hand on the desk — then felt someone steady his shoulders from behind.

He turned and saw it was Yang Shaoyu; he froze, shook off Yang Shaoyu's hand, and took a chocolate from the drawer.

Yang Shaoyu at once took the chocolate from Lin Zirui's hand, unwrapped it for him, and held it to Lin Zirui's lips. "Hurry and eat."

Lin Zirui ignored him, took out another chocolate, quickly unwrapped it, and ate it himself.

Yang Shaoyu's hand, holding the chocolate, froze; then he stuffed it into his own mouth and ate it, the cloying sweetness making him quicken his swallowing, only letting out a breath once it was down.

"Did you need something?" Lin Zirui looked at Yang Shaoyu.

Yang Shaoyu looked at the somewhat thinner Lin Zirui and said, "Let's be together."

Lin Zirui's heart was shaken, but not a trace of it showed on his face. "And then?"

"Let's be together; I've realized I like you." Yang Shaoyu said it again, earnestly.

"Oh, I see." Though he didn't know what madness had seized Yang Shaoyu to come rushing over and say all this, Lin Zirui stayed very composed.

Yang Shaoyu was startled; he'd thought Lin Zirui would be overjoyed.

"So do you agree?" Yang Shaoyu asked again.

"I only said I see; I didn't say I agree."

"Why? Don't you like me?"

Lin Zirui's heart ached; then he gave a mocking laugh. "I don't anymore." With that he walked toward the door.

"Impossible." Yang Shaoyu caught Lin Zirui's hand, shocked, but more than that feeling something like being torn apart — a pain he'd never felt before.

"Let go of me." Lin Zirui pulled hard but couldn't free the hand Yang Shaoyu held.

"I won't, and I never will again." Yang Shaoyu suddenly pulled Lin Zirui into his arms. "It took me so much to realize I like you; I won't let you go."

"Chase me?" Lin Zirui froze, then gave a cold laugh. "Want to chase me? Dream on."

At that Yang Shaoyu released Lin Zirui, lowered his head, and kissed the coldly-laughing mouth.

Lin Zirui was utterly stunned — what was Yang Shaoyu doing?

While Lin Zirui was dazed, Yang Shaoyu's tongue pried open his shut lips, slipped in, and hooked Lin Zirui's chocolate-sweet tongue.

Now Lin Zirui jolted fully awake; he pushed hard at Yang Shaoyu but couldn't push him off, so he bit down, and the taste of blood filled both their mouths.

Yang Shaoyu released Lin Zirui. "Why are you like a puppy?"

Lin Zirui, face dark, walked toward the door, not wanting to deal with Yang Shaoyu at all; Yang Shaoyu hurried after him.

"Zirui, where are you going?"

"Yang Shaoyu, let me tell you — I did like you, but I also said I'd give up, and I have. Don't appear in front of me." Lin Zirui, afraid Yang Shaoyu would shout in the corridor and wake the patients, stopped walking.

"No; even if you don't like me anymore, I'll make you like me again. I don't want to let you go." Yang Shaoyu shook his head; thinking that Lin Zirui might never like him again, he felt a suffocating tightness, his heart aching in waves.

Yang Shaoyu watched Lin Zirui walk away and remembered how mercilessly he had left after the first confession. How much courage had a second one taken? Yet Yang Shaoyu had wounded him again and again, until the heart that loved him finally gave up.

But Yang Shaoyu wasn't one to give up so easily; he liked Zirui, and he'd make Zirui have faith in him again, make Zirui fall for him again.

At this thought Yang Shaoyu turned and left.

Once Lin Zirui finished his rounds, his mood had calmed; he planned to go rest in the office a while.

But walking to the very spot where Yang Shaoyu had said he'd chase him anew, said he liked him, his heart churned again; faced with Yang Shaoyu's hold on his heart, he could only sigh.

He opened the office door — and the person he'd thought had gone home was sitting at Lin Zirui's desk, head down, looking at who-knew-what; the somewhat downcast heart was at once filled, and Lin Zirui shook his head and stopped letting himself think.

"You're back? Come rest a while." Yang Shaoyu turned to see when Lin Zirui would return, and saw him in the doorway watching him.

"How are you still here?"

"Waiting for you to come back, of course." Yang Shaoyu smiled at Lin Zirui.

"You can go now."

"Don't be like that; come, sit here, rest properly." Yang Shaoyu patted his own thigh, gesturing for Lin Zirui to sit, a roguish grin on his face.

Lin Zirui was furious; he grabbed the coat to one side and flung it at Yang Shaoyu. "Get lost."

Seeing Lin Zirui angry, Yang Shaoyu stood at once. "All right, all right, I'll go, I'll go right now."

The words barely out, the man was already walking out.

Lin Zirui shut the door, sat down in the chair, lay across the desk to rest a while; but he couldn't quite sleep, turning his head a couple of times on the desk before slowly dropping off — these days had truly worn him out.

After who knew how long, once Lin Zirui was fast asleep, Yang Shaoyu crept softly in, draped the coat over Lin Zirui, sat in the chair to one side, and watched Lin Zirui's face quietly, thinking he must have been blind before, must have had something wrong with his head, to have thought Lin Zirui no good.

To not have accepted Lin Zirui's feelings.

Though from Lin Zirui's attitude toward him today, it seemed being with him wouldn't be easy; he'd have to put in more effort.
