Chapter 56

Jiang Qi Caught a Cold 江奇感冒了

July 2026

~7 min read

After his shower, Jiang Qi waited for Ke Yan on the sofa. A sudden chill sent him to the kitchen for hot water.

He wouldn't have caught a cold, would he? On second thought — impossible; colds don't come that easily.

Shaking his head, Jiang Qi went back to watching TV unconcerned, and soon forgot about it.

Who says colds don't come easily? Sometimes they arrive whenever they please.

Where was this? Jiang Qi sat on the ground gazing, lost, at a vast spread of green grass before him. Hadn't he just been sleeping? How had he ended up here.

Jiang Qi stood and looked around. Where was Ke Yan? Hadn't he been lying right beside him? Where had he gone.

Unable to find Ke Yan, Jiang Qi walked on, but however far he went, the same endless grass stretched before him, the scenery never changing.

Jiang Qi grew a little panicked, but quickly steadied himself, and walked on — yet walk and walk as he might, he couldn't get out. Exhausted, Jiang Qi slumped to the ground. So tired, so thirsty.

Sitting there, Jiang Qi felt a little short of breath, and stood again, gulping in great breaths — when he suddenly saw what looked like a person ahead. Jiang Qi ran over, and found it was Ke Yan.

Jiang Qi ran toward Ke Yan in delighted surprise — and suddenly the ground gave way beneath his feet, and with a splash he fell into water.

At first Jiang Qi was glad he could swim. Then he found he couldn't rise; something gripped him and dragged him steadily down.

Jiang Qi felt he couldn't breathe, the arms and legs trying to swim up drained of all strength.

Ke Yan woke to Jiang Qi thrashing beside him, arms flailing and legs kicking as if he were swimming. Then he went still, rolled over, and burrowed into Ke Yan's chest with a whimper.

Now Ke Yan saw something was wrong, and quickly pulled him out of his embrace, patting his face lightly. "Jiang Qi, Jiang Qi, wake up."

Jiang Qi opened his eyes groggily, realized he'd been dreaming, then realized his nose was blocked, and hurried to open his mouth and breathe.

"Dreaming?" Ke Yan patted Jiang Qi's back lightly.

"Mm — my nose is blocked; I dreamed I fell into water and couldn't breathe."

"How did your nose get blocked." Ke Yan asked, worried.

"Hm? Probably kicked the covers off just now."

"Don't go catching a cold."

"I won't."

"Lie back down and sleep a while; tell me if you feel unwell."

"Mm." Jiang Qi nestled back into Ke Yan's arms.

In the morning Jiang Qi got up earlier than Ke Yan and went to brush his teeth; halfway through, his nose suddenly itched, and he scrunched it up.

"Ah-choo—!!" Jiang Qi couldn't help a sneeze.

Looked like he really had caught a bit of a cold. Rubbing his nose, Jiang Qi went to find cold medicine.

Whether through psychology or fast action, the medicine soon eased his body and stopped his nose itching.

After breakfast he went to the company with Ke Yan. Sitting in the car, Jiang Qi suddenly spoke. "Ke Yan, I think I should go get a driver's license, or it's really inconvenient."

"Mm, that'd be good — then when I'm away on business you won't have to wait for cabs."

"But I'm too busy with work, so you'll have to grant me leave."

"All right." Ke Yan agreed.

"And no docking my pay, or my vacation days."

"All my work is already yours — you think I'd dock your pay?"

"True enough." Jiang Qi answered with a grin.

"Jiang Qi."

"Mm?"

"Why do I feel like you've got a nasal twang."

"Hm? You must've misheard." Jiang Qi reflexively covered his nose.

"You didn't catch a chill, did you?"

"No — how would I catch a chill."

"Be careful, or you'll be the one who suffers if you get sick."

"I know." Jiang Qi hadn't expected Ke Yan to be so sharp.

Jiang Qi was very unproductive today, because he felt unwell — now cold, now his nose itching.

The morning medicine had worn off. His nose itched maddeningly, forever threatening a sneeze that wouldn't come.

"Ah-choo—" Jiang Qi finally got a sneeze out, and the moment it came his previously stuffed nose cleared at once, his breathing smooth.

"Phew." Nose comfortable, Jiang Qi let out a breath and went back to work. Looked like he was rather badly sick this time.

Before long his nose blocked again. Finding no medicine in his drawer, Jiang Qi searched the tea-room cabinet and found one last packet. Enough time had passed since the morning dose to take another.

Sickness comes like a collapsing mountain; even having taken cold medicine, Jiang Qi was still toppled by it. Tossing the tissue he'd blown his nose with, Jiang Qi had lost count of how many times he'd blown his nose in one afternoon; his nose had even started to hurt.

Better see a doctor — without it tonight would surely be miserable; his nose would surely block, and block all night.

Once decided, Jiang Qi was a man of action: he'd see the doctor after work and cab home himself; it was nearly the end of the day anyway. He picked up his briefcase and headed downstairs.

The one person you don't want to see is exactly the one you run into — and now, the person Jiang Qi didn't much want to see was Ke Yan, because he wanted to hide his cold from him. But the moment he stepped out of the elevator he saw Ke Yan, who for some reason was on the ground floor at this hour.

Jiang Qi's body stiffened. "Hi."

The moment it was out he felt utterly foolish.

Ke Yan glanced at the briefcase in Jiang Qi's hand, then at his face. "Where do you think you're going?"

"I, I..." Jiang Qi, remembering his voice might come out nasal, got stuck.

"Come up with me."

Ke Yan dropped those four words and walked into the elevator first, while the several people following behind him looked at Jiang Qi with a touch of gloating.

Jiang Qi followed resignedly.

The few front-desk staff watched Jiang Qi get told off by the CEO and follow him into the elevator with a wronged little face, and said to each other they couldn't take how adorable it was. Well — it seemed their auto-ignore systems had automatically ignored everyone else.

Had Jiang Qi known what they were saying, he'd surely have retorted: this isn't a wronged face — it's plainly a glum one.

The elevator was silent; Jiang Qi snuck a glance at Ke Yan. By Jiang Qi's years of experience, Ke Yan wasn't very angry just now — but also, by Jiang Qi's years of experience, in a while Ke Yan would surely be very angry.

Ke Yan brought Jiang Qi to his office, having had the several people who'd been following him shown to the meeting room for tea.

Jiang Qi didn't know them — probably people from another company, here to discuss business with Ke Yan.

"Ke Yan." Jiang Qi deliberately roughened his voice to call the Ke Yan who'd been ignoring him since the meeting room, because he feared Ke Yan would catch his nasal twang, so he roughened his voice to call him.

Called, Ke Yan looked up, eyeing Jiang Qi strangely. "Why has your voice gone like this?"

Jiang Qi only now realized that talking in a roughened voice was also rather strange.

"I, I, uh..."

Ke Yan walked over and felt Jiang Qi's forehead with the back of his hand — a bit warmer than usual.

"Did you catch a cold?" Ke Yan said, cutting straight through Jiang Qi's stammering mode. "No, no I didn't." Jiang Qi hurried to deny it — a reflexive denial, you could say.

Unfortunately, before he could win Ke Yan's belief, Jiang Qi couldn't help a sneeze — and a sneeze would've been one thing, but his nose ran along with it...

A silence fell in the room; Jiang Qi was so embarrassed he forgot to wipe his nose, wishing at that moment he could swat himself straight into the floor.

Suddenly, Ke Yan very roughly wiped away Jiang Qi's snot with his hand, then handed Jiang Qi a handkerchief. Jiang Qi took it and blew his nose, then obediently stepped forward to wipe Ke Yan's hand clean.

He snuck a glance at Ke Yan. Looked like he was angry this time.
