The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 87

Feelings 心意

July 2026

~7 min read

Jiang Qi stood at the roadside waiting for his jianbing, breathing out a puff of white; the weather had been getting colder lately, and Yang Shaoyu would soon be discharged.

Lin Zirui, in a white coat over a sweater, hurried toward Yang Shaoyu's room with a chart, carrying breakfast he'd brought from home.

As expected, Yang Shaoyu was still asleep. Lin Zirui entered quietly, set breakfast on the table, and smiled at his sleeping face. He reached out, remembered his hands were cold, then breathed on them and rubbed them together before slowly trying again...

Just then Yang Shaoyu suddenly opened his eyes; Lin Zirui froze, quickly withdrew his hand, and feigning composure picked up the breakfast. "You're awake; have your breakfast."

Yang Shaoyu pushed the quilt aside; Lin Zirui handed him the crutch set to one side, and Yang Shaoyu went to wash up on the crutch.

Lin Zirui watched Yang Shaoyu from the bathroom doorway; Yang Shaoyu finished quickly.

He came back on the crutch and sat at the little table, where Lin Zirui set out the breakfast.

"You're more dependable than a carer." Yang Shaoyu said with a smile, looking at the exquisite breakfast.

Lin Zirui smiled too. "Eat up; I'm off to the office."

"Right — I should be able to leave in a few days, shouldn't I."

Lin Zirui's hand, about to open the door, paused; after a while he said, "Mm." Then he opened the door and went out.

Lin Zirui leaned against the wall outside the room, an arm over his eyes, and sighed. When had he started feeling this way about Yang Shaoyu?

Was it since learning Yang Shaoyu liked men too? No, probably earlier than that.

Walking down the hospital corridor, head bowed, Lin Zirui's back looked rather forlorn...

But why had he fallen for Yang Shaoyu? Lin Zirui stopped walking, then gave a self-mocking shake of his head.

Yang Shaoyu could get up and walk now, though only on a crutch.

After walking the hospital a while, Yang Shaoyu came to Lin Zirui's office and saw him reading something intently; he knocked, and Lin Zirui looked up, then smiled and waved him in.

"What are you reading?"

"Nothing." Lin Zirui quietly covered Yang Shaoyu's chart with a book.

"I can leave by the end of the month, right?"

"Mm." So eager to leave? Lin Zirui thought glumly.

"Three months without work — I'm going stir-crazy." Yang Shaoyu sat down in a chair.

"You still need to rest, especially no running yet, and no strenuous movements."

"Strenuous exercise? Can I do that sort of thing?" Yang Shaoyu raised an eyebrow.

"What sort of thing?" Lin Zirui didn't catch it for a moment.

"Finding a woman, of course."

Lin Zirui's heart twisted hard, a little painful. "Why..."

"What did you say?" Yang Shaoyu didn't hear clearly.

"Couldn't you find a man?"

This time Yang Shaoyu heard, and laughed. "What's good about a man — all hard, and with the same thing I've got; no appetite for it."

Lin Zirui's body stiffened.

"Though..." Yang Shaoyu went on, "if it were you, I could give it some thought." Yang Shaoyu said this because he only thought of Lin Zirui as an ordinary friend, with no other idea in mind — simply joking with him, in short.

But Lin Zirui felt a flush of surprise, a sudden surge of happiness rising in him, and the corners of his mouth lifted.

"I'll head back to the room then; I'm back at the company in a few days, and there's a lot I need to review." Yang Shaoyu stood and left.

Once Yang Shaoyu had gone, Lin Zirui moved the book aside, exposing Yang Shaoyu's chart and the photo on it.

He reached out and touched it — maybe Yang Shaoyu would accept him; maybe he could give it a try? Maybe Yang Shaoyu would be with him?

At the thought that Yang Shaoyu might go off to find a woman the moment he left the hospital, Lin Zirui's heart felt stifled, and a little pained.

There were only a few days until Yang Shaoyu's discharge, and over those days Lin Zirui didn't go to see him, afraid he wouldn't know what to say.

Yang Shaoyu, for his part, was glum for a few days because Lin Zirui hadn't shown up.

Yang Shaoyu was to be discharged tomorrow; Lin Zirui lay in bed all night without sleeping — should he say it tomorrow?

If he didn't, he could still be Yang Shaoyu's friend, but if he did, they couldn't even be friends.

But... maybe Yang Shaoyu liked him too?

Lin Zirui looked at the moonlight outside the window, remembered the way Yang Shaoyu was with him, the ambiguous things Yang Shaoyu occasionally said, and thought maybe he could give it a try.

Yang Shaoyu hadn't told anyone he was being discharged today, because once out he urgently needed to go find a woman.

Three months of abstinence — it was time to eat some meat.

Though whether Yang Shaoyu would get his chance at "meat" today remained to be seen.

He took the slip Lin Zirui had given him to get his leg x-rayed, then went to find Lin Zirui to check on the situation.

"How is it?" Yang Shaoyu asked.

"Mm, healing well; you can be discharged." Lin Zirui had barely slept all night, looking a bit haggard, dark circles under his eyes.

"Great, I'll go pack my clothes and check out."

"Mm." Lin Zirui watched the eager-to-leave Yang Shaoyu, lost in thought.

Yang Shaoyu walked out of the hospital and saw Lin Zirui in casual clothes waiting for him.

"Not working?"

"No, I'm off this afternoon." Lin Zirui walked beside Yang Shaoyu. "Let's go have lunch first."

"All right, but I'm busy this afternoon."

"Mm, just lunch."

The two came to a quiet restaurant, and Lin Zirui got a private room.

"What do you want to eat?" Yang Shaoyu asked, holding the menu.

"Anything; I'm not picky." Lin Zirui seemed a bit distracted.

Yang Shaoyu ordered a few dishes, and once the waiter left asked Lin Zirui, "What's with you today?"

"Yang Shaoyu..."

"Hm?"

"I, I like you."

"..." Yang Shaoyu doubted his ears. "What did you say?"

"I like you." Lin Zirui felt as if he'd thrown caution to the wind, his face earnest.

Yang Shaoyu suddenly felt a headache coming on. "Zirui..."

Lin Zirui clenched both hands together under the table, his palms slick with sweat.

"Sorry." Yang Shaoyu frowned, deciding it was better to be clear.

"I don't like you, I don't like men; I didn't know you felt this way."

"But — how would you know without trying?" Lin Zirui felt his eyes heat, and quickly lowered his head.

"I don't want to try; what I like is women. I don't dislike homosexuals, but I couldn't be in a relationship with one." He paused. "I find it disgusting." Yang Shaoyu felt he had to be hard-hearted enough to cut off Lin Zirui's feelings.

"Would I disgust you too?" Lin Zirui raised his head.

"..." Yang Shaoyu looked at Lin Zirui's grief-stricken face and faintly red eyes, and felt a pang of reluctance, but still hardened his heart to say, "Yes. We'd better not see each other for now; think it over carefully yourself, or we won't even be able to be friends." Yang Shaoyu stood and walked to the door, paused, but didn't turn around. "I don't like you — not now, and I never will."

"But I like you." Lin Zirui rose from his seat and caught Yang Shaoyu's hand, not wanting him to leave.

Yang Shaoyu shook off Lin Zirui's hand. "I'll go first."

And with that he left.

Lin Zirui looked at the shut door, no longer able to see even Yang Shaoyu's back, and the tears he'd held back finally fell.

He'd thought Yang Shaoyu would like him — but no, he'd only been flattering himself.
