The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 153

The Paintings in the Bookshop 书店里的画

July 2026

~5 min read

Wang Xiaoyu had noticed the murals on her first visit; now she studied them closely.

Only now did she see a line of text beside them she'd missed before — the story of Jiang Qi and Ke Yan.

Ke Yan? Who was that?

She didn't ask, and read on.

The story began with a chibi figure dangling over a railing. It looked so much like the boss that she'd recognized him at a glance.

Below the boss was a chibi figure with a blank, stern face, a hand reaching out past a railing, catching something like a chain.

The one catching the chain, Wang Xiaoyu's instinct said, was the man she'd just seen. Unable to hold back her curiosity, she asked Jiang Qi, "Boss, who was that man just now?"

"Ke Yan." Jiang Qi answered offhandedly, reading.

Ke Yan? Really the man in the painting — though Wang Xiaoyu recalled that Ke Group's CEO was named Ke Yan. The same person?

Setting that aside, she read on. The second painting showed a stormy night, rain and wind, a figure under an open umbrella at the door of a building, the umbrella nearly torn away by the gale; behind the open door, Ke Yan in pajamas, gripping Jiang Qi's arm as if to pull him inside.

Next, a chibi figure asleep in a library, the setting sun through the window painted so vividly on Jiang Qi's face, Ke Yan laying a book over it as if to shield him from the light.

The fourth: a night road, Ke Yan holding Jiang Qi's hand, Jiang Qi's face flushed pink.

And Ke Yan asleep, Jiang Qi sneaking a look at him.

The fifth: in a forest, Jiang Qi asleep on Ke Yan's lap, Ke Yan reading with a serious face.

The sixth, also in a forest: Jiang Qi reading a book — the title unwritten, but his face very red — and Ke Yan, noticing, glancing at him.

The seventh: on a staircase, Jiang Qi falling, Ke Yan catching him, holding him.

The last: the two embracing beneath a Christmas tree.

When she reached the end, Wang Xiaoyu wanted more. The murals plainly told the story of how her boss and Ke Yan had met and fallen in love. But she didn't dare pry; what if the boss found her nosy and failed her trial period?

Through the morning, customers came and went — buying, browsing; few enough that it wasn't busy.

At noon Jiang Qi checked the time, closed his book, and went to find Ke Yan for lunch; before leaving he had Wang Xiaoyu and Zhu Zihao take turns at lunch, and close the shop if there were no customers, reopening after.

As employees they naturally wouldn't close up and go eat together, so the two ordered takeout.

Jiang Qi reached Ke Group; no one was surprised to see him, having long guessed he'd come for lunch with the CEO.

Even the guards weren't puzzled that someone who had resigned still came by; nearly the whole company knew Jiang Qi was with their CEO.

He came to Ke Yan's office and found him still working. He walked to the desk. "Still busy? Work after we eat."

"I'm waiting for you — come on, lunch." Seeing Jiang Qi, Ke Yan dropped his work at once, stood, and went with him.

In the canteen, Jiang Qi ate the dishes he liked. "The food here's so good — I can't bear not to come."

"Then should I be glad the canteen's good, so you'll keep me company at meals?"

"Yes." Jiang Qi laughed.

And got his cheek pinched by Ke Yan.

The other staff watched the two — clearly meant to needle all the single people.

After lunch Jiang Qi wanted to head back to the shop. Ke Yan, thinking of the mountain of work in his office, and guessing Jiang Qi's company friends would visit the shop today — and that his presence would only make them stiff — didn't go with him.

Sure enough, not long after Jiang Qi returned, Xiao An and Jiang Lü arrived, followed by Chang Qing, Lin Zheng, and several other employees. They were all curious to see the shop the CEO had given Jiang Qi.

Jiang Qi had Zhu Zihao bring them drinks and sat chatting with them. Lin Zheng first congratulated him on the opening, then asked, "The CEO didn't come today?"

"No, he's got work. Oh — Chang Qing, how's work? Settling in?"

"Don't worry, Manager — I've settled in." Chang Qing still called him Manager out of habit.

"I'm not a manager anymore — just call me Jiang Qi."

"I'll keep calling you Manager, I'm used to it."

"All right then." Jiang Qi didn't press; glad he'd settled in.

Xiao An and Jiang Lü couldn't sit still, roaming the shop, especially clustering at the murals to discuss them.

Xiao An, excited: "Last time I wanted to see these all the way through but had no time — now I can take a good look."

"Xiao An, this is exactly my brother and brother-husband's whole courtship — I never knew brother-husband was this romantic, painting it all out."

"Right?" Xiao An hadn't expected the usually cold CEO to be so romantic.

With customers still about, much as she was curious about Jiang Qi and Ke Yan's relationship, Wang Xiaoyu couldn't go over to join the discussion.

Having seen the murals, Xiao An and Jiang Lü were delighted to find Jiang Qi had taken their suggestion and made a "fujoshi corner" — every kind of BL manga and novel, enough to keep them reading their fill.

But time was limited; work hours came quickly, and the books they hadn't finished Jiang Qi simply let them take home.

The afternoon's customers were manageable; Jiang Qi sat taking payments and reading, waiting to go home with Ke Yan at quitting time.

At closing, Zhu Zihao was startled — he'd been told they finished early, but not this early.

Jiang Qi locked up and went to the company to wait for Ke Yan, who was about due to finish too.
