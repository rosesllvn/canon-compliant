Chapter 37

Throwing Myself at You 扑向你

July 2026

~5 min read

That night, back in the dorm, Jiang Qi couldn't sleep no matter what he did, until at last he got up, took the comic out of the drawer, grabbed the flashlight, and burrowed back under the covers to read.

Ye Yi pinned Pei Qian beneath him and worked at his clothes — but before he'd gotten far, found himself the one pinned instead. From startled to bashful, he peeled off his own clothes, leaving Jiang Qi gaping. Afterward, lying on Pei Qian, Ye Yi confessed; and Pei Qian told him he'd liked him for a long time, ever since Ye Yi had first set out to befriend him. And so the two ended up together. On the final page, Pei Qian said: "Perhaps you fell for the friend right beside you long ago, and simply didn't know it yet. Try to feel it out — just give in to him."

That line struck Jiang Qi hard. The friend right beside you. He instinctively thought of Ke Yan, and shook his head, trying to fling the notion out — and then thought of him again. Actually, it wasn't impossible between him and Ke Yan, was it? The thought made him smile despite himself; a minute later the foolish smile froze. Why would I think that? Do I like Ke Yan?

Wanting to be with him; feeling crushed when he couldn't see him; the urge to become his friend, to stand at his side; the way his heart raced at the slightest touch, the way his face heated so easily — was this what liking someone felt like? Did he like Ke Yan?

Jiang Qi let out a low groan and buried his head under the covers. When he got up the next day, Liu Hao took one look at the dark circles ringing his eyes. "Jiang Qi, did you go sleepwalking last night?"

Jiang Qi shot him a sour glare and yawned. Yes — he was fated to lie awake again tonight.

A whole night without sleep left him listless, yawning from the moment he rose. Books in hand, he came downstairs, and on the fourth floor his eyes drifted toward Ke Yan's dorm. He hoped Ke Yan had already gone out — for once he didn't want to see him, sure it would be awkward. Ever since finishing that comic, he wanted to see Ke Yan and didn't want to see Ke Yan, until he felt about to split in two.

And of course — when you'd rather not see someone, that's exactly when they appear. Caught in his half-wanting, half-dreading, Jiang Qi ran straight into him: he on the upper part of the staircase, Ke Yan on the lower. He was still searching for how to say hello when a boy came rushing down and shoved past him — and Jiang Qi went pitching forward.

He shut his eyes, reckoning that if he didn't crack his head open he'd at least land flat on his face — but the pain never came. Instead a warm embrace wrapped around him, and the scent of Ke Yan. So warm, so good; and just then Jiang Qi was sniffing at him like a little dog desperate to take in the smell.

Thinking he'd been frightened, Ke Yan drew him out of the embrace and patted his head. "You all right?"

His wits scattered, Jiang Qi stammered, "I'm — I'm fine."

"Why's your face so red?"

"Huh? Probably because it's too hot." He felt it go hotter still.

Ke Yan eyed the two layers he had on — the weather had turned, the mornings cold — and reminded him, "Don't take off any layers. Careful you don't catch a chill."

"Mm, mm, I know."

Jiang Qi thought: since it seemed he liked Ke Yan, he'd just stay obediently by his side, because it made him happy. Only — this secret absolutely mustn't be let known, for fear of losing him. His wish was simple: to be with Ke Yan. Just that. But — did he really only want, simply, to be together?

"Jiang Qi." Someone patted his shoulder.

He took off his earphones. "What is it?"

A classmate. "There are two pretty girls outside looking for you."

Pretty girls? Jiang Qi glanced over — sure enough, two girls stood at the doorway. He didn't recognize them. "Were you two looking for me?"

They exchanged a glance and said, all mysterious, "In a little while, go to the path that branches left from the library — we've something to find you about there."

"What is it? Can't you say now?"

"No. And besides..." They gave another eerie little smile. "The one really looking for you isn't us. It's someone else."

"Who?" Jiang Qi didn't understand — and looked at the strangely-smiling pair before him; had the cold frozen their faces? Why were their smiles so awkward? Strange as it was, at their urging he agreed.

He walked to the path branching left from the library, but found no one. Not here yet? He frowned and decided to wait. Five minutes; he waited patiently. Ten; a little less so. Twenty; bored, he gave up. For a stranger he'd wait half an hour at most — and since they'd plainly set the meeting and then turned up late themselves, his patience had dropped to twenty minutes. Just as he turned to go, someone called out to stop him.

The girl before him clutched the front of her clothes with both hands, her face so nervous that Jiang Qi puzzled over it, nagged by the sense he'd seen her somewhere.

"Hello, Jiang Qi, I — actually I, today, I, today I..."

Watching her stammer, Jiang Qi suddenly placed her — the girl who'd talked to him at the birthday party. Her name was something like Qiao Mi'er.
