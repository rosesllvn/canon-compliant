The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 158

Chewing His Fingers Means He's Hungry 咬手指说明肚子饿了

July 2026

~5 min read

Jiang Qi exchanged a look with Ke Yan, then looked down at the baby in his arms. Xiao Ke hadn't cried when his mother left. Instead, he grabbed Jiang Qi's hand, brought it to his mouth, and began chewing and licking it.

Bitten, Jiang Qi didn't react at first; when Xiao Ke licked his finger and it tickled, he pulled it from the baby's mouth. "Dirty finger, can't put it in your mouth." Then, remembering Xiao Ke couldn't understand him, he stroked the baby's cheek with his other, clean hand.

Ke Yan pulled two tissues for Jiang Qi to wipe his finger, and seeing Jiang Qi's good mood, couldn't help his own lifting. "Happy?"

"Mm." Jiang Qi nodded, then worried, "But I've never minded a child."

"Didn't Sis say he's easy? Don't worry." Ke Yan studied the bundled-up baby. "Why does he look like a round steamed bun?"

Jiang Qi snorted a laugh. "Little ones feel the cold. Want to hold him?"

"No." Ke Yan refused.

"He's adorable — hold him, just once." Jiang Qi set Xiao Ke on Ke Yan's lap; Ke Yan steadied him.

Xiao Ke didn't cry on Ke Yan's lap, but squirmed uncomfortably.

Jiang Qi adjusted Ke Yan's hold. "You have to hold him properly, support him, or he can't sit steady and he's uncomfortable."

Ke Yan held him right, then looked at Jiang Qi. "When we have a child, it'll call you Mama."

"Why? Not Papa?"

"Because you're more like the mama. Kid's mom." Ke Yan smiled.

Jiang Qi shot him a mock-glare. "When we have a child, don't ever say that in front of it. If the child really calls me Mama, you can sleep in the guest room." And he took Xiao Ke back.

"Understood, wifey." Ke Yan's smile widened.

"Ptt." Xiao Ke, not knowing what the grown-ups were discussing, smacked his lips, put a finger in his mouth and sucked, then kept it there sucking.

Jiang Qi noticed and pulled the finger out, wiping the drool with a tissue.

Xiao Ke had been sucking contentedly. When the finger disappeared, he tried to put it back, but Jiang Qi held his hand gently. Even with all his milk-drinking strength, he couldn't pull free. His mouth puckered and his face scrunched.

Sure enough, the face scrunched and then — wah — he cried, loud and pitiful, his face going red.

Jiang Qi panicked. "Ke Yan, what do I do? What's wrong with him?"

"It's fine, don't panic — let me see if Sis wrote anything." Clueless about babies too, Ke Yan remembered Ke Shao's note and opened the big bag to find it.

Meanwhile Jiang Qi soothed Xiao Ke, lifting and rocking him, walking him back and forth — but Xiao Ke kept crying hard.

Ke Shao hadn't shut the office door fully, leaving the room far from soundproof. At Xiao Ke's cries, the secretaries outside paused mid-keystroke. Silence fell as they exchanged curious looks.

Yang Shaoyu was curious too, but he simply stood and went into the office, remembering to shut the door behind him.

The gossiping secretaries went back to work, disappointed, hoping Assistant Yang would tell them what was going on when he came out.

Inside, Ke Yan rummaged through the bag while Jiang Qi paced with the crying baby. Yang Shaoyu watched for a moment, then slipped out before they noticed him; he had no wish to be roped into soothing a child.

Seeing Yang Shaoyu come straight back out, the secretaries grew more curious, staring at him — what on earth was going on in there?

Yang Shaoyu didn't answer, only told them to get back to work.

Getting nothing, they stopped asking and worked.

Ke Yan finally found Ke Shao's note in an inner pocket. Xiao Ke was hungry. The instructions said to sterilize the bottle, add the correct amount of water at 40–60°C, then mix in the proper ratio of formula.

He frowned — he hadn't expected it this complicated.

"What is it? Hard?" Jiang Qi saw Ke Yan frowning at the note as if mixing formula were difficult.

"Yes. I'll go mix it." Helpless, Ke Yan took the bottle and formula, meaning to do it in the break room.

"I'll go — you hold Xiao Ke a bit." Xiao Ke had stopped crying; held by Jiang Qi and unable to grab his hand, he'd put a finger back in his mouth.

"Better you hold him." Ke Yan's hold wasn't as good as Jiang Qi's; he feared dropping him.

Jiang Qi thought it better he go — what CEO carries a bottle and formula to the break room during work hours.

"Knock knock—" Two raps, and Yang Shaoyu came in. "The department managers are here."

Ke Yan remembered — a small meeting today, right on time.

"I'll take Xiao Ke back to the shop, then." Jiang Qi didn't want to disturb Ke Yan's work.

"It's fine — go mix the formula, then rest with Xiao Ke in the lounge." Ke Yan said it, and walked out with the bottle in one hand and a whole tin of formula in the other; Jiang Qi followed with Xiao Ke.

The managers waiting outside assumed the CEO had come to summon them—until they saw the bottle and formula in his hands and froze.

Ignoring them, Ke Yan set the bottle and formula on a secretary's desk.

Secretary Wu looked up, startled. "CEO?"

"I recall you've had a child — mix some formula for me."

"Ah? Yes, of course." Not fully processing it, her body moved on its own, carrying the bottle and formula to the break room.

Jiang Qi followed; passing Ke Yan, he was caught by the hand. "Wait — once the formula's done, come to my office, don't go back to the shop alone."

"I know — go on, get busy." Jiang Qi nodded and followed Secretary Wu.

The managers' faces quickly composed themselves, but they couldn't help wondering whose child that was.

"Come in." Without Jiang Qi beside him, Ke Yan's face turned cold again, his manner less than warm.

The managers filed in, thinking: the CEO really is less frightening when Jiang Qi's around.
