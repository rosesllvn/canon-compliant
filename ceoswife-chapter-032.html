The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 32

"I slept in the same bed as a guy."

"Oh." Perfectly normal — you're both men, after all.

"When I woke up I found we were both naked."

"Oh." Sleeping nude is healthier.

"But I realized we'd done it." His tone turned instantly mortified.

"Done what?" Jiang Qi tilted his head and blinked.

"You know — the mm-mm, ah-ah kind of thing." Liu Hao was beside himself.

Huh? Jiang Qi was stunned two seconds, then asked, "So what did it feel like?"

Furious, Liu Hao smacked the back of his head. "Why aren't you asking how I inexplicably ended up in bed with a man?"

Looking at the enraged face, Jiang Qi decided to go along. "All right — so how did you end up in bed with a man?"

"Mm... well... that..." Liu Hao jerked his head aside. "Don't ask me."

"Weren't you the one who told me to ask?"

"Argh... well..." Liu Hao tugged at his own hair. "Anyway — what do I do now?"

"Who was the man? Do I know him?"

Liu Hao nodded.

"Meng Qing?"

Liu Hao started. "How did you know?"

"You two came back together that day, and you both looked strange — though Meng Qing seemed normal enough yesterday."

Liu Hao heaved a sigh. "What's bothering me most is not knowing whether he knows we, well, did that." His face reddened a little.

"You don't know whether he knows?"

"Mm... when I woke and found us both naked, I helped him get his clothes back on..."

"Liu Hao, your face is so red. Are you all right?"

"Your face is the red one — redder than anything." And with that he ran off.

Watching him go, Jiang Qi touched his own face. Red?

Over here, Jiang Qi was wavering over whether to go into the library. He'd been rather rude yesterday, hadn't he? But Ke Yan was in the wrong too, he argued in his own defense.

Ke Yan spotted the Jiang Qi loitering at the entrance from a long way off. Seeing the conflicted look, he rubbed his nose, a little self-conscious, walked over, and clapped him on the shoulder.

Jiang Qi turned and managed a stiff little greeting.

Ke Yan ignored it and simply hauled him in. "Isn't it hot out there?"

"It's all right — it's starting to turn cooler now." Well — Jiang Qi could be quite dim sometimes.

"Come on, let's read." Ke Yan walked ahead; Jiang Qi tugged a smile onto his mouth and fell in behind.

"What's this?" Ke Yan picked up a bottle of water from the desk. Jiang Qi looked — the water he'd left there before, the little note still stuck to it.

"You didn't come to the library that day?"

"No."

"Oh." Jiang Qi took out his phone. "What's your number?"

Ke Yan told him; Jiang Qi pressed call, and the phone in Ke Yan's pocket rang twice. "Now you've got mine too." He smiled and carefully saved it.

The two sat down, and Jiang Qi noticed many more people in the library today.

Over here, Liu Hao finished eating, crept furtively into the building, up to the fifth floor, softened his steps, and quietly pushed the door open. No one? He breathed out — and then someone patted his back.

He whipped around in terror, then went a little awkward; it was Meng Qing. "What are you doing here?" Liu Hao asked an extremely stupid question.

Meng Qing looked at him, amused. "This is my dorm too."

"Right... oh."

"Why've you been so odd these past few days?" The two walked in.

"Haha... how would I be."

"Oh, right — that day you threw up all over both of us, so I got your clothes off you and gave you a wash while I was at it."

"And... anything else happen?" Liu Hao probed.

"No — just sleep, what else?"

So nothing happened? Liu Hao breathed an inward sigh of relief.

Watching it, Meng Qing very nearly laughed in his face, but held it back. He'd known Liu Hao had been off for the past two days — so it had all been over this. With the matter cleared up, Liu Hao stopped being awkward and dragged Meng Qing into chatting about this and that.

Setting down the book he'd finished, Ke Yan rubbed his neck, and saw an enormously thick book in front of Jiang Qi, who by the look of it had only just started it — absorbed, a rare sight. Though the two had read together a good while, they hadn't actually spent much time together; they'd each read their own book, then gone to eat, and over meals couldn't find much to talk about — no common language, never discussing what they read, at most a couple of words before reading and a goodbye after. And yet Ke Yan felt it was good. Perhaps that was exactly why he wanted to read with Jiang Qi.

When Jiang Qi registered how long he'd been reading, it was already closing time. He rubbed his stiff neck, put the book away, and said, a little sheepish, "I didn't even notice how long I read today."

"Was it good?" Jiang Qi was surprised Ke Yan would ask, but nodded. "It was good." A pause. "Want to read it next time?"

"Mm." A small nod. "All right."

Jiang Qi was so happy today that the smile was still on his face when he got back to the dorm. Why so happy? Probably because of Ke Yan.
