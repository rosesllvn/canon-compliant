Chapter 84

Buying Clothes 买衣服

July 2026

~6 min read

The next day, Jiang Qi went to work glumly; the morning was half meetings, the afternoon more work, and at quitting time he decided to go see Yang Shaoyu.

Yang Shaoyu lay in bed eating tangerines and watching TV, the very picture of leisure.

Jiang Qi sat down by the bed and took a tangerine to peel and eat.

Seeing Jiang Qi's color was a bit off, Yang Shaoyu asked, "What is it? Did Ke Yan bully you?"

"No, I'm trying to figure out how to go on the offensive against Ke Yan." Jiang Qi ate a segment — quite sweet.

Yang Shaoyu choked. "You want to top Ke Yan?"

"Mm, but it didn't work."

"It'd be strange if it had."

"It could have, actually." Jiang Qi pursed his lips.

"And then at the end you got too embarrassed to follow through, right." Yang Shaoyu laughed.

"How did you know!" Jiang Qi was surprised.

"That's the psychological process of a little shou — wants to top, but thin-skinned, so how could he go through with topping; better just be your good little shou."

"Hmph, you sure know a lot." Jiang Qi wrinkled his nose.

"Of course, I go both ways."

"Tch, aren't you afraid of catching something."

"I may be a flirt, but I don't go to bed with all of them." As Yang Shaoyu spoke he glanced toward the door — someone seemed to be outside.

Outside, Lin Zirui hid beside the doorway with a chart. So Yang Shaoyu went both ways and could accept a man. Nervous, Lin Zirui peeked into the room just as Yang Shaoyu looked over. He ducked out of sight and, rather spinelessly, fled.

Seeing Yang Shaoyu keep looking toward the door, Jiang Qi turned to look — no one there. "What are you looking at?"

"Nothing; someone seemed to be over there."

"No one; you must be mistaken. Right — where's Doctor Lin?"

"Zirui?" Yang Shaoyu gave a little smile.

"Zirui? You're on quite familiar terms with him."

Yang Shaoyu cleared his throat and shooed Jiang Qi off. "Hurry home, or Ke Yan will come round to drag you off."

"I'll head back then; take good care of that leg and get back to work soon."

"You really are getting more and more like Ke Yan."

The moment Jiang Qi walked out of the doctor's, he saw Ke Yan not far off; he smiled and made to dash over, but the big movement suddenly tugged at his backside, and he froze — sss, a bit sore.

Seeing Jiang Qi suddenly halt in place, Ke Yan came over. "What is it?"

"Nothing, nothing."

"Backside hurts?" Ke Yan guessed from Jiang Qi's silence.

"Mm, let's get in the car." Jiang Qi let Ke Yan lead him by the hand to the car.

In the car, Jiang Qi buckled his seatbelt; as they drove, he looked out the window, and several wedding cars passed by. Watching them, Jiang Qi felt he'd forgotten something.

"Right — when's Xue Cheng getting married." Jiang Qi remembered; he had no idea where he'd put the invitation Xue Cheng had given him.

"No idea." Ke Yan had clearly forgotten too.

"Didn't he mention it when you did business with him?"

"He may have." Ke Yan recalled Xue Cheng saying something, but hadn't bothered to remember.

"Let me look." Jiang Qi found a slightly crumpled invitation in his briefcase.

"It's the day after tomorrow." Jiang Qi checked the date.

"Mm." Ke Yan suddenly turned the car around. "Let's go buy clothes."

"Buy what clothes?"

"For the wedding banquet."

Actually, "the two of them going to buy clothes" really meant buying clothes for Jiang Qi.

Ke Yan brought Jiang Qi to a suit shop; looking at the various styles, Jiang Qi said, "Don't I have plenty of suits already?"

"How about this one?" Ke Yan held up a white suit.

"The style's a bit ornate." Jiang Qi looked it over — it looked exactly like something a groom would wear.

"Try it on." Ke Yan clearly very much wanted to see Jiang Qi in it.

"All right then." Jiang Qi took the clothes and went into the fitting room.

Ke Yan went on looking at suits in the shop; behind him, a staff member kept staring at Ke Yan — this man is really handsome.

When Jiang Qi came out changed, Ke Yan's eyes lit up; in the white suit, Jiang Qi looked indescribably exquisite.

"Why do you keep staring at me." Made uneasy by Ke Yan's gaze.

"Very handsome." Ke Yan walked up to Jiang Qi, lips curving in a smile.

Jiang Qi's face went a little red.

Ke Yan took the bow tie a staff member handed over, meaning to tie it on Jiang Qi.

"Switch to a different one; it's not as if I'm the one getting married." Jiang Qi stopped him.

At that Ke Yan changed the tie.

"I'll do it myself."

"Let me." Ke Yan tied the tie for Jiang Qi, then stepped back to look at him.

"You want me to wear this the day after tomorrow?" Jiang Qi looked at himself in the white suit in the mirror.

"No, wear something else." Ke Yan had originally meant for Jiang Qi to wear this, but reversed himself at once — he didn't want others seeing such a good-looking Jiang Qi.

At that Jiang Qi went back to the fitting room to change; he'd known it.

In the end Jiang Qi bought a black suit.

The two hadn't had dinner yet, so they decided to eat out before heading home; passing a barbecue place, Jiang Qi couldn't help glancing inside a couple of times.

Ke Yan gave Jiang Qi's hand a squeeze. "Want to eat?"

"Mm." Jiang Qi looked in, eyes hungry.

"Then have a bit." Ke Yan opened the shop door.

Jiang Qi hurried in, afraid Ke Yan would change his mind.

The two didn't order much; Ke Yan grilled the food for Jiang Qi to eat.

Jiang Qi was drinking his drink when he suddenly saw a familiar back behind Ke Yan.

It was a child's back, a chubby little child — chubby little child? Right, wasn't that the chubby kid he'd met at the airport?

The chubby kid was sitting there alone eating barbecue; after a while, a man sat down across from him, probably having just gone to the toilet.

Jiang Qi stared blankly at the man, whose face struck him as very familiar, like, like...

Jiang Qi glanced at the Ke Yan sitting across from him — right, wasn't it that he looked a lot like Ke Yan?

Ke Yan finished grilling the skewers and saw Jiang Qi staring past him; he turned to look too, and blurted out, "Dad?"

Dad? Was it, was it really Ke Yan's father? No wonder he'd looked so familiar — the two really did look alike.

No, no — this wasn't the time to be discussing resemblance; the point was that Ke Yan's father was sitting at the next table.

And Ke Yan's father, hearing Ke Yan's voice, looked over, then stood up and walked toward them.
