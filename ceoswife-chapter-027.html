Chapter 27

Liu Hao's Birthday Request 刘浩的生日要求

July 2026

~3 min read

Jiang Qi came back to the dorm to find Liu Hao and Meng Qing both there — Meng Qing on his bed fiddling with the laptop, Liu Hao at the desk in a daze. "Didn't you two go to the mixer?"

"We meant to," Meng Qing said offhandedly, "but Zhang Gen next door cancelled it at the last minute, so it fell through."

"Oh." Jiang Qi turned his eyes to Liu Hao, who'd most wanted to go — and there he sat, distracted, fingers tapping the desktop.

Something happen? Jiang Qi went to Meng Qing's bedside. "What's wrong with Liu Hao?"

Meng Qing leaned in and lowered his voice. "He's been like this since he got back. Why don't you go ask?"

Jiang Qi eyed him suspiciously; it wasn't just Liu Hao acting odd — Meng Qing was too. Given his usual happy-go-lucky nature, would it ever fall to him to do the asking? He'd surely have asked long ago.

Seeing the suspicion, Meng Qing coughed guiltily and urged, "Go on, then."

Half believing, half doubting, Jiang Qi went over and patted Liu Hao's shoulder. "What's wrong?"

Liu Hao lifted his head. "Oh — just thinking about how to spend my birthday."

"Your birthday — when?" Jiang Qi asked, surprised.

"Tomorrow."

"Tomorrow... then what present do you want?"

"Mm..." Liu Hao stroked his chin and gave it serious thought. "Just bring one person to my birthday party, and that'll do."

"Bring who?" I don't know any great beauties.

"Ye Xingwei." Liu Hao grinned, the flesh of his cheeks bunching, rounding his already-roundish baby face still more.

"Ye Xingwei? Just tell him yourself — you're in the same dorm; he ought to come..." Jiang Qi paused. "Unless... you want to throw that kind of party..."

Liu Hao stood and draped a hand over his shoulder. "Jiang Qi, you're so clever."

Jiang Qi quietly removed the hand. "Actually I'm quite dim. Meng Qing's the clever one..."

Meng Qing, who'd been happily watching the show, instantly howled, "How did this suddenly land on me? And besides — Jiang Qi, you're obviously closer to Ye Xingwei than I am; the two of you get on quite well."

Jiang Qi shook his head, resolute. "No. That'd be so awkward."

At that Liu Hao sat back down and heaved a sigh. "Haa — to think nobody's willing to help me celebrate this year. My people skills really must be terrible. Haa~~"

Seeing him so crestfallen, Jiang Qi panicked and shook his head. "Don't say that, I'll—"

"You'll what?" Liu Hao pressed.

"Haa — fine, I'll go. But I can't promise he'll come." Jiang Qi decided to count it as a birthday present — though, a person as a gift? What a strange feeling.

Hearing him agree, Liu Hao got happily up. In truth he had a shrewd little plan: as long as Ye Xingwei came, a great many girls would show, and a heartthrob like that would surely only have eyes for the beauties — and Liu Hao wasn't greedy; a middling girl would more than satisfy him.

That night, the dorm nearly at lockup, Ye Xingwei finally came strolling back. Just as he reached it, Jiang Qi was coming out of the bathroom freshly showered. Ye Xingwei froze at the sight. Jiang Qi pointed outside, signaling him to step out a moment.

The two leaned against the railing. Jiang Qi wore blue pajamas, a blue towel draped over his head — a tiny bear cub drawn on it — hands resting lightly on the rail. The wind stirred the towel and his loose pajamas. "Tomorrow's Liu Hao's birthday," he said softly. "Will you come?"

"Sure. I've nothing on anyway."

"Mm... and, um?"

"What?"

"Nothing." Jiang Qi decided to leave it and let Meng Qing sort it out. There was still one thing unresolved, though — he had to tell Ke Yan he wouldn't be at the library tomorrow.

"Go get some sleep." Ye Xingwei turned back toward the dorm — and as he went, tugged the towel off Jiang Qi's head, slung it over his own shoulder, and went inside.
