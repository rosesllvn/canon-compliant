Because a colleague had fallen ill lately, Lin Zirui had swapped shifts with him, so for a while now Lin Zirui had been working nights.

Hands in the pockets of his white coat, he hesitated outside Yang Shaoyu's room over whether to go in; really he only wanted to look in on him the way an ordinary friend would, and besides he was the doctor — yet for some reason, why was he so on edge?

He drew a deep breath, opened the door, went in — and froze. What had he just seen?

He'd seen Yang Shaoyu airing out his "bird"?

Lin Zirui stared blankly at Yang Shaoyu's "bird," not reacting.

Yang Shaoyu was startled by the suddenly-opened door — who comes in without knocking; seeing Lin Zirui frozen in the doorway, Yang Shaoyu yanked the quilt over to cover himself and called out, "Lin Zirui?"

"Hm?" Lin Zirui answered reflexively, then snapped to, cleared his throat to cover the awkwardness, shut the door, and said, "What were you doing just now."

"They took the catheter out today, and I can't pee." Yang Shaoyu had been holding it all afternoon and had just pulled his trousers down to go when Lin Zirui walked in.

"Oh, that's normal; if you can't pee, rub your belly — rub it a while and it'll come."

"Mm, I'll try."

"I'll step out then." Lin Zirui turned to go, and as he shut the door remembered he seemed to have forgotten to knock just now — but, Yang Shaoyu's seemed a bit bigger than his.

Lin Zirui's face went a little red, and he cursed himself inwardly for being out of his mind.

The next day, Jiang Qi visited Yang Shaoyu with soup made by Auntie Li. Outside the room, he met Lin Zirui, the surgeon who had operated on Yang Shaoyu, and the two entered together. Jiang Qi set down the soup. "Remember to drink it. Auntie Li made it."

"Lucky it's not your cooking." Yang Shaoyu breathed a sigh of relief.

"Actually my cooking's pretty good too." Jiang Qi tossed off a defense, then headed out. "I won't chat — the hospital's out of parking spaces, and Ke Yan's waiting for me at the curb." And he left.

Yang Shaoyu smiled — a treat for his palate today.

Lin Zirui watched Jiang Qi's departing back; he knew this man was Yang Shaoyu's friend, and he sensed something familiar about Jiang Qi — could Jiang Qi be the same as him?

Back at the car, Jiang Qi found Ke Yan reading documents, and asked, curious, "What work — still reading after hours?"

"Not work; the company wants to recruit someone. Shaoyu was supposed to handle the talks, but now I'll have to go myself."

"How rare — what sort of person could make you this serious?" Jiang Qi teased.

"I'm not being serious; it's just work. All my seriousness these past years has gone to you." One sentence from Ke Yan left Jiang Qi stunned; he looked at Ke Yan, and Ke Yan smiled, and Jiang Qi's heart went thump, thump, thump.

Ke Yan went on, "Back from studying in England; her name's Sun Nianqing — capable, sharp; several firms besides Ke Group want to recruit her."

"That impressive?" Jiang Qi was surprised.

"Mm, a rare talent." If you want to recruit someone, you absolutely have to look into their family situation thoroughly.

Jiang Qi nodded, then dropped the subject and said to Ke Yan, "I want to buy a car; I got the license, and a license is useless without a car."

"So you'll drive yourself to work in the mornings too?"

"Mm, and when you're away on business I can drive myself."

"Drive yourself when I'm away on business; the rest of the time just ride with me to work." Ke Yan still liked Jiang Qi riding with him.

"Why?"

"No reason." Even without Ke Yan saying it Jiang Qi knew; actually Jiang Qi thought riding to work with Ke Yan was nice too — he could laze a bit in the car in the morning, and catch some sleep if there was traffic.

"All right then." Jiang Qi said.

"We're here." With that Ke Yan stopped the car.

"Drive carefully on your way home." Jiang Qi said goodbye to Ke Yan.

Jiang Qi's department had two new hires today, so they were going out for a good meal to welcome the new staff, at a barbecue place.

"Eat less; it'll give you heat, and don't drink too much." Ke Yan instructed.

Jiang Qi nodded and went in; he figured he was late.

He looked around the barbecue place but didn't find them; the place was big, and with a crowd it was hard to spot people, though a dozen or twenty were findable enough. Jiang Qi looked here and there and finally found them.

He walked over; they'd already ordered. Seeing Jiang Qi, they first said he was late, and Jiang Qi smiled. "Sorry, traffic on the way."

Xiao An handed Jiang Qi the menu. "Want to order anything more?"

Jiang Qi took the menu but didn't look, shaking his head; he wasn't picky about food, and could eat all of this.

Jiang Qi soon found everyone loved spicy food; the sauce brushed on the grilled meat was strong and fiery.

But Jiang Qi ate with relish, getting through a dozen-odd skewers already.

"Manager, don't just eat grilled meat — have some beer." Chang Qing of Jiang Qi's department called out.

Jiang Qi didn't decline, and downed a big glass in one go.

Seeing Jiang Qi drink, everyone egged him on to have a couple more.

Jiang Qi usually wasn't too strict at work and was quite easygoing, so people weren't very afraid of him — but if someone made a mistake at work, Jiang Qi became like a different person.

Egged on, Jiang Qi couldn't help downing a few more glasses; the icy beer alongside the strong, spicy barbecue made Jiang Qi forget Ke Yan's instructions — without realizing it he'd both drunk, and eaten the spicy grilled meat.

When the gathering finally broke up, Jiang Qi felt a dull ache in his belly; stepping out of the barbecue place, the night wind blew over him, making his head ache a little but clearing it some too. He sniffed his clothes — all beer and barbecue smell, truly foul.

Jiang Qi didn't dare see Ke Yan like this; he cabbed home, took out his key, and opened the door as quietly as he could.

There was a bathroom downstairs at home too, though usually Ke Yan and Jiang Qi showered in the one off the bedroom; but today Jiang Qi didn't dare go up to the room to see Ke Yan smelling like this, so he showered downstairs, tossed his dirty clothes in the bathroom basket, and went up in a bathrobe.

Ke Yan wasn't asleep yet; when Jiang Qi came into the room he was still reading. Ke Yan looked up and saw Jiang Qi already showered. "Why didn't you come up to wash?"

"Oh, oh, because I was all over barbecue grease, so I washed downstairs." Jiang Qi was a little nervous.

"Mm." Ke Yan asked nothing more.

Jiang Qi let out a breath and quickly slipped off to the toilet to brush his teeth — he had to brush away the smell of liquor and barbecue.

Done brushing, Jiang Qi came out; Ke Yan set down his book to sleep. Jiang Qi climbed into bed, Ke Yan pulled him down to lie, and was about to turn off the light to sleep when he noticed Jiang Qi's red, swollen lips.

Not very swollen, just a little puffy — the way they usually looked right after kissing Ke Yan.

Ke Yan naturally wouldn't suspect Jiang Qi of kissing someone else, so he touched Jiang Qi's lips. "Why so red — did you eat something spicy?"

"No, no, no, absolutely not." Jiang Qi shook his head in denial.

"Oh, drink more water tomorrow; looks like you've got a bit of heat. Sleep." With that Ke Yan gathered Jiang Qi in to sleep.

Jiang Qi relaxed, nestled into Ke Yan's arms, and was soon drowsy.

In the middle of the night, Jiang Qi woke clutching his belly — sss, his stomach ached a little, and he badly needed the toilet.

Jiang Qi slowly eased out of Ke Yan's arms, got up from the bed, and walked slowly toward the toilet — mustn't wake Ke Yan.

Ke Yan hadn't been woken by Jiang Qi at first, but with Jiang Qi going back and forth, from toilet to bed and bed to toilet, however softly he moved, Ke Yan was woken in the end.

And so, Jiang Qi — done for!!!!
