The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 1

Good Morning 早上好

July 2026

~3 min read

Jiang Qi was a man, and the man he loved was Ke Yan.

Ke Yan was the CEO of the Ke Group — wealthy, handsome, born to all the right things. For all the coldness he wore like a second suit, he was the most eligible bachelor in the city, the man every woman pictured herself marrying. And the one he'd chosen was Jiang Qi.

Jiang Qi was nobody in particular — a junior manager at the same company. They had met by accident in their university years, two ordinary young men who, somewhere along the way, became something the world would have called otherwise. After graduation Jiang Qi joined the Ke Group and climbed from clerk to manager one unremarkable rung at a time, and all the while, beneath everyone's notice, he and Ke Yan — by then master of the company — carried on a quiet affair.

But their story is better begun on an ordinary morning.

Sunlight came through the window in a single thread and settled over the sleeping man. Jiang Qi surfaced slowly, groped for the clock on the nightstand, and squinted at its face: half past six. He turned to the man beside him — home only since the small hours, back at last from a contract abroad — and felt his chest tighten. Three nights of bad sleep had bruised faint shadows beneath those eyes, and still he was beautiful; without the frost he carried through his waking hours, there was something almost boyish in his face.

Jiang Qi pressed his palms to his own cheeks, then eased out of bed, careful as a thief, unwilling to cost the man a single minute of rest.

Washed and dressed, he went down to put breakfast together — set a pot of congee to simmer, took the small dishes his mother had made from the refrigerator, arranged them on plates and carried them to the table. Cooking was not among his gifts. He could manage plain rice and a stir-fry or two, and even those came out wrong, underseasoned one day and too salty the next. Ke Yan never said a word about it. He ate everything, every time.

A glance at the wall clock sent Jiang Qi back upstairs to see whether Ke Yan was awake.

He opened the bedroom door to an empty bed.

"Hm? Up already?" He tried the bathroom — no one. He tried the dressing room, and there was Ke Yan, dressed, in the middle of knotting his tie. Jiang Qi crossed to him, took the tie from his hands, and knotted it himself. "You're up so early. Why not sleep a little longer?"

"Mm — I noticed you were awake." Ke Yan drew the words out slowly, in the husky voice particular to early mornings.

Jiang Qi's hands didn't stop their work, but his face warmed despite him.

Ke Yan looked down at him — the faint flush, the soft pink mouth, unusually lovely — tipped up his chin, and kissed him.

His tongue slipped past Jiang Qi's lips, found his, and tangled with it.

Only when they were both breathing unevenly did he draw back. He pulled Jiang Qi into his arms. "Good morning, Jiang Qi," he murmured, low and rough.
