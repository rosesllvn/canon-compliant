The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 12

Love Rival, or Matchmaker? 做情敌，还是媒人?

July 2026

~4 min read

Jiang Qi got to the office very early that day — only to run into someone at the elevators. Wang Siqi?

"Good morning, Director Wang."

Wang Siqi wore her hair in shoulder-length curls today, with a pale lavender sleeveless top and a white skirt: the effect cool and refined. It was the very outfit Xiao An had held up from a magazine last week, murmuring over how nice it was.

"Oh — Manager Jiang. Good morning." Wang Siqi tugged her lips into an elegant smile, though inwardly she was put out; the mere thought of that meal the other day made her temper flare.

Ding— the elevator came, and they stepped in.

"I hear you're acquainted with Assistant Yang Shaoyu?" she asked, breaking the quiet.

"Yes, I know him."

"Then—" Before she could finish, the elevator arrived. Stepping out, she said, "Manager Jiang — if you're free after work, shall we have a chat?"

Jiang Qi hesitated. "All right."

As the doors slid shut, the realization caught up with him: where were they supposed to meet?

At lunch, he and Xiao An ate across from each other in the company canteen. Chopsticks in one hand, spoon in the other, Xiao An gestured as she talked. "Jiang Qi, did you see Wang Siqi today? Dressed to the nines, and gorgeous on top of it. Tell me, why am I so plain? Plain's one thing, but I've got a steamed-bun face besides." (On the clock she called him Manager; off it, by name.)

Looking at her woebegone face, Jiang Qi couldn't help a laugh. "There's nothing wrong with a steamed-bun face!"

She rolled her eyes to the ceiling. "You really are hopeless at this. This is the part where you say a steamed-bun face is cute, or something along those lines." She propped her cheeks in both hands and batted her eyes at him.

Jiang Qi promptly broke out in goosebumps.

"But I'd so much rather be an elegant beauty!"

"Stop thinking about it and eat. I'm done — I'll head off first."

"Ah — you can't not wait for me! Where are your gentlemanly manners?" Xiao An squawked at the top of her lungs.

Jiang Qi had no choice but to sit back down and wait.

✦   ✦   ✦

The workday ended. Jiang Qi met Wang Siqi at the company entrance.

"Shall we have a coffee across the way?" she asked, all smiles.

"All right — let's go."

They sat down, each ordered a coffee, and Wang Siqi came straight to the point. "You said you know Assistant Yang — so are you friends, or...?"

"Friends, and classmates too." Inwardly Jiang Qi wondered: does she like Yang Shaoyu?

"Oh." She paused. "Then are you close with CEO Ke?" A faint blush surfaced as she spoke.

Jiang Qi's heart gave a lurch. He understood her now, and all at once the coffee cup in his hand felt heavy — heavy with the urge to fling its contents in that exquisite face.

Oblivious, she went on, still smiling. "Assistant Yang and CEO Ke are good friends, so you must—"

"Assistant Yang and I are friends, but I'm not close with the CEO." Jiang Qi cut her off.

"Is that so?" The smile vanished in an instant, her tone cooling. She'd decided he was deliberately refusing to help her.

Well — she wasn't actually wrong.

Just then his phone rang: Auntie Li. "Hello? Jiang Qi, I'll be coming to cook rather late today, and there's nothing left in the fridge. Would you go to the supermarket and buy some? I'm sorry to ask."

"All right, I'll go. Goodbye."

He gave Wang Siqi an apologetic smile. "Something's come up — I'll head off. It's my treat today." He laid the money on the table and took his leave.

Watching him go, Wang Siqi's exquisite face gave way to a grinding of teeth. Just then a slightly plump man took the seat Jiang Qi had vacated, wringing his hands and stammering, "D-Director Wang, I — I've liked you for a long time. Please — please accept me." He bowed his head in his nerves, beads of sweat rolling off his forehead in fat drops.

Wang Siqi shot him a disdainful glance, picked up her handbag, stood, and tossed out one cold sentence: "You think you're worthy? Go take a look in a mirror." And without a backward glance, she walked out.
