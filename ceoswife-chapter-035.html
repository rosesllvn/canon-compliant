The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 35

The Place 地方

July 2026

~5 min read

Through a long, desolate little path, what met his eye was a stretch of shrubbery and a few tall trees. Parting the bushes, Jiang Qi found a very small lawn; parting another clump, he looked all around. Is Ke Yan really here?

He'd gone to find Ke Yan a little earlier and missed him — running into Yang Shaoyu instead, who'd looked at him with a face full of astonishment, then shot him a glare and told him where Ke Yan was. Watching Yang Shaoyu walk off muttering, Jiang Qi had wondered: is this guy all right? Why's he so strange?

Pushing through the leaf-covered bushes, the way seeming to grow more remote the further he went — where is this? Surely I haven't come to the wrong place.

By the time he pushed his way out and saw Ke Yan, he'd lost count of how many bushes he'd struggled through. Stepping softly across the grass, he came up before Ke Yan — who, perhaps from waiting too long, had actually fallen asleep, a half-turned book lying open over his face, sprawled there on the grass.

Jiang Qi couldn't help recalling a fairy tale from childhood: the prince hacks away the thorns outside the castle, enters, finds the sleeping princess, and presses a kiss to her lips. The thought made him want to laugh — he was no prince, Ke Yan certainly no princess, and besides, they were both men. His rising smile sank back down. He reached out and gently lifted the book from Ke Yan's face.

Ke Yan opened his eyes, startling Jiang Qi so badly he nearly clapped the book right back down. Ke Yan seized his hand, pulled him down, and with a turn pinned him beneath him.

"Hm? Ke Yan?" Jiang Qi looked up, eyes full of confusion.

Ke Yan's gaze darkened. He took the book that had fallen to the grass and laid it over Jiang Qi's eyes, blocking his view.

By the time Jiang Qi pulled it away, Ke Yan was already back against the tree trunk, reading.

The atmosphere was a little strange — or was it just something strange in his own heart? Jiang Qi glanced at the book in his hand — the very one he hadn't finished before. He went and sat beside Ke Yan, leaning against the trunk too, the rough bark prickling his back. "How did you find this place?"

"Shaoyu found it."

"Does he come here to read too?" It was a good spot — cool in the tree's shade, no glare in the eyes.

"He comes here to pick up girls."

"Huh?" That hadn't occurred to Jiang Qi. "Did we come to watch him pick up girls?"

Ke Yan was tickled. "I've no wish to watch that fellow chase girls." A pause. "Anyway, he's gone to the library to do it now."

Jiang Qi blinked, then laughed. "True enough — the library's got more girls."

Ke Yan narrowed his eyes and gave a cold little smile.

When Yang Shaoyu had seen that cold smile earlier, the grin on his own face had frozen. When someone who never smiles at you suddenly does, it's a most peculiar feeling — especially Ke Yan, who by Yang Shaoyu's reckoning had smiled fewer than five times in his life; a cold smile at that, so gooseflesh had risen all over him, and he'd forced out two dry laughs. "What do you need from me?"

And sure enough, a little while later, Yang Shaoyu — bent double, clutching his stomach, eyes brimming — had given up his precious dating spot, bitterness in his heart. Well — he'd had it coming. How did he manage to just blurt it out.

Giving it up was one thing, but as Yang Shaoyu turned to find a pretty girl to pour out his woes to, "Wait." Ke Yan stopped him. "Tell Jiang Qi I'm here."

So Yang Shaoyu turned around in grief and indignation and went off to find Jiang Qi, muttering all the way: such a good spot, and instead of a girlfriend he brings a man — what for? Can two men even date? How would two men go on a date? His dating spot stolen, his plan to vent to a beauty redirected into errand duty, he was more miserable than ever — but not daring to defy Ke Yan, he could only mutter to let off steam. Then a thought flashed through his head and stopped him short. Hold on. Two men dating, that's nothing, but — Ke Yan and Jiang Qi? No way.

His face turned somewhat ugly, and stayed ugly all the way to finding Jiang Qi — which was how Jiang Qi came to feel he was acting bizarrely.

As for the party in question — he was at that moment lying on Ke Yan's lap, sound asleep. A little insect flitted about the tip of his nose, and Ke Yan swatted it away with the book.

Jiang Qi felt a cool breeze across his face, opened his eyes, and what met them was a white shirt; following it up — Ke Yan. He scrambled up off the lap in fright, no idea when he'd fallen asleep or how long he'd slept. "How long was I out?"

Ke Yan didn't answer, only reached out and wiped at the corner of his mouth — at which Jiang Qi's face went instantly red, wishing he could find a hole to crawl into. He'd actually drooled.
