Chapter 121

Ke Yan, Who Makes Jiang Qi's Wishes Come True 实现江奇愿望的柯言

July 2026

~4 min read

Ke Yan's office, ordinarily a quiet place to work, was suddenly invaded by two people. Mo Lü glared daggers at Ke Yan and Yang Shaoyu.

"We vanished for days — did neither of you even notice?"

"Didn't you go to the old house?" Ke Yan went on reading his documents, unmoved.

"How did you know?"

"You didn't say you were going abroad, and vanishing for days for no reason could only mean the old house." Yang Shaoyu was loafing on the office sofa.

"Yes, we went to the old house." Ma Tong said it without expression. "We can spend the New Year in-country, but we have to get the domestic branch properly managed."

"Is that so? Tell me if you need help." Ke Yan looked up.

"Obviously, if we need help we'll come to you."

"By the way, Ke Yan — you're proposing?" Yang Shaoyu suddenly remembered what Sun Nianqing had asked him yesterday.

"Mm." Ke Yan thought of the ring hidden at home.

"Seems Sun Nianqing saw you buying the ring — she asked me about it the other day, whether the person was someone in the company."

"Did she?" Ke Yan smiled. She'd find out who soon enough.

"Really? You're going to propose to Jiang Qi?" Mo Lü and Ma Tong were astonished.

"Mm. This month."

"Which day?"

"…" Ke Yan didn't answer Mo Lü — she had a big mouth, and he was afraid she'd come back and make trouble.

"If you're proposing, it has to be romantic—it only happens once in a lifetime." Ma Tong's eyes sparkled.

"Right — others might get several, but for you and Jiang Qi it's just the once, your whole lives."

Ke Yan could not make everyone bless their relationship, but Jiang Qi's family had to accept them. Before Christmas, he would give Jiang Qi the best Christmas possible.

Ke Yan had considered going to Jiang Qi's parents himself and getting them to accept their relationship — but he knew Jiang Qi, and if he really did that, Jiang Qi would blame him.

In Jiang Qi's words, it was their relationship, so naturally they had to face it together.

So that evening at home Ke Yan brought it up, and Jiang Qi thought a moment, then nodded. "Let's go this weekend, then. But — why the sudden change of mind? Didn't you say we'd go after the New Year?"

"Because I want you to have a Christmas with your family's blessing."

"Hm? What do you mean?" The words struck Jiang Qi as a little odd.

"Nothing. You'll see when the time comes."

"All right then." Jiang Qi didn't press, only turned to talking with Ke Yan about the shop across the way. "Those two shops opposite the company seem to have changed hands — they've been gutting the fittings lately."

"Have they? You're interested?" Ke Yan caught that Jiang Qi cared about the shops changing hands.

"I am. It's a good location, right across from the company. If I retired early, I could open a bookshop there. Maybe we could adopt a child. We'd have breakfast together, take the child to preschool, then head to work in the same direction. I could meet you for lunch, pick up the child in the afternoon, and come to the company so we'd all go home together. Simple—but happy."

Ke Yan held him. "You'll have it. There'll be a child — whatever you want, I'll make it happen for you."

"Mm." Jiang Qi's nose stung with sudden tears. To be loved, cherished, and treated with such unreserved devotion left him deeply content.

"Silly." To the suddenly-emotional Jiang Qi, Ke Yan said only that one word, guessing his eyes had gone red.

They had — though the corners of his mouth were tilted high, very happy.

The next day Jiang Qi went to work in good spirits, not the least bit nervous about taking Ke Yan to meet his parents Saturday — because he had to believe Ke Yan could win them over.

And Ke Yan, on arriving, immediately went across the street again, because the gutting was done; the young man in charge had called to report.

Ke Yan hung up and went to see for himself, then told the young man, "Start the fit-out tomorrow. Take the workers for a good meal today — bill it to the company."

"Right, right." The young man nodded.

Ke Yan was soon back at the office. Xiao Feng, seeing him head out, wondered where the CEO kept going.

Back in his office, Ke Yan emailed Jiang Qi the thing he'd had prepared.

When Jiang Qi saw the email, he paused, then opened it: Design your own bookshop.

Because it came from Ke Yan, he assumed it followed last night's conversation. He never imagined that the bookshop he designed would actually be built—or that everything he wanted would come true.

This was Ke Yan. The Ke Yan who made Jiang Qi's wishes come true. The Ke Yan who made Jiang Qi happy.
