The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 132

Mother Jiang and Ke Yan sat facing each other on the sofa. She spoke first. "To begin with — your parents? Will they allow you and Jiang Qi to be together?"

"They've already agreed."

"Even if they have — what about children? Your family will want grandchildren. What will you do, find a woman to bear them?"

"No." Ke Yan's face went serious. "Never. In this life I have only Jiang Qi; I'll never look to anyone else. As for children — my sister has already had a boy, and will have more. There's no need to worry."

"Is that so." She relaxed a little, no longer so taut.

"We'll likely adopt someday, because Jiang Qi loves children. We can't have our own, but we'll find a newborn so the child can grow up close and devoted to him." Ke Yan spoke earnestly of plans already considered.

"Good, then. I was against the two of you — against it because I thought, with your standing, what if your family didn't agree? And there's one thing I worry about most: that one day you'll fall for someone else…"

"I'll stake my life on it — I will never love anyone but Jiang Qi. Jiang Qi is the only person I've loved my whole life, the only one I'd give all of myself to cherish. I can't leave him; I can't even imagine myself without him."

"…" This man was formidable to the world, yet before her he laid out his heart with unmistakable nerves, his whole face brightening whenever he spoke of Jiang Qi. She was moved despite herself.

And she couldn't bear for Jiang Qi to be hurt over this either. Perhaps Ke Yan could be trusted.

"I hope you'll do as you've said. If I find a single way in which you treat Jiang Qi badly, I'll make him leave you, and marry, and have children."

"Don't worry — it'll never happen. I'd never let such a thing happen." Ke Yan smiled, happy. "Thank you, truly."

"If you want to thank me, be good to Jiang Qi."

"I will." Ke Yan stood and bowed deeply to her in thanks.

Jiang Qi woke and looked around. How had he fallen asleep? Where was Ke Yan?

He sat up, smoothed his sleep-mussed hair, got out of bed, saw the thermos cup on the nightstand, poured a cup and drank — refreshing.

Leaving the room, he was astonished to see Ke Yan and his mother sitting in the living room talking — and seemingly quite happily.

He pinched his cheek. Ow — that hurt. But what was going on?

"What are you two talking about?"

Mother Jiang and Ke Yan turned to see Jiang Qi in his sweater, hair faintly mussed, standing in the doorway.

"Nothing much — come have some peanut soup." She ladled him a bowl of sweet peanut soup.

"Oh." Jiang Qi sat down beside Ke Yan and took a sip.

"All right, you two drink up — I'll go buy some groceries. Your brother's back today, and with you here too, I'll make some of the things you like." With that, under Jiang Qi's astonished gaze, she went to fetch Yun Hui to shop with her.

Jiang Qi turned to Ke Yan. "What happened? I take one nap and my mom seems to have accepted us?"

"She has." Ke Yan cupped Jiang Qi's face and kissed him — sweet, with a taste of peanut. "Mom agreed. She told me to be good to you all my life."

"Really?" Jiang Qi shot to his feet in excitement, the peanut soup nearly spilling.

He set the bowl down at once and threw himself at Ke Yan, hugging him. "Really? Really? I'm so happy."

"Silly." Ke Yan held him too.

"Ke Yan, Ke Yan, Ke Yan — thank you for letting me meet you." Jiang Qi murmured his name, over and over, in thanks.

Ke Yan said nothing, only held him tight, his chest brimming with a happiness that made him grateful to have met Jiang Qi.

Meeting was fate; love meant treasuring it. Jiang Qi's happiness was Ke Yan, and Ke Yan's was Jiang Qi. They would remain this happy, always.

When Jiang Lü got home, what she saw was her second brother, her second-brother-husband, her eldest brother, and Father Jiang all sitting on the sofa watching television and chatting.

She rubbed her eyes. Was she seeing right? Hadn't Mom refused to let brother-husband in?

And — thinking of what had happened at the company that afternoon — surely the CEO hadn't skipped the meeting to be here? He'd kept the whole upper management waiting nearly an hour.

That afternoon, all the company's senior staff had arrived at the conference room on time, ready to begin — all, of course, except Jiang Qi.

Yang Shaoyu had taken leave and gone to find Lin Zirui. Certain that Ke Yan would return for the meeting, he had left Xiao Feng in charge.

And so this was the result.

"Xiao Lü? What are you doing standing in the doorway?" Yun Hui came out of the kitchen with dishes and saw Jiang Lü standing there rubbing her eyes.

"How are Brother and Ke Yan here?" Jiang Lü found saying "Ke Yan" outright rather unfamiliar, and a touch irreverent.

"You don't know — Mom's already agreed to them being together." Yun Hui told her, smiling.

"Really? Wonderful." Delighted, Jiang Lü pulled out her phone and texted Xiao An.

At dinner Jiang Qi and Ke Yan sat together, Jiang Lü across from them, chopsticks in hand, watching the two of them smile their foolish smiles.

Wasn't this every fujoshi's wish? How wonderful that her brother had brought home a brother-husband like this.

Jiang Qi first served Father Jiang the things he liked, then put potato in Ke Yan's bowl.

Ke Yan took a bite — utterly unlike anything Jiang Qi cooked, very good — smiled, peeled a shrimp, dipped it in sauce, and set it in Jiang Qi's bowl.

Jiang Qi ate it, entirely used to this by now.

Watching the natural ease of it, Mother Jiang thought Ke Yan really was just as Father Jiang had said — he took good care of Jiang Qi. No — he doted on him.
