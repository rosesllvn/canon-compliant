Chapter 29

Drink, Then... 喝酒啊。。。

July 2026

~4 min read

While Jiang Qi listened to Qiao Mi'er stammer, Meng Qing — out hunting someone to drink with — spotted him and came over, a little tipsy, and clapped his shoulder. "Jiang Qi, what are you up to?"

Jiang Qi looked up. "Chatting."

"Stop chatting — come drink." At which Qiao Mi'er glanced up at Jiang Qi, nervous.

He didn't notice her watching, but refused at once. "Not going." He'd seen his own elder brother come home soused like mud of an evening, raving in his cups, and the next day laid up wilted with a headache.

"It's not every day it's Liu Hao's birthday — surely you'll drink one with him."

Jiang Qi thought it over and conceded the point. "One glass, then."

The words were barely out before Meng Qing hauled him off to Liu Hao's table.

Qiao Mi'er watched him go, so distressed her eyes reddened. I hadn't even said anything — how did he just leave? It's my own fault for being useless, not able to get such simple words out. Haa~ Her sisters, watching from the side, hurried over to console her. "It's all right, there'll be another chance — don't be sad."

The moment Jiang Qi sat down, Liu Hao, plainly drunk, thrust a glass of red wine at him. "Jiang Qi, drink up." Jiang Qi looked at the red liquid. One glass won't get me drunk, surely? He took a small sip and grimaced — truly nasty.

Meng Qing egged him on. "Down it in one." Liu Hao chimed in. "Yeah, yeah, another."

Jiang Qi couldn't help rolling his eyes. These two.

The girls at the table had drunk no small amount either, and had long since shed their daytime reserve, joining in the clamor of "Drink! Drink!" Girls in their cups are not to be underestimated. Helpless, Jiang Qi tipped his head back, and the glass went down.

After who knew how long, his head had gone fuzzy, the sounds around him seeming to shrink, and so sleepy — sitting there, his eyes drifted shut.

"Come on, time for cake," someone called near his ear. But he was so sleepy; better sleep a little more.

Deep asleep, Jiang Qi didn't notice the two who sat down on either side of him — but he heard the girls' piercing shrieks, frowned, and then heard the name Ke Yan. Ke Yan — how could he be here? I must have misheard.

Ke Yan, to Jiang Qi's right, looked at him sleeping soundly and couldn't help a frown. He can fall asleep even here? Far too unguarded. Ke Yan was here for Yang Shaoyu's birthday; the two had arrived earliest, and he'd gone straight to the corner sofa with its back to the door. Knowing he disliked noise, Yang Shaoyu had only called him over now, for the cake. Just then Jiang Qi's head dipped, as though he might wake, and Ke Yan drew his gaze back.

But Jiang Qi didn't wake. His neck only ached, so his head tipped sideways — and landed squarely on the shoulder of Ye Xingwei, to his left.

Mid-drink, Ye Xingwei felt the sudden weight and turned to find Jiang Qi asleep against him, sound as anything. His face betrayed nothing, but inwardly he was amused. Sleeping so soundly. He leaned in for a sniff at the flushed face. He's been drinking too? Then he felt a cold gaze on him, looked up — and found nothing. What the devil?

Looking at those thin shoulders, Ye Xingwei wanted to gather him in — but hesitated. Should I? Lately something had felt strange in the way he looked at Jiang Qi, as though things couldn't go on like this. He frowned, and the lifted hand dropped. But — I still want to.

His hand rose again — and didn't reach Jiang Qi, because halfway there someone blocked it.

Ye Xingwei looked up — Ke Yan. How is it him?

The two didn't know each other, but had each heard the other's name, and crossed paths now and then on campus.

"Something you want?" Ye Xingwei said.

Ke Yan raised an eyebrow. "No."

Ye Xingwei frowned and tried to shake the hand off — and couldn't. He was startled; he hadn't expected Ke Yan to be so strong. He put more force into it, and the motion jostled Jiang Qi, who lifted his head, rubbed his neck, rubbed his eyes, and stretched. "Mmh~"

Ke Yan let go, swept the half-awake Jiang Qi up, stood, and said to Yang Shaoyu at the cake, "I'm heading back."

Yang Shaoyu blinked. "All right."

Everyone watched them leave, and the girls breathed out in relief. "Thank goodness Jiang Qi isn't a girl."

"A guy's plenty dangerous too," Yang Shaoyu muttered, and as he muttered took out his phone and snapped a photo. "Come on, stop staring, have some cake."

As for Ye Xingwei — his expression just then was a hard thing to look at: the look of a man whose meat, already in hand, had been snatched clean away by someone else without the slightest effort.
