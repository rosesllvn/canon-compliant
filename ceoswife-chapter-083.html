Jiang Qi stayed two more days and finally came back with the deal negotiated.

It had been raining nonstop; Jiang Qi stood outside the airport with his luggage, waiting for a car.

Li Jing stood to one side; Sun Ye, looking at the rain, grumbled, "How does it just not stop."

"That's enough out of you — you've been going on about it nonstop since a moment ago." Li Jing, watching the downpour, was vexed too, and Sun Ye wouldn't stop talking.

"Yes yes yes, Sister Li, I'll stop." Sun Ye hurried to smile along.

Jiang Qi smiled and walked ahead; suddenly a child came running and bumped into him — a rather chubby child, looking fairly big, who slammed into Jiang Qi hard, then tipped backward and nearly sat down on his bottom.

With a suitcase in one hand, Jiang Qi reached for the child with the other. He missed and was pulled sideways instead. His grip loosened, the suitcase toppled, and Jiang Qi landed hard on the floor with the child in his lap.

The chubby child got up and said sheepishly to Jiang Qi, "Big brother, thank you." He paused, then added, "I'm so fat I made you fall." The little fatty even knew he was fat.

Jiang Qi got up too, patted his backside — and found his hand came away wet, covered in mud, because with the rain, the crowds coming and going had tracked muddy shoes over the once-clean floor outside the airport, so the ground was wet and muddy.

Because Jiang Qi was wearing a long coat, the coat was all dirty, though his trousers weren't.

"It's fine; just walk more carefully next time." Jiang Qi wiped his dirty hand on his dirty coat as he spoke to the little fatty.

"Sorry, sir; the child's mischievous." A pleasant, magnetic voice came from behind Jiang Qi.

Jiang Qi turned, and stared — this, how was this man so like him?

"I'll compensate you for your clothes." the man said again.

"No, no need, it's fine." Jiang Qi shook his head, then added, "I'll be off." He took the picked-up suitcase from Li Jing's hand.

The man holding the chubby child said nothing more; Jiang Qi walked on, hearing the little fatty's voice behind him, "Uncle, let's go."

Jiang Qi turned to ask Sun Ye, "Did you find a taxi?"

"No." Taxis on rainy days are always this popular.

"Wait a bit longer, then."

"Manager, want to go to the restroom and change clothes?" Li Jing said, pointing at the stains on Jiang Qi.

"No need; I'll change when I get home." Jiang Qi glanced down at his clothes.

"Jiang Qi." A familiar voice.

Jiang Qi looked up. "Why are you here?"

"Rain this heavy — to pick you up, of course."

Ke Yan got out with an umbrella, walked up to Jiang Qi, then handed another umbrella to Li Jing and Sun Ye.

Everyone put up umbrellas and loaded the luggage into the trunk; Ke Yan and Jiang Qi shared an umbrella over to the front passenger side. Jiang Qi opened the door, about to get in, then suddenly thought of something and took off his coat before sitting down.

Ke Yan shut the car door and asked Jiang Qi, "Did you fall?"

"No; I went to catch a kid and ended up falling myself." Jiang Qi smiled.

"You." Ke Yan shook his head with a smile, then went to turn on the heat; Jiang Qi stopped him. "It's not cold, what are you turning the heat on for."

At that Ke Yan didn't turn it on, and took off his own suit jacket to give to Jiang Qi.

The weather was turning cold by degrees now; even at home one layer felt a little cold.

Jiang Qi took the jacket and didn't dare look back, afraid of the strange looks from Li Jing and Sun Ye.

Li Jing watched the interaction between Ke Yan and Jiang Qi and was a little stunned — this was the CEO? How could he be so... uh, how to put it? How to describe it?

Right — Manager Jiang was the CEO's friend, but, this wasn't quite right...

Li Jing thought about it, then stopped thinking, and turned to look out the window.

Sun Ye wasn't as composed as Li Jing; riding in the CEO's beloved car — this trip was truly worth it — his eyes rolling busily between Jiang Qi and Ke Yan.

After dropping Li Jing and Sun Ye at a spot to catch their ride, Ke Yan drove home with Jiang Qi.

After dinner that evening, Jiang Qi rushed off to shower in a great hurry, and before going into the bathroom even told Ke Yan to hurry and shower downstairs.

Ke Yan laughed despite himself — we'll see whether he can still laugh in a bit.

When Jiang Qi came out of his shower, Ke Yan had already showered and was lying in bed reading.

"Ke Yan—" Jiang Qi called, and flung himself onto Ke Yan.

Ke Yan put an arm around Jiang Qi's waist.

Jiang Qi reached out to strip off Ke Yan's clothes; Ke Yan let him, so Jiang Qi smoothly got Ke Yan's clothes off.

Clothes off, looking at Ke Yan's bare chest, he got a bit nervous — usually Ke Yan undressed himself, all right?

Undressed, he kissed the corner of Ke Yan's mouth, lifted his head, and saw Ke Yan watching him with no good intent.

"What are you looking at?" Jiang Qi asked reflexively.

"Nothing, watching how you go on the offensive." Ke Yan answered with a smile.

At that, Jiang Qi, as if in challenge, kissed downward from Ke Yan's neck.

Ke Yan braced both hands behind his head and watched what Jiang Qi would do next.

Jiang Qi kissed down to the belly, looked at Ke Yan's trousers, reached out, hesitated a moment, pulled them down — and his face went red at once; somehow it felt rather shameless and roguish.

No, no — how's he to be the top like this, how's he to top Ke Yan, Jiang Qi thought, and made his movements a bit bolder than before.

His hands roamed over Ke Yan's body.

Jiang Qi figured he'd kissed everywhere worth kissing; it was time to... heh heh heh.

"You haven't taken your own clothes off yet." Seeing Jiang Qi grinning foolishly, Ke Yan reminded him.

Jiang Qi got awkward, face reddening, and slowly took off his own clothes.

Seeing Ke Yan watching him fixedly, that gaze plainly roguish.

What's there to be awkward about? What's there to be awkward about? Jiang Qi, growing more awkward the more Ke Yan watched, scolded himself inwardly for being so hopeless.

Head down, he sneaked a kiss on Ke Yan.

After kissing a while, Ke Yan, seeing Jiang Qi still kissing the upper half, took Jiang Qi's hand and guided it lower.

Jiang Qi's hand shrank back.

Ke Yan looked at Jiang Qi. "I do all this for you every time; if you want to top, you've got to make me feel good."

Jiang Qi thought about it — true enough; every time, Ke Yan made him feel wonderful, but...

"What is it, why'd you stop." Ke Yan urged.

Jiang Qi swallowed, and at last...

Jiang Qi suddenly collapsed onto Ke Yan. "Tired tonight; I'll top you tomorrow night."

"Actually tonight works fine."

So saying, Ke Yan steadied Jiang Qi, then took the lubricant and squeezed some onto him; one look and Jiang Qi understood at once, and said immediately, "No, this isn't what I want."

Ke Yan gave a push, and Jiang Qi let out a muffled grunt.

"Scoun— scoundrel Ke Yan. You liar."

"I didn't lie; I agreed to be on top, and you are pressed on top of me."

"That's not what this is." Jiang Qi said furiously.

Ke Yan quickened his pace, and Jiang Qi was driven past making any sound.

"Feels good, doesn't it." Ke Yan's voice murmured temptingly beside him; Jiang Qi, furious, gave Ke Yan a vicious pinch — how had he failed to top him after all.
