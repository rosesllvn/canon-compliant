Chapter 71

Ke Yan Wants a Gift 柯言要礼物

July 2026

~5 min read

Jiang Qi returned to the mall to buy Ke Yan a gift. Nothing in menswear appealed to him, so he decided on a tie. After searching several shops, he finally found one he liked—only for someone to snatch it up as he reached for it. It was Wang Siqi.

Seeing Jiang Qi, Wang Siqi was clearly startled too, then, noticing Jiang Qi seemed to want the very tie in her hand, went straight to pay for it.

Jiang Qi watched regretfully. It was rare to find a tie that suited Ke Yan so well. "Excuse me, do you have another one like that?"

"No, sir; we only had the one in the shop, and other shops likely won't have it either."

Jiang Qi felt regretful.

The clerk added, "But we'll have new stock tomorrow; you could come back tomorrow and look — you might find something you like."

Jiang Qi nodded.

He thought about whether to buy something else first, but decided to come back with Ke Yan tomorrow, and went home.

Back home he saw Ke Yan reading; Jiang Qi walked over and crouched by Ke Yan's feet.

"What is it?" Ke Yan asked.

"Afraid you'll be angry." Jiang Qi answered obediently.

Ke Yan sighed, pulled Jiang Qi up to sit on the sofa. "Get up; I'm not angry with you."

Sitting on the sofa, Jiang Qi heard this and lunged over to kiss Ke Yan hard; the kiss banged painfully against Ke Yan's mouth, but Ke Yan smiled.

Jiang Qi nestled contentedly into Ke Yan's arms and read along with him, his hand on Ke Yan's. After a while he said, "Come to the mall with me tomorrow."

"To do what?"

"Buy something."

"Mm, all right."

After work the next day, Jiang Qi brought Ke Yan back to the shop. The clerk remembered him at once. "The new styles are all over here."

Several looked promising. Jiang Qi chose one that suited Ke Yan, removed the tie he was wearing, and carefully knotted the new one in its place. The gesture was so routine at home that neither man considered how intimate it looked in public. The clerks certainly did.

Ke Yan watched Jiang Qi's hands tying his tie and asked, "You came yesterday?" From what the clerk had said to Jiang Qi, Ke Yan knew he'd been here the day before.

"Mm, I'd had my eye on a tie, but Manager Wang took it first." Jiang Qi got the tie tied and looked it over, very satisfied. "There — does it look good?"

"Mm." Ke Yan glanced in the mirror; he thought well of Jiang Qi's eye.

"Then try this one." Jiang Qi took out another.

Ke Yan let Jiang Qi fuss over him without stopping him.

"Ke Yan, your birthday's coming up — what gift do you want?" Jiang Qi asked.

"Isn't this it?" Ke Yan said, pointing at the tie.

"Of course not."

"Then I'll have to think." Ke Yan said with a soft laugh.

"Don't go thinking up anything outrageous." Jiang Qi said quickly.

"Mm." But how could he not?

When Wang Siqi came into the shop, this was the scene she saw: Jiang Qi tying Ke Yan's tie, the two talking, the picture warm beyond words.

Yesterday she had found Jiang Qi here; today, Ke Yan. Delighted, Wang Siqi checked her makeup and hair in a compact mirror before entering.

"Ke Yan, Jiang Qi, fancy running into you here."

Jiang Qi looked, puzzled, at the Wang Siqi who hadn't even acknowledged him yesterday — why so warm today.

Ke Yan only glanced at Wang Siqi, then said to the Jiang Qi who'd paused his hands, "Carry on."

"Oh, oh, okay."

Faced with Ke Yan's cold manner, Wang Siqi said, "I'm buying a gift for a friend. His birthday is in a few days." These things could not be rushed. Certain that her relationship with Ke Yan would soon progress, she said goodbye and went to shop elsewhere.

The two finished buying and decided to have dinner out before heading home; they ordered, and as they sat talking, they suddenly overheard the next table.

The next table was clearly a man and a woman; the man asked the woman, "What gift are you getting me for my birthday?"

The woman answered, "I don't know."

The man said, "Just give me yourself, then."

"You're awful." the woman said coquettishly.

Hearing this, Jiang Qi quickly drank some water, hoping inwardly Ke Yan hadn't heard.

But how could he not? The couple was so loud that only the deaf could have missed it.

Ke Yan said, "This year I won't ask for anything else; just wash up and give yourself to me."

Jiang Qi glared. "What night don't you do it — pick something else."

Ke Yan gave Jiang Qi a meaningful look and shook his head. "I don't mean the usual way."

"Then what way?" Foolish Jiang Qi walked right into the trap.

"Dress sexy."

Jiang Qi's face went red; he muttered under his breath: pervert.

Ke Yan heard. "What did you say?"

"Nothing."

"Is that so?" Ke Yan took a sip of water, rather looking forward to it.

After dinner, Jiang Qi kept turning the idea over. Did he really have to do that? No—his back would ache the next day, and his backside too.

Better think of something else to give; there were still a few days to Ke Yan's birthday anyway.

Ke Yan's birthday hadn't arrived yet, but Jiang Qi's driver's license had come through; he'd said before he'd take the test, and now he finally had it, so he eagerly had Ke Yan ride in his car.

Ke Yan got in, and Jiang Qi immediately told him to buckle his seat belt. "So little confidence?" Ke Yan asked.

"It's fine." Jiang Qi was a little sheepish.

"Drive, then." Ke Yan ruffled Jiang Qi's hair.

Jiang Qi eased the car into motion. Ke Yan watched his earnest face, unchanged after all these years, then turned to the passing scenery. Sitting beside Jiang Qi while he drove was not bad at all.

The day finally came — Ke Yan's birthday. From early morning Ke Yan was looking forward to what surprise Jiang Qi would give him.
