The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 122

Arriving 到家

July 2026

~7 min read

Ke Yan and Jiang Qi had already decided to visit Jiang Qi's family on Saturday when Mother Jiang unexpectedly called. "Xiao Qi, bring that friend you live with over for a meal this week."

"Why the sudden urge to have Ke Yan to dinner?" Jiang Qi was a little thrown — they'd already decided to go this week, and now his mother was calling to have him bring Ke Yan for dinner.

"To thank him for taking such good care of you. He's handsome, good-tempered, gentle, and well-mannered. Let your father meet such a fine young man too. Maybe we'll take him as a godson." Her enthusiasm mounted with every word.

"He'll be your son soon enough — no need to take him on as a godson," Jiang Qi muttered under his breath.

"What's that?" She hadn't caught it.

"Nothing. I said I'll definitely bring Ke Yan home this Saturday to see you and Dad."

"Good. I'll hang up, then." Satisfied and pleased, she rang off.

Jiang Qi promptly called Ke Yan, who picked up before the second ring. "Hello."

"Ke Yan, my mom just called — she wants you and me to come home for a meal Saturday."

"What?" Ke Yan didn't follow at first either.

"Mom doesn't know we were already planning to go this week. She just wants to thank you for looking after me, so she's having you to dinner — said she even wants to take you on as a godson."

Ke Yan laughed softly, not having expected Mother Jiang to think so well of him. "Let's go home together Saturday, then — to meet your mother-in-law."

"It's mother-in-law the other way round. All right, I'll let you go — bye."

Ke Yan hung up too, the warmth from talking with Jiang Qi still on his face.

But when he looked up at the two people still standing in his office, it went cold. "Continue."

Lin Zheng and Fan Teng had been reporting when the phone rang. Ke Yan initially frowned, but one look at the caller ID softened both his face and voice.

Both Lin Zheng and Fan Teng kept their heads down, eavesdropping. Fan Teng's mind worked: who was the CEO talking to? A girlfriend? Hadn't the word been there was no girlfriend? Maybe the rumor was wrong — there'd even been talk that the CEO was, well… seemed not, then.

Lin Zheng kept his head down too, face blank, but thought privately: it had to be Jiang Qi. Who else had the power to make Ke Yan like this?

Before long Ke Yan hung up and told them to go on. His voice and face weren't as gentle as before, but neither were they as icy — clearly he was in a good mood.

Which made the originally queasy-stomached Lin Zheng feel a good deal better.

Looked like he really did owe Jiang Qi some thanks.

Because they were meeting Jiang Qi's parents Saturday, Ke Yan asked, "What do your mom and dad like?"

Jiang Qi thought. "My mom likes practical things — clothes, pots and pans, kitchenware."

"Does she?" It was the first time Ke Yan had heard of someone who liked pots and pans.

"You're sure that's right?" He couldn't help asking again.

"I'm sure." Whenever someone gave the family a gift, his mother grumbled, "Why buy that? I'd rather have something useful, like pots and pans."

"My mom always says giving useless things is worse than giving her cookware — at least you can cook with it."

"I see." Ke Yan figured the things people gave the Jiang family must have been ones Mother Jiang didn't care for, or she wouldn't favor pots and pans.

"And your dad?"

"Dad likes chess — studying game records, going to the park to play."

"Chess, hm. I'm not much good. Looks like I'll have to learn." Ke Yan hadn't played much, but thinking of his future father-in-law's fondness for it, figured he'd better learn so he could play with him down the line.

Learning Father Jiang liked chess, Ke Yan went and bought game-record books to study.

Jiang Qi, seeing this, took the book from Ke Yan's hand and glanced at it. "I can't believe you'd actually go learn this. But my dad's quite the player — you'll have to put in real effort to reach the level I play at."

"You doubt me that much?" Ke Yan thought it looked simple enough.

"It's not that I doubt you — you've studied a few days; my dad's played for years." Jiang Qi knew Ke Yan was brilliant, but however brilliant, his chess couldn't outstrip someone who'd played for years.

Ke Yan did not aim to beat his future father-in-law, only to hold his own across the board.

With the trip to the Jiang house drawing close, Ke Yan studied the game records all the harder, and went to the mall to buy Mother Jiang a gift besides.

Xiao Feng, after his initial astonishment at Ke Yan poring over chess records these two days, had by now grown used to it.

And as luck had it, today was Friday — tomorrow they could rest, sleep in.

But Ke Yan and Jiang Qi didn't sleep in; they woke not long after daybreak.

After breakfast they changed and headed out, Ke Yan carrying several bags.

Jiang Qi cradled a fairly large, somewhat heavy box — unclear what was in it.

They put the gifts in the trunk and got in. Jiang Qi yawned, then suddenly hiccuped.

He thought it would pass after one, but the hiccups kept coming.

"Drink some water."

Jiang Qi drank several mouthfuls, but the hiccups wouldn't stop.

"Nerves after all, looks like — you've started hiccuping." He drank some more.

"Relax a little."

"I know." Jiang Qi tried to relax, but it had no effect. Or maybe it wasn't nerves at all — maybe he'd just eaten breakfast too fast?

The Jiang house was fairly far, so the drive took a good while, and by the time they arrived the hiccups had Jiang Qi rather miserable.

Carrying the gifts to the door, Jiang Qi's pulse suddenly quickened, a flash of nerves — because today wasn't simply coming home for a meal, but coming with Ke Yan to lay their relationship bare to his parents.

Jiang Lü opened the door and froze. The CEO truly had come to their house. She had doubted her mother, but now delight surged through her. He and her brother must be very close—perhaps close enough for their relationship to become something more.

"Xiao Lü? Hic." Jiang Qi called to her, capping it with a hiccup, not understanding why she stood there blocking the door, eyes shining, keeping him and Ke Yan from coming in.

"You're here, come in." Mother Jiang, hearing them in the kitchen, came out.

Seeing Jiang Lü blocking the doorway, she gave her a light smack on the back. "What are you standing in the door for?"

"Oh — nothing." Jiang Lü stepped aside.

With her out of the way, Jiang Qi and Ke Yan naturally saw Mother Jiang.

At the sight of his mother, Jiang Qi straightened up at once, his usual slouch gone.

He had a feeling he might get smacked by his mother today.

"Hello, Auntie." Ke Yan greeted Mother Jiang politely, his manner gentle.

"Good, good — come in, come in." She smiled, pleased, and ushered Ke Yan inside.

The two of them went in, leaving Jiang Qi alone at the door.

Has she forgotten me? Jiang Qi thought glumly.

He went in only after Ke Yan, his mother apparently caring about nothing but Ke Yan.

Still — he noticed his hiccups had stopped. Though what was far harder than stopping hiccups, he felt, was persuading his parents to agree to him and Ke Yan being together.
