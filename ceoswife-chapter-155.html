Chapter 155

I'll Remember For You 我会帮你记得的

July 2026

~5 min read

Lin Zirui took a cab to Jiang Qi's shop and recognized it at once from the plainly lettered sign: "Jiang Qi's Tree Nook." The wooden door and painted exterior immediately drew his eye.

He pushed the door open and went in, spotting at once Jiang Qi reading on the sofa.

Jiang Qi had only just come back from Ke Yan's; seeing Lin Zirui, he stood. "What brings you here?" He hadn't expected him.

"Shaoyu said you'd opened a bookshop, so I came to see — and to go home with him after work." Lin Zirui smiled too.

"Cold out, isn't it." Jiang Qi handed him the hand-warmer from his lap.

Lin Zirui took it, a little embarrassed, but didn't say thank you — that would be too formal.

"What would you like to drink?"

"Anything." Lin Zirui wasn't picky.

"Hot milk tea, then." Jiang Qi poured him a cup; Lin Zirui sipped it, looked over the shop, and lingered on the murals. "These are special — they're you and Ke Yan?"

"Mm." The murals were now the shop's most special, eye-catching feature.

"Drawn very true." He'd recognized them at a glance.

"I think so too. Chibi Ke Yan really looks like him; when we first met, he had that same cold, unapproachable face. But once you know him, he's truly wonderful." Once Jiang Qi started talking about Ke Yan, he could hardly stop.

Lin Zirui laughed, listening. The first time he'd met Ke Yan he too had wondered how anyone got close to such a cold man — but soon understood: Ke Yan's face only softened for Jiang Qi, utterly unlike with anyone else.

The two traded remarks; though they'd met only a few times, they got on well, their temperaments alike, easy together.

Lin Zirui spent the afternoon chatting with Jiang Qi, reading whenever Jiang Qi was busy, and occasionally helping out. Time flew; near quitting time, he texted Yang Shaoyu that he was waiting at the shop.

Yang Shaoyu, about to leave early as planned, got the text just then.

He was surprised Zirui would be at Jiang Qi's shop — he'd thought he'd sleep the afternoon away — but sped up packing, locking his important files in the cabinet.

He told Ke Yan and left, bringing the car from the lot and stopping outside Jiang Qi's shop.

Stepping inside, Yang Shaoyu recognized Lin Zirui at once despite seeing only his back. Zirui wore just a sweater, and as he bent over his book, the pale nape of his neck showed. Yang Shaoyu had kissed the front and sides of his throat often enough, but never that tempting stretch at the back.

But thinking that anyone walking in could see Zirui's pale neck too, Yang Shaoyu crossed quickly behind him, took the scarf draped over the sofa back, and wrapped it around Lin Zirui's neck.

Lin Zirui startled, looked back to see who it was, and relaxed at the sight of Yang Shaoyu. "You're off?"

"Mm. What are you reading?"

"Just browsing. Shall we go buy groceries now?" Lin Zirui stood.

"You're leaving?" Jiang Qi came down from the second floor and saw Yang Shaoyu.

"Mm — we'll be off." Yang Shaoyu helped Lin Zirui into his coat and wrapped the scarf well.

"Zirui, come again."

"I will." Lin Zirui waved goodbye.

In the car, Lin Zirui first texted the eldest brother to remind them not to forget dinner.

The eldest brother, who'd long since arrived at Shaoyu and Xiao Rui's place with the second, knew Xiao Rui wasn't home and was waiting at the door for them to return.

Lin Zirui and Yang Shaoyu went not to the supermarket near home but the one they passed on their usual way back — they liked buying groceries there.

Parking, they pushed a cart in; groceries were on the ground floor. Each time, Yang Shaoyu bought Lin Zirui's favorites first — Lin Zirui most liked dishes stir-fried with green pepper, though he never ate a single piece of the pepper itself.

While choosing meat, Yang Shaoyu asked, "What do Eldest and Second Brother like?"

"Eldest Brother likes fish. Second Brother likes meat and doesn't care for vegetables." Lin Zirui thought it over.

"Steamed fish and sweet-and-sour pork, then?" Yang Shaoyu had once known only two or three dishes; now, often learning new ones to cook for Lin Zirui, he'd picked up quite a few.

"All right." Lin Zirui nodded, nothing to worry about — Yang Shaoyu thought of it all.

Groceries bought, Yang Shaoyu drew him up to the second floor. "Buying something?" Lin Zirui asked.

"Didn't you say at dinner last night you wanted pudding? The snack section should have some."

"I forgot…" He'd wanted it badly last night, then forgotten today.

"I'll remember for you." Yang Shaoyu gripped his hand.

"Then always remember for me — remind me of what I forget, cook for me always." Heedless of being in public, Lin Zirui took his arm.

"Don't worry — I remember everything about you."

"Mm." Lin Zirui realized he'd grown fond of laughing since being with Shaoyu — because he was happy. It felt wonderful.

They bought everything and went home together.

By then the Lin brothers had been waiting at the door a long while. The eldest checked the time. "Why isn't Xiao Rui back yet?"

"Something must have come up." The usually impatient second brother wasn't impatient today, standing obediently at the door.

"Ding—" The elevator. Both looked over.

Out came Yang Shaoyu and Lin Zirui, Lin Zirui on Yang Shaoyu's arm.

Yang Shaoyu carried two big bags; Lin Zirui held only a small one with a few puddings.

Both smiling — Lin Zirui especially; the brothers rarely saw him this happy.

"Eldest Brother, Second Brother — you're here? Waited long?" Lin Zirui had been looking at Yang Shaoyu, and only now saw them.
