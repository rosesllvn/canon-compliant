The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 76

Lin Zirui (Part One) 林子睿上

July 2026

~4 min read

After that "blind date," Yang Shaoyu still hadn't called his mom once he got home; over here, Mrs. Yang, getting no call from Yang Shaoyu, grew anxious. She'd meant to wait for him to call her of his own accord, but after two days with no call, she couldn't wait any longer and rang Yang Shaoyu herself.

"Oh, it went well enough." Yang Shaoyu remembered that day with Lin Zirui, the corners of his mouth lifting into a smile.

"And how's Miss Lin?"

"No idea."

"No idea?" Mrs. Yang was baffled.

"Miss Lin didn't come that day; her brother did."

"What? Really?"

"Mm."

"I'll go ask." With that Mrs. Yang hung up.

Yang Shaoyu packed up to leave work, thinking it was lucky Miss Lin hadn't come the other day, because he and Lin Zirui had chatted quite happily.

From that day's talk, Yang Shaoyu had learned Lin Zirui was the third child of the Lin family, with two older brothers above and a younger sister below.

After two sons, the Lin couple had wanted a daughter, only for their third child to be another boy. When a daughter finally arrived, they poured all their attention into her and gradually neglected Lin Zirui, not yet two. His older brothers were still too young to know how to care for him.

Later, as Lin Zirui grew up, the Lin couple paid him even less mind.

And this time, the Lin family's youngest, Lin Le, hadn't wanted to come to the blind date, and after she wheedled their parents a little, they softened and let her off, sending Lin Zirui instead.

Of course, some of the less important details Lin Zirui had told him directly, while the rest Yang Shaoyu had drawn out roundabout; and Lin Zirui's own words hadn't been quite like this, but this was the gist as Yang Shaoyu pieced it together.

And so a few more days passed. One night, Ke Yan and Jiang Qi had just lain down in bed when Ke Yan's phone rang; he looked — Yang Shaoyu.

"Shaoyu, what is it?"

"I'm at the hospital right now; come quickly and help me pay the surgery and hospitalization fees."

Hearing this, Ke Yan frowned and asked at once, "Which hospital? All right, I'll come now."

"What is it?" Jiang Qi asked.

"Shaoyu's in the hospital; I'll find out the details when I get there." Ke Yan got out of bed to change.

"I'll come with you." Jiang Qi got up too, planning to go with Ke Yan.

"All right."

By the time Ke Yan and Jiang Qi reached the hospital, Yang Shaoyu was lying leisurely in a hospital bed watching TV, a doctor seated beside him.

"What happened to you?" Ke Yan glanced at Yang Shaoyu's leg, propped up high.

"Oh, I saved a kid and ended up hurt myself." Yang Shaoyu said.

"How is it now?" Jiang Qi asked with concern.

"It's fine, the surgery's done and it's all sorted; just waiting for you two to pay the surgery fee."

"I already paid the surgery fee; rest well, and if the wound really hurts, call me." said the Lin Zirui seated in the chair.

Once Lin Zirui had left, Ke Yan went to pay the hospitalization fee; Jiang Qi moistened Yang Shaoyu's lips with a damp cotton swab and asked, "You know that doctor?" Why else would the doctor pay the patient's surgery fee in advance?

"Mm, I know him — met him once, had a meal, chatted; right, we actually met at a blind date."

"Huh?"

Yang Shaoyu successfully saw a look of surprise on Jiang Qi's face.

"He actually came to the blind date in his sister's place; he's a decent guy."

"Oh." Jiang Qi figured if the other party were a woman, anyone Yang Shaoyu called "decent" would be someone he meant to pursue; if he didn't know Yang Shaoyu didn't like men, Jiang Qi would think he and Lin Zirui might have a chance. Then again — not liking men now didn't mean not liking them later; back in the day he hadn't liked men either.

Yang Shaoyu, watching Jiang Qi's expression, guessed he was off on some wild train of thought, and rapped Jiang Qi's head. "Don't go imagining things." That said, Yang Shaoyu couldn't help getting drowsy.

Jiang Qi tucked the quilt around Yang Shaoyu just as Ke Yan came back in from paying.

"I'll watch him here tonight; you go home and sleep first," Jiang Qi said.

"No need; a whole night watching is tiring — I'll stay." Ke Yan refused.

"You could hire a carer." came Lin Zirui's voice from the doorway.

Ke Yan and Jiang Qi turned to look; Lin Zirui said, "Let me find someone to look after him for you."

"All right, thank you." Jiang Qi said.

"He and Shaoyu seem to know each other?" Ke Yan asked.

"Mm." Jiang Qi told Ke Yan about Lin Zirui and Yang Shaoyu, and at the end added, "Shaoyu seems to think rather well of him."

"Is that so? They might just have a chance."

"How do you know?" Jiang Qi was surprised.

"That Lin Zirui seems to be the same as us."

"Really?"

"Just a hunch. Once the carer comes, let's go home and rest." Ke Yan casually left Yang Shaoyu to Lin Zirui and the carer, and went home with Jiang Qi.
