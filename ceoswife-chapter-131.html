Chapter 131

Pouring Water 倒水

July 2026

~4 min read

After talking a while with Jiang Qi and Ke Yan, Jiang Bu went back to his room, wanting a shower and a change of clothes.

Yun Hui followed to fetch his clothes for him.

Jiang Bu took off his coat and watched his wife's back as she got the clothes. "You don't seem to object to them being together?"

"No. Though you didn't object just now either — and you accepted it that fast?"

"I was surprised, sure, but objecting never crossed my mind. And looking at the two of them, there's nothing so wrong with it." Jiang Bu took the clothes from her to go shower.

"You're rather dashing, you know." Yun Hui smiled.

Jiang Bu blinked, then smiled too. It seemed his wife really did hope Xiao Qi and Ke Yan would be together.

While Ke Yan and Jiang Qi remained at the Jiang house, Yang Shaoyu checked the time at work. The meeting would begin soon; why wasn't Ke Yan back? Still, Ke Yan was rigorous and knew the schedule. He would surely return, so there was no need to postpone it.

Jiang Qi leaned against the sofa. He wanted Ke Yan instead, but self-consciousness in his family home held him back. The cushions were nowhere near as comfortable.

"I don't think I've seen your room yet." Ke Yan saw Jiang Qi's discomfort and guessed his thought, and proposed it.

"Right — last time we only got as far as the living room." Jiang Qi stood.

"Come on, show me your room."

"All right."

Jiang Qi led Ke Yan to his room.

Ke Yan looked it over — a blue double bed, a white desk, blue curtains, most things blue, but…

"No bookshelf?" Knowing how Jiang Qi loved to read, yet not a single shelf in the room, just two or three books stacked on the desk.

"There's a study at home — the books are all kept there."

And he made to take Ke Yan to the study.

The Jiang study had just one desk; the rest of the room held several bookshelves.

Ke Yan looked them over — every kind of book, and he pulled a manga off one shelf.

"That shelf's Xiao Lü's. We three siblings have two shelves each, one big one small — Dad bought them specially. The study's small, so it feels packed with shelves."

Ke Yan took Jiang Qi's hand and walked to one shelf. "This one must be yours."

"Mm." Jiang Qi stroked the books on it.

"Er — so you're in here." Father Jiang came in for his chess records and found the two of them there.

"Dad, we're heading back to my room. You're here for the records?"

"Mm. By the way — I get the feeling your mom isn't so against it anymore."

"Really?"

Jiang Qi looked at his father hopefully.

Father Jiang nodded.

Back in the room, the two sat on the bed.

"So this is the bed you always slept in." Ke Yan sat, stroking the blue quilt. "Tell me — if I hadn't caught your charm back then, if we'd never met, what would we have done?"

"But luckily we did meet. Luckily we're together." Jiang Qi hugged him.

Ke Yan pulled Jiang Qi down with him onto the bed. "Yes. Lucky indeed."

"Mm." Jiang Qi nestled against him.

After a while, Ke Yan realized Jiang Qi had fallen asleep in his arms. He didn't know whether to laugh. So sleepy.

He pulled the quilt up, took off Jiang Qi's coat for him, and turned on the heating.

Then he left the room to pour a cup of hot water.

In the kitchen he ran into Mother Jiang.

"Auntie."

"Where's Jiang Qi?"

"He fell asleep. I came out to pour him some water."

"Asleep? What does he need water for, asleep." She made her displeasure with Ke Yan plain.

"Jiang Qi's throat gets very dry mid-sleep and he likes a drink. Do you have a thermos cup?"

That Ke Yan understood Jiang Qi better than she did left her silent a moment; then she got him a thermos cup.

He took it, smiling his thanks.

Watching that smile, she thought her son must have been thoroughly bewitched by it.

Ke Yan poured in hot water, then a little cool, and took a sip — just right, not too hot, not cold.

"Let's talk." Watching all this, Mother Jiang suddenly spoke.

Ke Yan looked at her, gladly surprised. As long as she was willing to talk, that was good.
