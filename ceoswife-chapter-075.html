The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 75

The CEO's Wife 总裁夫人

July 2026

~6 min read

Ever since that meal at Ke Yan's home, Xiao Feng had taken a day off the very next day and holed up at home watching TV.

After a whole day of TV, Xiao Feng couldn't sleep all night, staying awake until the first ray of sunlight broke through the clouds the next morning without dropping off.

So she got up, washed, dressed, did her makeup, and once ready went to work.

Xiao Feng arrived at the company early; hardly anyone was in yet.

Sitting in her work seat, she sighed — after doing this job so long, she was genuinely a bit reluctant to leave.

As she was sighing, the elevator gave a ding; Xiao Feng looked up and saw her colleague Linda, another of Ke Yan's secretaries, step out.

Linda was startled to see Xiao Feng. "Xiao Feng, are you all right? Why's your face so pale?"

"It's nothing." Xiao Feng thought a moment and added, "Right — do you have anything to give me?" What Xiao Feng meant to ask was whether there was a resignation letter for her, but then thought, maybe there wasn't; wouldn't asking like that be strange?

"I do."

Xiao Feng's heart sank.

"I bought too much breakfast; let's eat together. You didn't come yesterday — were you sick?"

"It's nothing, just tired, took a day to rest." Xiao Feng let out a breath, then felt touched — the colleagues here were all so kind; how could she bear to leave?

After that people arrived one after another, and even Ke Yan came in, but no one brought up firing her, so Xiao Feng got through a whole morning on tenterhooks.

At the noon break, Xiao Feng quickly finished lunch and headed for the office; whatever the outcome, she had to go ask, because all this up-and-down churning in her heart was unbearable.

In the elevator she ran into Jiang Qi. Startled, she stepped in, glanced at the floor Jiang Qi had pressed — the same one she was headed to.

Beside Jiang Qi, Xiao Feng remembered joking with the other secretaries about the CEO's future wife. Surely she would be beautiful—a young lady from a good family, perhaps, or a Cinderella down on her luck.

But... no one had ever imagined it would be Manager Jiang, would be a man — fairly clean-cut in looks, decent family background, but most of all, a man?

Xiao Feng had never imagined Manager Jiang would turn out to be the CEO's wife.

Lost in thought, she was startled when Jiang Qi suddenly said, "Xiao Feng."

Xiao Feng jumped, feeling rather like she'd been talking behind someone's back, and with great relish, only to find the very person she'd been talking about had heard it all. Thoroughly guilty.

Guiltily, Xiao Feng looked at Jiang Qi, meaning to ask: Manager Jiang, what is it?

But in her nervousness, it came out as, "CE— CEO's wife, what is it?"

"..." Jiang Qi, doubting his own ears.

"..." Xiao Feng, unable to believe what she'd just said.

"Ahem." Jiang Qi cleared his throat, breaking the awkward air in the elevator.

"Ding—" The elevator arrived; Xiao Feng assumed it was the 39th floor and was about to step out when she realized it wasn't, and standing outside the elevator was — Ke Yan?

Ke Yan stepped into the elevator and saw Jiang Qi right there beside him; he gave Jiang Qi a smile and stood next to him.

"What — is the elevator broken?" Ke Yan usually took his private elevator.

"Mm, it's being repaired. Eaten yet?"

"Just did."

Xiao Feng watched the two talking in silence; a conversation that would have sounded perfectly ordinary before now sounded like there was an affair in it. She shrank to one side, trying her best to make herself unnoticeable.

Just then the elevator arrived; Ke Yan and Jiang Qi walked into the office, and Xiao Feng hesitated over whether to go in now.

After a while, Xiao Feng knocked on the door of Ke Yan's office; Ke Yan's voice came from within: "Come in."

When Xiao Feng came in she saw that Jiang Qi had somehow ended up sitting in the seat where Ke Yan worked, Ke Yan standing beside him, half-circling Jiang Qi in his arms, pen in hand, explaining something.

Actually what Xiao Feng saw — Ke Yan half-circling Jiang Qi — was just a matter of where they were positioned.

"What is it?" Ke Yan asked.

"Uh, I..."

"If it's about what happened before, don't worry; I won't fire you."

"Oh." Xiao Feng let out a breath. "Then I'll go now." And she went out, and as she shut the door she glanced again at Jiang Qi and Ke Yan, one seated, one standing, Ke Yan with a pen in one hand and the other braced on the desk, marking something on the document in front of Jiang Qi, Jiang Qi listening intently — truly well-matched, even if they were two men.

In the office, Jiang Qi said, "Xiao Feng actually called me 'CEO's wife' just now; I thought I'd heard wrong." Thinking it over, Jiang Qi found it funny — how had Xiao Feng suddenly called him that.

Ke Yan raised an eyebrow. "I'd actually like the whole company to call you that."

"What are you talking about." Jiang Qi took it as a joke.

"I'm not joking, my CEO's wife." Ke Yan bent down and kissed the side of Jiang Qi's face.

Jiang Qi's face went a little red; he didn't tell Ke Yan off for nonsense.

After the noon break Jiang Qi went back to his own office, and not long into work Li Jing knocked and came in. "Manager, there's someone named Xue Cheng downstairs in the lobby asking for you."

"Xue Cheng?" Jiang Qi was surprised. "Have him come up."

After a while Xue Cheng knocked on the door of Jiang Qi's office; Jiang Qi had him come in and had Li Jing make a cup of coffee.

"How is it you have time to come see me today?" Jiang Qi spoke first.

"I'm getting married; you absolutely must come to my wedding then, come have a celebratory drink."

"All right, I'll definitely come. Congratulations."

"Haha, then I'll be off; I still have to go deliver one to CEO Ke, though I don't know whether it'll reach him." Xue Cheng stood up.

"Take care then; congratulations." Jiang Qi didn't offer to pass it along for him.

"All right, I'll go then." With that Xue Cheng walked out; the door opened just as Li Jing was about to bring the coffee in, which shows how short Jiang Qi and Xue Cheng's exchange was.
