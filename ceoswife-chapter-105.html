The CEO’s Wife Is a Man

Chapter 105

The Search 找人

July 2026

~5 min read

Sun Nianqing frowned at her computer. She had assumed beauty could entice any man—and had complete confidence in her own—yet Ke Yan remained unmoved. Jiang Qi. The name tightened her expression further.

"Heh—Sun Nianqing, did you really think those looks would win Ke Yan over?" Wang Siqi sneered across the office. She had imagined the woman possessed some great talent, yet Sun Nianqing had fared no better than she had.

Leaving Jiang Qi and Ke Yan to their work, just then Yang Shaoyu was fretting. He'd said he'd take Lin Zirui on a date, but lately there'd been no chance. No — whatever it took, they were going to have one proper date.

Mind made up, he reached for his phone to call Lin Zirui — and it rang first. He looked at the caller ID and his brow furrowed at once, a bad feeling settling over him. Looked like another date dashed.

He answered, and a familiar voice came down the line. "Xiao Yangzi — you've fallen in love? With a man, no less?"

"Mm. Mm." Yang Shaoyu managed two listless grunts.

On the other end, Mo Lü and Ma Tong sat in a taxi headed straight for Ke Yan's company.

Ever since their father had hauled them overseas, Mo Lü and Ma Tong had been buried in introduction banquets and lessons in managing the company. They had found no time to return.

Now, at last, they had slipped away. They meant to enjoy themselves before going back to take over the company, after which there would be no more running wild.

As for why they'd come tearing back: in an email, Jiang Qi had let slip that Yang Shaoyu had someone he liked — and that the someone was a man.

They had not believed him at first. But Jiang Qi rarely lied, and once convinced, they simply had to meet the man who had captured Yang Shaoyu's heart.

The taxi pulled up at Ke Group, and they had the security guard at the door help carry their luggage down. As veteran Ke Group security, he naturally recognized the two of them.

Leaving the bags at the front desk, they took the elevator up to the 39th floor.

A new front-desk hire watched them go and asked a senior colleague in a whisper, "Who are they? They're gorgeous."

"Them? They're close with the CEO and Assistant Yang, so mind you treat them with the same respect as the boss," said the first front-desk girl.

"Mm — though I wonder if they're the CEO's and Assistant Yang's girlfriends," a second chimed in.

"No chance." A third shook her head.

"How would you know?" the others asked in unison.

"Because to my mind the CEO and Assistant Yang would look much better with men." The third girl's eyes went starry.

"Tsk—" came the collective response, everyone forgetting this particular colleague was a card-carrying fujoshi.

Ke Yan already knew from Yang Shaoyu that the two were coming back, so their sudden appearance at his office door gave him no fright.

Mo Lü was a touch let down by that, and Ma Tong promptly plopped onto the sofa and kicked off her heels.

"How come you two are back?" Ke Yan set aside his work and sat down with Mo Lü, Ma Tong, and Yang Shaoyu to chat.

"To meet Xiao Yangzi's boyfriend, of course." Mo Lü held out a hand to Yang Shaoyu.

"What?"

"A photo. Show me a photo of him — I want to see if he's good-looking." Mo Lü was dying for a look.

"I'm not showing you. Suffice it to say he's exceedingly good-looking, and you two are not to go bothering him." Yang Shaoyu was afraid the pair would make a mess of things — what if they scared Zirui off?

"You think we couldn't find out who he is on our own?" Mo Lü huffed.

Ma Tong, meanwhile, hadn't said a word this whole time — because she was busy texting Jiang Qi: Jiang Qi, come quick, there's a vixen in Ke Yan's office.

When Jiang Qi saw the message he blinked — not at the "vixen," but in surprise: they were back?

He stood and headed out, taking the elevator up to the 39th.

The others pretended not to notice him approach the elevator. Only after the doors closed did they look up. "He's definitely gone to the thirty-ninth floor."

Everyone nodded.

Reaching the 39th and seeing Mo Lü and Ma Tong, Jiang Qi cried, delighted, "You really are back!"

"Mm — Jiang Qi, come here, let us hug you, we've missed you." Mo Lü and Ma Tong opened their arms.

Jiang Qi went over and hugged them both.

As Jiang Qi bent down, Mo Lü winked at him. He caught on at once and murmured into her ear, "XX Hospital."

Mo Lü nodded.

Jiang Qi let go and sat, and they talked a while more — until Mo Lü, unable to stand it any longer, said she and Ma Tong had to be off.

"Wait and we'll all leave together after work," Yang Shaoyu suggested.

"No — we need to go buy some clothes first; we didn't bring many this trip." Mo Lü lied without batting an eye.

"All right then, I'll get back to work." Yang Shaoyu still had a mountain of it waiting.

Before Yang Shaoyu could grow suspicious, Mo Lü and Ma Tong slipped out, flagged a taxi, and headed for Lin Zirui's hospital.

Jiang Qi's email had given them a name—something like Lin Zirui. But even at the right hospital, they knew neither his department nor his floor.

Standing outside, Mo Lü thought she'd better call Jiang Qi and ask.

The call connected, but no one picked up.

She hung up and decided to go in and search.
